> Authoritative common-location table for all 70 characters in the English edition.
> The anchor is T1's base (Castle of Friendship, Ponyville); every bearing/band/confidence is measured from it.
> Data: v1 65-place coordinate set + v1 character cards + multi-site cross-checking (see `05-research-ledger.md`).

## 1. Anchor definition

- **Origin (0,0):** Ponyville town centre — Castle of Friendship (same coordinate as the former Golden Oak Library site).
- **Why T1:** per the client's requirement, the first team's location is the reference; T1 keeps the original first group and adds Spike.
- **Axes:** x = east, y = north (rough equirectangular estimate, km; not a survey).

## 6. Mirror world (Equestria Girls)

- The human world is a separate dimension with its own coordinates (CHS / Canterlot City origin); do not convert km.
- CHS is narratively the mirror of Canterlot, Camp Everfree the mirror of the Everfree area, but distances are human-world travel.
- Cross-realm travel uses the Crystal Mirror / portals (P4), not distance.

## 7. Travel modes and distance calibration

| Mode | Notes | Effect on bands |
|---|---|---|
| Walk / trot | Town-scale | adjacent 0-30 min; local 1-3 h |
| Flight | Cloud-dale to Ponyville about 1 h; weather-dependent | local; escort needed for high-speed transport |
| Train | Ponyville-Canterlot about 2-5 h; long routes 1-2 days | regional / far |
| Airship / balloon | Slower but crosses terrain | far / remote alternatives |
| Teleport | Short range, story-limited | plot travel only |
| Crystal Mirror / portal | Cross-dimension | not distance-based |

## 8. Uncertainty and source conflicts

- **Everfree direction:** Lauren Faust places it west; The Art of Equestria map places it east. Working value: at Ponyville's edge, Medium.
- **Sweet Apple Acres direction:** SW (Faust) vs SE (official map); both retained, High for adjacency.
- **Canterlot distance:** estimated from travel scenes only; no official km figure.
- **G5 settlements:** the official promotional map and the in-film hand-drawn map disagree; all mappings are Low.
- **Exact km values** are rough v1-project estimates, not canon measurements; prefer bearing + band + confidence.
