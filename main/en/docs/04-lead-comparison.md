## 4. Selection decision tree

```text
What does your mission need most?
│
├─ Structure, planning, traceability, many parallel tracks ──→ Candidate 1 Twilight Sparkle (default)
├─ Authority, diplomacy, legitimacy, long cycles ────────────→ Candidate 2 Princess Celestia / Daybreaker
├─ Night operations, dreams/subconscious, hidden crises, hard decisions ─→ Candidate 3 Princess Luna
└─ Relationship repair, family/faction fractures, ceremonies, gentle firmness ─→ Candidate 4 Princess Cadance
```

**If you want two of them:** choose one "lead overall commander" for the whole picture and make the other candidate a **chief adviser** (this does not change the chain of command, it only provides a contrasting view).
For example, candidate 1 leads planning + candidate 4 handles team emotions; candidate 2 leads diplomacy + candidate 3 handles nighttime risk.

## 6. Signals for changing the overall commander

- **Three key decisions in a row miss their deadlines**, or the team starts making decisions around the commander on its own.
- **The commander's core weakness keeps getting hit by the mission** (e.g. #1 freezes when there is no information, #2 loses trust by withholding information, #3 has no daylight check on nighttime judgment, #4 is too soft against pure malice).
- **The customer's needs shift**: from "planning" to "diplomacy/night/unity" or another kind of mission.
- **Team morale keeps falling**: no one speaks in meetings, members start "performing obedience".
- **Conflict between the commander's values and the customer's**: e.g. the customer rejects #2's secretive style, #3's hard night authority, or #4's idealism.

**Signals that do NOT justify a change:** one failed mission, one heated conflict, one captain who disagrees - handle these with P2/P11 first, rather than replacing the commander.
