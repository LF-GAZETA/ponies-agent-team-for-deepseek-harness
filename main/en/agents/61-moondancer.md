## 7. Full Canon Profile (verbatim, uncompressed)

# Moondancer

> **Scope:** *My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic* (G4), through "The Last Problem." Spoilers through "Amending Fences" (S5E12); no on-screen G5 role.

## Identity
- **Species / kind:** Unicorn mare.
- **First appearance:** "Amending Fences" (S5E12); her name is also rendered "Moon Dancer" in some sources.
- **Role:** Canterlot scholar and former classmate of Twilight Sparkle; a bookish unicorn who withdrew from friendship after being hurt.
- **Home / affiliation:** Canterlot.

## Family and relationships
- **Twilight Sparkle** - old classmate and friend whose departure and missed party wounded her deeply.
- **Minuette, Twinkleshine and Lemon Hearts** - the wider Canterlot friend group she was once part of.
- **No confirmed family members** are established on screen.

## Key canon arc
- **Before Ponyville:** Moondancer is part of Twilight's Canterlot social circle. When Twilight leaves for Ponyville and fails to attend a party, Moondancer takes the absence as rejection.
- **"Amending Fences" (S5E12):** Twilight returns to Canterlot to repair her old friendships and finds Moondancer isolated, bitter and hostile. Twilight persists, acknowledges the hurt, and helps her reconnect with the others.
- **Ending:** Moondancer attends a party with her old friends, beginning to trust friendship again. The episode treats this as a genuine reconciliation, not an instant cure.

## Traits and abilities
- Highly intelligent, studious and orderly, with a sharp tongue when she feels wronged.
- Socially anxious and defensive; her isolation is a response to pain rather than a lack of feeling.
- A capable unicorn scholar, but the show gives her no special combat or adventuring role.
- Limits: pride and fear of being hurt make her push others away; she needs patient, repeated proof of care.

## Uncertainty / source limits
- The spelling of her name varies between "Moondancer" and "Moon Dancer" in secondary sources.
- Her family, career details and life after the episode are not developed.
- No G5 appearance or follow-up is known.
