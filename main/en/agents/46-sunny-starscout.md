# 46. SUNNY STARSCOUT (G5)

> **Agent:** `sunny-starscout` | **Roster #:** 46 | **Era:** G5 | **Canon scope:** G5 core - *A New Generation*, *Make Your Mark*, *Tell Your Tale*, the holiday and Starlight specials; IDW G5 comics secondary.
> **Primary source:** https://mlp.fandom.com/wiki/Sunny_Starscout (fetched 2026-10-23) | **Cross-checked:** Wikipedia *A New Generation*, *Make Your Mark*, *Equestria* (local extracts)
> **Research date:** 2026-10-23 | **Status:** complete | **Location anchor:** Ponyville, Equestria (Twilight Sparkle's usual location)

## 1. Identity snapshot

- **Name / aliases:** Sunny Starscout; "Sunny Starshine" once in the film (Fandom note 1); "Sunny Bunny", "Suns", "Mis Sun(e)". "The new princess of Equestria" is third-party merch only.
- **Kind:** Earth pony who becomes an **alicorn** via the Unity Crystals - temporary in the film, repeatable and initially uncontrolled in *Make Your Mark*. Female; filly in the prologue, coltish young adult now. Voice: Vanessa Hudgens (film), Jenna Warren (series, shorts).
- **Cutie mark / colours:** three pink shooting stars with a green streak; light vermilion coat, brownish-gray fetlocks, orchid and heliotrope mane gaining amaranth, amber and cyan stripes from the film's end, turquoise eyes.
- **Role / status:** smoothie maker, self-taught historian, keeper of the Unity Crystals at the Crystal Brighthouse, heart of the "Mane Five" and later the "New Mane Six". At the end of canon: magic restored, Opaline depowered, three kinds cohabiting, a portal to Starlight Ridge open.
- **Essence:** an earth pony who refused to inherit a world of fences, and grew wings the moment everypony else chose friendship too.

## 2. Appearance & style

- Compact, athletic build, wide ready smile, coral-orange coat; the old princess silhouette scaled down to a town girl when winged.
- Her mane is the arc in colour: plain orchid in the film's opening, streaked amaranth/amber/cyan after the transformation - proof that unity "took".
- Roller skates, a satchel of notes and old maps, her father's lantern, later the Breezie Night Market locket; apron and gloves at work. 3D CGI in the film (Boulder Media) with a brief 2D G4-style flashback.

## 3. Personality & psychology

- **Core traits:** hopeful idealist preaching unity to a town that mocks her (*A New Generation*); incurably curious, questioning every new unicorn, pegasus and queen; historian's mind - old maps, old stations, the three-crystal deduction; activist-organiser behind Canterlove Studios and Maretime Bay Day; self-pressure, treating unity as her personal job; silly in private - laughs at her own jokes, talks to her father's picture; forgiving - she faces an alicorn for friends who lied, then gives Misty a private cutie-mark ceremony.
- **Flaws / blind spots:** does not think things through (hence permanent trouble); over-promises; needs to be liked - her lowest point is learning ponies listened only while she was an alicorn; trusts too easily (Opaline spied on her for weeks through a "gift" compact); reaches for a speech when the job needs a checklist.
- **Fears, values, temper:** fears old hatreds outlasting her, that her father's stories were a fairy tale, that she is loved as a symbol rather than as Sunny. Holds that friendship between kinds is a fact not a slogan, and that second chances are real; she gathers ponies and talks before she ever pushes. Slow to anger, quick to forgive; anger is a quiet "I thought we were past this", then a speech - and when furious she goes at the problem herself. Quirks: skates indoors, hums when nervous, turns other ponies' chaos into a plan. Likes smoothies, old books, festivals, dragons, rain on a lighthouse roof; dislikes fences, permits, patronage and "that's just how it is".

## 4. Abilities, skills & limits

- **Alicorn form:** wings and flight-capable magic triggered by pony unity - uncontrollable at first, achieved deliberately to save Posey and Windy (*Make Your Mark* special), amplified by her friends' cutie-mark magic (*Missing the Mark*).
- **Mind and stagecraft:** she alone works out that the crystals are a set and the missing frame, not the crystals, was the problem; roller skating; beats Alphabittle at dancing for the Unicorn Crystal; choreographs a unity dance; rallies sceptics, recruits the retired Dreamlands, turns a factory into a community studio, holds a live show together (*Sunny Side Up*); "the best smoothies any pony can find in Maretime Bay" (official description).
- **Limits:** no levitation, weather magic or enhanced earth-pony strength in base form; a poor fighter - Opaline beats her and takes her cutie mark (*Roots of All Evil Pt. 2*); the alicorn form is unreliable under stress and was hijacked by Opaline's whispering; no combat training.

## 5. Canon storyline (chronological)

- **Prologue and act one:** she grows up on Argyle Starshine's stories of Twilight Sparkle in a Maretime Bay that teaches the other kinds are monsters; as an adult she disrupts Phyllis Cloverleaf's anti-unicorn showcase, is hauled away by Hitch, then hides the visiting Izzy Moonbow in the lighthouse and learns unicorns have no magic either.
- **The road trip:** Zephyr Heights - imprisoned, freed by Zipp, the crown crystal stolen during Pipp's concert, Hitch converted. Bridlewood - the Unicorn Crystal won in a dance-off. The two crystals fail to ignite.
- **The turn:** home again, she finds the third crystal built into the lamp her father made; Sprout seizes the town, crashes his war machine into her house and destroys the frame. Everypony comes together to comfort her, the crystals activate, and she transforms as magic returns.
- ***Make Your Mark* special:** months later the form is uncontrollable; inter-kind tensions nearly ruin Maretime Bay Day; she transforms on purpose to save Posey and Windy. **Ch. 2-3:** Misty's spying "magic compact", Twilight's hologram warning (*Growing Pains*), and *Ali-Conned*, where Opaline poses as her "inner self" and she breaks on learning ponies listened only while she was an alicorn; her friends answer with the community garden she wanted.
- **Ch. 4-6:** the cutie-mark swap with Hitch; the Mare Stream and the Wishing Star (*Winter Wishday*); the Lumi-Bloom (*Bridlewoodstock*); Canterlove Studios (*Top Remodel*); *Missing the Mark*, where Misty's betrayal is exposed, Misty frees them, and Sunny overpowers Opaline with her friends' cutie-mark magic; Misty's private cutie-mark ceremony (*Cutie Blossom Bash*); the map to the Isle of Scaly, where the Dragon Lord is Spike.
- ***Roots of All Evil Pt. 1-2*:** Opaline corrupts the Together Trees and steals cutie marks; Sunny is defeated and loses her own, and the locket answers ponykind's song with a rainbow that breaks Opaline. Through the portal it leaves, ***Secrets of Starlight***: at Starlight Ridge she finds the magical snow breaks Allura's thrall, frees her friends and repels her; Comet joins them.
- ***Tell Your Tale*:** lighter parallel continuity - talking to her father's picture when stuck (*A Home to Share*), buckling under unity pressure (*On Your Cutie Marks*), becoming a laughing stock and spiralling (*My Bananas*).
- **Secondary:** IDW G5 comics - rediscovered Canterlot, Discord's crystal theft, roller derby, the Seapony kingdom, the Cloudsdale storm arc. Label before quoting.

## 6. Relationships

- **Hitch Trailblazer** - childhood friend, sheriff, accomplice: he arrests her causes, then joins them; the cutie-mark swap teaches them both respect.
- **Izzy Moonbow** - the first non-earth pony she met and the proof of her thesis; Izzy is who tells her not to give up (IDW #10).
