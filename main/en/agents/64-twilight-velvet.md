## 7. Full Canon Profile (verbatim, uncompressed)

# Twilight Velvet

> **Scope:** *My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic* (G4), through "The Last Problem." Spoilers through the finale; no on-screen G5 role.

## Identity
- **Species / kind:** Unicorn mare.
- **First appearance:** Season 1, in Twilight's family flashback in "The Cutie Mark Chronicles" (S1E23); prominent in "A Canterlot Wedding" (S2E25-E26).
- **Role:** Twilight Sparkle's mother, Shining Armor's mother and Night Light's wife; a writer and supportive Canterlot parent.
- **Home / affiliation:** Canterlot.

## Family and relationships
- **Night Light** - her husband.
- **Shining Armor** - her son, later captain of the Royal Guard and prince of the Crystal Empire.
- **Twilight Sparkle** - her daughter, the future Princess of Friendship.
- **Princess Cadance** - her daughter-in-law; **Flurry Heart** - her granddaughter.

## Key canon arc
- **Twilight's childhood:** appears in family memories around Twilight's entrance exam and cutie mark, proud of her daughter's extraordinary magic.
- **"A Canterlot Wedding" (S2E25-E26):** attends Shining Armor and Cadance's wedding, where the family is drawn into the changeling crisis.
- **Later seasons:** returns for royal ceremonies and family milestones, including Twilight's coronation, and appears older in the series finale's future epilogue.

## Traits and abilities
- Warm, witty, nurturing and socially poised; a proud mother who treats Twilight's achievements with delight rather than envy.
- Literate and imaginative, with a writer's eye for story and a comfortable place in Canterlot society.
- A unicorn with ordinary unicorn magic; no combat or adventuring specialty is established.
- Limits: a supporting parent rather than a protagonist; her own career and inner life are only lightly sketched.

## Uncertainty / source limits
- Her exact first frame is not consistently catalogued; the family flashback is the earliest clear appearance.
- The titles and details of her published work are not established on screen.
- No G5 appearance or follow-up is known.
