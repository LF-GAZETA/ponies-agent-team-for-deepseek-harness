## 7. Full Canon Profile (verbatim, uncompressed)

# Diamond Tiara

> **Scope:** *My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic* (G4), through "The Last Problem." Spoilers through the finale; no on-screen G5 role.

## Identity
- **Species / kind:** Earth pony filly.
- **First appearance:** "Call of the Cutie" (S1E12).
- **Role:** Wealthy Ponyville schoolfilly; former classroom bully and later a Cutie Mark Crusaders ally.
- **Home / affiliation:** Ponyville; the Rich family and its Barnyard Bargains business.

## Family and relationships
- **Filthy Rich** - her father, a businesspony who runs Rich's Barnyard Bargains.
- **Spoiled Rich** - her mother, whose social expectations drive much of Diamond's behavior.
- **Silver Spoon** - her closest friend and usual partner in the early bully scenes.
- **Cutie Mark Crusaders** - targets of her teasing, then ponies she makes peace with.

## Key canon arc
- **Early seasons:** Diamond Tiara and Silver Spoon mock Apple Bloom and the other "blank flanks," establishing the Crusaders' first recurring antagonists.
- **"Crusaders of the Lost Mark" (S5E18):** the pressure from her mother and a class election bring her insecurity to the surface. She finally resists her mother's cruelty, makes peace with the Crusaders, and begins a genuine reform.
- **Later appearances:** she is mainly a background classmate, shown to be on better terms with the Crusaders. Her redemption is real but is not given a second dedicated arc.

## Traits and abilities
- Sharp-tongued, status-conscious and socially confident, with a need for approval underneath.
- Socially capable and able to lead when she chooses; as an earth pony she has no special magic.
- Her cutie mark is a tiara, but the show does not spell out a single "talent" it represents.
- Limits: a child's social power, not physical or magical power; her growth depends on choosing friends over status.

## Uncertainty / source limits
- The exact meaning of her cutie mark and the full Rich family history are not established on screen.
- She is not a member of the Cutie Mark Crusaders; "ally" is the accurate term.
- No G5 appearance or follow-up is known.
