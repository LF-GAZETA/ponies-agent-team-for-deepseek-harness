## 7. Full Canon Profile (verbatim, uncompressed)

# Limestone Pie

> **Scope:** *My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic* (G4), through "The Last Problem." Spoilers through the finale; no on-screen G5 role.

## Identity
- **Species / kind:** Earth pony mare.
- **First appearance:** "Hearthbreakers" (S5E20).
- **Role:** Eldest Pie sister; hard-edged worker and authority figure at the family rock farm.
- **Home / affiliation:** The Pie rock farm outside Ponyville.

## Family and relationships
- **Igneous Rock Pie and Cloudy Quartz** - her parents.
- **Maud Pie, Pinkie Pie and Marble Pie** - her younger sisters.
- **The Apple family** - holiday hosts and rivals in "Hearthbreakers"; the visit exposes how differently the two clans keep tradition.

## Key canon arc
- **"Hearthbreakers" (S5E20):** Limestone runs the rock farm with a sharp tongue and little patience for the Apples' Hearth's Warming customs. The competing traditions nearly ruin the holiday before the families find a way to share it.
- **Later appearances:** she remains a supporting Pie family figure, defined by the rock farm and her uncompromising manner rather than a personal arc.

## Traits and abilities
- Abrasive, blunt, short-tempered and proudly unsentimental, but loyal to her family and the farm.
- Physically tough and practical, suited to heavy rock work and a harsh landscape.
- Protective of her home and suspicious of outsiders, though capable of grudging respect.
- Limits: an earth pony with no magic; her rigidity causes as many problems as it solves.

## Uncertainty / source limits
- The show does not detail the farm's ownership transition; her role as eldest does not automatically equal a formal title.
- Her private feelings and any life away from the rock farm are largely unexplored.
- No G5 appearance or follow-up is known.
