## 7. Full Canon Profile (verbatim, uncompressed)

# Silver Spoon

> **Scope:** *My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic* (G4), through "The Last Problem." Spoilers through the finale; no on-screen G5 role.

## Identity
- **Species / kind:** Earth pony filly.
- **First appearance:** "Call of the Cutie" (S1E12).
- **Role:** Affluent Ponyville schoolfilly; Diamond Tiara's closest friend, a former bully and later a reformed classmate.
- **Home / affiliation:** Ponyville; one of the town's wealthier families.

## Family and relationships
- **Diamond Tiara** - her best friend and usual partner in the early "blank flank" teasing.
- **Cutie Mark Crusaders** - the classmates she and Diamond torment before making peace with them.
- **No named parents or siblings** are established in the show; her family is identified mainly by its upper-class status.

## Key canon arc
- **Early seasons:** Silver Spoon follows Diamond Tiara's lead, supplying polished insults and reinforcing the school's social hierarchy.
- **"Crusaders of the Lost Mark" (S5E18):** Diamond's behavior and family pressure strain their friendship, and Silver Spoon has to weigh loyalty against what is right. By the end she and Diamond make peace with the Crusaders.
- **Later appearances:** she is written mainly as a background classmate, with small signs that her behavior has softened.

## Traits and abilities
- Articulate, well-mannered and image-conscious, with the confidence of a privileged upbringing.
- More of a follower than an initiator, but capable of independent judgment when the cost is clear.
- Wears glasses and uses a precise, upper-class speaking style; as an earth pony she has no magic.
- Limits: social influence rather than physical power; her development is limited by her supporting role.

## Uncertainty / source limits
- The show gives her little solo focus, so her life outside school and her family details are largely unknown.
- She is a reformed bully and ally of the Crusaders, not one of their members.
- No G5 appearance or follow-up is known.
