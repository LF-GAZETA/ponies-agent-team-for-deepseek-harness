# Babs Seed

> **Scope:** *My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic* (G4), through "The Last Problem." Spoilers through "One Bad Apple" (S3E4) and later Crusaders material; no on-screen G5 role.

## Identity
- **Species / kind:** Earth pony filly.
- **First appearance:** "One Bad Apple" (S3E4).
- **Role:** Applejack's cousin from Manehattan, a Cutie Mark Crusader and later a hairstylist.
- **Home / affiliation:** Manehattan; the Apple family; the Manehattan branch of the Cutie Mark Crusaders.

## Family and relationships
- **Sunflower** - her older sister.
- **Apple Bloom** - her cousin and closest link to the Ponyville Crusaders.
- **Applejack, Big McIntosh and Granny Smith** - extended Apple family.
- **The Cutie Mark Crusaders** - the friends who help her confront her fear and accept her as a member.

## Key canon arc
- **"One Bad Apple" (S3E4):** Babs arrives in Ponyville, is mocked as a "blank flank," and lashes out by joining Diamond Tiara and Silver Spoon against the Crusaders. When the Crusaders learn she is bullied in Manehattan, they choose to help her rather than retaliate.
- **Aftermath:** Babs makes peace with the Crusaders, joins their group and starts a Manehattan branch, becoming a recurring symbol that the Crusaders' mission extends beyond Ponyville.
- **"Bloom & Gloom" (S5E4):** she earns her cutie mark as a hairstylist, confirming that her own talent lies outside apple farming.

## Traits and abilities
- Tough-talking, guarded and cool on the surface, with a strong fear of looking weak.
- Loyal, resourceful and practical; she learns to own her mistakes instead of passing the pain onward.
- A talented hairstylist, with the manual dexterity and fashion sense the cutie mark implies.
- Limits: an insecure child who initially mistakes cruelty for protection; no magic or combat role.

## Uncertainty / source limits
- Her parents and home life in Manehattan are only sketched; the show focuses on the bullying cycle and her recovery.
- "Bloom & Gloom" establishes the hairstyling talent, not a detailed career.
- No G5 appearance or follow-up is known.
