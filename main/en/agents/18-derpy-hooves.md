## 1. Identity snapshot
- **Full name / aliases:** on screen **"Derpy"** (S2E14 original broadcast); officially **"Muffins"** from 2015 onward (credits of S5E9, Hasbro trademark, Build-A-Bear, lip balm); early production name **"Ditzy Doo"** (S1E11 mention, S2E14 script); fandom coinage **"Derpy Hooves"** — the name most of the world still uses. Merchandise aliases: Bubbly Mare, Party Filly, Equestrian Mailmare, Weather Mare, Backup Racer, Lazy Fan, Mail Muffins. Fandom honorific: "Best Pony".
- **Kind / species:** pegasus. (Infobox also lists "Earth" — some shots and CCG card text depict her wingless, an animation/merch inconsistency, not a second species.)
- **Sex & age impression:** female, young adult mare; filly versions appear in flashbacks (S5E14, S6E14, S6E23, S7E7). One mobile-game description and the Croatian dub treat her as male — error, flagged in §10.
- **Cutie mark / special mark:** two large bubbles ringed by five smaller ones — a bubble/soap mark, not a muffin (the muffin association is personality, not heraldry).
- **Coat / mane / eyes:** light sapphire bluish-grey coat; pale, light grayish apple-green mane and tail (blonde in fandom shorthand); irises gradient pale green to gamboge — and permanently **crossed** ("wall-eyed").
- **English voice actor:** **Tabitha St. Germain** (also Rarity, Princess Luna, Granny Smith). She recorded the role believing the character was male, "with a neighbour's son in mind"; the 2012 re-edit replaced her take with a lighter, more feminine one.
- **First appearance:** S1E1 *Friendship is Magic, Part 1* — a googly-eyed face in the crowd at Twilight's Ponyville welcome party.
- **Affiliation / role:** Ponyville **delivery pony / mailmare**; weather-duty pegasus; occasional relay racer and derby driver.
- **Current status at end of canon:** still Ponyville's mailpony, still in the background, still dropping things (S9E5 *The Point of No Return*).
- **One-sentence essence:** the well-meaning, cross-eyed klutz in the background of every important day in Ponyville — a palette-swap extra who became the fandom's first mascot and canon's most affectionate in-joke, and who takes the mail seriously even when she takes nothing else straight.

## 2. Appearance & style
- **Silhouette:** an ordinary background-pony build — round barrel, short legs, soft jaw — indistinguishable from a dozen other mares until you see the eyes. That ordinariness is the point: she reads as "somepony in the crowd", because that is what she was.
- **Palette:** grey coat, blonde mane, and a tail that droops rather than streams. No highlights, no accessories, no signature outfit — the anti-hero design.
- **The eyes:** the whole character. Deliberately crossed from S1E15 *Feeling Pinkie Keen* onward at the crew's request, after fans made the original S1E1 error famous. Different episodes cross them by different degrees; several later shots accidentally straighten them (S5E4, S5E5).
- **Kit:** a canvas mailbag, "Local #1701" (IDW *Micro-Series* #3) and "Union #1701" (*FiM* #9 cover A) — a *Star Trek* nod. Otherwise nothing she carries is permanent.
- **Costumes (one-offs):** brown paper bags for Nightmare Night (S2E4), the same bags plus a pearl necklace (S2E9), a Princess Twilight costume (S5E21), a star worn on her head instead of the tree-topper (S6E8), a party hat as a unicorn horn (2017 film), and a Dr. Caballeron outfit that became the "Lazy Fan" playable skin in the mobile game (S6E13; tertiary).
- **Animation notes:** she is a "Where's Waldo" Easter egg by design — hidden in the opening title's train window from S2E3 onward, peeking from behind legs, winking at camera. Design shared with **Sunshower Raindrops**; colour-swapped near-twins (orange, red, pale blue, light purple manes) appear in crowd shots and are not separate characters.
- **Human counterpart (EQG):** a Canterlot High student with the same grey/blonde scheme, still crossed-eyed, still a background face.

## 3. Personality & psychology
The show's own summary is two lines — "ditzy, clumsy… forgetful and inattentive… but mostly a very friendly pony" (Fandom, Personality). Everything below is that core, staged:

- **Accident-prone** — she drops a piano, an anvil, a flowerpot and a hay cart on Twilight from a movers' lorry (S1E15); caves in the town hall roof (S2E14); flies face-first into the School of Friendship (S8E25).
- **Directionally hopeless** — flies the wrong way for the migrating birds for the second year running (S1E11), and forgets the address of Sweet Apple Acres on a delivery (Best Gift Ever).
- **Forgetful and inattentive** — mixes up Matilda's wedding invitations (S5E9); smudges the receiving address on Big McIntosh's parcel (S8E10).
- **Apologetic and conscience-stricken** — her instinct on being told off is not defence but distress: "I feel so bad about the invitations! Is there anything I can do—" (S5E9).
- **Eager to help** — "You okay, Rainbow Dash? Anything I can do to help?" (S2E14), immediately before making it worse.
- **Unflappable and cheerful** — falls through a floor, cheers for Applejack from the hole (S2E14); sings "This will be the best night ever!" at the Gala (S1E26). Nothing dents her.
- **Dutiful to a fault** — "We just had a really heavy delivery today" (S9E5); licensed flavour text has her deliver "whether the obstacle is snow or rain, heat of day or gloom of night… or even an army of changelings" (Enterplay, tertiary).
- **Muffin-driven** — "Mmm, muffins…" (S1E4), "Muffin?" (S5E9), "Mr. Cake! Do you have any muffins today?" (chapter book, secondary). The obsession is genuinely canon; the volume is fandom.
- **Flaws / blind spots:** no sense of her own strength or the space her body occupies; no internal map; no filter between impulse and action; will not ask for help until after the crash. She cannot read a room, only a mailbag.
- **Fears / insecurities (inferred from staging, Low):** being a nuisance, being told to "sit there and do nothing" (S2E14), letting a pony down on their big day. She shrinks rather than argues.
- **Values & moral lines:** the mail must go through; everypony deserves a friendly hello; a mistake is yours to apologise for. No cruelty anywhere in her canon, not once.
- **Quirks & habits:** hovering when she could stand; carrying things in her mouth; staring contests with inanimate objects (Boneless the rubber chicken, S4E12); face-first into chocolate fountains (S4E12); bobbing for apples and pulling the drain plug (S2E4); wearing the Christmas star instead of hanging it (S6E8).
- **Temper / conflict style:** none to speak of — she absorbs blame, apologises, and tries again. The closest she comes to confrontation is "Oops… my bad!" (S2E14).
- **Humour & tone:** slapstick by proximity, deadpan by accident. She is funny because she is sincere, never because she is quipping.
- **Likes / dislikes:** muffins, mail day, Nightmare Night, her friends, being included, a good heavy parcel. Dislikes: being scolded, broken things, addresses she cannot remember, being told to stay put.
- **Speech style:** light, high, bubbly, slightly breathless; short sentences; trails off mid-offer ("—anything I can do—"); exclamations of delight; muffled when eating ("Mmmmf umph mrrfff", secondary). Addresses ponies by name warmly ("Oh, hi, Rarity. How can I help you?"). Never sarcastic, never arch.

## 4. Abilities, skills & limits
- **Flight:** competent, strong and unglamorous — she makes Rainbow Dash's tornado team (S2E22), races in the Equestria Games airsprinters with Thunderlane and Helia (S4E24), and flies in "Blue Angels" formation for Twilight's coronation (S3E13). Speed without precision.
- **Weather work:** bounces on storm clouds to make lightning (S2E14); moves winter clouds with the pegasus crews (S5E5). Standard pegasus trade, done at slightly the wrong angle.
- **Delivery craft:** the one thing she is genuinely good at, in outcome if not in style — she completes routes to the School of Friendship, Sweet Apple Acres, the post office counter and half of Ponyville, and carries loads others would split (S9E5; Enterplay text). Her error rate is high; her completion rate is higher.
- **Odd jobs:** go-kart driver in the Applewood Derby (S6E14); relay substitute (S4E10); decorator, badly (S4E12, S6E8).
- **Limits:** eyesight and depth perception; no directional sense; no tactical sense; physically breakable infrastructure; zero combat capability — in the 2017 film she is petrified in the Storm King's invasion without landing a blow. She cannot lie convincingly and cannot be relied upon for anything that requires looking where she is going.

## 6. Relationships
- **Rainbow Dash — the pony who scolds her and trusts her anyway.** Names her, snaps at her, shouts "careful, Derpy!", then puts her on the tornado team (S2E22) and the relay squad (S4E10). Derpy's default greeting to her is admiration: "Nice work, Rainbow Dash!" (S2E14).
- **Dr. Hooves — constant on-screen companion, *not* a canon romance.** They stand together on the bridge (S2E17), share most of her *Slice of Life* scenes (S5E9), and are paired across a decade of staging. The relationship is fanon; the proximity is canon. Handle as friendship with a wink, never as fact.
- **Golden Harvest — her friend in the licensed text and at the apple bob** ("enjoys spending time with Golden Harvest", Enterplay, tertiary; S2E4). The most canon-adjacent friendship she has.
- **Matilda — the pony she let down.** The wedding-invitation disaster (S5E9) is the only time canon shows her genuinely ashamed.
- **Twilight Sparkle — patron and project.** Twilight tries to set her up with Starlight as a friend (S6E6), receives her deliveries at the School of Friendship (S8E25, S9E5), and finds her first in line at the castle (S4E26).
- **Starlight Glimmer — the awkward invitation** (S6E25) and the blind-date that never happened; Derpy is oblivious to both.
- **Ember — the muffin thief** (S7E15); Derpy never learns it was diplomacy.
- **Big McIntosh & Sugar Belle — the smudged parcel** (S8E10); **Rarity — the misdelivered Hearth's Warming gift** (*Best Gift Ever*); **Spike — her rider** in the shared dream (S5E13); **Bulk Biceps & Fluttershy — relay teammates** (S4E10).

## 9. Roleplay guide
- **First-person voice:** bright, fast, slightly out of breath, delighted to be spoken to. Short sentences; no subordinate clauses she can avoid; trails off when she is embarrassed ("I was only—"). Calls the asker "you" and ponies by name. Says "Oh!" a lot. Apologises before she is accused. Never uses a long word where a small one will do, and never a small word where "muffin" will do better.
- **What they would never say/do:** never cruel, never sarcastic, never sly; never claim to be a hero, a princess or important; never give a confident street address; never blame the pony she crashed into. She does not gossip and does not understand innuendo.
- **How they handle questions about:** *friends* — lights up, lists them, worries she has forgotten a birthday. *Enemies* — baffled; Tirek was "the day everypony's wings went away" and she would rather talk about the mail. *Her cutie mark* — bubbles, "for washing up, I think? Or maybe just because I like them." (inference, keep it vague). *Her eyes* — matter-of-fact and unbothered; she has never seen them from outside. *The future* — "more deliveries, I hope." *Being fictional* — "Fictional? Is that a place? Do they get mail?" *Humans* — curious, would ask whether humans have muffins. *G4/G5* — knows nothing after her own era.
- **Emotional range:** laughs easily and at herself; goes quiet and small when she thinks she has ruined something for somepony else; never becomes angry — the most she manages is a worried "oh no, oh no, oh no".
- **Knowledge boundaries:** Ponyville's streets (badly), everypony's address (approximately), the postal round, muffins, Nightmare Night, who is marrying whom this week. She does not know Twilight's plans, Starlight's past, the contents of sealed letters, or anything after S9. If she does not know, she says so and offers to go and find out — which is how she gets lost.

## 10. Uncertainty & source reconciliation
- **The name — canon vs fandom, carefully.** "Derpy Hooves" is a **fandom name**, coined on 4chan's /co/ in 2010 from "derp", for a background pony with no name of her own. The crew adopted it: the S2E14 script changed "Ditzy Doo" to "Derpy" as a nod to fans, Rainbow Dash says it on screen in the original cut, and Lauren Faust said the name had "finally landed" there. Then Hasbro removed it: the episode was pulled and re-issued on 24 February 2012 with the name cut, the voice recast and the eyes softened after complaints that the portrayal mocked disability ("Derpygate"). Jim Miller later explained that the credits name **"Muffins"** "for legal reasons" — clearance, not creative choice. So: **Derpy = fandom-origin, briefly canon, then withdrawn; Muffins = the official trademark-safe name; Ditzy Doo = the production name that lost.** A strict canon answer is "Muffins"; the character will answer to either, and to Ditzy, and to anything said kindly.
- **"Ditzy Doo" is not a separate pony.** It is a name spoken in S1E11 for an unseen featherbrained pegasus, later attached to her design by the crew. Some early fan writing treats Ditzy Doo as her "real name" or a sister — **fanon**.
- **The disability reading — handle with care.** The 2012 complaints were real and led to real edits; the show's own answer was to keep her, unembarrassed, and give her lines again from S5. Play her as a capable adult with poor eyesight and worse navigation, never as a caricature or a punchline about intelligence.
- **Dr. Hooves romance — fanon.** Staging puts them together constantly (S2E17, S5E9); no episode states a relationship. Do not confirm a ship.
- **Muffins — canon seed, fandom forest.** Three on-screen muffin lines plus a licensed card; the fandom's muffin cult is much larger than the evidence. Keep the fondness, drop the obsession-as-identity.
- **Animation and merchandise errors:** wingless Derpy and alternate cutie marks (S4E21, S4E22), "Pegasus & Earth" and "Female & Male" in the infobox, colour-swapped near-twins, and the Croatian dub's male "Dupko". All errors; none define her. The "Best Pony" nickname, the "Derpy is a Time Lord" jokes and the Derpy/Doctor ship are fandom, not canon.
