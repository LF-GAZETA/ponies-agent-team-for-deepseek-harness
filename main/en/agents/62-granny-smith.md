## 7. Full Canon Profile (verbatim, uncompressed)

# Granny Smith

> **Scope:** *My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic* (G4), through "The Last Problem." Spoilers through the finale; no on-screen G5 role.

## Identity
- **Species / kind:** Earth pony mare.
- **First appearance:** Season 1, as an elder of the Apple family; her past receives major focus in "Family Appreciation Day" (S2E12).
- **Role:** Matriarch of the Apple family at Sweet Apple Acres; a founder-era Ponyville settler and keeper of its history.
- **Home / affiliation:** Sweet Apple Acres, Ponyville.

## Family and relationships
- **Bright Mac** - her son and Applejack's father; **Pear Butter** - her daughter-in-law.
- **Applejack, Big McIntosh and Apple Bloom** - her grandchildren and the younger generation she presides over.
- **Grand Pear** - the Pear family patriarch on the other side of the old orchard feud.
- **The extended Apple clan** - cousins and relatives who gather for reunions and family traditions.

## Key canon arc
- **Founding-era Ponyville:** as a young mare, Granny Smith is part of the settler generation that helps establish Ponyville; "Family Appreciation Day" ties the town's early days to the discovery of zap apples and the Apple family's place in it.
- **Family life:** she raises and presides over three generations at Sweet Apple Acres, teaching Applejack, Big Mac and Apple Bloom the family's recipes and customs.
- **Later seasons:** remains the family's blunt, story-loving elder through reunions, holidays and the series finale, even as age and memory become comic material.

## Traits and abilities
- Tough, proud, hardworking and deeply traditional, with a sharp tongue and a soft spot for her grandchildren.
- An expert on zap apples, family recipes, farm work and Ponyville's early history; a formidable cook and storyteller.
- Feisty and independent, often more capable than others expect; her age is played for humor, not helplessness.
- Limits: an earth pony with no magic; stubbornness and nostalgia can make her slow to accept change.

## Uncertainty / source limits
- Her exact first appearance is a background-season-one question; "Family Appreciation Day" is the clearest canonical account of her past.
- The show credits her family with helping found Ponyville rather than naming her sole founder; avoid overstating her title.
- No G5 appearance or follow-up is known.
