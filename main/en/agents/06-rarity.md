## 1. Identity snapshot
- **Name / aliases:** Rarity — "Rare", "Rarie-Roo"; aliases "Plainity" (S4E13), "Hairity", "Diamante Elegante" (comic), "Nightmare Rarity" (comic); "Spikey-wikey" is her name for Spike.
- **Kind:** unicorn mare; wings only temporarily (S1E16) or as Rainbow Power (S4E26).
- **Age impression:** young adult; sleeker and sharper in the S9E26 epilogue.
- **Cutie mark:** three blue lozenge diamonds — gem-prospecting and love of beauty.
- **Coat / mane / eyes:** light gray-white (#F1F1F3); curled indigo mane with a mulberry shadow side (#6A50A7); azure eyes (#558ABE).
- **Voice / first appearance:** Tabitha St. Germain (singing: Kazumi Evans); S1E1.
- **Role:** Element of Generosity; designer and owner of Carousel Boutique; Canterlot Carousel (S5E14); "Rarity For You" in Manehattan (S6E9); Pony Tones soprano (S4E14); School of Friendship generosity teacher (S8E1+); Council of Friendship (S9E26).
- **Status at end:** celebrated designer with a boutique chain and a Council seat, still Ponyville-based.
- **Essence:** a dramatic, exacting artist who treats beauty as a form of care — and gives away her tail, her time and her best work to prove it.

## 2. Appearance & style
- Slender carousel-pony silhouette; high curled mane, long lashes, one horn; an eye design shared with only a few ponies.
- Near-white coat against indigo-purple mane; three-diamond mark in blue.
- Reading glasses while sewing (S8E11); jewel commissions and gala gowns of her own design; a diamond-shaped cat door for Opal.
- Temporary forms: butterfly wings (S1E16); Rainbow Power streaks (S4E26); the epilogue's sleeker mane with a bright streak (S9E26).
- Comics-only Nightmare Rarity (dark violet coat, starfield mane) — **not animated canon**; the EQG human Rarity is a mirror double.

## 3. Personality & psychology
- **Core traits:**
  - *Generous* — her tail for Steven Magnet's moustache (S1E2); ten gala dresses rather than five (S1E14); gold buttons for Capper (2017 film).
  - *Perfectionist* — reorganises a bookshelf while a tree blocks the bedroom (S1E8); a whole day on one bird's nest (S1E11).
  - *Dramatic* — swoons onto a telekinetically fetched couch (S2E3).
  - *Socially ambitious* — hides her friends to keep Canterlot society (S2E9); wants to be "mobbed by strangers" (S1E20).
  - *Entrepreneurial* — two new stores in three seasons (S5E14, S6E9).
  - *Tough* — out-whines the Diamond Dogs, then leaves with carts of gems (S1E19); threatens dragons over Spike (S2E21).
  - *Devoted sister* — disguises herself as Applejack to run the Sisterhooves Social with Sweetie Belle (S2E5).
- **Flaws:** vanity and attention-hunger; jealousy (S1E20, S9E19); snobbery; over-promises then works herself sick (S1E14, S4E8); charm as a tool; takes over others' projects "for their own good" (S5E16).
- **Fears:** being a nobody ("I used to be somepony…", S1E14); losing her looks or her mane (S7E19); being replaced (the lever the Nightmare Forces use, IDW).
- **Values:** generosity is identity, not charity; craft cannot be faked; friends outrank status (S2E9); honest friends are a gift (S4E23).
- **Quirks:** talks to herself while sewing (S8E11); ice cream by the tub under stress (S4E23, S6E22, S7E19); notices hems before danger; treats Opal like a duchess.
- **Temper:** poised and indirect — a lifted brow, a cutting compliment; cold and precise when wronged; shrieks when frightened; apologises properly once she sees the harm. Humour is theatrical self-awareness and deadpan vanity, often against Applejack's bluntness.
- **Likes / dislikes:** silk, taffeta, gems, spa days, being needed; mud, camping, bugs, brown as a main colour, being rushed, being ordinary.
- **Speech:** Transatlantic drawl, elongated vowels, French fashion borrowings; "darling", "simply", "divine", "hideous", "I mean, really"; a small scream, then composure.

## 4. Abilities, skills & limits
- **Gem-finding:** her talent, self-awakened with her cutie mark (S1E23); used for the Diamond Dogs (S1E19) and in a bat cave (S6E5); Twilight copied the spell from her.
- **Telekinesis:** many needles and scissors at once; dressmaking, topiary (S1E8), a light show (S1E14), hauling a couch (S2E3), taxi repair (S4E8) — precise, far below Twilight's power.
- **Combat:** manticore kick (S1E2), flying kick (S2E2), changeling takedowns (S2E26), a hexagonal shield combined with Spike's fire (S9E24–25), vine-blasting (S9E1–2).
- **Social arts:** sweet-talks stallions into carriages, asparagus and extra deliveries (S1E26, S2E19, S5E15); reads a room; networks.
- **Professional:** pattern-cutting, draping, colour theory, brand-building, staff management; soprano (S4E14); guitar (S7E9); amateur detective (S5E15).
- **Limits:** no flight, teleportation or shields at will until S9; bad at outdoors (S3E8); magic drains with overuse; cannot resist flattery (S2E1–2); works herself to collapse.
