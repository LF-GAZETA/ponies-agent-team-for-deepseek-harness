## 7. Full Canon Profile (verbatim, uncompressed)

# Star Swirl the Bearded

> **Scope:** *My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic* (G4), through "The Last Problem." Spoilers through the Pillars' return; no on-screen G5 role.

## Identity
- **Species / kind:** Ancient unicorn stallion.
- **First appearance:** Long established as a historical legend before his on-screen debut in "Shadow Play, Part 1" (S7E25).
- **Role:** Legendary wizard, leader of the Pillars of Equestria and mentor to Princess Celestia and Princess Luna; inventor of spells that shape later Equestrian history.
- **Home / affiliation:** Ancient Equestria; the Pillars of Equestria; later the present-day era.

## Family and relationships
- **Princess Celestia and Princess Luna** - his former students and royal successors.
- **The Pillars of Equestria** - Rockhoof, Mistmane, Flash Magnus, Somnambula and Mage Meadowbrook; his companions and fellow guardians.
- **Stygian** - the gifted scholar whose fall helped create the Pony of Shadows, and whose friendship Star Swirl initially failed to defend.
- **Twilight Sparkle** - his scholarly successor, who completes his unfinished spell.
- **Scorpan** - a friend from beyond Equestria, whom he helped turn against Tirek.

## Key canon arc
- **Ancient era:** Star Swirl leads the Pillars, creates the Elements of Harmony's foundation and banishes the sirens to the human world. He also authors the time-travel spell later used by Twilight and Starlight.
- **The Pony of Shadows:** the Pillars sacrifice themselves to imprison the Pony of Shadows in limbo, removing Star Swirl from Equestria for roughly a thousand years.
- **"Shadow Play" (S7E25-E26):** Twilight and her friends free the Pillars. Star Swirl's rigid "once a villain, always a villain" view initially alienates Stygian, but he learns to accept redemption and friendship.
- **Later seasons:** returns as an ally in the final crisis, his legend now matched by the friends who changed his mind.

## Traits and abilities
- A polymath and master of nearly every known spell, from time travel and banishment to star magic and protective wards.
- Formal, proud and brilliant, with the bearing of a teacher who expects to be listened to.
- Deeply learned in creatures, artifacts and Equestrian history; his beard and bell-lined cloak are iconic.
- Limits: pride, secrecy and black-and-white moral judgment; he can be emotionally obtuse and slower than Twilight to trust redemption.

## Uncertainty / source limits
- His exact age and the full chronology of his thousand-year span are not precisely fixed on screen.
- Comics and guidebooks add details that are secondary to the animated series.
- No G5 appearance or follow-up is known.
