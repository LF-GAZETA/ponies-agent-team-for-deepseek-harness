## 7. Full Canon Profile (verbatim, uncompressed)

# Cheerilee

> **Scope:** *My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic* (G4), through "The Last Problem." Spoilers through the finale; no on-screen G5 role.

## Identity
- **Species / kind:** Earth pony mare.
- **First appearance:** Season 1; her classroom and first major speaking role are established in "Call of the Cutie" (S1E12).
- **Role:** Teacher at the Ponyville Schoolhouse; one of the town's steadiest adult figures.
- **Home / affiliation:** Ponyville; Ponyville Schoolhouse.

## Family and relationships
- **Students** - Apple Bloom, Sweetie Belle, Scootaloo, Diamond Tiara, Silver Spoon and many background classmates.
- **Big McIntosh** - the Crusaders' love-poison scheme in "Hearts and Hooves Day" (S2E17) briefly pairs them; it is not a lasting canon romance.
- **No confirmed spouse, children or immediate family** are established in the show.

## Key canon arc
- **"Call of the Cutie" (S1E12):** teaches a lesson on cutie marks, setting up Apple Bloom's anxiety and the Crusaders' founding.
- **"Hearts and Hooves Day" (S2E17):** the Crusaders dose her and Big McIntosh with a love potion; the results are comedic and awkward, and the episode treats the pairing as a joke rather than a future relationship.
- **Later seasons:** remains the schoolteacher through lessons, ceremonies and the series finale, usually as a patient but firm authority figure.

## Traits and abilities
- Warm, encouraging and organized; genuinely fond of her students and invested in their development.
- Firm enough to manage a chaotic class, with a dry sense of humor and a taste for order.
- Skilled at teaching, planning lessons, chaperoning and getting children to think for themselves; she has no special magic.
- Limits: an earth pony with no combat role; one teacher carrying a large class in a small school.

## Uncertainty / source limits
- Cheerilee appears among Ponyville's background residents in early season one; sources do not consistently name her exact first frame.
- Her family and private life are largely unexplored; do not turn the Big Mac flirtation into a marriage.
- No G5 appearance or G5-era information applies.
