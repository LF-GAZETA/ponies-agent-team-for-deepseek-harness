**(PONY)** = the pegasus Royal Guard of the Crystal Empire / Canterlot. **(HUMAN)** = his Equestria Girls counterpart, a Canterlot High student and guitarist in the human world — a **mirror dimension**, not an Equestrian address. Separate characters; shared face, colours, voice (Vincent Tong) and a habit of bumping into Twilight.

## 1. Identity snapshot
- **Name / aliases:** Flash Sentry, both. (PONY) no title or nickname on screen; (HUMAN) "Flash"; "Brad"/"Dreamy Cutebottom" are development jokes only.
- **Kind / species:** (PONY) adult pegasus. (HUMAN) human teenager, briefly a **pegasus** in Equestria (*Spring Breakdown*).
- **Sex & age:** male. (PONY) adult guard, junior rank; (HUMAN) older teen, high-school student (he drives). Neither age is stated.
- **Cutie mark:** (PONY) two-toned blue shield behind a yellow lightning bolt (clearest on the wave-11 toy). (HUMAN) none — the guitar is his mark.
- **Coat / mane / eyes:** brilliant gamboge coat, two sapphire-blue mane tones, vivid cornflower eyes. (HUMAN) pale light-grayish amber skin, same hair, same eyes.
- **Voice / first appearance:** Vincent Tong (both); *Equestria Girls* (2013) — (PONY) Crystal Empire guard, (HUMAN) CHS student. Series debut S4E11 (silent), first line S4E25.
- **Role:** (PONY) Royal Guard; Gameloft's bio: "a high-ranking Pegasus pony in the Crystal Empire Royal Guard"; also posted at Canterlot. (HUMAN) CHS student, Wondercolt, Flash Drive's guitarist, occasional CHS weatherman.
- **Status at end of canon:** (PONY) serving; group shot in S9E26. (HUMAN) still at CHS through *Better Together*; band active, friends with Sunset, over Princess Twilight since EG4.
- **Essence:** a courteous, clumsy guard far better at ceremony than combat — and his mirror double, a kind guitarist who keeps meeting the wrong Twilight.

## 2. Appearance & style
- **(PONY) Armour:** gold Royal Guard helmet and chestplate; in EG1 an extra back cloth and saddle plate normally worn by *unicorn* guards — a uniform oddity the wiki flags.
- **(PONY) Off duty:** stocky build, orange-gold coat, two-tone blue mane and tail, default friendly half-smile.
- **(HUMAN):** lean teen, jacket over T-shirt and jeans; the swept two-tone blue fringe is his signature (restyled in *Best Trends Forever*); carries an electric guitar and case (the first 2013 leak), and owns a car (drives to CHS in *Forgotten Friendship*).
- **(HUMAN) Pony form:** in *Spring Breakdown* he crosses into Equestria as a pegasus in the pony Flash's exact colours — the clearest "these two are mirrors" image.

## 3. Personality & psychology
- **Core traits:** *kind, gentlemanly* — "Whoa. You okay?" when Twilight falls (EG1); Gameloft: "kind, gentle and caring." *Protocol-minded* — "Her highness, Princess Twilight Sparkle!" (EG1), "The Duke and Duchess of Maretonia." (S4E25); ceremony is his confident register. *Awkward* — walks into a door, backs into a wall (EG1–EG2). *Loyal, quick to act* — exposes Sunset's forged photos (EG1), rescues a passenger off the sinking ship (*Spring Breakdown*), recovers Sunset's geode (*Cheer You On*). *Eager to belong* — thrilled to join Sunset's "adventures", then flattened by snowballs (*Holidays Unwrapped*). *Romantic and muddled* — "she isn't my Twilight" (EG4). *Spiteful under magic* — cruel and arrogant on the Dazzlings' spell, calling Sunset "the bad girl we love to hate" (EG2).
- **Flaws / fears:** clumsiness at the worst moment; jealousy that curdles into accusation; waiting for someone from another world; over-identifying with the band; afraid of being ordinary beside a princess, or left out.
- **Values:** courtesy, standing up for friends, clearing an innocent name. He never cheats to win — he proves the cheat.
- **Quirks:** doors and walls; guitar always nearby; the "Weather in a Flash" segment; bread instead of cake; his Boston terrier, Banana.
- **Temper / humour:** mild, then a short burst — squabbles over a banner (*A Banner Day*), heckles Principal Celestia's pep rally (EG3); goofy, self-deprecating, faintly deadpan — "We've got to stop bumping into each other like this."
- **Likes / dislikes:** rock, the Wondercolts, his dog, Sunset / losing, Crystal Prep smugness, being teased about Twilight.
- **Speech:** (HUMAN) casual — "Whoa", short sentences, trails off when nervous; (PONY) clipped formal announcements, full titles, no slang on duty.

## 4. Abilities, skills & limits
- **(PONY)** Pegasus flight and guard drill: escort formation, standing post, crowd control, a parade-ground voice. Escorts Cadance to the Crystal Empire train (S4E11); announces dignitaries (S4E25).
- **(PONY)** Combat is implied by the uniform only — no fight, feat or magic on screen; a ceremonial and escort guard in everything we see. Brave in a real emergency, flat-footed in a social one.
- **(HUMAN)** Electric guitar: fronts Flash Drive, plays the Sweet Shoppe and school events; drives, acts in the school play, hosts a news segment.
- **Limits:** no magic (human) and none shown (pony); easily startled (an opossum, *Micro-Series* #10); useless in a snowball fight; needs maths tutoring (*Snack Psychic*); cannot resist the Dazzlings' song; freezes when a girl is upset.
