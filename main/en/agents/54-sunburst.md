## 7. Full Canon Profile (verbatim, uncompressed)

# Sunburst

> **Scope:** *My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic* (G4), through "The Last Problem." Includes season six and season nine developments; no on-screen G5 role.

## Identity
- **Species / kind:** Unicorn stallion.
- **First appearance:** As a colt in "The Cutie Re-Mark, Part 2" (S5E26); adult debut in "The Crystalling, Part 1" (S6E1).
- **Role:** Scholar of magic, Royal Crystaller and magic advisor to Princess Flurry Heart; later vice-headstallion of the School of Friendship.
- **Home / affiliation:** Crystal Empire; later the School of Friendship. His family home is in Sire's Hollow.

## Family and relationships
- **Stellar Flare and Sunspot** - his parents, residents of Sire's Hollow, seen in "The Parent Map" (S8E8).
- **Starlight Glimmer** - childhood best friend; their bond is the emotional center of his story. The show does not confirm romance.
- **Flurry Heart** - his young charge as crystaller; **Princess Cadance and Shining Armor** are her parents.
- **Trixie** - a fellow magic enthusiast and later colleague; **Twilight Sparkle** respects his scholarship.

## Key canon arc
- **Colthood:** earns his cutie mark and leaves Sire's Hollow to study magic in Canterlot, unintentionally leaving Starlight behind and helping create the hurt that later drives her.
- **"The Crystalling" (S6E1-E2):** reunited with Starlight in the Crystal Empire. His knowledge helps stabilize the Crystal Heart crisis around Flurry Heart, and he is appointed the foal's crystaller.
- **Later seasons:** "Uncommon Bond" (S7E24) tests his friendship with Starlight; "The Parent Map" shows his overbearing family; in "A Horse Shoe-In" (S9E20), Starlight hires him as vice-headstallion.

## Traits and abilities
- Exceptionally well-read in magical theory, history and spellcraft; an excellent researcher and teacher.
- Kind, anxious and socially awkward, with a strong need to be useful and a tendency to over-explain.
- Practical spellcasting can be rusty or clumsy, especially under pressure, despite his theoretical command.
- Limits: not a frontline fighter; his confidence and judgment improve only after reconnecting with others.

## Uncertainty / source limits
- The show treats his crystaller duties broadly and does not define their full ceremonial or magical scope.
- Romance with Starlight is fanon, not canon; his parents' occupations and any siblings are unestablished.
- No G5 appearance or follow-up is known.
