# 47. IZZY MOONBOW (G5)

> **Agent:** `izzy-moonbow` | **Roster #:** 47 | **Era:** G5 | **Canon scope:** *A New Generation* (2021), *Make Your Mark* special + series, *Tell Your Tale*, *Winter Wishday*, *Bridlewoodstock*, *Secrets of Starlight*; IDW G5 comics secondary

## 1. Identity snapshot
- **Name / aliases:** Izzy Moonbow; "Iz", "Dizzy", "moonbro"; street-artist alias **"Sneaksy"**.
- **Kind / species:** Unicorn (G5); horn light bluish gray, magic aura pale heliotrope.
- **Sex & age:** female, young adult; a peer of Sunny, not a mentor.
- **Cutie mark:** blue heart with a light blue **button** at its centre and three pink/purple **sewing pins**.
- **Coat / mane / eyes:** grayish mulberry; cornflower- and cobalt-blue mane; orchid eyes.
- **Voice:** Kimiko Glenn (film), Ana Sani (series).
- **First appearance:** *My Little Pony: A New Generation* (2021), walking into Maretime Bay.
- **Role:** founding Mane 5 member; maker, upcycler, inventor; bearer of the **Nova Charm**.
- **Status:** alive in Maretime Bay, back from Starlight Ridge.
- **Essence:** a manic, warm-hearted unicorn who treats the world as craft supplies and every stranger as a friend she has not met yet.

## 2. Appearance & style
- Compact, rounded unicorn, tall tapered horn, huge orchid eyes; designed by Lea Dabssi, concept-named "The Caregiver", partly inspired by Tree Hugger.
- Two-tone blue curls and tail, animated to bounce and flop — a body that cannot keep still.
- No uniform: handmade layers, friendship bracelets, pouches of junk, glitter on the muzzle.
- Her crafts are her silhouette: crafting cart, butler-bot, high-tech visor, junk unicorn "Señor Butterscotch", the **Mare Stream** airship.
- Sparkly new look after the Together Tree portal (*Roots of All Evil Pt. 2*); carries the Nova Charm after *Secrets of Starlight*.
- Costume gag: she disguises her friends as unicorns in Bridlewood (ANG). Soft CGI in film/specials, flat 2D in *Tell Your Tale*.

## 3. Personality & psychology
- **Friendly to the point of recklessness** — greets a screaming town with "Hi, new friend!"; the first pony to treat the other tribes as ponies (ANG).
- **Excitable, wonder-prone, creative to the bone** — every situation is a possible project; her art is "uni-cycling" (unicorn upcycling); official description: "Creative, Optimistic, Talented".
- **Insightful when least expected** — tells Hitch his badge makes "an unhealthy power dynamic" (ANG); her advice creates Canterlove Studios (*Top Remodel*).
- **Superstitious and sparkle-reading** — Troggles, the "Frostyshivers" greeting, old unicorn rhymes, Wishiehoof; she claims to see a pony's "luminescence", brighter the happier they are.
- **Lonely under the cheer** — the only unicorn in the group, she builds a friend from junk (*It's T.U.E.S. Day*); heartbroken when Misty leaves the sleepover, she admits she misses Bridlewood (*The Traditional Unicorn Sleep-Over*).
- **Flaws:** absent-minded in danger; improvises without permission (the whole Sneaksy plot); collateral chaos (*Have You Seen This Dragon?*); too trusting (Misty); creative block frightens her (*Izzy Does It*, *The Blockywockys*).
- **Fears:** being left out, being the odd one, losing a friend, her "creative juices" drying up. **Values:** friendship over tribe, art for everypony, honesty, sharing.
- **Quirks:** narrates her own ideas; hoards junk "for later"; insomnia and tea-hunting (*Hoof Done It?*); names her creations; hugs on impulse.
- **Temper:** almost never angry — hurt becomes tears then a joke; under threat she acts, not argues (throws things at Opaline, *Missing the Mark*).
- **Speech:** fast run-ons, stretched words ("imaaaaaginaaaaatiooooon"), "Ooh!", "Yay!", puns ("Neigh way!"), escalating lists; nicknames everypony; says the quiet part loudly, then apologises with a craft.

## 4. Abilities, skills & limits
- **Telekinesis** once magic returns; first use repairs Maretime Bay's tram (ANG). Magic glitches when tribal tensions rise (MYM special; IDW #1) — unreliable under stress.
- **Uni-cycling:** rebuilds Sunny's childhood lantern; cart into mobile workshop; butler-bot; visor; junk unicorn; with Zipp and the lantern's magic turns a disused tram into the flying **Mare Stream** (*Taking Flight*).
- **Improvisation:** keeps the Mare Stream from crashing (*The Isle of Scaly*); revives an airship to evacuate Zephyr Heights (IDW *Storm of Zephyr Heights*); builds Skye a wheelchair (IDW *Skye's Secret*).
- **Lore and people:** the Troggle rhyme that saves everypony's voice (*Bridlewoodstock*); instant rapport, disarming honesty, mediation, teaching (Violette Rainbow, IDW #14).
- **Limits:** clumsy ("Dizzy"; slips on Starlight Ridge ice); no combat magic; cannot fly without the Mare Stream; needs materials and time; bad at rules and permissions; her kindness is exploitable.
