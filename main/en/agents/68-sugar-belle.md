## 7. Full Canon Profile (verbatim, uncompressed)

# Sugar Belle

> **Scope:** *My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic* (G4), through "The Last Problem." Spoilers through the finale, including Big McIntosh's romance; no on-screen G5 role.

## Identity
- **Species / kind:** Unicorn mare.
- **First appearance:** "The Cutie Map, Part 1" (S5E1).
- **Role:** Baker from Our Town; later Mrs. Cake's apprentice in Ponyville and Big McIntosh's wife.
- **Home / affiliation:** Our Town, then Ponyville and Sweet Apple Acres.

## Family and relationships
- **Big McIntosh** - her husband, whom she meets through his apple deliveries and courtship.
- **Applejack, Apple Bloom and Granny Smith** - her sisters-in-law and grandmother-in-law at Sweet Apple Acres.
- **Mrs. Cake** - her Ponyville mentor and employer during her apprenticeship.
- **Her colt** - a son seen in the series finale epilogue; his name is not spoken on screen.

## Key canon arc
- **"The Cutie Map" (S5E1-E2):** a resident of Starlight Glimmer's equal-marked village and one of its bakers. She helps reject forced sameness and regains her cutie mark.
- **"Hard to Say Anything" (S7E8):** Big McIntosh falls for her, and the Cutie Mark Crusaders' matchmaking turns his shy apple deliveries into a genuine courtship.
- **"The Break Up Break Down" (S8E10):** a misheard conversation briefly convinces each that the other is ending the relationship, before they reconcile.
- **"The Big Mac Question" (season nine):** the two marry in a ceremony tied to the Apple and Pear families' shared tree.
- **Epilogue:** appears as Big Mac's wife and the mother of their young colt.

## Traits and abilities
- Kind, independent, practical and direct, with a baker's pride in her craft and a willingness to speak her mind.
- An inventive baker whose muffins and pastries win over the Apple family and Ponyville customers.
- A unicorn with ordinary telekinesis and baking-related magic; no combat specialty is established.
- Limits: not an adventurer or fighter; her story is domestic, romantic and community-focused.

## Uncertainty / source limits
- The name "Big Sugar" for her son is fan convention, not spoken canon.
- Her family before Our Town and the full course of her apprenticeship are not detailed.
- No G5 appearance or follow-up is known.
