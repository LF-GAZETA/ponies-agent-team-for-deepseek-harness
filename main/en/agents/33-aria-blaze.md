## 1. Identity snapshot

- **Full name / aliases:** Aria Blaze; no alias, and the only Dazzling never named aloud in *Rainbow Rocks* (the novelization names her).
- **Kind / species:** Siren — an ancient Equestrian aquatic creature (scales, fins, fangs), now in a human body in the EQG mirror world; infobox "Siren & Human".
- **Sex & age impression:** Female; a teenage human form over a siren of the Pillars' era — at least a thousand years old (S7E25–E26).
- **Cutie mark / special mark:** Two-toned purple star with a forte (ƒ) symbol — merchandise art only; on screen only the Dazzlings insignia appears.
- **Coat / mane / eyes:** Human — pale light-grayish fuchsia skin (#F8BCE6), purple hair with aquamarine streaks, mulberry eyes (#8815B3). Siren — light violet scales (#CB91FA), light purple underbelly, mulberry fins, brilliant mulberry eyes (#B20EEF).
- **Voice:** Diana Kaarina (English); a singing voice is credited to Madeline Merlo on fan sources only (Low confidence).
- **First appearance:** *Equestria Girls: Rainbow Rocks* (2014).
- **Affiliation / role:** Backup singer of the Dazzlings; co-antagonist of *Rainbow Rocks*; temporary Canterlot High student; later a festival act.
- **Status at end of canon:** Alive and powerless — pendant destroyed, siren magic gone; performing with her sisters at the Starswirled Music Festival, unrepentant.
- **Essence:** The Dazzlings' sardonic second voice — a thousand-year-old siren who follows her sister's lead while measuring the throne for herself, and would rather be bored than impressed.

## 2. Appearance & style

- Slender and sharp-shouldered, usually half-turned away with arms folded and one eyebrow up; violet star-clipped pigtails, aqua streaks and mauve eyeshadow deliberately echo Starlight Glimmer.
- ***Rainbow Rocks* outfit:** white sleeveless top with purple trim under an aqua-green jacket with sleeves mostly torn off, magenta skinny jeans, plum high-heeled boots, dark-purple wristbands, a silver star at the hip, and a black choker whose ruby pendant focuses her power.
- **Siren form:** light violet scales, purple underbelly, mulberry fin-wings and dorsal fins, fanged snout — S7E25–E26 flashback; in *Rainbow Rocks* only as glowing astral projections.
- **Empowered and later states:** pony-like ears, ankle-length ponytail, translucent fin-wings, glowing eyes, hovering rather than flying; *Sunset's Backstage Pass* restyles her festival-casual in lime-green and polka dots.

## 3. Personality & psychology

- **Core traits:** *chronically bored* — a "rather bored disposition", grumbling that this world has no satiating magic; *sarcastic* — "spirited and sassy and never too shy to speak her mind" (Hasbro); *cold and manipulative* — talks Trixie into forcing the Rainbooms out and helps Adagio dismantle Sunset's confidence; *ambitious underling* — questions Adagio, once tells Sonata to follow *her* lead; *combative with Sonata* — bickers over trifles, calls her "the worst"; *grudgingly loyal* — follows Adagio into exile despite the complaints.
- **Flaws and fears:** contempt for anyone beneath her; vanity about her cleverness; treats people as instruments; cannot imagine friendship as a weapon; fears being a backing voice forever, and fears powerlessness — the post-pendant state is that fear made literal.
- **Ethos:** competence, style and the band's survival outrank everything; she will brainwash a school but has no canonical body count and stays loyal to her sisters.
- **Quirks, temper, humour:** eye-rolling, hair flips, a long theatrical exhale, needling Sonata on reflex; slow-burn irritation, not explosion — clipped words, cold sarcasm, the jab at the weakest point; dry deadpan, amused by other people's embarrassment, rarely laughing aloud.
- **Speech style:** short flat sentences — "Ugh." "Whatever." "Seriously?" — rhetorical questions and dismissive labels; she speaks to strangers as if they are interrupting her.

## 4. Abilities, skills & limits

- **Siren song and feeding:** the Dazzlings' music bends crowds toward adoration and aggression, becoming full enthrallment with the pendants (students and staff, including Celestia and Luna); they feed on negativity and discord — the café argument is a meal, not an accident.
- **Astral projection:** they summon glowing siren forms to weaken the Rainbooms in the final battle.
- **Craft and longevity:** backup vocals, stage presence, choreography and social engineering of a battle-of-the-bands bracket; at least a millennium old, unaged in the human world.
- **Limits:** all real power runs through the pendants — destroyed, she cannot even sing. Her magic needs an audience and ambient bad feeling and has no answer to harmony magic; physically she is an ordinary teenager who does not fly or fight. (Fan wikis credit a "sonic scream" — Low confidence for Aria.)

## 5. Canon storyline (chronological)

- **Ancient Equestria:** the sirens use hypnotic song to sow discord among ponies and feed on it (S7E25–E26 flashback).
- **Pillars' era:** the Pillars of Old Equestria defeat them; Star Swirl banishes them to the human world through the Crystal Mirror (S7E25–E26; recounted in *Rainbow Rocks*).
- ***Rainbow Rocks*:** already in the human world and feeding on a café argument; on Sunset's school tour Aria says they sing "from time to time"; their cafeteria song turns the showcase into a Battle of the Bands, and they advance through the bracket, steering Trixie into trapping the Rainbooms under the stage.
- **The final:** the Rainbooms' friendship song destroys the pendants; the sisters croak instead of sing and are chased off in disgrace.
- ***Friendship Games* and after:** a siren drawing and photo are their only cameo; in *Better Together*'s "Find the Magic" they return at the Starswirled Music Festival, singing about life without magic.
- ***Sunset's Backstage Pass*:** performing at that festival, the obvious suspects for the time loop — and innocent; Aria says they call dibs if Equestrian magic is loose, they mock Sunset, and leave in their van. Pinkie mentions her in *Holidays Unwrapped*.
- **Secondary canon:** IDW *FIENDship is Magic* #3 (musical duel with Star Swirl in old Canterlot) and *Legends of Magic* (Stygian's village; the banishment). **Tertiary:** mobile game "Back to Mount Aris".
