## 7. Full Canon Profile (verbatim, uncompressed)

# Night Light

> **Scope:** *My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic* (G4), through "The Last Problem." Spoilers through the finale; no on-screen G5 role.

## Identity
- **Species / kind:** Unicorn stallion.
- **First appearance:** Season 1, in Twilight's family flashback in "The Cutie Mark Chronicles" (S1E23); prominent in "A Canterlot Wedding" (S2E25-E26).
- **Role:** Twilight Sparkle's father, Shining Armor's father and Twilight Velvet's husband; a supportive Canterlot parent.
- **Home / affiliation:** Canterlot.

## Family and relationships
- **Twilight Velvet** - his wife.
- **Shining Armor** - his son and a Royal Guard captain.
- **Twilight Sparkle** - his daughter and later Princess of Friendship.
- **Princess Cadance** - his daughter-in-law; **Flurry Heart** - his granddaughter.

## Key canon arc
- **Family flashbacks:** appears with Twilight Velvet around Twilight's entrance exam and cutie mark, sharing the family's pride in her.
- **"A Canterlot Wedding" (S2E25-E26):** attends Shining Armor and Cadance's wedding and is caught up in the changeling attack.
- **Later seasons:** returns for family and royal occasions, including Twilight's coronation, and appears older in the finale's future epilogue.

## Traits and abilities
- Affectionate, calm and quietly proud, with a fatherly warmth that balances Twilight Velvet's more outgoing manner.
- Supportive of his children's ambitions without trying to manage them; comfortable in Canterlot's upper-class circles.
- A unicorn with ordinary unicorn magic; no combat, scholarly specialty or profession is established.
- Limits: a background parent whose work and private interests remain largely unexplored.

## Uncertainty / source limits
- His exact first frame is not consistently catalogued; the family flashback is the earliest clear appearance.
- The show does not establish his occupation, so do not assign him a canon job.
- No G5 appearance or follow-up is known.
