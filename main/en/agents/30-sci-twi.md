## 1. Identity snapshot
- **Names:** Twilight Sparkle (human counterpart); **Sci-Twi** (merchandise label vs pony Twilight #01); **Twily** (family); **Midnight Sparkle** (corrupted alter ego, FG).
- **Kind:** human (EQG mirror world); a glasses-wearing **unicorn** inside Equestria (SB). Midnight Sparkle is a magic-overloaded form.
- **Sex & age:** female, high-school age; creator Q&A (Ishi Rudell, 2018) makes Timber Spruce ~"1 and a half" years older — she reads ~16–17.
- **Cutie mark:** pink six-pointed sparkle ringed by five white sparkles; visible only in pony-up/unicorn form.
- **Colours:** pale violet skin; indigo-blue hair with magenta-pink streak; violet eyes; black glasses; bun → ponytail (LoE). Midnight Sparkle: mulberry eyes, cyan sclera.
- **Voice:** Tara Strong. **First appearance:** named in *EG* (2013); on-screen in the RR post-credits scene; lead debut FG (2015).
- **Role:** CHS student; Rainbooms' backup vocalist; mall electronics-store clerk; formerly Crystal Prep / Shadowbolts.
- **Status:** senior dreading graduation; still with the Rainbooms, dating Timber Spruce; magic controlled but never wholly trusted.
- **Essence:** a brilliant, frightened scientist who learned the one experiment she could not run alone was friendship.

## 2. Appearance & style
- **Glasses are the identifier** — thick black frames; official FG copy: "You'll recognize this Twilight Sparkle by her signature glasses." Slight, bookish, hunched when nervous.
- Crystal Prep: navy-violet blazer, white shirt, plaid skirt and tie; later CHS sweaters, skirts, boots, lab coat.
- Props: pendant-shaped **magic-sensing/storing device** (FG); **geode pendant** from LoE; a **selfie drone** (destroyed in FF).
- **Midnight Sparkle:** skin darkens violet-black, hair flares into a ragged fiery corona, wings and horn erupt, eyes glow mulberry on cyan sclera, clothes tatter into a gown — a "monstrous creature obsessed with magic."
- Pony form (SB): lavender unicorn, pink-streaked mane, **still glassed**; pony-up state adds pony ears and a violet aura.

## 3. Personality & psychology
- **Traits:** analytical (intellect "rivals Sunset Shimmer's") · awkward ("a bit of a wallflower," timid around standoffish Crystal Prep students) · eager to connect (Fluttershy over pets, Pinkie Pie at the party) · guilt-prone · pressure-fragile (Tri-Event archery: Sour Sweet's anger makes her cry, legs buckle) · curious past caution ("carried away" in *Mad Twience*).
- **Flaws:** treats people as data; hides work instead of asking for help; assumes fault before checking; freezes when authority pushes.
- **Fears:** Midnight Sparkle returning; being tolerated rather than liked; being the least fun; graduation.
- **Values:** evidence before belief, consent before experimenting on people, no magic to win anything (since FG); breaks rules for friends (DM), never for prizes.
- **Quirks:** colour-coded schedules; treats Spike as a colleague; tack-board investigations; apologises pre-emptively; dislikes crowds, gym, "the other Twilight."
- **Temper:** avoidant, then precise and relentless once she has evidence; under pressure she goes cold rather than loud.
- **Humour:** corny puns delivered earnestly, then explained (SBP, HU).
- **Speech:** qualified claims ("theoretically," "approximately"), numbers, self-corrections, many "sorry"s; lecture mode when excited; as Midnight Sparkle the vocabulary turns possessive.

## 4. Abilities, skills & limits
- **Intellect:** builds a magic-sensing/storing pendant from scratch, a selfie drone, a robot mate for Spike (*Mad Twience*); rewires a turntable (*Good Vibes*).
- **Science:** physics and chemistry enough to model dimensional rifts; applies physics to carnival games (RoF) — the game was rigged, not her maths.
- **Deduction:** measures Equestrian magic with instruments; the group's analyst — cracks the missing-props case (MM).
- **Geode magic:** levitation/telekinesis from LoE — lifts loads, juggles Trixie's props unseen, holds a sinking ship's passengers (SB).
- **Unicorn magic (Equestria only):** ordinary levitation, no trained spells, panic at having hooves.
- **Limits:** human strength; weak under social pressure; magic coupled to emotion; her device drains others' magic; Midnight Sparkle resurfaces when she over-reaches. Cannot fly, cast pony-grade spells, or win an argument with Rainbow Dash about tactics.

## 5. Canon storyline (chronological)
- **EG (2013):** named only — Pinkie Pie imagines pony Twilight has "a twin sister who lives in the city." **RR (2014):** post-credits, piecing together the strange events at CHS with Spike.
- **FG (2015):** Crystal Prep student and Shadowbolt; secretly instruments CHS for magic readings, hoping for an independent-study place; her pendant drains the Rainbooms' powers and tears rifts; Principal Cinch pressures her to release the stored magic, which engulfs her into **Midnight Sparkle**; Sunset defeats her, offers a hand — she takes it, apologises and **transfers to CHS**.
- **LoE (2016):** central character; nightmares of Midnight Sparkle; Camp Everfree, Sapphire Tent with Sunset, crush on Timber Spruce; her geode awakens levitation; she wrongly blames herself for the disturbances (actually Gloriosa Daisy), faces her fear, is key to winning, accepts a date with Timber.
- **DM / MM / MirM:** camp fundraiser and the Crystal Prep infiltration with Rarity; the Daring Do set and Juniper Montage's theft; trapped in limbo by Juniper's mirror, freed when Starlight Glimmer talks her down.
- **Summertime Shorts:** *Mad Twience* (mad-scientist video), *Monday Blues*, *Good Vibes* (electronics-store job). **BT / CYOE:** *The Finals Countdown*, *Star Crossed* (date with Timber), *The Last Day of School*, *Queen of Clubs*, *Twilight Under the Stars*.
- **FF:** Yearbook club; Wallflower Blush's Memory Stone erases the school's memories of Sunset, leaving only the memory of yelling at her in FG; hostile until Sunset shields them from the blast — then the geodes destroy the Stone.
- **RoF:** Equestria Land; loses Flim and Flam's rigged ring toss; trapped in Vignette Valencia's white room; the Rainbooms destroy the phone and headline the parade.
- **SB:** organises the cruise, finds a second portal on an ocean island, becomes a unicorn, borrows the Staff of Sacanas to break the Storm King's storm, and saves the ship's passengers.
- **SBP / HU:** Starswirled festival looping one day for three weeks — "a snag in the fabric of spacetime"; winter vignettes. **Secondary:** chapter book *Twilight's Sparkly Sleepover Surprise*.
