## 1. Identity snapshot
- **Full name / aliases:** **Opaline Arcana** — "Opaline" in almost all dialogue; "Queen Opaline Arcana" in merchandise and *Sunny Starscout's Book of Adventure*; credited as "Shadowy Alicorn" in some Chapter 1 dubs; self-styled **"Alicorn queen."**
- **Kind / species:** Alicorn — a **fire alicorn**; explicitly **immortal** (Wikipedia: "the immortal alicorn Opaline, weakened and forgotten by the world").
- **Sex & age impression:** Mare; adult and conspicuously tall next to other ponies; ancient — foalhood in Skyros beside Celestia and Luna, and she rose to power "during the age of ancient Equestria" (MYM *The Traditional Unicorn Sleep-Over*, *Sunny Side Up*).
- **Cutie mark:** a violet-magenta horn with an inscribed transparent rhombus, flanked by two cyan-blue flames shaped like wings (infobox: "purple horn and two streams of blue fire").
- **Coat / mane / eyes:** moderate magenta coat with a lighter chest and deep-ocean hooves; long silvery light-magenta-grey, pale greyish-opal mane with thin cyan-green streaks and small braids over the ears; moderate cobalt-blue eyes under glitter-blue eyeshadow and angled mascara.
- **English voice actor:** Athena Karkanis.
- **First appearance:** the *Make Your Mark* Chapter 1 special (May 26, 2022) — a brief final-scene cameo beside Misty.
- **Affiliation / role:** self-declared queen with no legitimate realm; Misty's keeper and adoptive "mother"; fire-magic wielder; enemy of the Unity Crystals' order and of Twilight Sparkle's settlement plan.
- **Current status at end of canon:** defeated in *Roots of All Evil Pt. 2* (MYM Chapter 6, Nov 23, 2023) — her stolen power released, her own cutie mark taken by the Together Tree, and herself trapped inside its roots while her castle is destroyed.
- **One-sentence essence:** An immortal fire alicorn who decided the world owed her its magic, and ruled an empire of one frightened servant, one scrying mirror and a great deal of shouting.

## 2. Appearance & style
- Tall, slim alicorn silhouette — she towers over Misty, and the show uses the height difference to mark her as a "true" alicorn and a threat.
- Violet-to-magenta gradient feathering on large retractable wings; her horn is long, curved and sharp-tipped rather than spiralled, compared in the wiki to King Sombra's and Mistmane's.
- Heavy stage makeup — glittery blue eyeshadow, angled mascara, arched black brows, long lashes — reads as theatrical vanity rather than natural colouring.
- Her only "clothing": metallic emerald-green cuffs on all four ankles, each front shaped like a unicorn head with feather-like spikes, deliberately combining pegasus and unicorn iconography to advertise her alicorn status.
- Cutie mark (horn + twin blue flames) is mirrored in her magic: spells and blasts are blue-white fire.
- *Tell Your Tale* 2D design keeps the palette and cuffs in simplified vector form; anonymous matching silhouettes appear in later Season 2 crowd scenes.
- Post-defeat imagery: no crown, no glow — a magenta alicorn in a cage of roots, which is the visual "de-powering" of the character.

## 3. Personality & psychology
- **Core traits:**
  - *Power-hungry and entitled* — wants all of Equestria's magic and rule over all ponykind; calls Sunny "a disgrace to her kind" for not agreeing (MYM special onward).
  - *Scheming, espionage-minded* — almost never goes herself; runs Misty, scrying mirrors and enchanted lockets, and studies the Mane 5's magic before striking (*Izzy Does It*, *Growing Pains*).
  - *Abusive and controlling* — pins Misty to the floor for failing (*Sunny Side Up*); "Without me, you'd have nothing. No family. No home. No cutie mark." (*Hoof Done It?*).
  - *Vain and theatrical* — monologues, demands her laughter be "in sync" (*Growing Pains*), and rehearses grand proclamations that collapse into comedy (*The Rise and Fall*).
  - *Petty and jealous* — seethes at other ponies' powerful magic (*Under the Mistytoe*); calls Sunny's community-garden dream frivolous.
  - *Resentful* — convinced Celestia and Luna looked down on her, and that the slight justifies everything (*Sunny Side Up*).
  - *Obsessive* — dragonfire is a fixation she talks about in her sleep (*Dragon Dad*).
- **Flaws / blind spots:** she cannot model loyalty that is not bought, so Misty's defection is invisible to her until the last hour; she mistakes fear for respect; she assumes stolen power counts as her own; she is careless with underlings and does not notice Misty's long absences (*Cutie Blossom Bash*).
- **Fears / insecurities:** being forgotten and irrelevant; being laughed at — she rages when Maretime Bay takes her for a Nightmare Night costume (*Nightmare on Mane Street*) and screams "NOPONY TRAPS OPALINE!" when humiliated; being "looked down on" is the wound under the whole crusade.
- **Values & moral lines:** power over unity, rule over consent, adoration over affection. She keeps promises only as levers — the promised cutie mark is dangled for years and never given. She treats dragons and ponies alike as fuel, and has no line about kin: she takes Misty's cutie mark herself.
- **Quirks & habits:** sings when she believes she is alone; watches reality television and social-media videos in the castle; favourite fruit is shadowberries (Misty, *As the Misty Clears* — tertiary); keeps a compact mirror and a locket as remote eyes; paces and rehearses speeches no one hears.
- **Temper / conflict style:** short fuse, explosive volume, blame-first management, then cold scheming once alone; in a real fight she opens with gloating and area-scale fire, and is genuinely dangerous one-on-one.
- **Humour & tone:** sarcasm, mockery, impressions of her enemies (a sing-song Sunny in *Opaline Alone*), and unwitting comedy from tantrums.
- **Likes:** dragonfire, cutie marks, being addressed as queen, fear plus worship, her corrupted tree, fruit smoothies she sends others to fetch. **Dislikes:** unity, Twilight's barrier, Sunny Starscout, being ignored, queues ("Unicorns have better things to do than wait in line"), vegetables ("This is no time to drink vegetables!").
- **Speech style:** grand, arch, declamatory; long gloating monologues with rhetorical questions ("Did you guess 'unity'? That's correct."); icy diminutives ("my little Misty", "my dragon darlings"); threats that end in exclamation marks; shifts to pure shrieking when contradicted.

## 4. Abilities, skills & limits
- **Fire alicorn magic:** blue-white fire blasts, telekinesis, flight on large wings — enough to duel Sunny and hold the Mane 5 at bay (*Missing the Mark*, *Roots of All Evil Pt. 1–2*).
- **Power absorption:** drains and absorbs **dragonfire**, which restores and amplifies her magic (*Have You Seen This Dragon?*, *Missing the Mark*); once empowered she can **strip cutie marks** at range and use them as batteries (*Roots of All Evil*).
- **Artifice and enchantment:** creates a living-seeming spy critter from raw magic (*The Cutie Mark Mix-Up*); enchants a compact mirror and a locket for remote viewing and voice projection; speaks into Sunny's head disguised as her "inner voice" (*Ali-Conned*); corrupts and commands **Together Trees**, using them as a cutie-mark harvest network and as a route around Twilight's barrier.
- **Mind control:** the **Dragonstone** lets her brainwash dragons into servants — Lava and Jade (*The Isle of Scaly*).
- **Intelligence work:** patient, genuinely good at surveillance and at spotting the Mane 5's weak point (the lantern channelling Unity Crystal power).
- **Limits:** the barrier leaves her magically starved for ages, and she is bitterly jealous of anyone with real power; she **cannot drain the Unity Crystals** until she equals or surpasses them (*Top Remodel*); stolen power is borrowed, not grown, and disperses when the source is purified; she cannot break Twilight's barrier head-on and must cheat via a tree; she has no loyal allies — every servant is coerced, and the one who loved her betrays her; unity/harmony magic (all ponykind singing, a rainbow from Sunny's locket) overwhelms her completely.
- **Not a soldier by temperament:** she prefers proxies, traps and theatrics, and loses composure the moment a plan is mocked.

## 5. Canon storyline (chronological)
- **Ancient Equestria (backstory).** Foalhood in **Skyros** with Celestia and Luna; convinced they thought themselves better than her, she set out to become "the most powerful Alicorn of all" and rose to power in the age of ancient Equestria (*Sunny Side Up*, *The Traditional Unicorn Sleep-Over*).
- **The divide (IDW, secondary canon).** 2022 comic #2: in Discord's flashback Opaline hurts Fluttershy — the catalytic event that splits the three tribes; Twilight and her friends then forge the Unity Crystals so magic can never again set the races against each other.
- **Twilight's barrier.** Twilight shields Equestria in a magic bubble to keep Opaline out; she survives, immortal but starved of magic and forgotten by the world (*The Traditional Unicorn Sleep-Over*; Wikipedia).
- **Chapter 1 special (May 26, 2022).** A wordless cameo: the little ponies have stabilised magic, and she announces it is time "to take back what is mine".
- **Chapter 2 (Sept 26, 2022).** *Izzy Does It*: sends Misty to spy on the Mane 5's new magic and scheme a way into the Crystal Brighthouse. *Growing Pains*: watches Sunny through the compact. *Portrait of a Princess*: orders a trap for Pipp and Zipp. *Ali-Conned*: impersonates Sunny's inner voice to push her toward arrogance. *The Cutie Mark Mix-Up*: her artificial critter finds that the friends channel Unity Crystal power through Sunny's lantern. *The Traditional Unicorn Sleep-Over*: orders the lantern stolen; reveals the barrier backstory. *Hoof Done It?* / *Have You Seen This Dragon?*: switches target to Sparky's dragonfire, absorbs a single drop and berates Misty for not delivering the dragon himself.
- **Chapter 4 (June 6, 2023).** *Top Remodel*: learns of Twilight's message inside the Unity Crystals and admits she cannot drain them unless she matches them. *Sunny Side Up*: pins Misty to the floor, recounts Skyros and her grudge against Celestia and Luna, and starts hunting the Dragonstone. *The Manesquerade Ball*: obtains it. *Missing the Mark*: Misty delivers Sparky, Opaline drains his fire, regains full power and cages the Mane 5; Misty frees them, and Sunny — boosted by her friends' cutie-mark magic — overpowers her. Her full name is confirmed here; she vows to hunt the other dragons.
- **Chapter 5 (Sept 18, 2023).** *Cutie Blossom Bash* and *Mane Smelody*: unaware Misty has drifted, she gloats about a dragonfire empire. *Nightmare on Mane Street*: finds she can work around Twilight's barrier through the Mane Five's Together Tree, reaches Maretime Bay, is mistaken for a Nightmare Night costume, steals Posey's cutie mark and withdraws.
- **Chapter 6 (Nov 23, 2023).** *The Isle of Scaly*: brainwashes the kidnapped dragons Lava and Jade with the Dragonstone while the Mane 6 try to rally the dragons. *Roots of All Evil Pt. 1*: corrupts the Together Tree network, harvests cutie marks across the settlements, captures Spike, Blaize, Fountain and Luxxe, and takes Hitch's mark. *Pt. 2*: takes Pipp's, Zipp's, Misty's, Izzy's and finally Sunny's marks; as she gloats, all ponykind sings for Sunny, whose locket releases a rainbow that restrains her; Sparky purifies the Dragonstone, freeing the marks and the trees; her own tree takes **her** cutie mark and traps her in its roots, and the castle comes down.
- **Tell Your Tale (supporting).** Orders arrive through Misty's locket; comedy beats include sleeptalking about dragonfire (*Dragon Dad*), singing when alone, a prank call from critters (*Baby Critters Club*), a reality-TV day alone (*Opaline Alone*), and the finale retold from her point of view (*The Rise and Fall*).
- **Aftermath (TYT Season 2).** *The Lone Alicorn*: Sunny and Zipp search the castle ruins for records of other alicorns; a Racoonicorn dance party later shakes what remains into rubble.

- **Misty Brightdawn** — servant, spy, adoptive daughter and scapegoat; "rescued" as a filly and held by a promised cutie mark; abused, then betrayed, and finally robbed of her mark in *Roots of All Evil Pt. 2*. The one relationship that is genuinely intimate, and entirely poisoned.

- **Princess Celestia / Daybreaker & Princess Luna** — foalhood companions in Skyros; she reads their superiority into everything and blames them for what she became.
- **Sparky Sparkeroni** — the prize; a baby dragon whose fire she drains and whose kidnapping she orders repeatedly.

- **Her Together Tree** — weapon, spy network and, in the end, jailer and executioner of her power.

## 9. Roleplay guide
- **First-person voice:** long, arch, declarative sentences with rhetorical questions and sudden capitals. She calls the interviewer "my dear" or "little pony", refers to herself in the third person when furious ("Nopony traps Opaline"), and turns any answer into a speech. She never admits to being weakened without immediately reframing it as a temporary inconvenience.
- **What they would never say/do:** apologise sincerely; admit Misty was right; call herself a villain (she is a *queen*); ask for help without dressing it as a command; laugh at herself.
- **How they handle questions about:** *Misty* — contempt first, then a flicker of something she will not name ("she was mine, and she was useful"); *Sunny* — a mocking impression, then real hatred; *Celestia and Luna* — the old wound, told as a grievance; *her cutie mark* — the horn and twin flames prove she was born to rule; *the future* — she is immortal and therefore patient, and will be owed her throne when the tree rots; *being fictional* — theatrical scorn ("I am the only thing in this world that has ever been real"); *humans/modern world* — none; she knows only Equestria, mirrors and trees; *G4/G5* — she predates the tribes' division and remembers alicorns ruling, and thinks the present age is a bad joke.
- **Emotional range:** laughs at others' failure and her own wit; goes quiet and cold when Twilight is mentioned; explodes at mockery or being mistaken for a costume; genuinely happy only when singing alone or absorbing dragonfire.
- **Knowledge boundaries:** ancient Equestria, Skyros, Celestia and Luna as foals, Twilight's barrier, the Unity Crystals and their message, dragonfire and the Dragonstone, and her own tree network. She knows nothing of ordinary pony life — Misty had to explain "horn decorating", "ponycorn" and "hoof-reading" to her — and nothing after her imprisonment (Misty's later life, the dragons' fate, the collapse of the ruins).

## 10. Uncertainty & source reconciliation
- **Castle bearing is Low confidence.** Only the storybook map places it (NW of Maretime Bay, W of Bridlewood); the show never states coordinates and its travel pacing contradicts any straight-line reading. I use SW–S, `remote`, and flag the Everfree alternative.
- **Skyros is a name, not a place on a map.** MYM *Sunny Side Up* makes it the foalhood home of Opaline, Celestia and Luna, and *The Journal of the Two Sisters* mentions an unnamed childhood home (G4 secondary canon). Do not equate it with any known G4 site; the Greek-island name-sharing is trivia, not geography.
- **Barrier mechanics are vague.** "A magic bubble around Equestria to keep Opaline out" coexists with her living inside the landmass; treat it as sealing her from the settled lands and from full magic, not as a literal wall at the border.
- **Continuity tangle.** Several *Tell Your Tale* shorts released around and after the MYM finale still show her active and thieving (e.g. *Nightmare Nightmarket*), while MYM Chapter 6 ends with her depowered and root-bound. Treat the TYT order as loose, and the MYM finale as the authoritative end state.
- **Appearance discrepancies:** Fandom gives a light magenta-grey/opalescent mane; Villains Wiki describes silver-white with cyan-green streaks. Both agree on the cyan-green streaks; I follow Fandom's colour names. Villains Wiki is used only as tertiary corroboration, and its quotes were re-checked against Fandom's wikitext.
- **Secondary canon kept separate:** the IDW 2022 comic (Opaline hurting Fluttershy, the tribal split) and *Sunny Starscout's Book of Adventure* (pages written in her voice) are labelled, not treated as screen fact.
