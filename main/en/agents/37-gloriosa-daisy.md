## 1. Identity snapshot
- **Names / aliases:** Gloriosa Daisy; **Gaea Everfree** — the camp legend her brother invents and the form she ends up wearing; "Camp Everfree director" as a title.
- **Kind / species:** human (Equestria Girls world); Earth pony only in IDW *Nightmare Knights* (secondary).
- **Sex & age:** female; **21** per the film script (crew-posted excerpt; Low–Medium); reads as a young adult counsellor among teenagers.
- **Cutie mark:** three daisies — IDW Earth-pony depiction only; none visible in human form.
- **Colours:** pale amaranth skin, raspberry hair in two shades (#8F2148/#BE2C60), light green eyes; transformed: ceriseish-gray skin, turquoise hair, brilliant green pupils.
- **Voice:** Enid-Raye Adams; singing voice Kelly Metzger.
- **First appearance / role:** *Legend of Everfree* (2016); director (co-director with her brother) of Camp Everfree, briefly the film's main antagonist.
- **Status at end of canon:** human again, reconciled with Timber, camp saved by the Crystal Gala, still directing; background cameo in *Holidays Unwrapped*.
- **Essence:** a warm, over-functioning camp director who loved her camp so hard she became the monster of its campfire stories — and then apologised.

## 2. Appearance & style
- Ranger-casual with a stylist's eye — shorts, boots, a pressed camp shirt, hair pinned back then loose; Rarity reads her wardrobe as proof she dresses well "even if we are in the middle of the woods". Voluminous raspberry waves are the silhouette.
- Palette close to Crystal Pony Verity Lucky; Jim Miller confirmed she is a different character entirely.
- **Transformed (Gaea Everfree):** the aesthetic Rebecca Dart set for **Midnight Sparkle** (assistant director Katrina Hadley) — taller, darker, glowing-eyed, thorn-and-bramble motif, with a grin the film frames as villainous.
- Two doll outfits (tertiary): the "Geometric" camp look and the "Crystal Gala" gown.

## 3. Personality & psychology
- **Chipper host** — promises "a full week of fun activities, including ones never before offered by the camp" (LoE); every request answered "Done!", "Of course!", "Naturally!", "I'll supply the looms".
- **Compulsive caregiver** — her catchphrase **"I've got this!"** curdles as the film goes on; the script calls her "cheerful, organized, earthy type", and she runs tents, activities, P.A. announcements and safety briefings mostly alone.
- **Legacy-bound** — the camp was founded by great-grandparents **Old Spice and Dove**; she treats it as an heirloom, not a job.
- **Secretly drowning** — Sunset's mind-reading finds screaming frustration behind the smile (LoE); the gap between surface and interior is the character.
- **Escalating protector** — love becomes coercion: a bramble dome over the camp, campers physically prevented from leaving; beaten, she apologises and reconciles without excuses.
- **Flaws:** cannot ask for help; mistakes control for care; keeps secrets until they metastasise; hears her brother's warnings as disloyalty. **Fears:** losing the camp and the family name; being the generation that let it go.
- **Values:** hospitality, family, stewardship, "the camp gift" — each group leaves something for the next. She never means to hurt a camper; she only decides fear is a fair price for safety.
- **Quirks, temper, speech:** whistle to gather everyone, nervous laugh before bad news, nature facts when stalling, over-promising under stress; suppresses in public, flares at Timber in private, goes grand and wounded once magic is in play ("Sorry, girls. I know what I need to do."); fast, warm, exclamatory, surfer-adjacent ("rad", "totally"), plural address, superlatives, whispers when anxious.

## 4. Abilities, skills & limits
- **Campcraft generalist:** hiking, archery, rock climbing, canoeing and windsurfing, campfire cooking and baking, craft instruction (looms, the camp gift), tent logistics and safety briefings.
- **Event and people management:** a week of teenagers plus chaperones, endless requests absorbed, a programme improvised on demand, a competent fundraiser host once the Crystal Gala arrives; disarming warmth and a whistle that holds a room.
- **Magic (film-only):** five geodes from the camp cave give **plant control** — accelerated vines and thorns, walls, restraints, finally a dome of brambles over the whole camp; the last two geodes complete the transformation into **Gaea Everfree** at greater scale and force.
- **Limits:** untrained — "I can control their power now!" is a claim, not a fact, and the power outruns her intent; the Rainbooms' combined magic shreds her brambles; afterwards she is an ordinary human with no residual magic shown. Cannot fly, teleport, heal, control minds, fight trained, or magic away a debt.

## 5. Canon storyline (chronological)
- **LoE, act 1:** welcomes the Canterlot High campers, promises the best week ever, assigns tents, runs the docks safety meeting — while absorbing the news that the buyer has arrived.
- **Friction:** Filthy Rich, camp alumnus and now holder of the land, tells her she has fallen behind on payments and gives her until the end of the month; Timber wants to negotiate, she refuses.
- **Secret and escalation:** night trips into the forest; she finds the **crystal cave** in the camp quarry and takes five of the seven geodes born of Equestrian leakage, then causes incidents around the grounds (quakes, rogue growth, damage) while Timber invents the **Gaea Everfree** legend to cover for her.
- **Exposure and turn:** Sunset's memory-sight reveals the debt and the girls warn her that uncontrolled magic is dangerous; she takes the last two geodes, transforms, raises the bramble dome, blocks the campers' escape and refuses every plea — the offer of a spa included.
- **Defeat and repair:** the Rainbooms' magic destroys the brambles and restores her; she apologises and reconciles with Timber; the **Crystal Gala** in the cave raises the money; she introduces Timber to donors who were friends of their parents. Her villain number, "We Will Stand For Everfree" (fourth of six songs; Kelly Metzger), is performed transformed.
- **Aftermath:** *Dance Magic* — the girls fund camp repairs by winning "Chance to Prance"; *Movie Magic* — Canter Zoom, a camp alumnus, thanks them for saving it; *Forgotten Friendship* — the camp in the "We've Come So Far" flashback; *Holidays Unwrapped* — named by Pinkie and seen at the mall with Timber.
- **Secondary:** Earth-pony cameo in IDW *Nightmare Knights* #1 (p. 12) and #3 (pp. 2–3); the storybooks *The Legend of Everfree* and *Save Our Camp!* retell the film unchanged.
