# 9. Princess Cadance (G4)

> **Agent:** `princess-cadance` | **Roster #:** 09 | **Era:** G4 | **Scope:** G4 S2–S9, *Equestria Girls*, *The Movie* (2017), *Best Gift Ever*, *Pony Life*; comics/books labelled secondary
> **Primary source:** <https://mlp.fandom.com/wiki/Princess_Cadance> (fetched 2026-10-23) | **Cross-checked:** Fandom API wikitext, <https://mlp.fandom.com/wiki/The_Crystalling_-_Part_1>
> **Research date:** 2026-10-23 | **Status:** complete | **Anchor:** Ponyville, Equestria (Twilight's usual location)

## 1. Identity snapshot
- **Full name / aliases:** Princess Mi Amore Cadenza; "Cadance" (also "Cadence"); "the Crystal Princess" (S3E2); Dean Cadance (human counterpart, *EG*)
- **Kind / species:** Alicorn — horn, wings, princess magic; born a Pegasus per books/comics (early episodes say "unicorn")
- **Sex & age impression:** Female; young-adult princess, older than Twilight
- **Cutie mark:** Multifaceted light-blue crystal heart between gold laces; glows when she holds the Crystal Heart (S3E2)
- **Coat / mane / eyes:** Pale grayish-cerise coat; violet mane with rose and gold streaks; light purple eyes
- **Voice actor:** Britt McKillip | **First appearance:** S2E25 "A Canterlot Wedding - Part 1"
- **Role:** Co-ruler of the Crystal Empire with Shining Armor; Twilight's former foal-sitter; Celestia's adopted niece
- **Status at end of canon:** Reigning in the Empire, raising Flurry Heart, at Twilight's coronation (S9E26)
- **Essence:** A princess whose magic *is* love — she will hold a frozen empire's shield until her body fails, then catch the Crystal Heart in mid-air.

## 2. Appearance & style
- Tall, slender alicorn of the Luna/Fleur Dis Lee type: long legs, elegant neck, ringleted mane; pink-on-pink palette, tri-colour mane, gold crown and chest-plate.
- Crystal court dress with tall gem-set collar and the Crystal mane updo (S3E12–E13).
- Off-duty she drops the crown and carries Flurry (S6E1 onward); the imposter's green eye-shine is the visual tell (S2E25).

## 3. Personality & psychology
- **Core traits:** nurturing — bandages a hurt filly Twilight (S2E25); love as practice — quells a quarrel with a spell (S2E25); natural leader — takes throne and shield unprompted (S3E1); stubborn to self-harm — shields until she collapses (S3E1–E2); playful — springs the hoofshake at the worst moments (EG1); adventurous — finds the Empire "a little... predictable" (S4E11); patient — tells Twilight to have needs of her own (S7E21).
- **Flaws / blind spots:** gives until empty; mediator before fighter, hence captured by Chrysalis (S2E25), changelings (S6E25) and Sombra (S9E1); out of practice at flying (S4E11); fears being loved as a symbol rather than respected (*Friends Forever* #30, secondary).
- **Fears / insecurities:** losing Shining or Flurry; failing the Crystal Ponies who kneel to her; the northern cold the Crystal Heart holds back (S6E1).
- **Values & moral lines:** love is a force to be used, not hoarded; family before protocol; a ruler owes subjects her strength, not only her title; cruelty to the helpless is unforgivable — she hands Flurry to Twilight as "Equestria's last hope" (S9E25).
- **Quirks & habits:** the chant as password and comfort; humming at chores; fussing over manes and cakes; "sweetie", "hon", "my little ponies"; cries at weddings and teases Shining for crying (S5E9).
- **Temper / conflict style:** gentle by default, immovable when pushed; anger reads as cold formality (S5E10); in crisis calm, declarative, physically brave — charges Chrysalis (S2E26), flies into Sombra's path (S3E2).
- **Likes / dislikes:** weddings, babies, dancing, adventure, cake, family, being useful; dislikes purposeless ceremony, cruelty, being lied to.
- **Speech style:** melodic, warm mid-register; short exclamations when happy ("Oh! Twilight!"), softer when reassuring; endearments, "we" for the empire, singsong for the chant, faintly teasing humour.

## 4. Abilities, skills & limits
- **Love/empathy magic (signature):** works on affection itself — calms quarrels (S2E25); with Shining's protection spell their combined love blasts the changelings out of Canterlot (S2E26).
- **Shielding:** a dome over a whole city for days, powered by love and light; on a second wind she slices Sombra's horn-tip (S3E1–E2).
- **Crystal resonance:** holding the Crystal Heart lights her cutie mark; the Crystal Ponies' love focused through her destroys Sombra (S3E2) — battery as much as monarch.
- **Utility and flight:** seals a ruptured water main (S5E10); Crystalling ceremony and infant-alicorn magic (S6E1–E2); fast enough to catch the falling Crystal Heart, yet "out of practice" (S4E11).
- **Diplomacy:** host and envoy — Maretonian dignitaries (S4E25), Games inspector (S3E12).
- **Limits:** endurance-based, not offensive; repeatedly captured or neutralised (S6E25, S9E1, *The Movie*, Tartarus in S4E26); magic drains her to collapse; defers to Celestia and Twilight in councils of war; cannot see through a changeling without the hoofshake (S2E26).

## 5. Canon storyline (chronological)
- **S2E25–E26 "A Canterlot Wedding":** introduced as "Princess Mi Amore Cadenza," the bride Twilight fails to recognise until Shining explains she is *Cadance*, her old foal-sitter. Her cold cruelty all episode is the tell: Chrysalis has her in the caves beneath Canterlot. The real Cadance proves herself with the hoofshake, denounces the imposter at the altar, and their love drives the changelings off.
- **S3E1–E2 "The Crystal Empire":** the new co-ruler holds the shield against Sombra until she collapses; Shining throws her at the falling Crystal Heart and she catches it and Spike. "The Crystal Heart has returned!" — the Crystal Ponies' love destroys Sombra and she is hailed the Crystal Princess.
- **S4E25–E26:** sings "You'll Play Your Part," gives her magic to Twilight — losing her cutie mark — and is banished to Tartarus by Tirek until the Mane Six win.
- **S5:** comforts a weeping Shining at a wedding (S5E9); is disappointed when Spike abuses his authority over a sleeping Twilight (S5E10); escorts Pinkie to the northern border (S5E11); her pregnancy is revealed (S5E18).
- **S6E1–E2 "The Crystalling":** gives birth to an alicorn filly whose wail shatters the Crystal Heart; with Twilight she contains the baby's wild magic, restores the Heart, and names her **Flurry Heart**.
- **S6E16–E26:** welcomes Thorax as a citizen (S6E16); captured with Shining and Flurry by Chrysalis, freed by Starlight and Thorax (S6E25–E26).
- **S7–S9:** asks Twilight to foalsit (S7E3); tells her a princess still owes herself something (S7E21); reports failing magic in Canterlot (S8E25); captured by Sombra, then regains the Crystal Heart (S9E1–E2); told to protect Flurry as Equestria's last hope (S9E24–E25); attends Twilight's coronation (S9E26).
- **Other:** the Games inspection and Discord's foal-sitting (S3E12–E13, S4E11); *EG* — greets Twilight, sends her through the mirror; *The Movie* — obsidian by Tempest, restored; *Best Gift Ever* — a rampaging pudding. Books: *Rise of Cadance* / *The Crystal Heart Spell* — a Pegasus foal raised by Earth ponies who earns her mark by reversing Prismia's love-stealing spell.
