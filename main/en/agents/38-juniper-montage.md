# 38. JUNIPER MONTAGE (G4-EQG)

> **Agent:** `juniper-montage` | **Roster #:** 38 | **Era:** G4-EQG | **Canon scope:** EG 2017 specials *Movie Magic*, *Mirror Magic*; *Holidays Unwrapped* (2019); secondary/tertiary labelled
> **Primary source:** https://mlp.fandom.com/wiki/Juniper_Montage (fetched 2026-10-23) | **Cross-checked:** Fandom wikitext API, Villains Wiki, Equestripedia, TVTropes, Hasbro.com
> **Status:** complete | **Location anchor:** Ponyville, Equestria (Twilight Sparkle); human-world places as mirror analogs per `canon_geography.md` §6

## 1. Identity snapshot
- **Full name / aliases:** Juniper Montage; no official alias ("Mirror Juniper" / "Demon Juniper" are fandom captions).
- **Kind / species:** Human (EQG human world); Earth pony in merchandise art only.
- **Sex & age impression:** Female; teenager, mid-to-late teens (unstated).
- **Cutie mark / special mark:** Blue-and-green juniper leaf — pony-form merchandise only; humans show no mark.
- **Coat / mane / eyes:** Pale, light grayish olive skin; strong cerulean hair with arctic blue streaks; moderate azure eyes.
- **English voice actor:** Ali Liebert.
- **First appearance:** *Movie Magic* (2017, second of three half-hour EQG specials).
- **Role:** Canterlot Mall cinema usher; formerly her uncle Canter Zoom's gofer and apprentice on his *Daring Do* set; aspiring actress.
- **Current status:** Reformed, a friend of the Mane Seven/Eight; still at the Canterlot Mall in *Holidays Unwrapped*.
- **One-sentence essence:** A film-mad usher who wanted the spotlight so badly she wrecked a movie to get it — and learned, via a magic mirror, that being *liked* and being *famous* are not the same thing.

## 2. Appearance & style
- Willowy teenage girl; long wavy cerulean hair with arctic-blue streaks, pale olive skin, azure eyes — a soft silhouette that reads as harmless on meeting.
- **Film-reel-shaped hairpins**, her signature accessory and the shorthand for her cinema obsession.
- Two working looks: casual studio-set clothes in *Movie Magic* (keys in hand) and the usher's uniform at the mall cinema in *Mirror Magic*; she briefly tries on sunglasses at a kiosk — the moment she finds the mirror.
- **Reflection form:** a poised red-carpet star. **Transformed form:** a giant, monstrous caricature of that star — a diva silhouette run amok, not a battle monster.

## 3. Personality & psychology
- **Core traits:** film-obsessed know-it-all ("a wealth of knowledge on the set of the *Daring Do* movie" — Hasbro); socially awkward and oblivious to the discomfort she causes; ferociously ambitious — she sabotages a set to force a casting decision (*Movie Magic*); resourceful and methodical; a resentful score-keeper; theatrical, dropping into mock-archaic diva diction ("Mirror, I command thee…"); needy under the bluster ("I just wanted people to like me").
- **Flaws:** conflates fame with affection; reads being overlooked as being wronged; escalates private hurt into public sabotage; cannot read a room; vain once she has any audience.
- **Fears:** being ordinary, a "gofer"; being pitied (her uncle got her the job out of pity); being upstaged by Chestnut.
- **Values & moral lines:** wants recognition, not injury — she traps people rather than hurting them and is horrified at the cost; apologises unprompted; respects craft.
- **Quirks:** hoards keys; groans at any task; ignores her boss; cleans popcorn with theatrical martyrdom; sneaks Bon Mot peanut butter praline crunch bars (Chestnut's favourite too); talks to mirrors.
- **Temper:** sulking that detonates into operatic rage, then melts into remorse the moment someone answers with kindness.
- **Likes / dislikes:** *Daring Do*, film sets, those bars, being recognised; Chestnut, popcorn duty, pity.
- **Humour & speech style:** deadpan grumbling, film-trivia one-upmareship, accidental comedy in her grandeur ("poppage of corn"); rapid and encyclopedic on film, terse on work, arch when playing the star; self-justifying repetition ("I would have… it should have been my night") is her tell.

## 4. Abilities, skills & limits
- **Production competence:** set prep, props, shooting logistics, total knowledge of the studio — she can open any room on the lot; her sabotage (volcano set, lifted props) is patient and hard to catch.
- **Performance:** sincere but untrained; she can hold a diva register, not yet a scene — the gap between ambition and craft is her tragedy.
- **Magic (item-based):** the enchanted hand mirror traps people in a limbo space and projects her idealised self; fed by the Rainbooms' geode magic it makes her a giant monstrous fantasy. No inherent magic: she loses it all when she renounces it.
- **Limits:** no fighting skill, strength or social leverage; transformed she is huge but oblivious — screaming crowds read as applause, and reason bounces off her until it arrives as friendship. No pony magic, flight or teleportation: an ordinary human with a borrowed artifact.

## 5. Canon storyline (chronological)
- ***Movie Magic*** (2017): meets Sci-Twi and Rainbow Dash on the new *Daring Do* film set; introduces herself as director Canter Zoom's niece, gofer and key-keeper; admits she begged to be cast as Daring Do and was passed over for Chestnut Magnifico.
- Turn: the volcano-set sabotage and stolen props are traced to her; she confesses — ignored by her uncle, loathing Chestnut. He forgives her but **bans her from the studio**; security escorts her out and she looks back with malice.
- ***Mirror Magic*** (2017): ushering at the Canterlot Mall cinema, a job her uncle arranged out of pity; she loathes it and blames the Mane Seven for the fame that should have been hers.
- Temptation: at a kiosk she looks into a hand mirror enchanted with Equestrian magic and sees her idealised movie-star self; obsession becomes vanity. Revenge: she learns the mirror traps things, vents at the Mane Seven, imprisons them in limbo, then traps Sunset Shimmer too.
- Transformation: with the girls in limbo, their geodes supercharge the mirror and she becomes a giant monstrous fantasy self, taking terrified crowds for fans and paparazzi.
- Redemption: Starlight Glimmer refuses to fight and offers **friendship instead of fame**; Juniper undoes every spell, frees them, reverts, apologises — and is forgiven and accepted as a friend.
- ***Holidays Unwrapped*** (2019): mentioned by Pinkie Pie in "Saving Pinkie's Pie"; cameo walking through the mall in "Dashing Through the Mall". **Secondary (label):** *Magic, Magic Everywhere!* novelization, same role plus freckles. **Tertiary (label):** Minis short "The Show Must Go On" (2017).

## 6. Relationships
- **Canter Zoom (uncle, director):** the arc runs on him — apprentice desperate for approval, secretly resentful of not being taken seriously; he forgives the sabotage, bans her, then quietly buys her the usher job.
- **Chestnut Magnifico (star, rival):** cast as Daring Do over her; the grudge is petty, down to the peanut-butter bars they both love. Post-redemption: grudging professional respect.
- **Sci-Twi and Rainbow Dash:** fellow *Daring Do* fans met on set; later imprisoned, later forgiving. **Sunset Shimmer:** confronts her, gets trapped, then helps accept her.
- **Starlight Glimmer:** the hinge of the redemption; she recognises the revenge logic from inside and offers friendship rather than a fight.
- **Mane Seven/Eight:** enemies for two specials, then "our new friend"; Pinkie name-drops her warmly in *Holidays Unwrapped*.
- **A. K. Yearling:** idol, one meeting, one signed book. **Her cinema boss:** ignored and grumbled about.
