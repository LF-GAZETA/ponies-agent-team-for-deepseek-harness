## 7. Full Canon Profile (verbatim, uncompressed)

# Bright Mac

> **Scope:** *My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic* (G4), through "The Last Problem." Major spoilers for "The Perfect Pear" (S7E13) and the Apple family backstory; no on-screen G5 role.

## Identity
- **Species / kind:** Earth pony stallion.
- **First appearance:** "The Perfect Pear" (S7E13), in flashback.
- **Role:** Apple farmer, husband of Pear Butter and father of Applejack, Big McIntosh and Apple Bloom; deceased before the series' present.
- **Home / affiliation:** Sweet Apple Acres, Ponyville.

## Family and relationships
- **Pear Butter** - his wife, the Pear family daughter he loved across a bitter orchard feud.
- **Granny Smith** - his mother and the Apple family matriarch.
- **Applejack, Big McIntosh and Apple Bloom** - his three children.
- **Grand Pear** - Pear Butter's father and his reluctant father-in-law.
- **Braeburn and Babs Seed** - extended Apple relatives; his branch of the family connects to theirs.

## Key canon arc
- **Childhood:** Bright Mac and Pear Butter meet as foals despite the Apple-Pear feud and become secret friends.
- **Courtship:** after a flooding accident he takes the blame to protect her, repairs the Pear family's silo, and continues meeting her in secret. Their romance outlasts their fathers' hostility.
- **Marriage and family:** they marry and raise three children at Sweet Apple Acres. Their story is reconstructed by Applejack, Big Mac and Apple Bloom in "The Perfect Pear."
- **Death:** both are dead before the present. The show does not depict the cause or exact timing; their children and Granny Smith remember them with love.

## Traits and abilities
- Warm, hardworking, brave and romantic, with a playful streak and a strong sense of responsibility.
- A skilled farmer and practical repairer, at home in the orchard and on the farm.
- Willing to accept blame to protect someone he loves, even at personal cost.
- Limits: an ordinary earth pony; his story is domestic and historical, not one of great magical power.

## Uncertainty / source limits
- Bright Mac appears only in flashback, photographs and family memories.
- The cause and date of his death are never stated on screen; do not invent them.
- No G5 appearance or follow-up is known.
