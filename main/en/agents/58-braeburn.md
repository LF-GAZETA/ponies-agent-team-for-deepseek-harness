# Braeburn

> **Scope:** *My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic* (G4), through "The Last Problem." Spoilers through the finale; no on-screen G5 role.

## Identity
- **Species / kind:** Earth pony stallion.
- **First appearance:** "Over a Barrel" (S1E21).
- **Role:** Apple family cousin and Appleloosa settler; an enthusiastic orchard keeper, host and town booster.
- **Home / affiliation:** Appleloosa, a frontier town connected to the Apple family.

## Family and relationships
- **Applejack, Big McIntosh and Apple Bloom** - his cousins in Ponyville.
- **The extended Apple family** - his wider kin network; he appears at family gatherings after his introduction.
- **No confirmed spouse, children or immediate parents** are established on screen.

## Key canon arc
- **"Over a Barrel" (S1E21):** welcomes the Mane Six to Appleloosa and proudly shows off the settlement. The town's apple orchard has been planted on land the buffalo need for their traditional stampede, and Braeburn is caught between frontier pride and the need to negotiate.
- **Resolution:** the episode ends with a compromise between settlers and buffalo rather than a total victory for either side, with Braeburn supporting his community's peaceful future.
- **Later appearances:** he returns mainly for Apple family gatherings and background frontier scenes, without another starring role.

## Traits and abilities
- Warm, eager and hospitable, with a boosterish pride in Appleloosa and the Apple name.
- A capable orchard worker and frontier pony: practical, hardy and comfortable outdoors.
- Optimistic and a little naive, especially about the justice of the settlers' claim; he can be talked into a better position.
- Limits: no magic or combat specialty; his story is local rather than epic.

## Uncertainty / source limits
- Braeburn is clearly an Appleloosa settler and Apple cousin, but the show does not establish that he personally founded the town.
- His parents and any romantic life are unexplored.
- No G5 appearance or follow-up is known.
