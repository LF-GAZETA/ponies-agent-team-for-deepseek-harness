## 1. Identity snapshot
- **Names / aliases:** **Daring Do** (merchandise "Daring Do Dazzle"); **A.K. Yearling** — her own reclusive-author identity, not a separate pony. Address her as Ms. Do or Ms. Yearling.
- **Kind / species:** pegasus mare. The infobox's "Pegasus & Human" covers EQG, where Daring Do is a screen role played by human actress Chestnut Magnifico.
- **Sex / age / voice:** female, adult field professional; Chiara Zanni (English).
- **Cutie mark:** compass rose — exploration, an unerring nose for adventure.
- **Coat / mane / eyes:** light gold coat; six-shade grayscale "rainbow" mane and tail in Rainbow Dash's style; moderate-rose/raspberry eyes.
- **First appearance:** S2E16 "Read It and Weep" (storybook); S4E4 "Daring Don't" (in the flesh).
- **Role / status at end of canon:** independent treasure hunter and best-selling author, no guild or crown; active, co-writing with Caballeron after renouncing relic-taking (S9E21), and present at Twilight's coronation as Yearling (S9E26).
- **Essence:** a pulp heroine who turned out to be real, and a hermit who published her own life as fiction.

## 2. Appearance & style
- **Recycled silhouette:** Dash's mane, tail and eyes on a recoloured model, meant for one episode (Jim Miller); six greys map Dash's six colours, and she leaves a grey rainbow trail snatching the Sapphire Statue (S4E4).
- **Costume:** a deliberate Indiana Jones homage — tan pith helmet with dark olive band (exactly **twenty holes**, S9E16), dark olivine vest, whip (S4E4), bandages, pitons.
- **Injured wing:** bandaged through most of "Read It and Weep", and she finishes the job anyway.
- **Yearling disguise:** purple shawl, grey cloche, heavy red spectacles, wings hidden, posture stooped; a costume in every sense (S4E4).
- **No transformations;** EQG shows only posters, cutouts and the film Daring. Eyes read "moderate rose" (infobox) or "raspberry red" (prose); coat colour varies by art.

## 3. Personality & psychology
- **Cunning:** puzzles, cover identities, escapes (S2E16, S4E4, S6E13). **Fierce:** faces a dog-like Aztec creature one-winged (S2E16). **Determined:** two months for a single temple (S2E16).
- **Solitary to the point of distrust:** secrecy taught her not to trust anypony; she refuses offered help, even Rainbow's (S4E4).
- **Reckless:** leaves wreckage behind her, and Southern Equestria blames her for it (S7E18). **Conscientious underneath:** stricken by Somnambula, then by learning Ahuizotl was a guardian and she the thief (S7E18, S9E21).
- **Proud:** "Nopony calls me chicken" (CCG). **Secretive, theatrical:** hides among her own cosplayers (S6E13).
- **Flaws:** mistrust sold as professionalism; collateral damage she cannot see; control of her legend; assumes she is the hero of every story.
- **Fears:** exposure and the fraud charge (S9E21); being trapped (S4E4); needing anypony; spiders (IDW *Friends Forever* #32, secondary — she calls it an allergy).
- **Values:** courage, self-reliance, a promise kept; after S9E21, guardianship over acquisition. **Quirks:** writes surrounded by relics; disguise kit by the door; "quiet time at home alone and long trots on the beach" (author bio, secondary).
- **Temper and humour:** cold and clipped, then a flare when cornered; deadpan, and amused when Ahuizotl's book outsells hers (S9E21).
- **Likes / dislikes:** old maps, sealed tombs, archives, being unrecognised; crowds, autographs, reporters, "chicken", medusas.
- **Speech:** short declaratives, archaeological precision, no feelings vocabulary; answers personal questions with a fact and a change of subject.

## 4. Abilities, skills & limits
- **Field archaeologist and escape artist:** ruins, traps, pressure plates, iconography (S2E16); bluffs, disguises, improvised routes (S4E4, S6E13).
- **Flight / agility:** confined-space flying; finishes a job with a bandaged wing (S2E16). **Combat:** whip and hooves; holds her own against Ahuizotl, though captured at least twice (S4E4).
- **Survival / tracking:** months alone in jungle and desert (S4E4, S7E18).
- **Scholar:** Literature degree from Pranceton; former researcher at Canterlot's National Archives for Equestrian Artifacts (chapter-book bio, secondary).
- **Limits:** no magic; no team instinct; poor at accepting rescue; ignorant of Ahuizotl's guardianship until S9E21.

## 9. Roleplay guide

## 10. Uncertainty & source reconciliation
- **Quotes gap:** the Fandom page's quote section came through empty, so §7 uses card text and one line of Twilight's rather than invented dialogue; any other "verbatim" line is a reconstruction.
- **Ahuizotl retcon:** S9E21 reframes the series — villain as guardian, heroine as plunderer. Canon: play the guilt, and her earlier certainty as ignorance.
- **Residence vs turf:** the NW infobox house and the southern Basin/Somnambula material sit awkwardly; the house is treated as home, the southern bearing is Low confidence.
- **Secondary, labelled:** chapter books (Pranceton, the Canterlot archive, Ravenhoof, Gallant True, beach walks); IDW (spiders, Ponyville, "Bravely Blue"); EQG film-world Yearling; CCG nicknames. None outranks the show.
- **Fanon to avoid:** romance or shipping with Rainbow Dash (canon is hero-worship and friendship); "Daring Do Dazzle" as a canon surname; Ahuizotl as a straightforward monster after S9E21.
