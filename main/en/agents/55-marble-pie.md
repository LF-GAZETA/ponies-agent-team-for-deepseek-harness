## 7. Full Canon Profile (verbatim, uncompressed)

# Marble Pie

> **Scope:** *My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic* (G4), through "The Last Problem." Spoilers through the finale; no on-screen G5 role.

## Identity
- **Species / kind:** Earth pony mare.
- **First appearance:** "Hearthbreakers" (S5E20).
- **Role:** Member of the Pie family who lives and works at the family rock farm; one of Pinkie Pie's younger sisters.
- **Home / affiliation:** The Pie rock farm outside Ponyville.

## Family and relationships
- **Igneous Rock Pie and Cloudy Quartz** - her father and mother, traditional and dour heads of the family.
- **Limestone Pie, Maud Pie and Pinkie Pie** - her sisters; Limestone is the eldest and Maud the older rock specialist. Marble is the quiet one in a loud family.
- **Big McIntosh** - a shy, wordless mutual spark during the joint Hearth's Warming in "Hearthbreakers." The show leaves it as a brief hint; Big Mac later marries Sugar Belle.

## Key canon arc
- **"Hearthbreakers" (S5E20):** the Apples and Pies try to share Hearth's Warming traditions, and the visit nearly becomes a clan quarrel. Marble's silent understanding with Big Mac is one of the episode's small warm beats.
- **Later appearances:** she remains a background member of the Pie family, associated with the rock farm and family gatherings rather than a personal storyline.

## Traits and abilities
- Extremely shy and soft-spoken, often barely audible; more comfortable in the background than at the center of attention.
- Gentle, observant and kind, with a strong attachment to her family and home.
- Competent at the physical work of the rock farm, though the show emphasizes her temperament more than a specific special talent.
- Limits: social anxiety and a tendency to withdraw; no combat or magic role is established.

## Uncertainty / source limits
- The show does not build a romance arc for Marble and Big Mac; the Hearth's Warming moment is a hint, not a relationship.
- Exact birth order details, including whether any sibling bond is a twin bond, are inconsistently described in secondary sources. Keep to "Pinkie's younger sister."
- No G5 appearance or follow-up is known.
