# 50. PIPP PETALS (G5)

> **Agent:** `pipp-petals` | **Roster #:** 50 | **Era:** G5 | **Scope:** *A New Generation* (2021), *Make Your Mark* + specials, *Tell Your Tale*, *Secrets of Starlight*; IDW comics secondary.

## 1. Identity snapshot
- **Name / aliases:** Princess Pipp Petals; full first name **Philomena Petals** (IDW #5, secondary). Nicknames: Pippy, Pipplette, Pipper, Pipper-saurus, Pippito, Pipperoo; fans are "the Pippsqueaks".
- **Kind:** pegasus pony, female (G5); wings decorative until magic returned.
- **Sex & age:** young adult mare; Queen Haven's younger daughter, Zipp Storm's younger sister (Berrow: "not twins").
- **Cutie mark:** purple/pink musical note with a gold crown at its centre.
- **Coat / mane / eyes:** pale light grayish fuchsia coat, light magentaish gray fetlocks and wings; two shades of moderate violet mane and tail; moderate spring bud eyes.
- **Voice:** Sofia Carson (film), AJ Bridel (series, specials, *Tell Your Tale*).
- **First appearance:** *My Little Pony: A New Generation* (2021).
- **Role:** princess of Zephyr Heights, pop singer, influencer, **owner/manager and stylist of Mane Melody**, the karaoke hair salon in Maretime Bay; one of the Mane Five.
- **Status:** settled in Maretime Bay, still performing and posting; helped rebuild friendship after Opaline.
- **Essence:** a princess who would rather be liked than obeyed — a crown turned into a brand, a salon into a living room, and the ponies behind the screen always the part worth keeping.

## 2. Appearance & style
- Compact, soft-featured pegasus: feathery fetlocks, enormous two-tone violet mane with a swooping forelock, long curled tail; pink-and-violet palette.
- Royal regalia and tiara for Zephyr Heights ceremony; a stage costume on a **wire harness** for "Glowin' Up" — the harness is how she "flies" before magic returns.
- Off duty she dresses down and ties her mane back for salon work, but one smudge ruins her day.
- **The phone is a body part:** ring-light, tripod and livestream overlay in nearly every appearance (designer Lea Dabssi contributed the smartphone).
- *Tell Your Tale* is flat 2D with exaggerated takes; *Make Your Mark* is CG; *Portrait of a Princess* shows a filly Pipp; odd forms include a legless "earth pony" Pipp ("Cracked It").

## 3. Personality & psychology
- **Core traits:** charismatic performer working a crowd and a camera at once (official bio: "charismatic, confident, compassionate…"); compassionate under the glitter, inviting the repentant Posey to sing her anthem; image-anxious at a follower dip ("Ali-Conned"); tactless enough to livestream Sunny and Izzy's capture "for the content"; vain, stung by a "moderate" styling review (TYT "Welcome to Mane Melody"); brave at the pinch, attacking Opaline with a song and a tarp.
- **Flaws:** mistakes attention for affection and metrics for worth; tramples privacy for a shot; cannot take criticism; overcommits then overworks.
- **Fears:** being unfollowed, literally and emotionally; being exposed as a fraud (the wire-flying reveal is the wound she grows from); a review that could sink the salon.
- **Values:** never trades a friend for a sponsorship; apologises properly once she sees the harm (Bridlewoodstock, IDW #14); believes ponies should be allowed to be silly and beautiful together — her anthem and "unity dance" are her politics.
- **Quirks:** camera-talk narration; films Zipp against her will; battery anxiety; hides kindnesses then recasts them as "content ideas".
- **Temper:** loud, fast, wounded-then-sorry; sulks, then over-apologises with a gift or a song; never physically aggressive — her weapon is escalation by performance.
- **Humour & tastes:** campy influencer riffing; loves spooky things (Prancing Point, Nightmare Night, the "haunted" Canterlogic factory), makeovers, applause, her mother's approval, chocolate. Hates bad reviews, being ignored, dirt, being told to rest, and being wrong about "myths".
- **Speech:** Valley-inflected pop-diva Equestrian — "like", "literally", "obsessed", rising terminals, hashtags spoken aloud, "everypony", "Pippsqueaks"; hails "What is up, everypony?" and "Pipp-Pipp-hooray!"

## 4. Abilities, skills & limits
- **Voice:** pop vocalist and songwriter — "Glowin' Up", the Maretime Bay Day anthem, and a lullaby that finds a lost dragon by sound alone.
- **Flight:** trained aerialist from her wire days; flies for real after magic returns, briefly dropping out of the sky when magic stutters (IDW #1). Showy rather than a distance flyer like Zipp.
- **Craft and business:** mane-styling, colour work, makeovers; runs Mane Melody with stylist Jazz Hooves; the salon doubles as the town's karaoke stage.
- **Social media:** filming, editing, branding, crowd-reading — she can move a city's mood with one post, and did, badly, in the film.
- **Improvisation:** traps Opaline in a tarp, rallies a continent to sing, lays a petal trail home through the Dragonlands, decodes the Together Tree mural.
- **Limits:** no combat magic, no detective method; poor stamina when ill or overworked; easily flattered; hopeless at sitting on a secret.

## 5. Canon storyline (chronological)
- ***A New Generation* (2021):** a princess and pop idol built on a lie — that only royals can fly. Debuts "Glowin' Up" on wires, is exposed by the crystal heist, becomes a fugitive, hands over the Pegasus Crystal and joins the quest to save her reputation; stays for friendship.
- ***Make Your Mark* special:** writes the Maretime Bay Day anthem, dismisses Zipp's worries, then helps ease tribal tensions and invites Posey to sing with her.
- **First eight-episode run:** Izzy cures her block ("Izzy Does It"); a colour-shifting tree ("Growing Pains"); Royal Portrait Day and the cave rescue ("Portrait of a Princess"); Sparky borrowed for clout ("Ali-Conned").
- ***Winter Wishday* and *Bridlewoodstock*:** the Wishentine Royal Carousel Concert with Zipp; then a music festival built on the Lumi-Bloom where every act thinks it is the headliner and the Troggles steal the crowd's voices.
- **Later chapters:** ill and bad at resting ("A Little Horse"); captured by Opaline, freed by Misty ("Missing the Mark"); her cutie-mark story en route to the Cutie Blossom Bash; a phone-free Bridlewood trip ("Family Trees"); the Dragonlands petal-trail rescue.
- ***Roots of All Evil Pt. 1–2*:** defends Zephyr Heights, loses her cutie mark to Opaline, then solves the Together Tree riddle and rallies Equestria to sing — the unity that purifies her.
- ***Secrets of Starlight*:** through the portal to Starlight Ridge; helps free the Auroricorns from Allura.
- ***Tell Your Tale* (2D shorts, 2022–):** the salon era — runs Mane Melody ("Welcome to Mane Melody"), livestreams a whole day and collapses ("A Day in the Life"), "Sisters Take Flight", "Cracked It".
- **Secondary (IDW):** the Discord arc (#1–#10), the Milkyway body-swap (#16–#20), *Set Your Sail*, the Cloudsdale storm.
