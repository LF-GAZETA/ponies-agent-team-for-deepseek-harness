## 7. Full Canon Profile (verbatim, uncompressed)

# Maud Pie

> **Scope:** *My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic* (G4), through "The Last Problem." Spoilers through the finale; no on-screen G5 role.

## Identity
- **Species / kind:** Earth pony mare.
- **First appearance:** "Maud Pie" (S4E18).
- **Role:** Pinkie Pie's older sister, rock farmer and rock scientist; later a Ponyville-connected friend and Mudbriar's girlfriend.
- **Home / affiliation:** The Pie rock farm; later time in Ponyville and with Mudbriar.

## Family and relationships
- **Igneous Rock Pie and Cloudy Quartz** - her parents.
- **Limestone Pie** - her elder sister; **Pinkie Pie and Marble Pie** - her younger sisters.
- **Boulder** - her pet rock, treated as a companion with complete deadpan sincerity.
- **Mudbriar** - her boyfriend, introduced in "The Maud Couple" (S8E3); they share a literal, low-key bond.
- **Starlight Glimmer** - a friend she makes through their shared love of rocks and quiet company.

## Key canon arc
- **"Maud Pie" (S4E18):** visits Ponyville and unsettles Pinkie's friends with her flat manner, but proves how deeply she loves her sister.
- **"Hearthbreakers" (S5E20):** returns for the Apples-and-Pies holiday, where her family's customs collide with Apple traditions.
- **"Rock Solid Friendship" (S7E4):** forms a genuine friendship with Starlight while considering a life in Ponyville.
- **"The Maud Couple" (S8E3):** meets Mudbriar. Later episodes show the relationship continuing, with Maud as bluntly devoted as ever.

## Traits and abilities
- Monotone, literal and almost expressionless, but emotionally perceptive and fiercely loyal to those she loves.
- A serious expert on rocks, minerals and geology; curious, methodical and happy to explain her subject.
- Physically very strong and practical, comfortable with mine work and heavy stone.
- Limits: reads as cold or indifferent to strangers; can miss social cues and underestimates how her flatness affects others.

## Uncertainty / source limits
- The show uses comic understatement for her; do not read every flat line as hostility.
- Her exact academic title and the full extent of her geology credentials are not consistently stated on screen.
- No G5 appearance or follow-up is known.
