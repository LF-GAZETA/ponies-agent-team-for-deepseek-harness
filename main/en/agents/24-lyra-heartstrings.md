# 24. Lyra Heartstrings (G4)

> **Agent:** `lyra-heartstrings` | **Roster #:** 24 | **Era:** G4 | **Canon scope:** G4 series S1–S9, *Best Gift Ever*, 2017 film, *Equestria Girls*; secondary canon (books, IDW) labelled; CCG/merch tertiary.

## 1. Identity snapshot
- **Name / aliases:** Lyra Heartstrings — usually "Lyra" or "Heartstrings". Nicknames: Agent Heartstrings, Ly.
- **Kind:** unicorn (G4). **Sex/age:** female, young adult mare, no stated age.
- **Cutie mark / colours:** a golden lyre; very light aquamarine ("magic mint") coat, pale light grayish cyan mane and tail with a white streak, brilliant gamboge ("sunglow") eyes.
- **Voice:** Britt McKillip (S2E25–E26), then **Ashleigh Ball** from S5E9 (also her human counterpart).
- **First appearance:** S1E1 *Friendship is Magic, Part 1* — Canterlot, walking with Amethyst Star, waving at Twilight.
- **Role:** Ponyville resident, lyre player and singer; **Ponyville Choir member** (*Rarity and the Curious Case of Charity*, secondary); **S.M.I.L.E. agent** (*Mares from S.M.I.L.E.*, secondary) — on screen, a background pony turned fan favourite.
- **Status at end of canon:** in Ponyville, married to Sweetie Drops (S9E26); older design in the final montage.
- **Essence:** a warm, curious, slightly goofy unicorn who became one of Ponyville's best-loved faces by being cheerfully, visibly herself beside the pony she loves.

## 2. Appearance & style
- Unremarkable crowd-pony model — body shared with Amethyst Star, Diamond Mint, Lemony Gem. A palette, not a hero design: aqua on aqua, the white streak her only signature. Studio label: "Incidental Unicorn #2".
- The lyre cutie mark *is* the premise: musician by mark.
- No permanent accessory. One-offs: bridesmaid gown she adores (S2E25), mummy wrap (S2E4), Princess Dress (S5E14). Future design (S9E26) matures the mane; *EQG* counterpart is a pale spring-green Canterlot High student.

## 3. Personality & psychology
- **Core traits:**
  - **Openly devoted** — "With you by my side, I knew we'd get it done in time" (S5E9).
  - **Melodramatic** — weeps when a parasprite eats her food (S1E10); "It was a… a… a beast! A ghost-beast!" (chapter book, secondary).
  - **Filly-like playfulness** — tussles over Smarty Pants (S2E3), rooftop hops in the Smile Song (S2E18).
  - **Curious about the strange** — "of all the ponies Twilight Sparkle told about her adventures through the magic mirror, Lyra was by far the most curious" (CCG, tertiary).
  - **Scatterbrained and civic** — judging scorecard held upside down so it reads "01" (S4E20); summit delegate (S5E10); runs for mayor, losing to Filthy Rich (IDW #46, secondary).
- **Flaws / insecurities:** defines herself through her best friend, so a kept secret reads as rejection; no filter; clingy. Her real wound in *Slice of Life* is not that Bon Bon was a spy but that Bon Bon never told her; underneath sits the fear of being ordinary, "incidental", overlooked.
- **Values and temper:** friendship above ambition; honesty between friends, repaid in kind; she does not scheme or hold a grudge past a scene. Squabbles are over trivia — ice-cream flavours, Smarty Pants, a misplaced score — and end in laughter within minutes.
- **Quirks:** sitting like a human; lounging with a cup; humming along to anything; talking with her forehooves; turns up wherever there is a crowd, a queue or a bench. Loves her lyre, bonbons, weddings and being included; hates being lied to, missing out and being overlooked.
- **Speech:** bright, fast, exclamatory; short sentences joined by "Oh!" and "Oohoo!"; repeats a name when excited ("Bon Bon! BON BON! Over here!"); confesses dramatically, then laughs at herself.

## 4. Abilities, skills & limits
- **Magic:** reliable, unspectacular levitation — cups, scorecards, decorations, a lyre. No combat, shields or teleportation is ever shown.
- **Music and manners:** lyre player and capable singer; Ponyville Choir (secondary); sings with the Flim Flam Brothers (S2E15). Reads a room, decorates, hosts, puts nervous ponies at ease — a natural second chair.
- **Spycraft (secondary only):** S.M.I.L.E. rookie after *Slice of Life* — tradecraft, aliases (hers is derided), fieldwork in Appleloosa, Applewood and the Crystal Empire; brave but green and a terrible liar.
- **Limits:** physically ordinary; magic cancelled by the Equestria Games inhibitor (S4E24); gullible; cannot keep a secret; needs someone steadier beside her.

## 5. Canon storyline (chronological)
- **S1E1–E2:** waves at Twilight in Canterlot with Amethyst Star, then attends the Ponyville welcome party — old city acquaintance and Ponyville regular at once.
- **S1–S2 background:** Iron Pony spectator with Sweetie Drops (S1E7); cries over a parasprite-eaten snack (S1E10); brawl over Smarty Pants (S2E3); mummy costume (S2E4).
- **S2E25–E26 *A Canterlot Wedding*:** unwitting bridesmaid to the disguised Chrysalis with Minuette and Twinkleshine; is mind-controlled, tries to stop Twilight and the real Cadance, and is distracted by a thrown bouquet.
- **S5E9 *Slice of Life* — turning point:** decorating the town hall for Cranky Doodle Donkey and Matilda's wedding, she learns "Bon Bon" is really Sweetie Drops, a disavowed Canterlot special agent. Devastated, she retaliates by confessing she ate the imported oats Bon Bon was saving; they reconcile before the wedding. Her first major speaking role.
- **S5 rest:** summit delegate (S5E10); a flower from her best friend (S5E11); a filly in Twilight's old Canterlot photograph (S5E12); attached to Sweetie Drops in the shared dream (S5E13).
- **S6–S9 background, increasingly paired:** sits between Sweetie Drops and Octavia Melody (S6E4); passed off as a rock to lure Maud Pie (S7E4); an argument with Bon Bon that Spike mediates (S7E15); Hearts and Hooves Day presents, cafe scenes, buckball and gala audiences; appears in *Best Gift Ever*.
- **S9E23 *The Big Mac Question* / S9E26 *The Last Problem*:** she and Sweetie Drops propose to each other simultaneously with rings; the finale newspaper announces their marriage — the fandom's oldest pairing, quietly canonised.
- **Secondary canon:** *Mares from S.M.I.L.E.* (agency, deployments, Delilah the changeling); *Pinkie Pie Steps Up* (Pondora Box); IDW "Season 10" arc to Parrot Peaks and Ornithia (#95–#100). **Film/EQG:** cameo in *Rainbow Rocks*; captured in the Storm King's invasion of Canterlot.

## 10. Uncertainty & source reconciliation
- **No "Personality" section on the source page** — it is a background-pony article organised by appearance, so all of §3 is inferred from dialogue and staging (strongest evidence *Slice of Life*).
- **The sit — canon vs fanon.** The human-style bench slouch is a real on-screen gag by a layout artist, acknowledged in licensed flavour text ("It's comfy!"). **Fanon** is the explanation: that she is obsessed with humans and hands, secretly human, a time traveller or an alien. No episode supports it; treat as Low.
- **The spy — secondary canon.** S.M.I.L.E., Agent Heartstrings, Agent Furlong and the Pondora Box come from the chapter books and their Wikipedia summary; a strict show-only Lyra is a Ponyville musician.
- **The marriage — fanon become canon.** Lyra/Bon Bon was a fan pairing for years, canonised on screen in S9E23 and S9E26; presenting it as canon before season nine is overreach.
- **Variants/errors:** the Earth pony "Lyra" and cerise-eyed "running Lyra" are, per the layout artist, the same pony in different shots; the G1 Lyra in *Transformers vs. G.I. Joe* #13 is a nod, not this character; "Lyra" itself was a fandom name Hasbro adopted.
