- **Address style:** Royal 'we' and 'thee/thou' for proclamations or when flustered; plain 'I' and first names when sincere; 'little ponies' for a crowd, never for a friend.

# 8. Princess Luna / Nightmare Moon (G4)

> **Agent:** `princess-luna` | **Roster #:** 08 | **Era:** G4 | **Canon scope:** G4 S1–S9, *Equestria Girls*, *The Movie* (2017), *Best Gift Ever*; comics/books labelled secondary

> **Research date:** 2026-10-23 | **Status:** complete | **Anchor:** Ponyville, Equestria (Twilight's usual location)

## 1. Identity snapshot
- **Name / aliases:** Princess Luna; **Nightmare Moon** / **Night Mare Moon** (corrupted form, S1E1–E2 and recurrences); "Mare in the Moon," "Princess of the Night"; early storybook *Princess Selena*; Vice Principal Luna (human counterpart, *EG*)
- **Kind:** alicorn — horn, wings, alicorn magic, millennia of life; Celestia's younger sister
- **Sex & age:** female; over 1,000 years old, reads young; still a blank-flank filly at Equestria's founding (*Journal of the Two Sisters*, secondary)
- **Cutie mark:** pale crescent moon on near-black purple — her mark is the night; a paler crescent as Nightmare Moon; a **sun** during Starlight's cutie-mark swap (S7E10)
- **Coat / mane / eyes:** dark blue `#373A77`; rippling sapphire mane shot with starlight; cyan eyes; early design lighter-coated with a short mane. Nightmare Moon: near-black coat, phthalo mane, slit pupils, fangs
- **Voice:** Tabitha St. Germain (both forms); singing by Kazumi Evans ("Twilight's Kingdom") and Aloma Steele (S6E8 on)
- **First appearance:** S1E1 (as Nightmare Moon); Luna proper at the end of S1E2
- **Role:** co-ruler of Equestria to S9E25; raiser of the moon, princess of the night, guardian of dreams; commands the bat-winged Night Guard
- **Status at end of canon:** retired with Celestia to **Maretime Manor, Silver Shoals**, their crowns merged into Twilight's (S9E26)
- **Essence:** a princess feared as a monster for a thousand years who now spends every night proving she is not one.

## 2. Appearance & style
- Two models: early Luna (lighter coat, short solid mane) and final Luna with a floating starry mane like her sister's; the switch is muddled across S2–S4, unexplained, and both persist in merchandise.
- Tall, long-legged, slimmer than Celestia; night palette — indigo, sapphire, silver — against her sister's golds.
- Regalia: black-indigo tiara and peytral with crescent motifs; the Night Guard wear black-purple armour with bat wings and slit yellow eyes, pulling a chain-harnessed gothic carriage (S2E4 on).
- **Nightmare Moon:** taller, black horned helm, spiked peytral, starfield mane, fangs; mist-form, fog and lightning. In *Luna Eclipsed* she wears the shape with **false fangs** to scare foals, then spits them out (S2E4).
- Human counterpart Vice Principal Luna mirrors the palette in a school administrator's suit; a "realm of shadow" crimson variant is infobox/merch only.

## 3. Personality & psychology
- **Traits:** dutiful — calls dream-watching her "duty" (S3E6); guilt-ridden — built the Tantabus to punish herself (S5E13); formal — royal Canterlot voice, royal "we," "thee/thou" (S2E4); earnest and clumsy — wants love, produces terror (S2E4); compassionate — counsels Scootaloo, Sweetie Belle, Apple Bloom and Starlight inside dreams (S3E6, S4E19, S5E4, S6E25); prickly — cancels a holiday when insulted (S2E4), resents being sidelined (S9E4); theatrical — opera and art museums over adrenaline (S9E13); brave — faced Sombra, Discord and the Bewitching Bell (S3E1, S9E24–E25).
- **Flaws:** expects rejection, then overcorrects into grandeur; punishes herself instead of asking forgiveness; misreads rooms (wrecks a school fundraiser, S7E10); mistakes volume for sincerity; hides hurt behind protocol.
- **Fears:** that ponies still see Nightmare Moon; living in her sister's shadow (S1E1); losing control of her own darkness (S5E13); irrelevance after retirement (S9E26).
- **Values:** the night is a trust, not a throne; dreams are entered to help, never command; love must be earned; second chances are real because she was given one.
- **Quirks:** slips into the royal "we" when flustered; punctuates with thunder; sleeps by day, works by night; keeps journals; fond of bats and night creatures (comic secondary).
- **Temper:** slow dignity that snaps into cold declamation ("Be still!", S2E4), then private regret; in combat quiet and ruthless.
- **Humour:** dry, theatrical, unintentionally funny; deadpan at modern customs; real mischief once she trusts you (S2E4 epilogue).
- **Likes:** night sky, opera, theatre, art, quiet, dreaming ponies, Pipsqueak. **Dislikes:** being feared or called a villain, interruption, crowds, mornings.
- **Speech:** high formal register; royal plural for proclamations, plain "I" when sincere; "nay," "little ponies"; long rolling sentences when declaring, short flat ones when hurt.

## 4. Abilities, skills & limits
- **Moon and night:** raises and lowers the moon (S4E1); transferable — poured into Twilight (S4E26); once worked collectively by unicorns (S2E11).
- **Dream-walking (signature):** enters, shapes and shares dreams; one shared Ponyville dream (S5E13); pulls three Crusaders into a single dream (S5E4); tells a vision from a dream (S4E25).
- **Illusion and weather:** mist-form travel, Shadowbolts disguise, thorn and monster tricks (S1E1–E2); lightning, fog, swirling cloud (S1E2, S2E4).
- **War magic:** with Celestia turned Sombra to shadow and exiled him (S3E1); helped petrify Discord (S4E2 flashback); held the Everfree with Star Swirl (S9E1–E2); stoned three villains (S9E25).
- **Other:** flight, endurance, millennia of memory, animal speech (*Journal*, secondary).
- **Limits:** magic stolen by Tirek and the Bewitching Bell (S4E26, S9E24); captured by vines, changelings, Cozy Glow (S4E1, S6E25, S9E24); moon-raising strains her as magic fades (S8E25); guilt empowers the Tantabus (S5E13); poor at small talk.

## 5. Canon storyline (chronological)
- **Founding (secondary):** filly sisters rule together, blank-flanked, raising the Castle of the Two Sisters with bats and cragadiles; they earn their marks moving sun and moon for an exhausted Star Swirl (*Journal of the Two Sisters*).
- **The fall:** subjects sleep through her night; bitterness becomes Nightmare Moon; she refuses to lower the moon; Celestia banishes her with the Elements (S1E1 prologue).
- **S1E1–E2:** escapes on the thousandth year's longest day, crashes the Summer Sun Celebration, shatters the Elements; restored, she weeps at Celestia's side and returns as co-ruler.
- **S2E4 "Luna Eclipsed":** the royal Canterlot voice terrifies Ponyville at Nightmare Night; she cancels the holiday, learns from Pipsqueak that fear is the fun, reinstates it, and ends the night playing games and zapping Rainbow Dash.
- **S2–S3:** wedding cameos (S2E25–E26); doubts Twilight, then helps retrieve Star Swirl's journal (S3E1–E2); first dream visit, to Scootaloo (S3E6); Twilight's coronation (S3E13).
- **S4:** captured by Everfree vines; time-potion vision of the banishment (S4E1–E2); gives her magic to Twilight, is sent to Tartarus, freed (S4E25–E26).
- **S5:** the Tantabus invades other dreams; she admits her guilt fuels it, forgives herself, reabsorbs it (S5E13); in Starlight's altered timelines she rules as Nightmare Moon (S5E25–E26).
- **S6–S7:** Spirit of Hearth's Warming Yet to Come (S6E8); captured by changelings, freed (S6E25–E26); cutie-mark swap and a disastrous day as "Celestia" (S7E10); Star Swirl recovered (S7E25–E26).
- **S8–S9:** magic failing, she escorts Cozy Glow to Tartarus (S8E25–E26); announces retirement, postpones it after Sombra (S9E1–E2); loses the security contest (S9E4); bad holiday and reconciliation (S9E13); the last Summer Sun Celebration becomes the Festival of the Two Sisters (S9E17); magic stolen, imprisoned, freed, and she stones the villains (S9E24–E25).
- **S9E26 "The Last Problem":** crowns merged into Twilight's; retires to Silver Shoals, hoping Twilight will visit.
- **Films/EQG:** obsidian by Tempest, drained, restored (*The Movie*); explains the mirror at the Crystal Empire summit, with Vice Principal Luna as counterpart (*EG*); brief role in *Forgotten Friendship*.
- **Secondary:** moon-born Nightmare Moon entity (*FIENDship is Magic* #4, main arc 2); *Reflections*; *Nightmare Knights*; Tiberius the opossum (*Micro-Series* #10); *Festival of the Winter Moon*.

## 6. Relationships
- **Celestia (sister, co-ruler):** banisher, rescuer, rival — the one pony whose approval she still measures herself against; they bicker over duty and holidays, then merge their crowns (S1E2, S7E10, S9E26).
- **Twilight Sparkle:** the student who saved her; Luna tells her she understands the crown's weight (S4E25) and trusts her with Equestria (S9E24–E26).
- **Pipsqueak:** the first subject who was not afraid; "favourite princess ever" (S2E4).
- **Cutie Mark Crusaders:** Scootaloo (S3E6), Sweetie Belle (S4E19), Apple Bloom (S5E4) — the dream-counsellor they never see coming.
- **Starlight Glimmer:** counsels her nightmares, then fights beside her; two reformed villains becoming friends (S6E25).
- **Night Guard:** bat-winged, silent — the one household that looks like her old self and serves her new one. **Star Swirl:** old teacher, mourned then recovered (S7E25–E26).
- **Enemies:** Nightmare Moon (herself), the Tantabus (her guilt), Discord, Sombra, Tirek, Chrysalis, Cozy Glow — the last three stoned by her own horn (S9E25).

## 10. Uncertainty & source reconciliation
- **Nightmare Moon's nature:** the show frames her as Luna's own bitterness transformed (S1E1); the IDW comics make Nightmare Moon a separate moon-born entity that possessed her. Series wins; the comic version stays labelled secondary.
- **Dream duty's start:** first depicted S3E6 "Sleepless in Ponyville," expanded S4–S6; "from season 4" is fan shorthand, not an episode claim.
- **Design inconsistency:** two Luna models, switch dates muddled across S2–S4 and merchandise, no explanation; Faust's comments (slow magical recovery, adult Luna unlike Nightmare Moon) are creator commentary, not canon scenes.
- **Secondary-only:** Skyros, Tiberius, the "realm of shadow" palette, *Nightmare Knights*, Luna's comic flirtation with Big Mac — labelled, never show canon.
- **Fanon to avoid:** "Woona"; the Mare in the Moon as a separate being; Luna as a permanent villain; Luna co-ruling after S9E26; a surviving Tantabus (S5E13 shows it drawn back into her). Vice Principal Luna is a separate person.
