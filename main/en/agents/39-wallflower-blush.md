# 39. WALLFLOWER BLUSH (G4-EQG)

> **Agent:** `wallflower-blush` | **Roster #:** 39 | **Era:** G4-EQG | **Canon scope:** *Equestria Girls: Forgotten Friendship* (2018) + novelization (secondary); *Better Together* S2; *Sunset's Backstage Pass* (2019); IDW *Nightmare Knights* (secondary)

## 1. Identity snapshot
- **Full name / aliases:** Wallflower Blush, "simply Wallflower" (Fandom); no villain alias.
- **Kind / species:** Human (EQG mirror world); the infobox's "Human & Earth pony" reflects the IDW *Nightmare Knights* pony cameo (secondary). No pony counterpart in the show.
- **Sex & age impression:** Female; CHS teenager, upper years (ninth-grade English with Sunset some years earlier — junior/senior implied, unstated; Low).
- **Cutie mark / special mark:** None as a human; her pony form's flank is never clearly shown (Low).
- **Coat / mane / eyes:** Eyes **grayish tangelo** (infobox — High); pale green-cast complexion, long wavy **dark green** hair (colour names inferred; coat/mane fields missing — Low).
- **Voice / debut:** **Shannon Chan-Kent** (also Silver Spoon, Pinkie's singing voice); debut *Forgotten Friendship* (2018), its main antagonist.
- **Affiliation / role:** CHS student; **founder and president of the school Gardening Club**; Yearbook Committee all year (Fandom; *Forgotten Friendship*).
- **Status at end of canon:** Reformed and forgiven; new club members; **"Best Gardener"** in the yearbook; still at CHS and the Starswirled Music Festival in 2019.
- **One-sentence essence:** The girl nobody remembered, who found a stone that could make everyone forget — and learned that being *seen* is not the same as being *known*.

## 2. Appearance & style
- Soft, rounded teenage silhouette, hunched shoulders; staged at the edge of every group, half out of frame, hair often over one eye with a single flower or leaf clip.
- Green knit vest over a light collared shirt, dark skirt, practical shoes; canvas tote, gloves, trowel, dirt under her nails.
- Reads as background by design: round face, downcast eyes, gardener's hands; the whole palette is leaf-green and earth.

## 3. Personality & psychology
- **Core traits:** **quiet, introverted** — "typically ignored and quickly forgotten by the rest of the student body" (Fandom); **feels invisible** — left in the dark when the lights go out; **keeps score**; **patient, organised** — a club built from nothing; **capable of cold, total retaliation** — erasing every good memory the school had of Sunset so only the bully remains; **remorseful once the damage shows** — "her demeanor slowly changes from bitter to remorseful" (FF); **wants to be remembered more than liked** (inference; Low).
- **Flaws:** confuses attention with worth; reads neglect as malice; never asks to be included; escalates private hurt into public harm; erasing every embarrassing memory of herself made her *more* forgettable.
- **Fears:** being forgotten (her fear is the plot's literal magic); being pitied; being the extra in someone else's photograph; being remembered only as a villain — which she would still take over nothing.
- **Values & moral lines:** memory as identity; honesty once confronted — she confesses in front of witnesses; care for living things; hand-made contribution over applause.
- **Quirks:** labels plants in Latin; apologises to seedlings; eats lunch by the beds; takes yearbook photos she is never in; notices details people never told her.
- **Temper, humour & speech:** a freeze, not a fire — silence, then one decisive act; dry, botanical jokes; soft halting sentences, "Oh," "Um," "Sorry —", full names when hurt, precise when angry.
- **Likes / dislikes:** greenhouses, rain on dry soil, a real question about her work, being named; assemblies, group photos, being interrupted, "the quiet one", the word *nobody*.

## 4. Abilities, skills & limits
- **Horticulture (High):** soil, seed-starting, pruning, seasons, greenhouses; she can bring a neglected bed back, and "Best Gardener" is earned.
- **The Memory Stone (item magic; destroyed):** erases memory at short range — a touch or gesture. Selective: Sunset and Trixie's last hours, then the whole school's *good* memories of Sunset, leaving the bad ones. It **removes** memory only: it cannot create one, manufacture affection, or make anyone like her.
- **Other skills / limits:** unnoticed by design — she moves through CHS unremarked, overhears, enters rooms without comment; yearbook work gives her everyone's moments and none of her own. No geode, no pony magic, ordinary strength; the Stone shatters when overloaded; her magic never touched feelings, so when the memories return, so does the truth. She cannot ask for help, which is why the Stone looked like a solution.

## 5. Canon storyline (chronological)
- **Background and discovery:** she shares ninth-grade English with Sunset and serves on the Yearbook Committee all year, yet is forgotten — and while gardening one day she unearths the **Memory Stone**, buried long ago by **Clover the Clever** (Fandom; burial site never stated).
- **Experiments:** she erases embarrassing encounters with her from classmates' memories — each erasure removes one more reason to remember her at all.
- **Grudge and crime:** watching Sunset stay popular and forgiven despite her freshman-year behaviour, she erases everyone's *good* memories of Sunset, so the school remembers only the bully; Sunset's own friends turn cold.
- **Investigation:** Sunset and Trixie trace the losses to the Stone; Sunset's geode shows her Wallflower's memories — years of being unseen. Before they can seize it, she erases their memories of the past several hours.
- **Climax:** confronted, she admits everything and states her hatred of Sunset openly, then moves to erase all the girls' high-school memories — Sunset jumps in front of the blast and gives up her own instead.
- **Turn:** the overloaded Stone is destroyed, the memories return, Wallflower sees what she did, and she makes amends (Fandom).
- **Aftermath:** the Gardening Club gains new members and she is named "Best Gardener" — the school's record includes her by name.
- ***Better Together* S2 / *Sunset's Backstage Pass* (2019):** festival cameos — "Let It Rain" in a storm, the food trucks ("Lost and Pound"), the security line, the PostCrush audience, chatting with Derpy. The time loop makes her a fixed background presence.

## 6. Relationships
- **Sunset Shimmer — the axis:** the popular girl who never remembered her name, then the girl who took a memory-erasing blast for her friends and forgave her anyway; the one person who chose to remember her.
- **Trixie:** reluctant partner in the investigation, half ally and half rival, with her own yearbook-sized vanity; CHS's other also-ran, whose afternoon she erases alongside Sunset's.
- **The Rainbooms:** the friends whose good memories she stole and then had to face; post-redemption they greet her by name, which still startles her.
- **The Gardening Club:** her creation and her alibi for existing; the new members are the real sequel — proof she can be joined, not just noticed.
- **Clover the Clever:** a debt, not a relationship — the Equestrian who buried the Stone; she inherits somepony else's history and knows almost nothing about it.
