## 7. Full Canon Profile (verbatim, uncompressed)

# Mrs. Cup Cake

> **Scope:** *My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic* (G4), through "The Last Problem." Spoilers through the finale; no on-screen G5 role.

## Identity
- **Species / kind:** Earth pony mare.
- **First appearance:** Season 1, among Ponyville's background residents; her family life becomes central in "Baby Cakes" (S2E13).
- **Role:** Co-owner and baker at Sugarcube Corner with her husband; Pinkie Pie's employer, landlady and surrogate family.
- **Home / affiliation:** Sugarcube Corner, Ponyville.

## Family and relationships
- **Mr. Carrot Cake** - her husband and business partner.
- **Pound Cake and Pumpkin Cake** - her twin son and daughter, born in "Baby Cakes"; one is a pegasus and one a unicorn.
- **Pinkie Pie** - her employee and tenant, treated as a member of the Cake family.
- **Pear Butter** - a close friend from the past, remembered in "The Perfect Pear" (S7E13).

## Key canon arc
- **Running Sugarcube Corner:** with Carrot Cake, she makes the bakery a Ponyville institution and gives Pinkie a home, a job and room to be herself.
- **"Baby Cakes" (S2E13):** the twins are born, and the Cakes' exhaustion leaves Pinkie to babysit. The episode balances parental love with the comedy of an overwhelmed household.
- **Later seasons:** continues catering, celebrating and keeping the bakery running through town events and the series finale.

## Traits and abilities
- Warm, motherly, hardworking and practical, with a sharp eye for both baking and people.
- A skilled baker and caterer who can run a business, manage a chaotic kitchen and comfort a frightened child.
- Affectionate but not a pushover; she and her husband balance each other's temperaments.
- Limits: an earth pony with no magic; a supporting domestic figure rather than an adventurer.

## Uncertainty / source limits
- Her exact first appearance is a background-season-one question.
- Secondary sources give her maiden name as Chiffon Swirl; the show itself uses "Mrs. Cup Cake" and "Mrs. Cake."
- No G5 appearance or follow-up is known.
