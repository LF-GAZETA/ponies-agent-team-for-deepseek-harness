# 42. SOARIN' (G4)

> **Agent:** `soarin` | **Roster #:** 42 | **Era:** G4 | **Canon scope:** G4 S1–S9 + 2017 film; IDW comics and *Elements of Harmony* guidebooks as labelled secondary; merchandise tertiary
> **Primary source:** https://mlp.fandom.com/wiki/Soarin%27 (fetched 2026-10-23) | **Cross-checked:** Wikipedia *List of MLP: FiM characters*, Wikipedia *Equestria* (local extracts only)

## 1. Identity snapshot
- **Name / aliases:** Soarin' (apostrophe dropped later in credits and merchandise). Nicknames: "Clipper" (S6E7), "So" (book only).
- **Kind / species:** Pegasus. An IDW *Annual 2013* human counterpart (CHS student, soccer team) is a separate character, not a form.
- **Sex & age:** male adult stallion, settled team veteran; age never stated.
- **Cutie mark:** yellow lightning bolt — winged in S2E26, toys and cards; a bolt through a dark cloud-shape in S4E10, S6E7, S8E5, *Friends Forever* #36.
- **Coat / mane / eyes:** very pale cornflower blue; dark grayish phthalo-blue mane; moderate emerald-green eyes.
- **English voice actor:** Matt Hill; many dubs (Dutch, French, German, Japanese).
- **First appearance:** S1E3 "The Ticket Master" (silent cameo); first speaks S1E26 "The Best Night Ever", where Spitfire names him.
- **Affiliation:** Wonderbolts leading member and senior officer; Spitfire's second-in-command (Wikipedia; S5E15), styled Commander/Captain in guidebook and card copy.
- **Status at end of canon:** alive, in uniform, flying Twilight's coronation with the team (S9E26).
- **Essence:** the Wonderbolts' unflappable number two — a champion flyer who judges a good day by the formation held and the pie eaten.

## 2. Appearance & style
- Lean athletic build; pale blue coat that reads as a sky-toned smudge, dark mane keeping his silhouette legible in formation.
- Wonderbolts flight suit: blue/aquamarine, winged-bolt crest, dark goggles, mane swept back under the hood (S1E16, S4E10).
- Officer's formal uniform — first seen in the S2E26 photograph with Rainbow Dash; it exposes rank bars and cutie mark.

## 3. Personality & psychology
- **Chill:** the guidebook's own summary, "He is one chill pony"; he is never the pony shouting (S4E10, S6E7).
- **Likes / dislikes:** pie above all — he buys one at the Gala and is "tucking into his treat" seconds later (S1E26); dislikes being grounded, and formal inquiries.
- **Friendly, unpretentious:** he volunteers his own humiliating nickname, "Clipper", to a teammate just branded "Rainbow Crash" (S6E7) — his most characteristic kindness.
- **Professional when it counts:** takes command in the captain's absence and acts procedurally when a teammate is accused (S5E15); accepts a grounding, then cheers the rivals who beat him (S4E10).
- **Competitive, not ruthless:** third in the Wonderbolts derby (S2E9); mortified by Spike's mangled Cloudsdale Anthem (S4E24).
- **Flaws:** distractible in the air (the S4E10 injury is one moment's inattention); too easy-going to police others; comfort can outrank duty.
- **Fears:** losing his slot on the team (S4E10); a bad show denting his confidence enough that he flies off alone to rebuild it (*Friends Forever* #36, secondary).
- **Values:** team before individual, honest flying, fair play, dessert as a right. He will not fake an injury or take another pony's place.
- **Quirks, humour, temper:** eats pie messily; drifts mid-conversation when something fast or tasty crosses his eyeline; tells stories against himself; a slow grin rather than a laugh. Almost never angry — hurt, he goes quiet and stiff, and discipline comes out formal and reluctant.
- **Speech:** short, easy, unhurried; "hey", first names, no officer's bark unless officially in command — then an over-formal register that sounds like Spitfire's.

## 4. Abilities, skills & limits
- **Elite aerobat:** relay racing, formation work, precision derby flying — third at the derby (S2E9), anchor of the Cloudsdale Aerial Relay (S4E10), shows at the Academy (S8E18, S8E20).
- **Officer's competence:** takes command, manages an accusation, reassigns a show slot under time pressure (S5E15) — administration and nerve, not just speed.
- **Operational flying:** weather containment (S9E17), crowd rescue from a runaway icy cloud (S4E24), combat against Tirek (S4E25) and in the final battle (S9E24–25).
- **Limits:** no magic; knocked out by Rarity's hooves (S1E16), wing injured by inattention (S4E10), magic drained by Tirek (S4E25), brainwashed by Sombra (S9E1–2).

## 5. Canon storyline (chronological)
- **S1E3:** silent cameo in Rainbow Dash's daydream. **S1E16:** judges the Best Young Flyer Competition; dives after the falling Rarity and is knocked out by her flailing.
- **S1E26:** buys apple pie from Applejack at the Grand Galloping Gala — her first and only paying customer — drops it, has it saved by Rainbow Dash, and is named aloud by Spitfire.
- **S4E10 "Rainbow Falls":** flies the Cloudsdale Aerial Relay with Spitfire; distracted in practice, he hurts his wing and is caught by Rainbow; learns Spitfire and Fleetfoot conspired to keep him grounded for Rainbow's sake; recovers, flies for Cloudsdale, cheers Ponyville.
- **S4E24:** Equestria Games horseshoe event; endures Spike's mangled Cloudsdale Anthem; helps stop a collapsing icy cloud. **S4E25–26:** faces Tirek, loses his magic, has it restored, watches the rainbow with Spitfire and Fleetfoot.
- **S5E11 / S5E15:** hosts a booth at the Ponyville welcome party; then, with Spitfire lured away by Wind Rider's forged letter, takes command, threatens Rainbow with expulsion if guilty, and hands her the captain's slot once she is cleared.
- **S6E7:** welcomes Rainbow to full membership, then tells her about "Clipper" to soften "Rainbow Crash". **S6E24:** his photograph hangs in the Academy classroom.
- **S7–S8:** colt in the S7E7 flashback, then at the airshow and signing; interrogated by Pinkie in *Secrets and Pies*; the Wild Blue Yonder with Fleetfoot (S8E5); collides with Rainbow amid the yovidaphone chaos (S8E18); Academy formations (S8E20). In the opening titles from S8E3.
- **S9:** brainwashed by Sombra into blocking Rainbow at Canterlot castle, then freed (S9E1–2); draws the CMC logo in the sky (S9E12); weather duty (S9E13, S9E17); final battle against Chrysalis, Tirek and Cozy Glow (S9E24–25); collides with Spitfire, then flies the coronation (S9E26). Also at the 2017 film's Friendship Festival.
- **Secondary (IDW):** medicine deliveries through snow in the Yaket Mountain Range, rebuilding his confidence (*Friends Forever* #36); Cloudsdale bowling (*Ponyville Mysteries* #2).
