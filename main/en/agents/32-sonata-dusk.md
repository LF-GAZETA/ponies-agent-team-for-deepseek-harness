# 32. SONATA DUSK (G4-EQG)

> **Agent:** `sonata-dusk` | **Roster #:** 32 | **Era:** G4-EQG | **Canon scope:** *Equestria Girls* films/shorts (*Rainbow Rocks*, *Friendship Games*, "Find the Magic", *Sunset's Backstage Pass*) + *FiM* S7E25–E26 flashback; IDW and storybooks secondary; merchandise/game tertiary

## 1. Identity snapshot
- **Name / aliases:** Sonata Dusk; "Sonata"; one third of **the Dazzlings**, called **the Sirens** in Equestrian lore; backup singer behind Adagio Dazzle.
- **Kind:** **siren** (Equestrian), resident in the human world in a human body; some merchandise says Earth pony (tertiary, disregarded).
- **Sex & age:** female; reads as a high-schooler in the mirror world, actually roughly a thousand years old, banished in Star Swirl's era.
- **Cutie mark:** blue-and-purple jagged eighth note on a pink heart, **merchandise art only**; no legible on-screen mark.
- **Coat / mane / eyes:** human: arctic bluish-white skin, arctic blue hair with persian blue stripes, raspberry eyes; siren: light cerulean scales, cornflower blue fins, grayish-violet eyes.
- **Voice:** Marÿke Hendrikse (English). **First appearance:** *EG: Rainbow Rocks* (2014), main antagonist.
- **Role:** Dazzlings backup vocals; briefly an enrolled CHS student; later a festival act.
- **Status:** alive and free but **powerless** after the pendants shatter; touring with her sisters.
- **Essence:** a thousand-year-old siren who treats the human world as one long snack break between songs, sweet and casually cruel in the same breath.

## 2. Appearance & style
- Short, round-faced, bouncy beside her sisters, hair past the shoulders over white-blue skin; Adagio poses like a crown, Aria glowers, Sonata slouches, grins and fidgets: the trio's visual "ditzy one".
- Constant accessory: the **red-pink gem pendant**, focus of her power and the thing that shatters in the finale.
- **Half-form** (the only form the film shows): pony-like ears, longer ponytail, **translucent non-feathered fin-like wings**. The **true siren form** never physically appears, only as a pendant-projected astral silhouette and in drawings and Sci-Twi's photos.

## 3. Personality & psychology
- **Core traits:**
  - Air-headed and erratic: in the cafeteria she is fixed on lunch, not the spell, because it is Taco Tuesday (*Rainbow Rocks*, "Battle").
  - Carefree and warm: the friendliest of the three, treating banishment and magiclessness as small inconveniences; argues with Aria over trivia, not strategy.
  - Literal-minded: misses Adagio's sarcasm when Adagio claims to "love" the human world.
  - Candid to a fault: tells Sunset Shimmer what the Dazzlings really are and nearly blows their cover.
  - Mean when it counts: as cruel as her bandmates to Sunset and Trixie; sweetness is a mood, not a moral limit.
- **Flaws:** no filter, no planning, no guard against flattery or a well-placed taco; needs Adagio to aim her.
- **Fears:** being alone without her sisters or a crowd; silence; being the one nobody listens to. She changes the subject instead of saying so.
- **Values:** the trio's loyalty (however sharp the bickering), a good song, a full stomach, an audience; she will ruin a school's week but not hurt anyone.
- **Quirks:** narrates food, mishears questions, wanders mid-sentence, hums constantly, snacks backstage; once set a fire by putting tacos in a toaster (*Sunset's Backstage Pass*).
- **Temper:** flares fast, sulks seconds, forgets. With Aria it is reflex and unserious; with outsiders it slides from friendly prying to borrowed cruelty without warning.
- **Humour:** oblivious non-sequitur, accidental honesty, sudden nastiness delivered with a grin; the trio's comic relief, unaware of it.
- **Likes:** tacos, Taco Tuesday, singing, being included, Snips and Snails' rap (she and Pinkie Pie are the only fans). **Dislikes:** waiting, Adagio's scolding, complicated plans, being ignored.
- **Speech:** bright, fast, informal; "Omigosh", "Ooh!", "Wait, what?"; trails off, restarts, asks about food at the worst moment; takes everything literally; whines her sisters' names; goes purring when cruel.

## 4. Abilities, skills & limits
- **Siren song (trio):** hypnotic harmony that bends a crowd's opinion and amplifies friction already present, from a café to a whole school (*Rainbow Rocks*).
- **Feeding:** the trio draw power from the disharmony their songs provoke; Sonata is a load-bearing third of the harmony, not a soloist.
- **Pendant magic:** the gems focus, sustain and project the spell and the astral siren forms; destroy them and everything collapses.
- **Performance:** backup vocals, dance, stage presence; the Dazzlings are genuinely good, which is why the Battle of the Bands works.
- **Limits:** no combat feats, no physical aggression; thin human-world magic needing an audience and a grudge; the voice dies with the gems; no way home, and no plan that survives contact with lunch.

## 5. Canon storyline (chronological)
- **Ancient Equestria:** the sirens spread discord with hypnotic song and feed on it; the **Pillars** defeat them and **Star Swirl** banishes them to the human world before they can conquer Equestria (S7E25–E26; *Rainbow Rocks*).
- **A thousand years stranded:** weakened and increasingly at each other's throats; the Dazzlings arrive already squabbling.
- ***Rainbow Rocks*, café:** first appearance; their song turns patrons on each other while Sonata bickers with Aria.
- ***Rainbow Rocks*, CHS:** they enrol and steer the Musical Showcase into a **Battle of the Bands** so the school fights itself for them.
- **Bracket:** they advance by song; Sonata's lunch distraction and her candour with Sunset are the comic leaks in the scheme.
- **Finale ("Welcome to the Show"):** the Rainbooms' friendship magic **shatters the pendants**; the sirens croak, the spell dies, the trio is chased off in disgrace.
- ***Friendship Games*:** absent; a siren-form drawing in the CHS Rally Song and in Sci-Twi's photos.
- ***Better Together*, "Find the Magic":** they return as performers at the **Starswirled Music Festival**, singing about life without magic.
- ***Sunset's Backstage Pass*:** festival performers and time-loop red herrings; Sunset misreads their looped song, searches the van, reads Adagio's mind and clears them; Sonata tells the toaster-taco story, and they harass Sunset before driving off.
- **Secondary:** IDW *FIENDship is Magic* #3 and *Legends of Magic* add the attack on Stygian's village; storybooks *The Mane Event*, *Starswirl Do-Over*; tertiary mobile game and CCG.

## 6. Relationships
- **Adagio Dazzle:** sister and bandleader; adoration, dread of her annoyance, endless following. Adagio calls her and Aria "idiots"; Sonata misses the sarcasm.
- **Aria Blaze:** sister and sparring partner; constant, trivial mutual bickering, the closest thing either has to conversation.
- **Sunset Shimmer:** favourite target; Sonata is candid with her about their nature, then needles her with her sisters: casual cruelty, not hatred.
- **The Rainbooms / Twilight:** the enemies who beat them; Twilight names the sirens, friendship magic ends the spell.
- **Pinkie Pie:** the one kindred spirit in the film, the only other person enjoying Snips and Snails' rap.
- **Trixie:** a pawn the trio flatter and use; Sonata is as mean as her sisters are. **Snips and Snails:** useful, eager, genuinely entertaining.
- **Star Swirl and the Pillars:** the reason she is stuck in the mirror world; she knows the name, not the history.

- **Siren form:** never physically shown, only projections, drawings and storybook art; fin and scale shades are infobox design data, not plot fact.

- **Fanon to avoid:** secret genius, sea monster or pony Sonata; any romance pairing; "misunderstood Dazzlings"; tacos as her whole personality.
