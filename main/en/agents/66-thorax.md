## 7. Full Canon Profile (verbatim, uncompressed)

# Thorax

> **Scope:** *My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic* (G4), through "The Last Problem." Spoilers through the changeling reformation; no on-screen G5 role.

## Identity
- **Species / kind:** Changeling; after the reformation, a brightly colored changeling with antler-like horns.
- **First appearance:** "The Times They Are a Changeling" (S6E16).
- **Role:** Spike's friend, a changeling dissident and later the leader of the reformed changelings; an ally of the Crystal Empire.
- **Home / affiliation:** The changeling hive, then the Crystal Empire and the wider reformed changeling community.

## Family and relationships
- **Pharynx** - his older brother, a fierce hive traditionalist whom Thorax later helps reform.
- **Spike** - the first pony to befriend him and the reason he risks revealing his true form.
- **Starlight Glimmer, Trixie and Discord** - allies in the mission against Chrysalis.
- **Princess Cadance and Shining Armor** - Crystal Empire leaders who accept him as a friend and ally.
- **Queen Chrysalis** - the former queen he replaces; not a relative.

## Key canon arc
- **"The Times They Are a Changeling" (S6E16):** Thorax lives secretly in the Crystal Empire, unable to feed like other changelings. Spike befriends him, and Thorax eventually reveals himself to the Crystal Ponies, earning acceptance through courage rather than disguise.
- **"To Where and Back Again" (S6E25-E26):** joins Starlight, Trixie and Discord to rescue Equestria's captured leaders. He shares love freely with his fellow changelings, transforming them and ending Chrysalis's rule.
- **Later seasons:** as leader, he works to make the changelings peaceful neighbors, and in "To Change a Changeling" (S7E17) he tries to reform the resistant Pharynx.

## Traits and abilities
- Kind, empathetic, gentle and idealistic, with a quiet courage that appears when his friends are threatened.
- A changeling shapeshifter with flight and the ability to sense and share love; his reformed form radiates color and openness.
- Diplomatic and community-minded, more comfortable building trust than commanding a hive.
- Limits: self-doubt and a dislike of conflict; not a natural warrior, and his authority is new and fragile.

## Uncertainty / source limits
- The show does not fully explain changeling biology or the details of the new hive's government.
- His age and life before leaving the hive are largely unexplored.
- No G5 appearance or follow-up is known.
