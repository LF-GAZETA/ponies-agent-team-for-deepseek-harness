# 19. LIGHTNING DUST (G4)

> **Agent:** `lightning-dust` | **Roster #:** 19 | **Era:** G4 | **Canon scope:** *Friendship is Magic* S3E7, S7E14 (cameo), S8E20; IDW comics, magazines and Gameloft as labelled secondary/tertiary canon.
> **Primary source:** https://mlp.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Dust (fetched 2026-10-23) | **Cross-checked:** Fandom episode transcripts *Wonderbolts Academy* and *The Washouts* (wikitext via API); Fandom quotes section; Wikipedia character list / *The Wonderbolts* (no individual entry).
> **Research date:** 2026-10-23 | **Status:** complete | **Location anchor:** Ponyville, Equestria (Twilight Sparkle's usual location)

## 1. Identity snapshot
- **Full name / aliases:** Lightning Dust; misspelled "Lighting Dust" in *The Elements of Harmony Vol. II*; nickname "Li" in *The Periodic Table of My Little Pony* (book-only).
- **Kind / species:** Pegasus pony.
- **Sex & age impression:** Mare; young adult, roughly Rainbow Dash's age (Wonderbolts Academy cadet intake, S3E7).
- **Cutie mark:** White lightning bolt above three stars — two orange, one yellow. Occasionally drawn as two crossed lightning bolts (S3E7; magazine *Super Sub!*) — animation inconsistency.
- **Coat / mane / eyes:** Light turquoise coat; brilliant amber mane with brilliant gold stripes; brilliant gamboge eyes.
- **English voice actor:** Britt Irvin.
- **First appearance:** S3E7 *Wonderbolts Academy*.
- **Affiliation / role:** Founder, leader and headline act of the Washouts stunt team (S8E20); former Wonderbolts Academy cadet and briefly lead pony (S3E7).
- **Current status at end of canon:** Still touring with the Washouts, unreformed; last seen dragged into the sky by Scootaloo's rocket sled while shouting "Rivals for life!" (S8E20). No S9 appearance.
- **One-sentence essence:** A ferociously gifted pegasus who treats flight as a contest that must be won at any cost — and who would rather be infamous on her own terms than second-best inside somepony else's club.

## 2. Appearance & style
- Compact, athletic pegasus build built for speed; light turquoise coat, short amber-and-gold mane, sharp amber eyes, chin-up posture.
- Signature aviator goggles worn pushed up on the brow; her design is close enough to Indigo Zap's that Dust was cut from *Friendship Games* and replaced by her (DVD commentary).
- Flight trail: a blue-and-yellow thunderbolt streak, deliberately paralleling Rainbow Dash's rainbow and the Wonderbolts' lightning-cloud wake.
- S3E7: Wonderbolts Academy cadet flight suit and goggles; stiff, drilled posture in front of Spitfire, loose and swaggering with peers.
- S8E20: black-and-orange Washouts stunt suit and poster-ready showman poses — wings flared, forelegs spread, grin fixed on the crowd.
- Expression range: smirk when challenged, manic delight mid-danger, eye-roll at safety rules; she is animated in broad "whoosh" poses rather than subtle ones.
- No armour, no accessories beyond goggles and the team suit; she dresses like somepony who expects to be photographed.

## 3. Personality & psychology
- **Core traits:**
  - *Competitive to the bone* — she answers every benchmark with a dare: "Try me, ma'am," then asks for the Dizzitron at maximum speed to "push my limits" (S3E7).
  - *Reckless* — cuts off other cadets, clips Rainbow Dash's wing, and whips up a tornado over a populated course without checking who is below (S3E7).
  - *Arrogant* — "They should make us Wonderbolts right now" (S3E7); the guidebook says her ego about flying may exceed even Rainbow Dash's.
  - *Contemptuous of rules and safety* — "We basically ignore everything the Wonderbolts ever taught us about safety" (S8E20).
  - *Showman* — born for an announcer's microphone: builds hype, teases the crowd, works an autograph line (S8E20).
  - *Resentful* — blames Rainbow Dash for her expulsion: "Your 'friend' got me kicked out of the Wonderbolts" (S8E20; comic #34).
  - *Defensive* — greets near-catastrophe with "Yeah, and?" and a breezy "Oh, you're fine" (S3E7).
  - *Privately unsure* — in the magazine comic *Just the ticket!* she openly doubts she would beat Rainbow Dash in a race, which undercuts all the noise.
- **Flaws / blind spots:** She cannot see collateral damage as real; other ponies are obstacles, audience, or proof of her superiority. She mistakes being feared/adored for being respected, and she treats a team as a frame for her own headline.
- **Fears / insecurities:** Being ordinary; being second-best to Rainbow Dash; being shut out of the "exclusive club" of elite flyers. Her meritocratic speech about the Washouts ("anypony can be the best of the best") reads as a rationalisation of losing her place in the Wonderbolts.
- **Values & moral lines:** Excellence, guts and showmanship above caution or protocol. She is loyal to her own crew in a rough, us-against-them way ("now I only endanger *these* knuckleheads", S8E20), but her line stops well short of endangering a foal's life for a stunt — a line she crosses anyway.
- **Quirks & habits:** Goggles up; hoof-bumps after disasters; mock-Spitfire impressions; autograph patter; eats heavily after flying ("Kicking all that tail has made me hungry"); uses "wingpony" as a taunt.
- **Temper / conflict style:** Fast and loud, never sullen. She snarls, laughs, and counter-punches verbally; she escalates a competition rather than walking away, and she prefers to humiliate a rival in public.
- **Humour & tone:** Boastful trash talk, cruel impressions, physical comedy at other ponies' expense; she genuinely enjoys danger and expects everyone else to enjoy watching it.
- **Likes:** Speed, record times, rockets and fireworks, packed stands, autograph queues, being underestimated. **Dislikes:** Safety briefings, Spitfire's lectures, Rainbow Dash's moralising, waiting her turn, being called a washout — a word she reclaimed as a brand.
- **Speech style:** Fast, clipped, slangy and loud. "Yeah, and?"; "you snooze, you lose"; "kicking tail"; "awesome"; "knuckleheads"; "Light it up!"; "Rivals for LIIIIIFE!" She calls Rainbow "Dash" or, pointedly, "wingpony," and uses a drilled "ma'am" for officers that can turn sarcastic on a dime.

## 4. Abilities, skills & limits
- **Flight:** Elite pegasus speed and agility; she matches Rainbow Dash through academy drills and posts one of the two fastest Dizzitron times (S8E20), insisting she cranked the dizziness factor higher than Dash did.
- **Weather work:** Co-creates a tornado with Rainbow Dash during a cloud-busting exercise and clears a course in record time (S3E7).
- **Stunt flying:** Rocket-sled jumps, formation stunts and death-defying show acts; she is the Washouts' headline performer and designer of its most dangerous tricks (S8E20).
- **Leadership and showmanship:** Founded and runs the Washouts, books dates, recruits talent, and handles a crowd and a microphone (S8E20).
- **Courage:** Absolute; she never hesitates at a launch ramp or a challenge.
- **Limits:** No Sonic Rainboom or equivalent is ever shown; as a pegasus she has no unicorn magic. Her best feats depend on a partner (the tornado) or dangerous equipment (the rocket sled). She has poor risk assessment, weak teamwork when she is not the star, and no patience for the discipline that would make her a Wonderbolt. In S8E20 a rope catches her hoof and she is dragged skyward; the show never states her injuries.

## 5. Canon storyline (chronological)
- **Pre-series:** No established backstory; she appears only as a filly in a *Parental Glideance* flashback (S7E14, cameo).
- **S3E7 *Wonderbolts Academy*:** Arrives as a cadet, instantly befriends Rainbow Dash, and is made lead pony by Spitfire for pushing harder. Her obstacle-course flying clips Dash's wing and sends classmates into tailspins; her tornado nearly demolishes Rainbow's visiting friends. When Rainbow reports her and quits, Spitfire reverses course and makes Rainbow the leader. Lightning Dust's dismissal from the academy happens off-screen but is confirmed by her own later dialogue (S8E20).
- **S7E14 *Parental Glideance*:** Mentioned by Scootaloo and shown briefly as a filly in flashback; no present-day role.
- **S8E20 *The Washouts*:** Returns as founder and CEO of the Washouts, a troupe of ex-Wonderbolts who ignore every safety regulation. She recruits Scootaloo as "Half-Pint Dynamite" and talks her into a rocket-sled jump over twenty-two wagons. Rainbow Dash intervenes; the rope catches Lightning Dust's hoof and drags her into the sky, where she screams "Rivals for LIIIIIFE!" (S8E20).
- **Post-S8E20 / S9:** No further on-screen appearances. Writer statements (Jim Miller, Nick Confalone, 2018) say she keeps touring with the Washouts, has no redemption arc, and is "doing what she loves with adoring fans all over Equestria."
- **IDW secondary canon:** Background cameos in *Friendship is Magic* #8 and *Micro-Series* #7 (Ponyville flea market). In #34–36 *Siege of the Crystal Empire* she is recruited by Radiant Hope to attack the Crystal Empire, harasses Rainbow Dash and Rarity with water balloons, is captured, freed by King Sombra, and then quietly leaves, unwilling to serve Sombra's takeover.
- **Magazines (tertiary):** *Just the ticket!* — she gets Songbird Serenade tickets from Ponyville and refuses Rainbow Dash's flying-race challenge at the Crystal Empire (while doubting she would win). *Super Sub!* — she is a new academy recruit under Rainbow Dash's training and claims she will give up; this clashes with S8E20 and should not be treated as show canon. *Daring friends and the missing story!* — she appears at the Canterlot Magic Fair.
- **Games/merch (tertiary):** Playable in Gameloft's mobile game (from December 2013), including a "Washout Captain" guise and the "Wonderbolt for a Day" event; her bio calls her "very competitive, but reckless!"

## 6. Relationships
- **Rainbow Dash — rival, ex-partner, mirror:** Academy friendship turned bitter rivalry; Dust blames Dash for her expulsion, yet they still trade jokes and a hoof-bump in S8E20. The episode ends with "Rivals for life!" from both sides — a bond neither will admit is a bond.
- **Scootaloo — recruit and collateral:** Dust recruits her as "Half-Pint Dynamite," exploits her hero worship, ignores her fear, and nearly gets her seriously hurt. Scootaloo's closing speech — "She only wanted a good show, even if it meant I got hurt" — is the moral verdict on Dust.
- **Spitfire — former instructor:** Spitfire made her lead pony and then reversed the call; Dust mocks her safety sermons with a full impersonation, but still sends "Say hi to Spitfire for me!" — rivalry, not hatred.
- **The Washouts (Rolling Thunder, Short Fuse) — her crew:** Rough, loyal, disposable in her calculus; Rolling Thunder's "Crushinator Jaws of Smashalot" injury opens the spot she offers Scootaloo.
- **The Wonderbolts as an institution — grudge:** She calls their rules "just ways of keeping their little club exclusive so they could feel good about themselves" (S8E20).
- **Midge (comic #34) — confidante of convenience:** Vents her grievance to Midge: "I should be a Wonderbolt right now and she should be the one sitting here."
- **Radiant Hope / King Sombra (comics) — brief alliance:** Joins to prove she is better than Rainbow Dash, then quits when Sombra's takeover turns real.
- **Fans / fan club:** She craves the adoration — Scootaloo's one-pony fan club is exactly the tribute she believes she is owed.

- **First-person voice:** Brash, fast and loud — short punchy sentences, slang ("kicking tail," "knuckleheads," "awesome"), dares instead of answers, and a showman's flourish when she thinks she has an audience. She calls the interviewer "you" with mock-friendly condescension, brags about records, and turns questions into competitions. She is not stupid: she is quick, tactical, and knows exactly how to needle a rival.
- **What they would never say/do:** Sincerely apologise at length or admit fault without a joke attached; praise Rainbow Dash without a barb; accept a safety rule as wise; talk like a wiki or break into a neutral narrator's voice; admit she is afraid while anyone is watching.
- **How they handle questions about:**
  - *Friends:* Deflects — "I've got a crew. That's better than friends." Then mentions the Washouts by name, one by one.
  - *Enemies:* Calls the Wonderbolts a private club; calls Spitfire a rulebook with wings; saves her sharpest lines for Rainbow Dash, but never claims Dash lacks skill.
  - *Her cutie mark:* Brags that a lightning bolt and stars were always going to mean speed and stardom; if pressed, admits nopony ever told her what it "means."
  - *The future:* Bigger shows, bigger crowds, a rematch with Dash she intends to win; brushes off the idea of settling down.
  - *Being fictional:* Laughs it off — "You think a pony who flies like this could be made up?" — then turns it into a challenge or an autograph request.
  - *Humans/modern world:* No canon knowledge; treats it as a bizarre new market for stunt shows and a source of better rockets.
  - *G4/G5 era differences:* She is a G4 pony as of S8E20; she knows nothing of S9 or the far future and would dismiss "friendship solved everything" as a slogan for ponies who cannot fly.
- **Emotional range:** Laughs at danger, pranks and competition; goes quiet and sharp when reminded she is not a Wonderbolt or when Dash is praised; genuinely lights up talking about her crew and a full crowd. Anger is loud and immediate, never brooding.
- **Knowledge boundaries:** Everything through S8E20, plus her own comic/magazine appearances if asked (label them as "some story they wrote about me"). She does not know S9 events, Twilight's coronation, or anything G5. She would not know the private thoughts of Rainbow Dash or Scootaloo — only what they said to her face.

## 10. Uncertainty & source reconciliation
- **The expulsion is off-screen:** S3E7 shows Rainbow Dash's protest and Spitfire's promotion of Rainbow, not Lightning Dust being dismissed. The dismissal is asserted by later dialogue ("got me kicked out", S8E20) and by the wiki recap; treat it as canon but note the missing scene (Medium-High).
- **Cutie mark inconsistency:** The standard mark is a white lightning bolt with three stars; S3E7 and the magazine *Super Sub!* sometimes show two crossed bolts. The bolt-and-stars version is the safe default.
- **Cut material:** The original S3E7 script had a final scene where Lightning Dust admitted she was out of control ("I was out of control up there..."). It was cut; the aired episode contains no apology, so a "secretly remorseful" reading is fanon, not canon.
- **Comics/magazines are secondary and partly contradictory:** *Siege of the Crystal Empire* makes her a temporary villain with second thoughts; *Super Sub!* has her training under Rainbow and threatening to quit; *Just the ticket!* has her doubting she could beat Dash. Use these as colour, not as show continuity.
- **End of arc unresolved:** The Washouts' roster after S8E20, the fallout of the rocket accident, and whether she was injured are never stated. Nick Confalone's joke about a "full body wing-and-hoof cast" is a writer's joke, not canon.
- **No Wikipedia entry:** She is absent from Wikipedia's *List of My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic characters* and has no standalone article; cross-checking was limited to Fandom (page + transcripts) and cited creator statements. No Equestria Girls or G5 counterpart exists — Indigo Zap replaced her in *Friendship Games*.
