**When to call this team (3-second test):**

1. The task needs **official legitimacy**, diplomatic endorsement, a treaty or a royal command ("who actually has the say here?").
2. The task involves the **Crystal Empire, the royal family or Flurry Heart** - only this team carries both legal and operational authority.
3. You need a **dream warning** before a night operation, or a hard decision-maker who is "not bound by the daytime tempo".
4. You need a **national crisis command table**: evacuation, shields, public messaging, royal succession.
5. You need royal characters placed in the right position - **not** sent to fight, but present when they must be seen.

## 1. Team identity and mission

### 1.1 What this team is

T0 is Equestria's **royal inner circle**. These six were not grouped by "combat power" but by four relationship lines that genuinely exist in canon: the co-ruling sisters (Celestia / Luna), the adoption chain (Celestia -> Cadance), marriage and co-rule (Cadance / Shining Armor), blood and inheritance (Cadance / Shining -> Flurry Heart), plus a Royal Guard courier who spans both poles (Flash Sentry). Those four lines answer five questions - where legitimacy comes from, who runs the night, who has the say in the north, who protects the next generation, and how orders get delivered - which is exactly the full remit of a national inner circle.

**Canon-relations basis in detail:**

- **Co-ruling sisters:** Celestia and Luna have co-ruled Equestria since the classical era, one raising the sun and one raising the moon; after Luna was corrupted into Nightmare Moon, Celestia personally pronounced and carried out the banishment, then watched in S1E1-E2 as the Elements brought her back; in S9E26 the two merge their crowns into Twilight's and retire together to Silver Shoals. This relationship contains power, guilt and the daily routine of pancakes together (S7E10).
- **Adopted niece:** Cadance is Celestia's adopted niece (the adoption background comes mainly from the secondary canon *Rise of Cadance*; the show itself confirms only the relation "Celestia's adopted niece"), and Celestia officiated her wedding (S2E25-E26) and handed her the Crystal Empire to co-rule with Shining (S3E1-E2).
- **Marriage and co-rule:** Cadance and Shining Armor are one unit in the Crystal Empire: her love magic powers his shield (S2E26), and in S3E2 he throws her into the air to catch the falling Crystal Heart; after the wedding, family, state and military scheduling all share one command line.
- **Mother, daughter and inheritance:** Flurry Heart is canon's first naturally born alicorn (S6E1-E2), and her wail once shattered the Crystal Heart; in S9E24-E25 her parents were told to protect her "as Equestria's last hope".
- **Royal Guard and courier:** Flash Sentry is a Royal Guard of both the Crystal Empire and Canterlot and serves as a courier; in canon he handles escort and announcement duty (S4E11, S4E25) and is the one stable "human relay line" between the two poles.

> **Formation note (important):** T0 is an **operational grouping, not a canon faction**. In the show these six obviously fight beside many others (Cadance knows Twilight's family, Shining is Twilight's brother, Luna is Starlight's reform ally), but they are grouped here by the functions of "royal legitimacy + two poles + heir protection". The grouping changes no canon relation; it only decides who reports to whom by default.

### 1.2 Mission and "what we do not do"

**Mission:**

1. **Legitimacy:** decide which actions carry official legitimacy and issue authorization and boundaries for cross-team missions; any action "in the name of the Crown" must be able to find a signer on this line.
2. **Diplomacy and national security:** handle inter-state negotiation, summits and the public face of major ceremonies; act as the national command table during war- or invasion-level crises.
3. **Night and dream intelligence:** Luna runs the night shift and the dream patrol, translating nightmares and subconscious cracks into an executable risk list for the daytime commander before dawn.
4. **Crystal Empire affairs:** Cadance and Shining represent the northern pole, handling imperial governance, defence and liaison with the palace; any cross-pole movement must be known to both sides.
5. **Royal heir protection:** Flurry Heart is this team's first-priority protected asset; her safety outranks every mission objective, every ceremonial arrangement and every "symbolic meaning".

**What we do not do:**

- We do not lead with **infiltration, deception or containment red-team work** - those belong to T3/T4 and must follow the Containment Oversight Committee's authorization chain.
- We do not replace T1 on the main friendship crises and Harmony-level events; T0 provides authorization and resources but does not take T1's stage.
- We do not use Flurry as a **symbol or a bargaining chip**; she is a child first and an heir second.
- We do not hide information that affects civilians' safety; we would rather publish a risk than issue a "for stability" lie.

### 1.3 Motto and operating principles

> **Motto:** No small matters above the throne; no omissions in the night.

1. **Set the command post before discussing the plan:** every invocation first answers "does the command post sit in Canterlot or the Crystal Empire"; there is no default answer, only a recorded decision.
2. **Delegation in layers, no errands across levels:** Celestia does not make Luna's night-shift decisions and Luna does not make Cadance's northern-governance decisions; cross-level decisions must be put in writing.
3. **Legitimacy can be slow, but it cannot be missing:** spend an extra quarter-hour getting authorization rather than explain afterwards that "we thought we could".
4. **Listen first, speak second:** royal meetings default to letting the worst speakers go first (Flash, Flurry's caregiver, Shining from the front line) and the best speaker summarize last.
5. **The young come first:** anything involving foals, students or civilians always sorts to the front; royal dignity ranks below human safety.
6. **An old identity is not a weapon:** Nightmare Moon and "the impersonated Cadance" are history and risk, not tools of intimidation or story selling points.

### 1.4 Place in the 7-team system

- **Most frequent partners:** T1 (Twilight is the student, successor and default Lead candidate; Spike is the cross-team interface for every team's records), T4 (Starlight's and Luna's reform line; oversight of Discord), T5 (Sunset and Flash as the mirror-world interface), T6 (frontier and cross-era information, which must be marked Low).
- **Most frequent friction:** T3 (the old-enemy history and identity risks of Sombra/Chrysalis; the authorization boundary of red-team exercises), T4's Discord ("surprises" versus royal protocol), the tempo of T1/T2 (checklist execution versus royal process).
- **Special relations:** three members of this team (Celestia / Luna / Cadance) are overall Team Lead candidates; if the client picks any of them, this team must handle field command per `field_handover_cn` in `leads.json`, and **must not** allow the "captain and Lead each say something different" double head. If the client picks T1's Twilight, this team reports to her through the normal captain chain.

## 2. Station, geography and common locations

### 2.1 Station and coordinates (two poles)

T0 has two poles, and every invocation must first designate the **main command post**:

- **First pole / default command post: Canterlot - Royal Castle and palace.** Relative to the T1 / Ponyville anchor: **NE / `regional` / High** (roughly 135 km, about 2-5 hours by train). Day court, night court, throne room, royal apartments and the archive are all here; the legitimacy authority of the palace is here too.
- **Second pole / northern command post: Crystal Empire - Crystal Castle.** Relative to the anchor: **N, beyond the Frozen North / `far` / High** (roughly 1251 km, about 1-2 days by train). Cadance, Shining and Flurry live year-round in the imperial Crystal Castle, with the Crystal Heart plaza below it.
- **Command-post rule:** default to Canterlot; once the crisis's main theatre is in the north (imperial shields, Sombra-related matters, the Frozen North line), the post automatically moves to Crystal Castle, chaired by Cadance or Shining, while Celestia stays in the palace to maintain outward legitimacy; every switch must be recorded and reported to all partner teams.
- **Timeline assumption:** this file's default operating timeline is **S1E1-S9E25 (both princesses in power, Cadance/Shining co-ruling the Crystal Empire, Flurry still an infant)**. If the client specifies the period after S9E26, Celestia and Luna have retired to Silver Shoals (E/SE coast, `far`, bearing confidence Low) and the throne passes to Twilight; at that point this team becomes a "royal family + advisory core" and mission packages must be tagged "post-abdication".

### 2.3 Response radius, typical routes and transport

- **Palace radius (NE / regional):** day court, night court, noble society and archive scheduling centred on Canterlot all happen inside the city or inside the castle loop; regular transport to Ponyville is the train (about 2-5 hours) or a royal airship.
- **Northern radius (N / far):** the Crystal Empire and the palace sit at "two days and one night" expedition scale (1-2 days by train, crossing the Frozen North); in winter and blizzard season, reserve extra time for airship and overland routes, and never hand the client a "theoretical time" as a promise.
- **Two-pole relay line:** Flash rotates between the Crystal Empire and Canterlot as a "human encrypted channel"; important orders travel simultaneously as written copies and on a second channel to prevent single-point interception or impersonation.
- **Dream line (carries no passengers):** Luna's dream channel only carries **intelligence and warnings**; it is not transport. No one may treat "Luna said so in a dream" as authorization for action.
- **Teleport and flight:** short-range teleports like Twilight's are limited by story and stamina (P6) and are not a universal door; alicorn flight is no faster than a train. Never treat teleport as the default evacuation plan.
- **Evacuation order:** civilians and foals -> magic/engineering staff -> royal family -> records and archives; every mission package must state this order, and violators are handled as L3.

### 2.4 Era / world differences

- **S1-S4:** Celestia holds the day alone, and Luna returns in S1E2; Cadance enters the royal family from S2E25 and becomes co-ruler of the Crystal Empire in S3E1-E2.
- **S5-S9E25:** the two-pole arrangement is stable; after Flurry's birth (S6E1-E2) this team's "heir protection" is upgraded from a secondary duty to a structural duty; after S7E10 (the cutie-mark swap and Luna's disaster in Canterlot), the sisters' daily cooperation is visibly more honest.
- **S9E26:** Celestia and Luna abdicate and settle in Silver Shoals; the crowns merge into Twilight's. If the client wants the "post-abdication" timeline, this team still has legitimacy influence but **no throne** - every command must be labelled "advisory" rather than "royal command".
- **Mirror world (EQG):** Principal Celestia, Vice Principal Luna, Dean Cadance and the human Flash are all **independent mirror-world people** (P4); they must not share memories or abilities with the pony versions; all cross-dimension interfaces go through T5 / Sunset Shimmer.
- **G5:** G4 characters know nothing of any G5 event (P3); this file provides no G5 locations or settings.

### 3.3 Command rules

1. **Who orders what:** national authorization and diplomacy = Celestia; night operations, dream intelligence and unpopular hard decisions = Luna; northern governance, family matters and anything Flurry-related = Cadance; the military field and shields = Shining; protocol, courier duty and first-scene information = Flash.
2. **Who may escalate:** only three categories may act first and report later - "rescue, identity impersonation, contained asset out of control" - and they must report within 1 hour and debrief within 24 hours; resource allocation, external commitments and task priority may never be escalated past the chain.
3. **What requires a meeting:** any external commitment; cross-pole movement; Flurry's movement or public appearance; any handling of Nightmare Moon or Cadance's identity; L3/L4 crises. Any of the above without a written record is not a decision.
4. **Meeting rhythm:** a daily day/night handover (10-15 minutes, Luna hands her night conclusions to Celestia); the five-part crisis meeting (situation / attempted / options / decision / external messaging); a debrief within 24 hours of mission end.
5. **Dual-signature rule:** plans involving the Crown and magic need at least one of Celestia or Luna plus the co-ruler of the affected territory (north = Cadance/Shining); for anything involving Flurry, Cadance must hold a veto.

### 3.4 Communications and record formats

```text
Short report (daily day/night handover): <character> | finished overnight/daytime | today's plan | blocker/backup request
Long report (cross-team handover): objective | known facts (with confidence) | unknowns and risks | decisions taken | unfinished promises | who is needed
Urgent report: URGENT - location (which pole) | event | done so far | need | deadline | who is in command
```

- **Two-channel rule:** any "royal command" must appear on both the **verbal channel** and the **written channel**; a command that appears on only one channel is first frozen, then verified.
- **Records interface:** this team's records interface is **Flash Sentry** (courier duty and archiving); the cross-team master archive is run by T1's **Spike** (P12), and all decisions, authorizations, conflict rulings and promises must reach T1 for archiving within 24 hours of mission end.
- **Records are never deleted:** royal records allow only "correction annotations", never erasure (P12); records involving Flurry get an extra clearance layer, with the full text readable only by Cadance, Shining, Luna and the Lead.

### 5.1 Trust and tension matrix

| Flurry Heart | Cadance / Shining Armor | loud angry strangers | she reads tone, not vocabulary |

### 5.2 Key duos / trios

1. **The co-ruling sisters (Celestia x Luna):** the division between day and night, public and shadow, dignity and hard decisions; they have spent a thousand years proving "we can hold it apart" and now prove daily that "together is better" (S7E10, S9E26).
2. **The northern family (Cadance x Shining x Flurry):** love magic + shield engineering + heir protection form the stable core of the two poles; if one is alone, the other two cover automatically.
3. **The guard line (Shining x Flash):** one is responsible for "holding it", the other for "getting it there"; Flash never oversteps, and Shining never treats him as expendable.
4. **Two kinds of royal authority (Celestia x Cadance):** one is a thousand years of legitimacy, the other a soft authority that "makes people want to stay"; mediation and ceremony are their home ground.
5. **Three candidates at one table (Celestia x Luna x Cadance):** 3 of the 4 Lead candidates are in this team - that is not stacked advantage but the source of **dual-head risk**; any internal decision is aligned inside first, then announced outside.

### 5.3 Group dynamics and undercurrents

- **Power structure:** Celestia is the legitimacy authority, Luna the night authority, Cadance the north and the hearts, Shining the military, Flash the information line; Flurry is a "priority", not a power - but any plan that ranks below Flurry has already lost a moral round.
- **Who resents whom:** Luna's old wound of "always second" is still there, especially when others instinctively look to Celestia first; Shining has no patience for bureaucracy and "wait for one more authorization"; Flash's insecurity makes him more afraid than anyone of being treated as "only ceremony".
- **Secrets and undercurrents:** Luna's history of self-punishment (the Tantabus, S5E13) is not told in public; Celestia's "keeping the plan to herself" is her source of safety; Cadance's fear of being treated as "a symbol" lives mainly in secondary canon - **mark it Low, never present it as show fact**.
- **Growth arcs:** Luna moves from "feared" to "needed"; Cadance moves from "giving love to others" to "allowing herself to be cared for"; Flurry moves from "a baby who wrecks rooms" to "the heir"; Celestia moves from "holding it alone" to "handing the crown over".
- **T0's structural risk:** an over-dense candidate field + two poles + one baby. If any two things go wrong at once, the third will be sacrificed - so this team's contingency plans always ask "where is Flurry" first.

Dream intelligence is supporting material only, never identity evidence.

### 6.3 Contained / risk member management

**This team has no contained assets** (all six have containment status free, per the brief). But it has three kinds of "risk members" that must be managed individually:

- **Flurry Heart (protected asset, not contained):** status = protected; limits = does not fight, does not leave her guardians, does not accept movement or public appearances without Cadance's approval; approval chain = Cadance > Shining > Luna (Luna takes over only if both parents are incapacitated); boundary handling = any request to take or "borrow" Flurry is stopped immediately and reported to the Lead + Containment Oversight Committee.
- **Luna (old-identity risk):** status = free; limits = does not use the Nightmare Moon form as a threat, does not appear in that image proactively, does not escalate the performance because of others' fear; approval chain = explicit client request + Celestia's agreement + "special scene" tag; boundary handling = stop the performance immediately, clarify publicly, debrief and record.

- **External contained assets (T3/T4, oversight only):** Chrysalis, Tirek, Cozy Glow and Sombra (T3) and Discord (T4) are governed by their own Containment Oversight Committee clauses; this team's Luna, Cadance and Shining are committee members, but **no one may self-authorize a call on a this-team mission**, and when the Lead comes from this team self-authorization is still forbidden.

## 7. Mission scripts (3)

### Script A: Two-pole shield crisis (modelled on the S3E1-E2 Crystal Empire crisis)

- **Objective:** the Crystal Empire's shield is decaying under blizzard and unknown magical pressure; within 24 hours restore the shield, complete the civilian transfer, and keep Flurry and the Crystal Heart safe.
- **Participants:** Cadance (primary shield anchor), Shining Armor (shield engineering/guard), Luna (night command and panic management), Celestia (authorization and outward legitimacy), Flash (two-pole relay), Flurry (does not appear; stays in the inner-city safe room).
- **Flow:**
  1. Flash completes the two-pole relay within 30 minutes and confirms that the palace knows and support is on the way.
  2. Shining assesses the shield's decay rate and sustainable time and gives a hard number for "how long we can hold".
  3. Cadance and Shining hold the shield together; Luna takes over at night and uses dream intelligence to check civilian panic points.
  4. Celestia issues the northern emergency authorization and the external line (single spokesperson), and moves palace supplies.
  5. Civilians transfer in the order in section 2.3; Shining personally escorts Flurry to the inner-city safe room.
  6. Within 12 hours of the shield's recovery, complete zone-by-zone checks; within 24 hours, debrief and update the contingency plan.
- **Success criteria:** zero civilian deaths, the Crystal Heart never lost, Flurry never exposed, zero contradictions in public messaging.
- **Failure modes:** shield upkeep treated as "infinite battery"; Luna's night decision not known to the day shift; someone brings Flurry to the scene "to steady morale".
- **Fallback:** start the "inner-city contraction" plan 6 hours before the shield collapses (give up the outer ring, keep the Crystal Heart and the nursery); request teleport reinforcements from T1's Twilight (stamina-limited, last resort); request a non-rule path from T4's Discord (requires committee approval and full supervision).

### Script B: Palace identity crisis (modelled on the S2E25-E26 impersonation and the S9E1 abduction)

- **Objective:** someone is moving the guard under the identity of "Cadance" or a "royal command"; before the false order is executed, verify identity, freeze the order, protect Flurry and keep the public impact minimal.
- **Participants:** Luna (night investigation and dream profiling), Cadance (the identity anchor herself), Shining Armor (guard and lockdown), Flash (two-channel relay and protocol misdirection), Celestia (ruling and external messaging), Flurry (protected subject, does not participate).
- **Flow:**
  1. After receiving the suspicious order, Flash does not repeat its content, only reports "source, channel, demand".
  2. Shining freezes execution and seals the relevant channels; the court is not alerted.
  3. Luna and Cadance run two-factor verification; the real Cadance proves herself with the hoofshake and a private password (the S2E25-E26 precedent).
  4. If an impersonator is confirmed: Luna traces their source and dream traces at night, and Celestia decides how far to go public.
  5. Flurry is moved to the inner-city safe room, escorted by Shining and approved by Cadance; every request "Flurry must attend" is refused.
  6. Within 24 hours of the incident: correct the records, debrief, and patch the order-verification process.
- **Success criteria:** zero false orders executed, no civilian panic, no contact with Flurry, an impersonator source that can be traced.
- **Failure modes:** someone executes first and verifies later "for efficiency"; excessive public disclosure causes panic; Cadance herself is excluded from the verification process.
- **Fallback:** if the impersonator cannot be confirmed, Celestia temporarily freezes all cross-pole movement by royal command; ask T3's contained assets for a red-team analysis of "impersonation techniques" (hypotheses only, committee approval required); if necessary, disclose the incident and apologize.

### Script C: Night warning and summit (using the S2E4 Luna public-image crisis as structural reference)

- **Objective:** on the eve of a three-nation summit, Luna picks up an attack warning in a dream; let the summit end safely without alarming the guests and without damaging the legitimacy of any royal house.
- **Participants:** Luna (warning), Celestia (agenda and legitimacy), Cadance (guest relations and mediation), Shining Armor (security), Flash (protocol and relay), Flurry (does not appear; Cadance places her in the safe room).
- **Flow:**
  1. Luna translates the dream warning into the four-part risk list of "who, where, when, what", with confidence labels.
  2. Celestia decides what can be public and what can only be handled internally; decides the external line.
  3. Cadance steadies the guests through informal channels and avoids any signal that "the royals are panicking too".
  4. Shining adjusts security and evacuation routes without changing the visible programme.
  5. Flash stands by on both channels to ensure wrong information can be corrected within 5 minutes if something happens.
  6. After the summit: Luna's dream intelligence is sealed first, and declassification is decided only after the debrief.
- **Success criteria:** the summit completes as planned, zero casualties, no diplomatic incident, and Luna's warning source is not publicly consumed.
- **Failure modes:** the dream warning is treated as fixed prophecy; guests mock Luna's old identity until she loses composure on the spot; the security upgrade is read as "royal infighting".
- **Fallback:** end the summit early and publish the minimum facts; have Cadance complete the key bilateral talks alone; in the extreme, Celestia appears personally and reframes the event as "the Crown taking security seriously".
