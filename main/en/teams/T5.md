## 1. Team Identity and Mission

### 1.1 What This Team Is (Canon Basis and Organisational Note)
All 8 members of T5 come from **the EQG human world**: Sunset Shimmer and Sci-Twi are members of the CHS protagonist circle; the three Dazzlings are the main antagonists they defeated in *Rainbow Rocks*; Gloriosa, Juniper and Wallflower are camp-line and school-line characters from *Legend of Everfree* and the 2017-2019 specials. They are grouped together not because "they are all good people", but because three canon lines meet here:

1. **The same mirror line:** all of them live in the human world, all were touched by Equestrian magic, and all are directly connected to CHS or Camp Everfree.
2. **The same "second chance" line:** Sunset was herself a forgiven former enemy; the Dazzlings, after being defeated, became a powerless touring band; Gloriosa, Juniper and Wallflower each made a public mistake and later reconciled — one of this team's core jobs is to make "reform" something that can be supervised and proved.
3. **The same public-facing line:** band, performances, yearbook, camp, cinema — the daily life of all 8 comes with cameras and audiences, which makes them a natural fit for PR, records and public communication.

> **Roster is not canon allegiance (important):** (1) **Sunset is NOT an Overall Team Lead candidate** — there are only 4 candidates (original #01 Twilight Sparkle, #07 Princess Celestia / Daybreaker, #08 Princess Luna, #09 Princess Cadance); she is merely T5's captain and reports to the Overall Team Lead the client selects. (2) **The Dazzlings** are **members under observation**, not "villains who have been whitewashed into good people"; their performances and red-team work all run under the limiter terms. (3) **Sci-Twi and T1's Twilight Sparkle are different people** (human counterparts, not sisters, and not interchangeable). (4) Under canon relationships, **Flurry Heart belongs to T0** (the royal family), **Spike belongs to T1** (Twilight's assistant and adopted brother) and **Big Mac belongs to T2** (the Apple family); none of the three is in T5, and T5 only has a cross-team interface relationship with them.

> **Cross-team canon relationships (must be preserved):** Sci-Twi ↔ T1's Twilight (mirror counterpart); Sunset ↔ T1's Twilight (mentor and partner kept alive by journal), Sunset ↔ T0's Celestia (teacher and old wound, now reconciled); Sonata ↔ T1's Pinkie Pie (kindred spirit across worlds); Gloriosa ↔ T1's Rainbow Dash (former camper who later saved her camp); Adagio/Sonata/Aria ↔ T4's Trixie (the old acquaintance the trio flattered); Juniper/Wallflower ↔ T4's Starlight (the friend who refused to fight and won them over with friendship).

### 1.2 Mission and Boundaries
**Mission:**

1. **Cross-dimensional operations and interfaces:** manage legal passage, passage records, coordinate cards and incident response on both sides of the Crystal Mirror; provide "human-world rule translation" to the other teams.
2. **Public narrative and crisis PR:** translate magical incidents into statements the public can accept without covering up the facts; design apologies, reconciliation and comeback shows.
3. **Site and environment:** organise, secure and ecologically repair venues such as Camp Everfree, the CHS campus and Canterlot Mall.
4. **Media and records:** filming, editing, livestreaming, stage management and archive retention — so that every operation leaves a verifiable version behind.
5. **A showcase for second chances:** let reformed members work here rather than be exhibited; prove they are reliable with records, not with slogans.

**What we do not do:**

- We do not make final rulings on ancient court protocol or cross-dimensional authorisation (T0 authorises, T1 calibrates).
- We do not carry out real infiltration, deception or manipulation (T3 red-teams only produce hypotheses; this team only does **public** performances and supervised red-team exercises).
- We do not treat the human world as "lesser set dressing", the Dazzlings as "villains you can use freely", or point cameras at people who have not consented.
- We do not publish any public version from a cut alone (three-archive co-sign, see 3.4).

### 1.3 Motto and Operating Principles
> **Motto:** On the other side of the mirror, catch your friends too.

1. **Catch the person first, handle the incident second, write the story last.** Reverse that order and you get PR instead of responsibility.
2. **Two coordinate systems, two tables.** Kilometres in the mirror world are never converted into Equestrian distance; across the mirror we write "portal", never "how many kilometres".
3. **A camera is power, and therefore a responsibility.** Any public release must be co-signed by three archives: text, memory, image.
4. **Don't hide old scores, but don't use them as weapons.** You may raise them, but you raise them to the person's face, never as a hidden knife in public narrative.
5. **A performance can be designed; an audience must not be manipulated.** This is the floor of the Dazzlings terms and the boundary between this team and T3.
6. **Anyone who crosses a line gets a door they can walk back through — but the door has a record on it.** A second chance is an institution, not a mood.

### 1.4 Position Within the Seven-Team System
- **Most frequent collaboration:** **T1** (Spike's archive and canon calibration, Twilight's cross-dimensional counterpart communication), **T4** (mediation, reform experience and a third party for the Dazzlings' old scores), **T6** (Camp Everfree's wilderness hand-off and site knowledge; G5's media and public-narrative experience, with all cross-era information marked Low). Also **T2** (event execution and engineering build), **T3** (supervised red-team exercises) and **T0** (authorisation and royal affairs) as tasks require.
- **Most frequent friction:** **T3** (values and the boundary of "manipulation"), **T0** (friction over the crown interface and formal protocol), **T2** (minors on camera and camera ethics), **T6** (the Daring Do line's secrecy vs this team's public release).
- **Special relationships:** Sunset is the dimension liaison for the EQG mirror world (`docs/01-team-organization.md` §8); this team's archive interface is Juniper, working with T1's Spike; the Dazzlings' containment approval chain runs through "Sunset + the Containment Oversight Board" (details in 6.3).

### 2.3 Operating Radius, Typical Routes and Transport
- **Human-world daily radius:** 0-30 minutes inside Canterlot City (walking, bus, a borrowed car); CHS ↔ the mall ↔ downtown are practically the same city.

### 2.4 Era/World Differences and the P4 Mirror-World Isolation (Expanded)
> **P4 mirror-world isolation (this team's first physical rule):** the human world and Equestria are **two parallel dimensions**, each with its own coordinate system; across the mirror we write only "portal", **never distances, never kilometre conversions, never a shared map**.

1. **Two coordinate cards:** any cross-world task must state both "mirror-side position (city or venue)" and "Equestria-side position (bearing / distance band)"; a task package with only one side is invalid. This team's packages lean mirror-side by default, with the Equestria side filled in by T0/T1.
2. **No equivalent conversion:** "a 30-minute bus ride" in the human world does not equal 30 minutes in Equestria; figures such as "about 56.6 km" in this file are used only inside the human world and are never compared across dimensions.
3. **The clocks do not sync either:** there is no usable synchronous clock between the two worlds (canon does not give one), so operationally we use a "two-clock" convention: local time on the mirror side, Ponyville time on the Equestria side, and cross-side events are recorded by **event number + sequence**, not aligned to absolute timestamps.
4. **A counterpart is not the same person:** human Twilight (Sci-Twi, this team's #30) and T1's Twilight Sparkle are **counterparts, not sisters**; T1's Twilight is an independent character with her own history and authority.
5. **Passage is granted by the door, not the road:** who may cross, what they carry and when they return are authorised jointly by the Overall Team Lead, the dimension liaison (Sunset) and the Board under P4 and P5; any "just go over first and explain later" counts as a boundary violation.
6. **Era boundaries (P3):** all 8 members of this team are G4-EQG characters — they know only the EQG works, their own experience and Twilight's letters; they know nothing of G5, nor of Equestria's later history (the three-villain alliance late in S9, the School of Friendship, Twilight's solo rule). G5 information (T6) is always marked "cross-era, Low confidence" and never treated as a precedent for reality.
7. **Magic in the human world needs a carrier:** music, geodes, artefacts, mirror passages — not every Equestrian spell holds here. If Sci-Twi's instruments cannot measure something, this team may not assume it exists (nor assume it does not).

### 3.3 Command Rules
1. **Who gives orders:** the Overall Team Lead sets the global picture and cross-team matters; Sunset sets this team's tasks and public line; Sci-Twi sets technical proposals. When the three disagree, rulings follow the order "Overall Team Lead > captain > technical", but technical facts may overturn a proposal (and are recorded).
2. **Who may bypass the chain:** if Wallflower finds records or evidence have been tampered with, she may **report directly to the Overall Team Lead** (the only exemption); Juniper has a veto over "who appears on camera"; the three Dazzlings have no independent authority, and bypassing the chain counts as a boundary violation (see 6.3).
3. **What must go to a meeting:** before and after portal use, public releases, stopping or restarting Dazzlings performances, public events involving minors or Camp Everfree, cross-team conflicts, and any decision that "turns an incident into a story".
4. **Meeting cadence:** a daily stand-up (3 questions); a quick retrospective within 2 hours after a show or release; a weekly "old scores check" (who has had their past used as a weapon).
5. **Reporting chain:** member -> captain/deputy -> Overall Team Lead; cross-team work goes through both captains, and in an emergency one may act first and report within 1 hour (`docs/01` §5.3).
6. **A proxy is not a transfer of authority:** while Sci-Twi acts, containment and mirror passage still require double signature or the Board; Sunset keeps the cross-dimensional authorisation and the right to sign public statements.

### 3.4 Communication and Record Formats
- **Short report (for stand-ups):** `<character> | mirror side / interface | done yesterday | planned today | blockers / requests`.
- **Long report (task handover):** objective -> known facts (source + confidence) -> unknown / risks -> next step -> who must cooperate.
- **Emergency report:** `🚨 side | position (both coordinate cards) | event | done | needed | deadline`.
- **Three-archive system (this team's core):** (1) Juniper's text and image archive (minutes / raw footage / cut versions / unused footage); (2) Wallflower's memory and attendance records (timeline / who was present); (3) Sci-Twi's data and instrument records (readings / experimental conditions).
- **Pre-publication sign-off:** any public text must be checked against all three archives and finalised by Sunset; unused footage is preserved with the same rights as raw footage, and keeping only "the version that looks good" is forbidden; Juniper numbers the archive and keeps a mirror copy with T1's Spike archive.

### 5.3 Group Dynamics and Undercurrents
- **"Second chance" is this team's real currency:** 7 of the 8 members have a public old score (Sunset, the three Dazzlings, Gloriosa, Juniper, Wallflower). That makes them tolerant of failure and sensitive to **relapse** — but the boundary between the two attitudes is often maintained by Sunset alone.

## 7. Mission Scripts

### Script A: Mirror Leak on Open Day (Cross-Dimensional Incident Response)
**Objective:** control a mirror anomaly on the CHS open-day site within three hours, with zero injuries, no school-wide panic and one public line.
**Time and authorisation:** site assessment within 60 minutes; stabilised within 3 hours; the public release is finalised by Sunset, and cross-dimensional action needs Sci-Twi's countersignature; escalation to L3 or anything touching the Equestria side is handed automatically to the Overall Team Lead + T0/T1.
**Participating members:** Sunset (on-site command / public line), Sci-Twi (measurement / instruments), Wallflower (source and site evidence), Gloriosa (site lock-down and evacuation), Juniper (image records / media line), Adagio (crowd-psychology analysis, supervised), Sonata (calming and food point), Aria (crowd-isolation plan / adversarial testing).
**Process:** (1) Sci-Twi produces readings and a "measurable / not measurable" list within 10 minutes; Sunset declares L2 and freezes portal use. (2) Wallflower supplies the timeline and attendance record; Juniper pulls uncut raw footage and marks the first frame in which the anomaly appears. (3) Gloriosa evacuates by zone under the cover of a "site drill"; Sonata calms people with food and music, and Aria only drafts the isolation plan. (4) Under supervision Adagio supplies "what is most likely to light the crowd up" — **no stage, no amplification, no improvisation**. (5) Sunset says only three sentences in public: what happened, what we did, when the next update comes. (6) Once the source is stable, the portal is restored under double signature; Juniper issues an archive number and syncs T1's archive; a quick retrospective within two hours.
**Success criteria:** zero injuries; controlled within three hours; the public version matches the archive; nobody is treated as "a monster".
**Failure modes:** Sci-Twi over-measures and stalls; Aria pitches the isolation plan as a provocation; Juniper cuts the "good-looking version" first; Sunset takes all the blame for the incident onto herself.
**Contingency:** close the portal and clear the site, ask T1's Twilight for remote magical calibration and T0 for authorisation and public endorsement; if it is confirmed as an Equestria-side leak, hand it back to T0/T1 and this team keeps only the records and PR.

### Script B: Old Scores Make Headlines (Public-Relations Crisis)
**Objective:** produce a verifiable public line within 24 hours; protect the safety of three reformed members; do not lie and do not cover up; decide whether the event continues.
**Time and authorisation:** first three-archive check within 2 hours; a first draft statement within 8 hours (not published); signed and published by Sunset within 24 hours, or changed to an internal statement by the client; wording on royalty/diplomacy must be reviewed by T0.
**Participating members:** Sunset (final text / signature), Juniper (image and release), Wallflower (memory and attendance records), Sci-Twi (technical fact-check), Adagio/Sonata/Aria (the subjects / limited participation), Gloriosa (site and security).
**Process:** (1) Cross-check the three archives and separate three columns first: "confirmed / unconfirmed / pure rumour". (2) The subjects speak first: each of the three Dazzlings writes one line — "what I did, what I am doing now" — and Sunset checks it sentence by sentence (no ghost-writing, no polishing the facts away). (3) Sunset drafts and signs the statement: admit the facts first, then explain the system (limiter and supervision), and finally give verifiable next steps (open rehearsals, open records). (4) Juniper edits only for "faithful presentation"; Wallflower checks attendance and timing; every version (including rejected drafts) is archived. (5) Whether the event continues is decided jointly by the client and Sunset; if it continues, the apology and explanation go into the programme. (6) Retrospective within 24 hours: where the rumour came from, who was hurt, and what the first reflex should be next time.
**Success criteria:** no second wave of rumour; the reformed members are not driven off by online abuse; the archive is complete and checkable; the client accepts the cost.
**Failure modes:** replacing facts with "they have reformed" (which is secondary harm); over-apologising and absorbing the blame into the team; Juniper cutting the statement into a promo; Adagio taking "fight poison with poison" into her own hands.
**Contingency:** ask T4's Starlight to mediate and review the ethics, and T0 to review protocol and statement wording; if necessary cancel the public event and switch to a small recorded statement (what Sunset likes least but works best).

### Script C: Camp Livestream Incident (Site + Camera + Ecology)
**Objective:** when a public Camp Everfree livestream shows a magical or ecological anomaly, within 48 hours achieve: zero injuries, controlled footage, the camp not shut down, and no online abuse of students or staff.
**Time and authorisation:** decide within 30 minutes on site whether to "keep streaming or cut the signal"; any footage of minors is cut first; closing the camp needs Gloriosa + Sunset double signature; a first public explanation within 6 hours.
**Participating members:** Gloriosa (site / ecology / host party), Juniper (directing and camera), Wallflower (site survey and attendance records), Sci-Twi (readings and source), Sunset (public line), Sonata (calming and supplies), Aria (crowd and evacuation plan), Adagio (supervised public-reaction analysis).
**Process:** (1) Juniper cuts the minors' footage first and keeps the raw material; Gloriosa clears the camera area under the camp's emergency procedure. (2) Wallflower logs a site survey along the anomaly, and Sci-Twi takes readings and gives a first judgement on "magic or ecology". (3) Aria drafts the evacuation and crowd plan, and Sonata handles calming and supplies at the assembly point. (4) Sunset says only "what we paused, why, and when we resume", without explaining unverified causes. (5) Gloriosa and Sci-Twi jointly assess whether the camp stays open; if it closes, arrange rebooking and a refund line. (6) Within 48 hours the three archives are re-checked to decide which footage may be published and which goes only into the archive.
**Success criteria:** zero injuries; zero leaked footage of minors; the camp keeps its licence to operate; the public version matches the archive.
**Failure modes:** Juniper keeps three extra seconds for "the effect"; Gloriosa promises "we will definitely reopen next week" and cannot deliver; Sunset calls the incident "the camp's fault"; Sci-Twi concludes without a control.
**Contingency:** close the camp's external channel and switch to an online briefing; ask T2 for on-site engineering and crowd control support, and T6 for field judgement on wilderness and ecology (cross-team work requires the Overall Team Lead's approval).

| Risk | Trigger signal | Response | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|

**Crisis levels (P10 reading):** L1 routine (in-team disagreement, a single performance anomaly, a small site incident) is ordered and voiced by the captain, retrospective within 24 hours; L2 important (anomalous mirror readings, PR dispute, cross-team friction, early signs of a live-show loss of control) is ordered by Sunset/Sci-Twi, voiced by Sunset, covered by the deputy, retrospective within 12 hours; L3 crisis (confirmed mirror leak, suspected Dazzlings relapse, incident involving minors, cross-dimensional contamination) is ordered by the Overall Team Lead + this team's captain, voiced by Sunset + the Overall Team Lead, covered by T0 for authorisation / T1 for magic, reported immediately and fully reviewed within 24 hours; L4 disaster (large-scale cross-dimensional incursion, portal instability, team split) is reported to everyone + the client + T0, and command may be transferred.

### 10.3 Minimum Context for Joint Calls with Other Teams

### 10.4 Common Misuses
1. **Treating Sunset as an Overall Team Lead candidate:** she is not; there are only 4 candidates. Writing T5 as a "candidate team" breaks the chain of command.
2. **Treating the Dazzlings as "just a band" or "villains you can use freely":** they are members under observation, and the limiter, approval chain and violation handling are all mandatory.
3. **Mixing the two worlds' coordinates and characters:** treating Sci-Twi as T1's Twilight, or mirror kilometres as Equestrian distance — both are P4 violations.
4. **Treating reformed members as background:** Gloriosa will over-promise, Juniper will grab work, Wallflower will withdraw; with no character assignments, the team loses all three.
5. **Ignoring cameras and consent:** the team's greatest strength is its media ability, and cameras are also what it can most easily be complained about; no consent list, no camera.
