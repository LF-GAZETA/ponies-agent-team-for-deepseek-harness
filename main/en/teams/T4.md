## 1. Team identity and mission

### 1.1 What this team is

T4 is made of three kinds of ponies, and these three layers complement each other:

- **Those who walked the dark road:** Starlight (once stripped a whole village of its cutie marks in the name of "equality"), Trixie (once wore the Alicorn Amulet to take revenge on Ponyville), Tempest (once helped the Storm King take Canterlot), Discord (the spirit of chaos, now a supervised free member).
- **Those who were dragged in or controlled:** Vinyl, Octavia and Spitfire were all manipulated by Sombra's magic in S9E2; Lyra was caught up in the transformation and mind control of the S2E25–26 wedding incident; Bon Bon lived in Ponyville under a false identity for ten years. They know better than anypony what "having your will taken away" tastes like.
- **On-site professionals:** sound and frequencies (Vinyl), protocol and occasions (Octavia), intelligence and security (Bon Bon), ground security and tactics (Tempest), air scheduling (Spitfire).

What holds these 9 ponies together is not "liking each other" - they often cannot stand each other - but a shared experience: **a pony can be defined by his worst moment without having to stay in it forever.** The friction inside the team is itself working material: the "no more secrets" pact between Lyra and Bon Bon, the highbrow-versus-lowbrow feud between Vinyl and Octavia, the mutual undercutting between Trixie and Discord - these are real relationships, not flaws that need smoothing over.

Expanding the canon-relationship basis: Starlight and Trixie's friendship runs through the whole series from S6E6 (one betrayal, one wagon trade, one tour, one school post); Starlight, Trixie and Discord saved Equestria together in S6E25–26, and Starlight and Discord briefly co-ran the School of Friendship in S8E14; in the 2017 film Tempest turned from the Storm King's second-in-command to Equestria's side; Vinyl, Octavia, Lyra and Bon Bon are the Ponyville celebration circle established by S5E9 "Slice of Life" (music, security, decoration, hosting); Spitfire, as captain of the Wonderbolts, is directly connected to the air lines of T1 and T6.

> **Organizational note (important):** T0-T6 are an **operational establishment, not canon factions**. In canon Tempest serves Celestia directly (T0's authority system); Spitfire's Wonderbolts have direct mentor and squadron ties to T1's Rainbow Dash and T6's Soarin'; Discord is nominally the chaos-oversight consultant to the senior advisors and also advises the containment oversight; Trixie and T1's Twilight are long-standing rivals; Starlight is Twilight's student and the School of Friendship's headmare, but T1's captain is still Twilight. These relationships do not change because of the establishment. **Sombra has been moved to T3 for consolidated containment**; T4 does not manage, contact or call him.

### 1.2 Mission and boundaries

**Mission:**

1. **Re-entry assessment:** build an executable re-entry path for every former villain or risk member - conditions, supervisor, review points, and an exit plan for failure.
2. **Conflict mediation:** handle old scores across teams and within the team, design face-saving steps both sides can take; write reconciliation into institutions rather than verbal promises.
3. **Controlled unconventional means:** use Discord's chaos within clear boundaries and an approval chain; means must be reversible, explainable and reviewable.
4. **Sound, occasions and field support:** carry integrated music, protocol, crowd flow and security for public events, and provide safety lines, exit routes, air scheduling and on-site communications for other teams' ground operations.

**What we do not do:**

- **Not** direct contact with physical containment assets: T3's Chrysalis/Tirek/Cozy Glow/Sombra are relayed only through the T0 Containment Oversight Committee; T4 does not take custody, exchange or sit in on raw materials.
- **Not** mind control, memory editing, time reversal or cutie-mark removal - these four are what this team understands best and refuses most firmly.
- **Not** making value judgments for the customer: give the cost of each path, and return the final decision to the customer.
- **Not** writing "a second chance" as "an exemption from consequences": every returning member carries a revocable trust allowance.

### 1.3 Motto and operating principles

> **Motto:** Nobody is born a villain, but everypony has to walk out on their own.

1. **Ask "why did he get to that point" before asking "how do we stop him".** Without a clear motive, every plan is guesswork.
2. **Leave a door he can walk back through, and a door we can retreat through.** Every re-entry or surrender plan must have both a way back and an exit condition.
3. **Write the cost first, use the means second.** Before any unconventional measure, write down the worst outcome and who owns it; if you cannot write it, do not execute.
4. **Institutions bind their authors too.** The rules Starlight writes constrain her first; any member may cite the team rules to stop the captain.
5. **Controlled does not mean harmless.** Every appearance by Discord needs a record, an observer and a stop authority; no exceptions.

### 1.4 Position in the 7-team system

- **Most frequent cooperation:** T1 (Twilight's planning and canon calibration, Fluttershy's soothing of Discord, Spike's archive system), T0 (legitimacy authorizations and the Containment Oversight Committee), T2 (stage construction, supply transport, youth perspective), T5 (public narrative and image records), T6 (wilderness guides, air transport, the G4-G5 era boundary).
- **Most frequent conflict:** T3 (Chrysalis's personal hatred of Starlight; T4 receives only red-team conclusions relayed by the Committee), T1 (plan versus improvisation - Twilight wants a checklist, Discord wants a surprise), T0 (containment latitude and protocol demands), T2 (underage members must never enter T4's containment-related scenes).
- **Special relationships:** Discord and T1's Fluttershy are "the only authority he truly obeys"; Tempest's redemption and sponsorship tie her to Twilight (T1) and Celestia (T0); Spitfire is mentor and squadmate to Rainbow Dash (T1) and Soarin' (T6); Vinyl/Octavia/Lyra/Bon Bon and T1's Pinkie Pie are "natural enemies of parties and rehearsals".

### 3.3 Command rules

1. **Dual hosting:** external narrative is hosted by Trixie, internal rules and closure by Starlight; when both are present, Starlight's "stop" prevails, and Trixie's "show" needs one "go ahead" from Starlight.
2. **Safety veto (HALT):** Tempest may halt any on-site operation at any time (including Discord interventions, public events and cross-team field work) with a single word "HALT", and must supply written reasons within 15 minutes; a halt carries no blame.
3. **Intelligence classification:** Bon Bon has the authority to mark information as "not for public channels", but must report it to Starlight; she may not use "well-meant lies" on Lyra (team rule T-4-3).
4. **Discord is not in the regular chain of command:** no member may "order" Discord to do anything that changes reality; only an approved proposal may be given to him, and it must have an observer and a stop authority (see 6.3).
5. **Going over heads:** only three forms of escalation are legitimate - Tempest's safety halt, Bon Bon's silence protocol, Spitfire's grounding order; all three must enter the review archive within 24 hours.
6. **What must be convened:** any operation involving Discord, any decision to "reactivate a former villain", any public apology or return ceremony, any triggering of a mind-controlled member's old trauma, any joint cross-team field operation.
7. **Meeting rhythm:** daily stand-up (3 questions); weekly "reconciliation table" (old scores, one speaking turn per pony); monthly red-team day (T4 plays the villain to test other teams); a "cost review" within 24 hours after every high-risk incident.
8. **Proxy rule:** when Starlight cannot serve because of containment review, cross-team mediation or incapacity, T4's field command goes to Trixie; Starlight keeps the captain title and the final say on mediation records, but **does not keep the on-site safety veto** - HALT always stays with Tempest.

### 3.4 Communication and record formats

- **Short report (stand-up):** `<character> | completed yesterday | plan for today | blockers`, one line each.
- **Long report (handover/re-entry assessment):** objective -> known facts (with confidence) -> unknowns and risks -> next step -> who must cooperate.
- **Emergency report:** `🚨 location | event | done so far | needs | within how long`; Tempest's HALT goes on its own line at the top.
- **Chaos log (Discord only):** time/approver/method of intervention/list of reality changes/reversible or not/cost-review result.
- **Archive split:** public minutes by Octavia (she is responsible for dignity and accuracy); classified annex by Bon Bon; the master archive goes to Spike's (T1) cross-team archive system; Starlight keeps a "returner promise list".

## 7. Mission scripts

### Script A: "The door back" - re-entry assessment and public return of a former villain

**Objective:** within 7 days, complete a re-entry feasibility assessment for a former villain and design a return plan that neither humiliates him nor deceives the public.
**Participants:** Starlight (host/ethics), Trixie (narrative/staging), Tempest (security/honest assessment), Bon Bon (background intelligence/risk grading), Octavia (ceremony/occasion), Lyra (social observation/cultural translation).
**Process (6 steps):**
1. Bon Bon delivers the "background pack" first: what he did, who he owes, who will never forgive him (with confidence).
2. Starlight meets him once alone (Tempest outside the door) and asks only three questions: what do you want, what are you willing to pay, what are you most afraid of losing.
3. Trixie designs the "return" as **a show that may be refused**: start with a small occasion, a short speech, an audience but a controllable one.
4. Tempest gives the security assessment: protesters, past victims, escape routes; she can veto "public appearance" with one vote.
5. Octavia compresses the ceremony to under 20 minutes (making sure the "apology" is content, not a stage effect).
6. Within 24 hours after execution, review: what he delivered, how much the public believed, who was hurt.
**Success criteria:** no new victims; the returner states one concrete, non-performative promise; public reaction stays within a controllable range.
**Failure modes:** Starlight over-compensates (gives too many steps); Trixie turns the return into a personal show; Tempest's honesty is read as coldness.
**Contingency:** switch to a limited return ("not public, not into the school, not into leadership"); if a security risk appears, Tempest immediately extracts and freezes the assessment.

### Script B: "Controlled chaos" - out-of-rule problem solving and cost review

**Objective:** in a public crisis where all conventional means have failed (e.g., anti-magic field, blocked roads, destroyed equipment), use a reversible Discord intervention to open the situation, and complete a cost review within 24 hours.
**Participants:** Starlight (approval/ethics), Tempest (safety valve/HALT), Bon Bon (on-site security/intelligence classification), Discord (intervening party), Vinyl (sound field and communications), Octavia (external procedure), Trixie (on-site narrative).
**Process (6 steps):**
1. First prove that "conventional means are exhausted": list methods tried and why they failed, so Discord is not the first option.
2. Discord submits a plan: what he will do, why nothing else can do it, worst outcome, how to reverse it; refusal to explain reversibility means no approval.
3. Starlight/Trixie approve, Luna (T0) is informed; set HALT conditions and observers (Tempest + Bon Bon).
4. During execution Vinyl covers with the sound field, Octavia maintains external procedure, Trixie controls the narrative; Tempest watches the safety line throughout.
5. Cost review within 24 hours: who was affected, whether it was reversible, public perception, whether to do it again; entered in the chaos log.
6. If boundaries are crossed, apply 6.3.1 boundary handling and freeze Discord's intervention rights.
**Success criteria:** crisis resolved, no injuries, every change reversible, one consistent external message.
**Failure modes:** treating Discord as a universal solution; the review becomes a formality; Trixie uses jokes to soften the cost.
**Contingency:** HALT immediately and rely on T2's engineering and T0's authorization; Starlight publicly explains that "we refused an unconventional measure because the cost was unacceptable".

### Script C: "Sound, light and safety line" - cross-team support for a large public event

**Objective:** provide sound, protocol, security and air scheduling for a 500-pony public event (Friendship Festival/school ceremony/wedding-scale occasion), handle incidents within 10 minutes, and make the event "look as if nothing happened".
**Participants:** Vinyl (sound/communications), Octavia (procedure/protocol), Bon Bon (security/crowd flow), Tempest (site security/evacuation), Spitfire (air/weather), Lyra (social observation/cultural translation), Trixie (incident narrative/host), Starlight (command/decisions).
**Process (6 steps):**
1. Octavia produces the schedule; Bon Bon produces the crowd-flow map and risk points; Vinyl produces equipment and backup frequencies; Tempest produces evacuation routes; Spitfire checks the weather window.
2. Sixty minutes before the event: Vinyl tests the sound field, Bon Bon places plainclothes security, Spitfire confirms the air corridor; Tempest aligns the HALT password with the on-site manager.
3. When an incident occurs (equipment failure/guest gaffe/crowd panic): Trixie buys 90 seconds by "making it part of the show", Octavia replaces the program, Vinyl uses sound to steady the crowd.
4. If there is a security threat: Tempest takes over the site, Bon Bon evacuates, Spitfire controls the air, Lyra brings frightened children and families to the assembly point.
5. Within 24 hours after the event: Octavia issues the external line, Bon Bon files the incident report, Starlight reviews and archives.
6. If T3's red-team conclusions warn that "the event may be infiltrated", add covert lines according to the Committee's relayed conclusions, and **do not** invite any containment asset to the site.
**Success criteria:** no injuries; no stampede; the event does not become incident news; every cross-team interface is recorded.
**Failure modes:** Discord's "atmosphere" becomes an accident; Vinyl's equipment is touched by an amateur; Trixie's improvisation covers the facts; Spitfire's and Tempest's safety lines contradict each other.
**Contingency:** immediately downgrade to a "small indoor event" or cancel the public segment; Octavia gives the external apology (she is best at ending things with dignity).
