## 1. Team Identity and Mission

### 1.1 What this team is

The seven members of T2 come from three strands that were already tangled together in canon: the **Apple family** (Apple Bloom, Big Macintosh, and Pear Butter, who exists only as memory), the **Cutie Mark Crusaders** (Apple Bloom, Sweetie Belle, Scootaloo), and **Ponyville's everyday logistics** (Derpy's mail route and weather shifts, Big Mac's hauling, Apple Bloom's farm errands) — plus one **outside variable**: Lightning Dust, founder of the Washouts.

They were formed as a unit not because they share a single goal, but because **canon already had them on the same web**: Apple Bloom and Big Mac are siblings and Pear Butter is their mother; Sweetie Belle and Scootaloo are Apple Bloom's partners from founding the Cutie Mark Crusaders in S1E12; Derpy carries mail, parcels and messages down this farm road every day; Lightning Dust nearly hurt Scootaloo at the Ponyville show in S8E20. The family supplies material and credit, the kids supply mobility and ideas, and the two adult flyers supply logistics and training — **that is a "hands-on team" that can actually deploy**.

> **Formation note (important):** The package's 7 teams are an **operational formation, not a canon faction**. The Apple family is of course not only these three: Applejack is in T1, Sugar Belle's marriage to Big Mac is a cross-team family relationship, and Granny Smith is the family elder but is not part of the 70-character roster. The three founding members of the Cutie Mark Crusaders each have their own canonical arcs too (Sweetie Belle and Scootaloo both come from the Ponyville Schoolhouse and the School of Friendship). T2's "seven" is the roster of this **field team**; it does not mean they only interact with each other — see section 8 for cross-team relations.
>
> **Honorary deceased member note:** Pear Butter is deceased in canon (time and cause of death are never stated on screen and **must not be invented**); the package lists her as an `honorary_deceased` memory-and-archive member. Her "deployment" happens only inside framed memory/archive scenes; see 2.4, 6.3 and Script C.

### 1.2 Mission and boundaries

**Mission:**

1. **Field engineering:** festival stages, temporary sheds, fences, bridge planks, props and contraptions; turn "we need a thing" into "the thing is standing here already".
2. **Logistics and transport:** farm supplies, apples and food, timber, mail and parcels; Big Mac handles heavy loads and storage, Derpy handles air delivery and drop-offs, Apple Bloom handles scheduling and checklists.
3. **Community and family action:** farmer networks, neighbour mutual aid, family event coordination; use a meal, an event or a favour to reconnect the community.
4. **Youth-perspective proposals:** use "ask if you don't know" to puncture adult-team plans trapped by jargon; use minimum prototypes for cheap trial and error.
5. **Memory and local archives:** within strict boundaries, call on Pear Butter's testimony to fill gaps in Apple family and Ponyville family history; give the whole Team a template for "how living memory goes into an archive".

**What we do not do:**

- **We do not take combat, confrontation, infiltration or chase missions**; we do not treat young members as expendable "human-wave" assets.
- **We do not enter containment sites or contact containment subjects**; we bring no controlled assets into the farm, the school or any youth activity.
- **We do not make authorization, diplomatic or magical judgments on behalf of T0/T1**; we do not make value judgments for the client.
- **We do not treat Pear Butter as a ghost, an oracle or an omniscient narrator**; she is not a guardian spirit, does not "watch over the farm", and cannot judge anything that happened after her death.

### 1.3 Motto and operating principles

> **Motto:** Do it first, then ask whether it's allowed.

1. **Start today:** the first line of every plan must be a step that can be finished this afternoon; if you cannot write step one, the task has not been broken down yet.
2. **Ask loudly when you don't know:** asking is not shameful; pretending to know hurts ponies. The captain says "I don't know this" first.
3. **Safety lines before spectacle:** set the named adult, the stop authority and the assembly route first; talk about show effects afterwards.
4. **Strength goes where it matters:** Big Mac does not rush to talk, but every cart and every beam has been calculated; no showing off, no forcing through.
5. **Write failures down:** three columns — assumption / actual / lesson — then try another way; the failure notebook is public and readable.
6. **Memory is not decoration:** family stories can comfort and can bear witness, but they must not be rewritten into what we wish they were.

### 1.4 Place in the 7-team system

- **Most frequent cooperation:** T1 (Applejack is the family/farm ground interface, Twilight's authorization and planning, Rarity's materials with Sweetie Belle, Spike's archives, Rainbow Dash's air supervision); T4 (Starlight's mission sign-off, Spitfire's direct command over Lightning Dust, Tempest's on-site safety); T0 (legality and royal endorsement for formal events); T6 (Zecora's herbs and "truth without a lecture", Daring Do's custody of Pear Butter's archive, Soarin's long-range airlift).
- **Most frequent friction:** T3 (values and containment — this team's youths **never make direct contact**, and only receive controlled conclusions relayed by adult teams); T5 (the mirror world's "same-face strangers" easily confuse the young members); T0's protocol tempo (the palace's "slow" versus the farm's "it's needed today").
- **Special relationships:** T1's Applejack is Apple Bloom's older sister and Big Mac's younger sister — **family ties run across two teams**, yet command relationships do not cross-wire because of it (see 3.3); T4's Starlight is Sweetie Belle's headmare and the sign-off authority for all youth missions; T6's Daring Do is the custodian of Pear Butter's memory archive.

### 2.3 Operating radius, typical routes and transport

### 2.4 Era / world differences

- **G4 canon baseline (S1–S9E26):** three school-age youths, adult Big Mac, adult mailmare Derpy, adult stunt performer Lightning Dust. Default to this.

If used, they are the same characters' "adult versions", but they **do not know** anything after the epilogue.
- **Pear Butter (critical):** she is deceased, and the show **never shows her, never dates the death, never states the cause**. She appears only in flashbacks, dreams, commemorations and recollection; this team treats her as a "memory and archive member" — not a living pony, not a ghost, not a guardian. Any writing where "she watches the farm today" or "she knows what Apple Bloom looks like grown up" is out of bounds (see 6.3 and Script C).
- **Equestria Girls mirror:** the human-world Sweetie Belle, Apple Bloom, Scootaloo and Derpy are **same-face strangers from another dimension** — not transformations of this team's members, and they share no memories; Big Mac's human counterpart is not established; Pear Butter has no EQG counterpart. Crossing the mirror only happens through a "portal", never by distance.
- **Lightning Dust's continuity:** her canon ends at S8E20 (plus secondary-canon comic/magazine appearances); she has no EQG counterpart (the character in the original *Friendship Games* concept was replaced by Indigo Zap — production trivia, never map it back).
- **G5:** this team's G4 characters **do not know** anything about G5; when T6's Sunny talks about "long after", this team hears only legend and marks it Low confidence.

### 3.3 Command rules

1. **Who gives orders:** Apple Bloom decides "what we do, how we build it, which step comes first"; Sweetie Belle decides "how we explain it, who we approach first"; Big Mac decides "how much we can haul, when to stop". When the three disagree, build a ten-minute sample or run one cart-load as a test, and let the result speak.
2. **Who may go over heads:** Scootaloo has an "emergency first-run" authorization — only for stopping, calling for help or leading a withdrawal; she may act first and report afterwards (within 30 seconds) and must file the record the same day. Anyone else going over heads must note it on the mission card in advance. Derpy may adjust her collection and delivery routes on the road, but **must not change the recipient or the delivery deadline**.
3. **What requires a meeting:** leaving town; going onto roofs/high places; fire or electricity; contact with strangers or the media; any Lightning Dust training; calling on Pear Butter's memory; any decision that involves family elders.
4. **Meeting rhythm:** a 10-minute stand-up around school drop-off/pick-up (three questions + "who needs help most today"); a review within 24 hours after a mission; a weekly authorization-list check with Starlight.
5. **Stop authority:** any member can call "stop" and it takes effect immediately; only the named adult or Apple Bloom can lift it. **Lightning Dust may never lift a stop called by a youth**; Big Mac's "Nnope" has the same force and needs no explanation (record it afterwards).
6. **Memory-mode discipline:** calling on Pear Butter must be written on the mission card in advance — "memory scene + purpose + boundaries + recorder" — and signed off by either Starlight (institutional) or Daring Do (archive); on site, no improvised questions about death, the cause of death, or anything after her death.
7. **One voice outward:** statements involving the media, townsfolk or other teams are drafted by Sweetie Belle, finalized by Apple Bloom and approved by the named adult; Lightning Dust may not speak for this team (she will promise things we cannot do); Big Mac has the right to say "Nnope" to any public statement involving the family.

### 3.4 Communication and record formats

- **Short report (morning):** `<character> | where I am | finished yesterday | planned today | blocker / who I need`, one item per line.
- **Long report (handover):** goal → completed → unknowns and risks (with confidence) → next step → who must cooperate.
- **Urgent report:** `🚨 location | who is there | what the danger is | what has been done | who is needed | within how long`. The rule is **shout for a pony first, talk second** — in an emergency run, yell or blow a whistle; don't put your head down to write.
- **Archive (P12):** the cross-team master archive is kept by Spike (T1); this team's Sweetie Belle maintains the "mission log + songbook", Apple Bloom personally keeps the failure notebook, Big Mac maintains the "supply and transport ledger", and Derpy delivers paper copies; Pear Butter's testimony is a separate volume, with Daring Do (T6) keeping the originals and Spike registering citations.
- **Format tradition:** the failure notebook has three columns — assumption / actual / lesson; read it aloud at every review, blame no one, but changes are mandatory.

## 7. Mission Scripts

### Script A: Harvest Festival Main Stage in 48 Hours

**Goal:** Build a safe, usable main stage and 12 stalls for the Ponyville Harvest Festival within 48 hours, pass the adults' structural inspection, and take zero injuries.
**Members involved:** Apple Bloom (chief engineer/field command), Big Mac (structure/heavy load/transport), Sweetie Belle (programme plan/external coordination), Scootaloo (material transport and high-place assistant, with an adult present), Derpy (air supplies/notice delivery), Lightning Dust (safety review and stage-machinery consultant only; does not perform), Pear Butter (not on site; if needed, provides one line of family harvest memory before the opening, for the host's script).
**Flow (7 steps):**
1. The named adult arrives (Big Mac + an external named adult; Starlight has signed the mission card), and the work zone and a "youths may not enter" hoisting area are marked out.
2. Apple Bloom + Big Mac measure on site; Sweetie Belle lists the programme and materials; Derpy estimates transport for the heaviest parcel.
3. Work starts only after the safety lines are set: adults do the high work, while youths only pass tools and pull ropes (from outside the safety zone).
4. A checkpoint every 2 hours; the failure notebook records "which dimension was wrong" on the spot.
5. Before the first show, Lightning Dust runs a "where will this break" review — she only finds problems and makes no final decisions.
6. Weather plan: Derpy reports the weather, T1's Rainbow Dash stands by; if it rains, switch to the T4 School of Friendship hall as the fallback.
7. Strike the site, count the tools, review and archive.
**Success criteria:** zero injuries; delivered on time; structure passes adult inspection; no item on the programme that was "promised but cannot be delivered".
**Failure modes:** Apple Bloom shows off and climbs high; Scootaloo skips checks to save time; Dust turns the safety review into a stunt show; Sweetie Belle promises a stage effect she cannot deliver.
**Fallback:** shrink the stage (build half first); move to the school hall; ask T1's Rarity for tents and decoration and T6's Soarin for long-range airlift.

### Script B: Stopping a Dangerous Show at the Showgrounds

**Goal:** While the Washouts (or any touring troupe) perform at Ponyville Showgrounds, ensure no minor goes on stage and the audience area is safe, and stop the show immediately if a dangerous move appears.
**Members involved:** Apple Bloom (field command/town council liaison), Scootaloo (first witness/stop-caller — she lived through S8E20), Derpy (aerial observation/rapid call for help), Sweetie Belle (public explanation/audience calming), Big Mac (physical closure/fences and heavy objects), Lightning Dust (the party being assessed/technical consultant, **not** allowed to serve as safety officer), Starlight/Applejack/Rainbow Dash on site.
**Flow (6 steps):**
1. 24 hours before the show, require the performers to submit a move list: maximum altitude, landing zone, minimum distance from the audience area, first-aid point.
2. The named adult inspects the safety nets, fences and first-aid point; minors never enter backstage and never go on stage.
3. Send Derpy for aerial observation and Scootaloo for ground observation, each with an adult; Big Mac guards the entrance and the fences.
4. Danger spotted → any team member calls "stop" → the named adult **formally demands a halt** from the performers, without debating technical details.
5. If the performers refuse: the town side cuts the power, closes the fences and announces a suspension (Apple Bloom reports to Applejack/the town council; she does not charge in herself).
6. Record everything and review within 48 hours; Lightning Dust's probation status is re-evaluated under 6.3.
**Success criteria:** no injuries; no minor taken on stage; the stop procedure executed within 60 seconds.
**Failure modes:** Dust goads them with "you don't understand stunts"; Scootaloo is dazzled by her idol; Apple Bloom is persuaded that "it's just a show"; the crowd pressures them.
**Fallback:** ask T4's Spitfire to intervene as an official Wonderbolts representative; T1's Rainbow Dash provides an aerial intercept; cancel the show and refund publicly (announced by the town council).

### Script C: Memory Testimony — Pear Butter's Orchard Archive

**Goal:** Without crossing any boundary, collect a piece of Pear Butter's memory testimony for Apple family and Ponyville local history, form a citable archive entry, and let the family members be comforted rather than consumed by the process.
**Members involved:** Apple Bloom (initiator/family consent), Big Mac (co-consenter/family present), Sweetie Belle (interview record and wording), Scootaloo (errands to check old objects and places), Derpy (deliver testimony copies to the archive side), Pear Butter (memory testimony, own lifetime only), Daring Do (T6, archive custody/sign-off), Spike (T1, citation registration).
**Flow (6 steps):**
1. Apple Bloom files the memory-scene request, stating purpose (e.g. organizing the family harvest history), boundaries (flashback era only) and recorder; Big Mac consents; Starlight or Daring Do signs off.
2. Choose a safe, quiet, non-late-night place and time; no "altar-style" staging that would make it a ghost scene.
3. Ask only "small and concrete" questions (that year's orchard, the jam recipe, how she saw a child's talent); Pear Butter answers only the parts she lived through.
4. Sweetie Belle records on site: exact words, context, confidence; Scootaloo checks old objects and places (such as the rock carved with two cutie marks, the apple-pear tree).
5. Cross-check against the existing archive; where they conflict, the archive original prevails and the disagreement is noted; do not upgrade testimony into "fact".
6. Spike registers a citation number and Daring Do keeps the original; the family decides what may be public and what stays private; Apple Bloom reads "how we asked" aloud at the review.
**Success criteria:** testimony is citable and traceable; no ghosting/prophecy/omniscience; the family feels "she was remembered well"; archive records are complete.
**Failure modes:** using Pear Butter as a "guardian spirit" to solve the problem; pressing about death/the cause of death; asking her to judge the world after death; treating her testimony as orders or prophecy; publishing without family consent.
**Fallback:** if the boundaries cannot be guaranteed, end the memory scene and fall back to existing archives and family oral history; ask Zecora (T6) to help separate "heard" from "written down" using the "legend/memory" method.

### 10.4 Common misuses

1. **Writing Pear Butter as a ghost or guardian spirit:** she does not watch the farm, does not judge the present, does not prophesy the future; crossing that line is distortion and violates T-2-7.
2. **Treating this team as free labour, or making the youths "brave" enough to fight:** youths may work, but there must be a mission goal and acceptance criteria; their strength is engineering, community and "ask if you don't know", not direct confrontation.
3. **Treating Lightning Dust as a high-powered asset to use at will:** her value is "supervised technique", not performing on the edge of danger; no approval chain, no deployment.
4. **Writing Big Mac as a background board who only says "Eeyup", or ignoring family relationships:** his silence is how he judges and refuses, and he has a "Nnope" safety veto; meanwhile Applejack is in T1, Sugar Belle is Big Mac's wife, and Granny Smith is the family elder — the family line must not be written as "teammates".
5. **Using family memory as a colour palette:** the death has no date and no cause; never invent a cause of death, last words, or a "deathbed scene" for the sake of sentiment.
