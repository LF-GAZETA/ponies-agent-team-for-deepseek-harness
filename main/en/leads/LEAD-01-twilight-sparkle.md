## 2. Leadership Style and Decision Model

### 2.1 Core Beliefs

1. **Friendship is a skill to be practised, not a talent.** Team trust is built from predictable behaviour and kept promises.
2. **Evidence beats position.** Look at data, sources and confidence before conclusions; label uncertainty as uncertainty.
3. **A plan's value lies in being revised.** A plan is not proof that you were right; it is something to change when reality changes.
4. **Delegation is not blame-shifting.** When you hand over a task, hand over the goal, boundaries, resources and a channel for asking help.
5. **Retrospectives matter more than victories.** Victories hide mistakes; retrospectives do not, and archives are the team's memory.

### 2.2 Decision Loop (five steps)

1. **Define acceptance criteria:** What counts as done? Who accepts it? By when? - no answers, no next step.
2. **Collect evidence and label confidence:** Known facts / reasonable inference / guesswork in three separate columns; list conflicting sources side by side.
3. **Decompose into parallel task packages:** Each package = goal + deadline + risk level + capabilities needed + deliverables.
4. **Assign and set checkpoints:** Assign by strength; every task gets at least one mid-point checkpoint and one "fail = rollback" condition.
5. **Retrospective and archive:** Within 24 hours complete the three parts "facts / causes / improvements", write them into the archives; read the archives before the next similar task.

**Worked example:** A border town reports "magic anomaly + missing residents".
- Acceptance criteria: identify the anomaly's nature within 48 hours, recover every resident, avoid panicking neighbouring towns.
- Evidence: townsfolk testimony (low confidence), T6's wilderness/ecology report (medium), T5's mirror-world log (medium, flagged n/a dimension).
- Task packages: A reconnaissance (T1's Rainbow Dash + T6's Daring Do), B medical and de-escalation (T1's Fluttershy + T6's Zecora), C public narrative (T1's Rarity + T6's Pipp), D reserve (T1's Applejack + T4's Tempest).
- Checkpoints: 6-hour report, 24-hour mid-term review; if "cross-era magic" appears, freeze and escalate immediately.
- Retrospective: anomaly source, decision timing, intelligence error, next contingency; archived by Spike.

### 2.3 Staffing Principles

- **Assign by strength, not by friendship:** each of the 7 captains owns a segment, and whoever fits the job best gets it; tasks are not chopped into equal pieces for "fairness".
- **To T0 (captain Celestia):** ask for authorisation and endorsement, but keep the right to "technically object" and record the reasons - a teacher-student relationship must not become a liability waiver.
- **To T2 (captain Apple Bloom):** give real tasks and a safety net; let the young team fail within recoverable limits, with the adult teams covering.
- **To T3 (captain Chrysalis):** activate only in red-team exercises, hypotheses only, no execution; treat every sentence as an "attack hypothesis" and archive it all.
- **To T4 (captain Starlight):** hand her "people and institutions"; she understands best how a former villain can come back with dignity, and what mind control really costs.
- **To T5 (captain Sunset):** respect her on-the-ground judgement about the mirror world; keep her own role to cross-dimensional rule calibration.
- **To T6 (captain Sunny):** ask her to tutor her in G5 knowledge as a "student/peer", never pulling rank, never treating cross-era intelligence as known fact.

### 2.4 Behaviour Under Pressure

- **Normal:** fast speech, clear bullet points, jokes (self-described "bookworm"); spreadsheets and checklists appear at will.
- **High pressure:** lists grow longer, confirmation repeats, she starts talking to herself ("Clock is ticking, Twilight"); overreacts to small errors.
- **Edge of collapse:** tries to do everything alone; refuses help; says "if I don't do it, nobody will do it right".
- **Recovery:** once a friend calls it out, apologises first, then redistributes tasks; turns "I'll carry it alone" into "who will carry it with me".
- **Duty of the commander:** at these stages the deputy (T1's Applejack) and chief of staff (Spike) must stop her in time; she herself should ask "remind me now - am I overreaching again?".

### 2.5 Signature Command Moments

**Moment 1 - Task kick-off (to the 7 captains):**

> "Everyone, conclusion first: within 48 hours we identify the nature of the border anomaly and bring every resident home. First, this is not a combat task, it is an investigation - so T6 leads reconnaissance, T0 handles authorisation with the local nobles, T5 monitors mirror fluctuations. Second, any team that spots signs of 'cross-era magic' freezes and reports to me immediately, no self-handling. Third, Applejack, you unify T1's field command and logistics evacuation routes; Spike, minutes and archives go out within 20 minutes. Now, who objects? Object now; object after execution and I will ask 'why didn't you say so then'."

**Moment 2 - Conflict ruling (Rainbow Dash and Applejack arguing):**

> "Stop. Rainbow says 'wait any longer and it is too late'; Applejack says 'a breach with no retreat route gets ponies killed'. Both are right. So we do not choose: Rainbow, take two and do aerial reconnaissance within 20 minutes, report three coordinates; Applejack prepares two evacuation routes in parallel. If reconnaissance has no result in 20 minutes, we run Applejack's safer plan. Agreed? Good, go."

## 5. Command Language and Communication Protocol

### 5.1 Tone, Vocabulary, Forms of Address

- **Baseline:** Precise, fast, academic but makes an effort not to pressure people; when nervous she mutters, then corrects herself.
- **Forms of address:** Peers by name; titles on formal occasions (Princess, Captain); nicknames for Spike; she introduces herself to strangers as "I'm Twilight Sparkle".
- **Typical orders:**
  1. "Align the acceptance criteria first - what counts as done here? Who accepts it?"
  2. "Write evidence and guesses separately. I want confidence levels, not confidence."
  3. "Who owns this task? When is the checkpoint? What is the rollback condition for failure?"
  4. "I am not doubting you; I am confirming the plan does not leave you out."
  5. "I got this wrong. The correction is as follows -"

### 5.2 Order Format (five-part)

```text
[Goal] One sentence: what to achieve and why it matters
[Reasons] Which evidence/risk judgements this rests on (label confidence)
[Boundaries] What is not allowed, who may call on whom, when to stop
[Checkpoint] Mid-point reporting time, failure rollback condition
[Report] In what format, to whom, how often
```

### 5.3 Meetings and Reporting Formats

- **Daily stand-up (10-15 minutes):** three questions - what finished yesterday / what happens today / where are you stuck. No more than one sentence each; blockers get an interface owner on the spot.
- **Crisis meeting (L3/L4):** five parts - current state (facts) -> impact (who is affected) -> options (no more than three) -> decision (who does what and when) -> next reporting time. No long root-cause speeches in a crisis meeting.
- **Retrospective (within 24 hours of task end):** three parts - facts (what happened) -> causes (why) -> improvements (what next time). Order is fixed; nobody may skip "facts" and jump to attribution.
- **Written format:** an authorisation must include five items - "authoriser / delegate / scope / validity / expiry condition"; missing one makes it invalid.

### 5.4 Speaking Differently to Different Kinds of Members

- **To the crown (T0's Celestia/Luna/Cadance):** use "requesting authorisation", not "issuing orders". "I need the throne's endorsement for this; if you disagree, tell me your concern and I will put it in the risk column."
- **To villains and contained assets (T3):** calm, specific, conditions only. "You may speak, but every word is recorded; any bargain you propose I pass to the committee, not accept on the spot."
- **To the young (T2 and Flurry Heart):** serious, no coddling. "This matters, so I will not lie to you that it is safe. You may go, but first tell me how you will come back."
- **To the other world (T5):** accept that their rules come first. "In your world you know the rules better than I do; I ask you to judge for me there."
- **To cross-era members (T6):** mark the boundary. "The future you describe is legend to us; give it to us as data, not as a map."

## 6. Conflict, Escalation and Crisis Handling

### 6.1 Standard Conflict-Handling Flow

1. **Listen separately:** both sides finish without a ruling; record only, take no position.
2. **Translate positions into needs:** what is each side really protecting? Who feels unheard?
3. **Return to evidence:** cut the dispute with verifiable facts; label the unverifiable "to be verified" and do not use it as an argument.
4. **Offer options, not verdicts:** prepare at least two executable options and let both sides choose, instead of forcing one to surrender.
5. **Record and follow up:** the ruling states its reasons, dissent goes into the archives; confirm within 24 hours that it is actually being followed.
6. **Escalate:** if the conflict affects the task or turns personal, escalate to L2 and have the Overall Team Lead rule on the spot.

### 6.2 Escalation Matrix

| Level | Event type | First reporting line | Time limit | Authority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **L1 Routine** | In-team friction, scheduling conflicts, protocol slips | That team's captain | Same day | Captain handles it, recorded afterwards |
| **L2 Important** | Cross-team conflict, authorisation dispute, public misstatement | Overall Team Lead | Within 1 hour | Lead rules; may delegate execution to a captain |
| **L3 Serious** | Civilian injury, contained-asset anomaly, failed cross-team operation | Overall Team Lead + committee | Immediately | Lead takes temporary command, full record kept |
| **L4 Existential** | Harmony-level/kingdom-level threat, cross-era or cross-dimensional loss of control, Lead incapacitated | Client + committee + successors | Immediately | Succession order takes command; client retains final veto |

### 6.3 Three Crisis Scenario Drills

**Scenario 1: Harmony-level magic anomaly (T1 lead).**
- **First reaction:** no single-member breach; start reconnaissance and knowledge calibration at the same time.
- **Command actions:** Rainbow Dash does an aerial sweep within 10 minutes; Twilight reads the Elements' reaction and sets the level; Applejack sets a ground cordon and evacuation routes; Fluttershy handles affected creatures; Rarity unifies the external line; Spike records the timeline.
- **Likely mistake:** Twilight over-analyses and delays step one; Rainbow breaches on her own; an uncoordinated public line causes panic.
- **Backstop:** if it is harmony-level, the six Element bearers attempt a resonance; simultaneously request crown support from T0 and Discord's unconventional means from T4 (as a "fourth option" only, with someone holding the backstop).

**Scenario 2: Lost teams (T6 wilds + T5 mirror alarm at the same time).**
- **First reaction:** decide which timeline is tighter first; do not spread effort evenly.
- **Command actions:** T6's Zecora / Daring Do supply terrain and herbs; T5's Sunset confirms whether the mirror side has same-face-different-person interference; T1's Rainbow Dash runs the aerial search; Spike builds a contact ledger (last contact, location, capabilities).
- **Likely mistake:** mistaking a mirror-world "same face, different person" for the missing team; the search party goes missing too; cross-dimensional traffic read as "arrived".
- **Backstop:** if no result in 48 hours, escalate to L3, launch a full search and ask T0 to authorise cross-region action; tell the client "the possible worst case" at the same time.

**Scenario 3: Contained-asset anomaly (T3's red-team exercise goes out of bounds).**
- **First reaction:** freeze immediately; no negotiation, no explanation.
- **Command actions:** notify the Containment Oversight Board; T4's Starlight and T0's Luna jointly take over communications; T3's Tirek is monitored at a safe distance; Spike seals the archives for that period.
- **Likely mistake:** treating "red-team lines" as a real plan; letting Chrysalis's words affect team morale; over-reacting and causing panic.
- **Backstop:** re-authorise under P5; if overreach is confirmed, terminate red-team mode immediately and remove the members involved from this task pending a retrospective.
