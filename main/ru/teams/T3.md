# T3

> **Команда:** T3 | **База команды:** No fixed base: Grogar's lair / the badlands / Tartarus / petrified Canterlot; Sombra is linked to the old Crystal Empire palace | **Участники:** 5

## 0. Назначение (упрощённо)

Contained villains and risk assets: the three endgame villains (Chrysalis/Tirek/Cozy Glow) plus the Crystal Empire's former king Sombra, all serving as contained assets for red-team exercises, stress tests and villain-role-play performances; they take no part in normal field duty.
