> **Команда:** T0 | **База команды:** Canterlot · royal castle and palace; the Crystal Empire is the second pole | **Участники:** 9

## 0. Назначение (упрощённо)

The royal inner circle: legitimacy, diplomacy, national defence, night and dream intelligence, Crystal Empire affairs, protection of the royal heir. On-screen relationships bind the two co-ruling princesses, the Crystal Empire's co-ruling couple, the royal guard and the royal heir into one team.
