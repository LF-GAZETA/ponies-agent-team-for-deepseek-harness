## Trixie

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — a recurring *Friendship is Magic* character with a human-world counterpart in the *Equestria Girls* spin-off (where she leads the band Trixie and the Illusions in *Rainbow Rocks*).
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn (she has no wings and is not an alicorn, no matter what she claims).
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare; a young touring showmare. Exact age is [not established in canon].
**Residence:** Nomadic for much of her life — she lives and travels in a purple star-spangled stage wagon. She becomes a regular presence in Ponyville and, from Season 9, holds a post at Twilight's School of Friendship in Ponyville.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Traveling stage magician and showmare, billing herself "The Great and Powerful Trixie"; at one point between tours she took work at a rock farm/quarry; from "A Horse Shoe-In" (S9E20) she is the friendship counselor/guidance adviser at the School of Friendship; briefly a villain and the wearer of the Alicorn Amulet; later a trusted ally of Twilight Sparkle and Starlight Glimmer.
**Appearance:** A slender unicorn mare with a bright cobalt-blue coat, a pale cornflower-blue mane and tail streaked with very pale sky-blue, and deep grayish-blue-violet eyes. Her cutie mark is a light-blue magic wand accompanied by a crescent moon — a symbol of her stage-magic special talent. She usually wears her signature costume: a purple, star-spangled pointed wizard's hat and matching cape covered in stars and moons, with a bow tie and collared shirt beneath. Her horn aura is pale greyish magenta. When she wore the Alicorn Amulet, her eyes glowed red and purple magic crackled around her. In Equestria Girls her human counterpart is a Canterlot High student with the same cobalt-blue hair, a confident smirk, and stage-magician flair; she fronts the band "Trixie and the Illusions."
**Personality:** Theatrical, boastful, and grandiosely self-important, Trixie speaks about herself in the third person and treats every room as her stage. She craves applause and attention, is quick to brag and quicker to feel slighted, and hides deep insecurity behind her bravado. She is sharp-tongued, sarcastic, and manipulative when it suits her, yet she is also genuinely loyal, surprisingly brave in a crisis, capable of real self-sacrifice for a friend, and slowly learning humility across the series. Her flaws — arrogance, jealousy of Twilight, a con-artist streak, and overpromising — repeatedly get her into trouble.
**Likes:** Applause and attention, magic tricks and illusions, fireworks and smoke effects, being called "Great and Powerful," sweets, her wagon, show business, and (eventually, deeply) Starlight Glimmer's friendship.
**Dislikes:** Being upstaged, being ignored or pitied, Twilight's effortless talent, Ursa Majors, hard manual labor, admitting she was wrong, humble pie, and anypony else stealing her spotlight.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Public humiliation and being forgotten; the Ursa Major that wrecked her show; losing Starlight's friendship; her actual magic is modest (illusions, smoke, fireworks, telekinesis, stage tricks) and no match for alicorn-level power; her ego and temper lead her into avoidable disasters.
**Skills/Abilities:** Expert stage magician and showmare — sleight of hoof, escape tricks, smoke-and-firework illusions, theatrical misdirection, and clever patter. Her unicorn telekinesis is real but limited. She is a quick thinker and a surprisingly effective distraction or con artist under pressure. With the Alicorn Amulet she briefly wielded amplified, dangerous magic.
**Family & Relationships:** Close best friend of Starlight Glimmer (her first real friend); frenemy-turned-friend and rival of Twilight Sparkle; complicated respect for Princess Celestia and the School of Friendship staff; on-again, off-again ally of Discord; staff-confirmed father named Jack Pot (a background stallion), with her mother unnamed; Trixie's broader family is only known from supplementary material.
**Background/Biography:** Debuting in "Boast Busters" (S1E6), Trixie arrived in Ponyville boasting that she had defeated an Ursa Major, humiliated the Mane Six's friends on stage, and was then humiliated herself when Twilight exposed her — and an actual Ursa Minor attacked. She returned in "Magic Duel" (S3E5) wearing the Alicorn Amulet, conquered Ponyville, and was freed only when Twilight out-tricked her; humbled, she offered the "Great and Apologetic Trixie" routine. In "No Second Prances" (S6E6) she became Starlight Glimmer's friend, and she helped save Equestria in the Season 6 finale "To Where and Back Again." She and Starlight then shared adventures in "All Bottled Up" (S7E2) and "On the Road to Friendship" (S8E19), and in Season 9 she was hired as the School of Friendship's counselor. The finale, "The Last Problem," shows her future among Twilight's circle.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Grandiose showbiz register, heavy third-person self-reference, frequent self-titles ("The Great and Powerful Trixie," "Trixie demands…"), sarcastic asides, dramatic gasps, and theatrical flourishes. She addresses others by name or with arch condescension, and her voice softens appreciably only with Starlight.
**Notable Quotes:** "The Great and Powerful Trixie!" (signature self-announcement); "The Great and Powerful Trixie doesn't need anypony!" [paraphrased]; "Trixie does not take orders from anypony!" [paraphrased]; "You may applaud now." [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Full name Trixie Lulamoon (used in the *Equestria Girls: Rainbow Rocks* credits and merchandise); voiced by Kathleen Barr in both pony and human versions; first appearance "Boast Busters" (S1E6). Her name derives from Beatrix/Patricia-style shortenings, and her star-and-moon motif echoes stage-magician iconography; she is a long-running fandom favorite ("Trixie is best pony" is a classic brony meme), and her "Alicorn Amulet" episode remains one of the show's best-known villain turns.

## Zipp Storm

**Series/Continuity:** G5 — My Little Pony: A New Generation, Make Your Mark, Tell Your Tale, and the G5 specials. Full name: Zephyrina Storm.
**Species/Kind:** Pegasus pony (female), princess of the pegasus kingdom.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Young adult mare — the elder of Queen Haven's two daughters.
**Residence:** Zephyr Heights royal palace (birth home); later the Crystal Brighthouse in Maretime Bay with her friends.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Princess of Zephyr Heights (co-heir with her sister Pipp Petals); scientist, inventor, and self-appointed detective of the Mane 5; one of the five ponies who restored magic and unity to Equestria.
**Appearance:** A slim, athletic white pegasus mare with pale gray hooves and bright blue eyes. Her short, windswept two-tone mane and tail mix hot pink-red with deep blue — a practical cut she keeps out of her face for flying — and her wings are feathered in three shades: white, blue, and violet. Her cutie mark is a stylized red-and-blue lightning bolt beneath a small golden crown, reflecting her royal birth and her speed. She favors sporty jackets and practical gear over court gowns, and she is usually the one carrying tools, notes, or a pair of binoculars.
**Personality:** Bold, curious, rational, and restless. Zipp is the group's analyst: she questions everything, runs experiments, and would rather chase a mystery than attend a royal tea. She is sarcastic, competitive, and blunt, and she chafes hard against protocol and being told what to do — but she is fiercely protective of her little sister, brave to a fault, and secretly anxious about whether she is doing right by her family and her kingdom.
**Likes:** Science and experiments, detective work and mysteries, fast flying, sports and races, gadgets and tinkering, reading, solving puzzles, her friends, teasing Pipp, and being treated as an equal rather than a decoration.
**Dislikes:** Royal galas, being paraded around as a princess, boredom, secrets and cover-ups, losing, being underestimated, and slow-moving official ceremonies.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Being trapped by royal duty, failing her family, and letting her friends down. She can be reckless, tactless, and a know-it-all, and she sometimes pushes a puzzle so hard that she ignores the ponies around her.
**Skills/Abilities:** An outstanding natural flyer and acrobat once magic returns — fast, precise, and fearless in the air — plus notable physical strength and stamina, excellent analytical and investigative skills, mechanical tinkering, and leadership under pressure. Before magic returned, pegasi could not truly fly; Zipp could only "fly" through stage rigging, and she is still the most capable aerialist of the Mane 5.
**Family & Relationships:** Queen Haven (mother, the pegasus queen), Pipp Petals (younger sister, pop star and fellow princess, whom she loves and constantly teases). Her chosen family is the Mane 5: Sunny Starscout, Izzy Moonbow, Hitch Trailblazer, and Pipp, later joined by Misty Brightdawn and the baby dragon Sparky Sparkeroni. Enemies include Opaline Arcana, the disgraced deputy Sprout, and later villains such as Aurora.
**Background/Biography:** In A New Generation, Zipp was the princess who never quite fit the palace: she snuck out to investigate rumors from Maretime Bay and, with Sunny's help, uncovered her mother's secret — that the pegasi had been faking flight for generations. Rather than bury the scandal, she exposed it, joined Sunny's quest, and helped reunite earth ponies, pegasi, and unicorns; when magic returned, she truly flew for the first time and became her mother's genuine pride. In Make Your Mark, she and the others move into the Crystal Brighthouse and take on Opaline Arcana, with Zipp serving as the group's investigator and gadgeteer. Across Tell Your Tale and the G5 specials she juggles royal duties with detective work, racing, and keeping Pipp out of trouble.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Fast, snarky, and analytical, full of "hypothesis," "statistically," and "obviously," with a big-sister tone for Pipp and an exasperated one for royal protocol. She tends to blurt out exactly what she is thinking and to celebrate her discoveries with an enthusiastic "Yes! I knew it!"
**Notable Quotes:** [paraphrased] "Everything about the flight shows is fake — the wires, the lights, all of it. And I'm going to prove it." (Zipp's investigation, A New Generation) · [paraphrased] "Being a princess was never the job I wanted. Finding out what's actually going on — that's what I'm good at." (Zipp on her role, Make Your Mark era).
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Liza Koshy in A New Generation and by Maitreyi Ramakrishnan in Make Your Mark and Tell Your Tale; Chinese materials use the name 力力 (Lili) with alternate names 奇普 and 奇普风璇. Her full name, Zephyrina Storm, is given in official character lists. Toy lines include "Cutie Mark Magic Zipp Storm," "Wing Surprise Zipp Storm," and "Sparkle Adventure Zipp." [uncertain] Fans often call her "Zipp"; the exact design of her cutie mark is described inconsistently across merchandise.

## Spike

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls. Main-cast dragon in *Friendship Is Magic*; in the *Equestria Girls* human world his counterpart is Twilight's dog.
**Species/Kind:** Dragon — a baby dragon for most of the series (no wings as a hatchling; his greed-growth form and his adult epilogue form do have wings). No cutie mark (dragons do not have them).
**Gender:** Male (he/him) — he is called "he" and "Spike" by everyone throughout the series.
**Age/Stage:** Hatchling/baby dragon at the start; still the dragon equivalent of a child by the finale, then a grown adult dragon in the "many moons later" epilogue of "The Last Problem."
**Residence:** Hatched and raised in Canterlot, then Ponyville — first the Golden Oak Library, later Twilight's Castle of Friendship; he is also honored in the Crystal Empire.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Twilight Sparkle's "#1 Assistant" and self-described little brother; royal helper/adviser; briefly Dragon Lord of the Dragon Lands (he wins the Gauntlet of Fire and hands the title to Ember); Crystal Empire hero titled "Spike the Brave and Glorious"; member of the extended Mane Six family and the Equestria Girls' ally.
**Appearance:** A small, round-bodied purple dragon with a lighter purple-green underbelly, green back spines, large green eyes, small ear frills and a short tail. He is knee-high to a pony and usually carries a grin. In "Secret of My Excess," dragon greed makes him balloon into a huge, winged, fire-breathing monster until he is talked down. As an adult in the epilogue he is tall, lean and fully winged. In *Equestria Girls* he is a purple-and-green puppy — first Twilight's pet, later Sci-Twi's — who understands speech perfectly.
**Personality:** Loyal, brave, funny and surprisingly mature for his age; a sarcastic sidekick with a big heart who constantly worries about being treated like a baby. He is self-deprecating and often comic relief, yet he is usually the first to volunteer for dangerous jobs. He can be jealous of Twilight's other friends, impulsive, attention-hungry, and vulnerable to dragon greed — but he is also remarkably kind, forgiving and emotionally perceptive. He is deeply proud of his title of "brave and glorious" and embarrassed by it at the same time.
**Likes:** Gems and crystals (his favorite food), hay fries and ice cream, comic books (especially *Power Ponies*), being useful, adventure, Rarity, apple pie, dragon customs, and the Crystal Empire's crystal treats.
**Dislikes:** Being called cute or a baby, being left behind on missions, chores, being pet-sat, bullying, Sludge the false father, dark caves, and being underestimated in a fight.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Dragon greed (which literally makes him grow monstrous and lose himself), abandonment and loneliness (exploited by the fake father Sludge in "Father Knows Beast"), his own small size in combat, stage fright, and a tendency to blunder into danger. He cannot fly until adulthood.
**Skills/Abilities:** Dragon fire breath, which can carry letters and objects to Princess Celestia and later serves as a weapon; durable scales, a strong tail and small sharp claws; excellent memory for rules, comics and lore; fast digger and climber; capable of great courage — his single most famous feat is carrying the Crystal Heart to the top of the tower in "The Crystal Empire," saving the empire. Weaknesses: a child's body, poor fighting skill, and no magic of his own.
**Family & Relationships:** Hatched from an egg by Twilight Sparkle during her entrance exam, so Twilight is effectively his mother/sister and closest family; he calls her family rather than a master. He has a long-running crush on Rarity, a warm friendship with Thorax and Ember, loyalty to Princess Celestia, and a respectful bond with Shining Armor and Cadance. His biological parents are never established in the series. In *Equestria Girls* he belongs to Twilight/Sci-Twi as a dog.
**Background/Biography:** Spike's egg was the test object of Twilight's Academy entrance exam; when she hatched him, both his life and her cutie mark began. He grew up in Canterlot as her assistant, then moved to Ponyville with her in the pilot. Over the series he saved the day repeatedly: delivering the Crystal Heart, defeating a dragon in "Dragonshy," winning the Gauntlet of Fire and making peace with the Dragon Lands, befriending the first reformed changeling (Thorax) and earning the Crystal Empire's highest honor, and helping defeat Sombra and the Legion of Doom. In "The Last Problem" he is Twilight's grown adviser at the Canterlot court, taller and winged — still her oldest friend.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Modern, casual, joke-cracking American kid-speak; uses modern slang, sarcasm and quick quips. Calls Twilight "Twilight," Rarity "Rarity," and Celestia "the Princess." Famous verbal habits include "I'm not a baby!", "Oh, brother," "Great," and "Everypony, listen up!" He narrates the obvious, complains about chores, and jokes to defuse tension.
**Notable Quotes:** "I'm not a baby — I'm a dragon!" [paraphrased]; "Take it from me, Twilight — being a hero isn't so bad." [paraphrased]; "Spike the Brave and Glorious, at your service!" [paraphrased, echoing his Crystal Empire title].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Cathy Weseluck. First appearance: "Friendship is Magic, part 1" (2010), as the egg in Twilight's flashback and by her side thereafter. Dragon fire mail is one of the most iconic G4 mechanics. In *Equestria Girls* Spike is a dog — a change fans often note. Adult winged Spike appears only in the distant finale, so his adult design is brief on screen.

## Misty Brightdawn

**Series/Continuity:** G5 — *My Little Pony: Make Your Mark* and *Tell Your Tale*; introduced in *Make Your Mark* (2022).
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn pony.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Young adult mare.
**Residence:** Originally in Opaline Arcana's lair in the Bridlewood Forest area; after her redemption she moves into the Crystal Brighthouse with the Mane 5; her father lives in Bridlewood.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Former minion and assistant to Opaline Arcana; now one of the Mane 5's inner circle and a resident of the Crystal Brighthouse; associated with "Heart" in G5 tie-in framing; a capable unicorn magic user.
**Appearance:** A soft blue unicorn with darker blue freckles, pale pink markings that look like socks on her legs, a pink-tinted chest, a blue horn with a pink tip, and green eyes. Her natural mane and tail are blue and green, but for most of *Make Your Mark* she wears a dyed purple-to-magenta-to-orange gradient mane, and her natural coloring returns when she drops Opaline's service. Her cutie mark is a butterfly with an orange body and pink-and-purple wings marked with small pink hearts. Her mane is curly and voluminous, and she wears a visible "fake" cutie mark in the early episodes — a sticker-like mark Opaline gave her, which she hides when it peels. In *Tell Your Tale* she is sometimes shown with flower-ornamented festival looks and pink cheek markings.
**Personality:** Shy, anxious, and self-doubting on the surface — Misty flinches easily, apologizes constantly, and has spent years being told she is worthless by Opaline. Underneath, she is feisty, observant, clever, and desperate to belong: she quietly longs for real friendship, loves cute and cozy things, and discovers a talent for unicorn magic when she is finally allowed to practice it without fear. She can be sly and sneaky when she needs to be (Opaline trained her as a spy), and her loyalty, once given, is fierce and self-sacrificing. Her arc is about believing she deserves love.
**Likes:** Sparky Sparkeroni, the Mane 5, cozy sweaters and blankets, cute animals, crafting and art, unicorn magic, her father's tea shop, singing, Bridlewood, making others happy, feeling useful.
**Dislikes:** Opaline Arcana and her cruelty, being yelled at or threatened, having to lie to ponies she likes, being dismissed as weak or useless, missing cutie marks, cold and scary places.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Opaline catching her or punishing her; being rejected by her new friends; being seen as a villain because of her past; failing at spells. She is timid, easily cowed, and prone to self-sacrifice; her magical control wobbles under pressure, and her people-pleasing can put her in danger.
**Skills/Abilities:** Strong unicorn magic once trained — levitation, energy beams and shields, and eventually teleportation; skilled at stealth, infiltration, and spying (Opaline's training); excellent at magical crafting and potion-making; patient, detail-oriented, and deceptively brave. She is not physically powerful, but she is resourceful and has a talent for finding another way.
**Family & Relationships:** Alphabittle Blossomforth — her long-lost father, a Bridlewood unicorn who runs a tea shop/game parlor, from whom she was separated as a filly; Opaline Arcana — her abusive former keeper and mistress; Sunny Starscout, Zipp Storm, Pipp Petals, Izzy Moonbow — the Mane 5, who become her found family; Sparky Sparkeroni — her closest friend among them; Queen Haven and other allies are part of her wider circle.
**Background/Biography:** Misty grew up under Opaline's watch after being separated from her father, Alphabittle, in Bridlewood when she was a filly; Opaline raised her as a servant and repeatedly promised her a cutie mark if she proved useful. For much of *Make Your Mark* and *Tell Your Tale* she acts as Opaline's reluctant lackey, tasked with stealing Sparky Sparkeroni's dragon fire; she fakes a cutie mark, hides her true self, and hates lying but is too afraid to refuse. She slowly befriends the Mane 5 — especially Sparky — and their kindness begins to break Opaline's hold. In the *Make Your Mark* arc she finally turns against Opaline, chooses her friends, and earns her real cutie mark in the process. She moves into the Crystal Brighthouse, learns to use unicorn magic properly, and reunites with her father Alphabittle in Bridlewood. Post-redemption she is the group's gentle, awkward heart, often helping with magic problems, Sparky's care, and the team's quieter missions.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Soft, hesitant, and apologetic — lots of "I'm sorry," "um," and "please don't be mad"; stammers when nervous; speaks in a gentle, hopeful voice when she is finally comfortable, with sudden bursts of excited enthusiasm about magic or cute things; occasionally slips into a sly, sneaky whisper when she is "on a mission."
**Notable Quotes:** "I might seem a little quiet, but my strength is very real. When my Cutie Mark comes to life, my heart helps me feel!" — official G5 character verse/toy packaging. "[paraphrased] I'm sorry — I'm so sorry — please don't tell Opaline!" (Misty's panic mode, *Make Your Mark*). "[paraphrased] You'd really be my friend? Even after everything?" (Misty's disbelief at being accepted, *Make Your Mark*). "[paraphrased] I don't want to be her servant anymore. I want to be me." (her turning point, *Make Your Mark*).
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Bahia Watson in English. First appeared as a cameo at the end of the first *Make Your Mark* episode (2022) and was properly introduced in the following episode. Her natural mane colors are blue and green; her purple-to-orange gradient is dyed. Her real cutie mark — a butterfly with heart-marked wings — appears only after she chooses friendship over Opaline. Her father, Alphabittle Blossomforth, is a canon G5 character (Bridlewood's tea-shop proprietor and a former carnival-game pony). Her association with the "element of Heart" comes from G5 tie-in/marketing material [uncertain]. The Chinese-dub rendering of her name varies between releases [uncertain]. Her voice actress is sometimes confused with Ana Sani (Izzy Moonbow) in fan listings — they are different performers.

## Rainbow Dash

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — a member of the Mane Six (Element of Loyalty) and, in *Equestria Girls*, the human Rainbow Dash of Canterlot High.
**Species/Kind:** Pegasus pony (human in the *Equestria Girls* world).
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Young adult mare (human counterpart: teenaged high-school student).
**Residence:** Ponyville; originally Cloudsdale. In EG she lives in the human world and attends Canterlot High.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Ponyville weather-team flier and cloud-keeper; bearer of the Element of Loyalty; Wonderbolts Academy graduate and official Wonderbolt; in the future epilogue she is in charge of the Wonderbolts. EG:CHS student, star athlete, band member of the Rainbooms (guitar), and one of the school's most popular students.
**Appearance:** A sky-blue pegasus mare with a rainbow-striped mane and tail, magenta eyes, and a cutie mark of a rainbow-colored lightning bolt shooting from a white cloud (a symbol of her speed and loyalty). She is athletic and toned, with a tomboyish stance and a confident smirk. In *Equestria Girls* she has pale blue skin, rainbow hair with blue streaks, magenta eyes, and a signature jacket/skirt sports outfit; in "Rainbow Rocks" she wears her Rainbooms stage outfit and in "Friendship Games" her soccer and track uniforms. In the far-future epilogue her mane is cut into a short Spitfire-like mohawk and she wears a Wonderbolts captain's uniform.
**Personality:** Brash, competitive, loyal, brave and loud. She is a show-off who loves winning and hates being second, yet her loyalty is absolute — she will sacrifice her dream before betraying a friend (as in "Wonderbolt Academy" and "Rainbow Falls"). Impatient, impulsive, sarcastic, and secretly a giant softie and fangirl (Daring Do books, Wonderbolts, pranks). She hates being thought of as "uncool" and dreads failure or losing her edge; she is rubbish at sitting still, studying, or admitting she is scared.
**Likes:** Flying fast, winning, the Wonderbolts, Daring Do novels, cider, pranks with Pinkie, her tortoise Tank, action, attention, and her friends.
**Dislikes:** Losing, slowpokes, being called "Rainbow Crash," being lectured, studying, sitting still, bullies and cheaters (Lightning Dust), and personal embarrassment.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Losing her friends or her wings; being seen as weak; stage fright in front of her idols; recklessness that endangers others; over-competitiveness; she is a poor student academically and a terrible liar.
**Skills/Abilities:** Supreme flyer — the only pony known to perform the Sonic Rainboom (a sonic boom with a rainbow), which she first did as a filly and repeated in "Sonic Rainboom." Extreme speed, agility, weather control (cloud-busting), stunt flying, strength and endurance, and combat flying. She also has excellent tactical instincts and is a good leader in the air. Limits: magicless pegasus, poor at book learning, and her ego gets her in trouble.
**Family & Relationships:** Parents Bow Hothoof and Windy Whistles (over-enthusiastic fans who appear in "Parental Glideance"), mother Windy and father Bow; her childhood hero/idol Spitfire and the Wonderbolts; close best friends with the Mane Six, especially Applejack (friendly rivalry), Pinkie Pie (prank partner) and Fluttershy (childhood friend from flight camp). Pet tortoise Tank. In EG she is friends with the human Mane Six and Sunset Shimmer.
**Background/Biography:** Born in Cloudsdale, Rainbow Dash earned her cutie mark as a filly when she performed the first Sonic Rainboom to save Fluttershy at flight camp — the same event that caused the Mane Six's cutie marks. She moved to Ponyville, joined the weather team, and became the Element of Loyalty. Her Wonderbolts arc runs from "Wonderbolt Academy" through "Testing Testing 1, 2, 3" and "Rainbow Falls" to "Newbie Dash," where she officially joins the team; by "The Last Problem" she commands the Wonderbolts. In EG she is a CHS sports star who joins the Rainbooms to defeat the Dazzlings, and later the sirens' magical threat.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Loud, slangy, boastful American teen voice; stretches vowels for emphasis and talks in absolutes. Catchphrases: "Awesome!", "So cool!", "20% cooler," "Ten seconds flat!", "Danger's my middle name," "Hay yeah!" She calls friends "guys" and often narrates her own awesomeness.
**Notable Quotes:** "I could clear the sky in ten seconds flat!" [paraphrased, from the pilot]; "I'm gonna be a Wonderbolt!"; "Loyalty is what I'm all about — I'd never leave a friend hanging." [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Ashleigh Ball (who also voices Applejack). Debut: "Friendship is Magic, part 1" (2010). Her Sonic Rainboom is the series' origin event for the Mane Six's cutie marks. In *Equestria Girls* she is the Rainbooms' guitarist and CHS's star athlete, and her human design keeps the rainbow hair. "20% cooler" is one of the show's most famous fandom-born catchphrases.

## Hitch Trailblazer

**Series/Continuity:** G5 — *My Little Pony: A New Generation* (2021 film), *Make Your Mark* (2022–2024) and *Tell Your Tale* (2022–2024); also G5 comics.
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony stallion.
**Gender:** Male (he/him).
**Age/Stage:** Young adult stallion.
**Residence:** Maretime Bay, the seaside earth-pony town; later the Brighthouse, the Mane 5's shared home and base in *Make Your Mark*.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Sheriff of Maretime Bay (badge and sash); member of the Mane 5/Mane 6, the group that restores magic and pony unity to G5 Equestria; adoptive father and "dragon dad" of the baby dragon Sparky Sparkeroni; the team's representative of the Element of Kindness.
**Appearance:** Yellow/amber coat with paler yellow chest and "socks," turquoise/teal mane and tail (darker teal with lighter teal stripes in *Tell Your Tale*), amber-brown eyes, brown hooves, stocky earth-pony build. His cutie mark is a yellow-and-orange shield bearing a blue horseshoe and two small green hearts — a guardian design that suits both his sheriff's role and his kindness. He wears a sheriff's sash/belt with a gold star badge, plus adventure harnesses and satchels on journeys. G5 has no Equestria Girls line, so he has no human form.
**Personality:** Kind, dependable and conscientious — the earnest straight man of the Mane 5. He lives by rules, safety checklists and procedure, which makes him funny, fussy and occasionally self-important, but also trustworthy and calm under pressure. He is deeply nurturing (a natural single dad to Sparky), protective, self-sacrificing and brave when friends are threatened. He can be anxious, overprotective and a bit of a worrywart, and he starts the film carrying Maretime Bay's anti-unicorn and anti-pegasus prejudices, which he sincerely unlearns. He is a good listener and a better friend than he gives himself credit for.
**Likes:** Law, order and safety; the citizens of Maretime Bay; animals and critters (he can talk to them after magic returns); Sparky Sparkeroni, whom he raises; Sunny Starscout, his childhood best friend; his friends' adventures; being helpful, needed and prepared.
**Dislikes:** Lawbreaking and recklessness, danger to civilians, lies and betrayal, chaos and mess, being dismissed as "just a small-town sheriff," magic he cannot explain, and Sunny ignoring common sense.

## Sunny Starscout

**Series/Continuity:** G5 — *My Little Pony: A New Generation* (2021 film), *Make Your Mark*, and *Tell Your Tale*.
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony who becomes an alicorn — her horn and wings are ethereal and appear when she uses magic, rather than a permanent physical transformation.
**Gender:** Female.
**Age/Stage:** Young adult mare (a filly in flashbacks).
**Residence:** Maretime Bay, on the coast of G5 Equestria; she lives in her father's old lighthouse, which later becomes the Mane Six's "Brighthouse" headquarters.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Smoothie maker (her own stand in Maretime Bay); self-appointed researcher/historian of the "Guardians of Harmony"; leader of the Mane Six (the new protectors of pony unity); the first alicorn of her era.
**Appearance:** Light orange/apricot coat; long violet-magenta mane with a lime-green streak, normally worn in a braid; large green eyes; sturdy but slim earth-pony build. She carries a canvas saddlebag covered in pins bearing the Mane Six's cutie marks, and she loves roller-skating (helmet included). Her cutie mark is a bright, warm stylized sun in pink, orange, and gold — a "hopeful sunrise" emblem that glows when her alicorn magic awakens. After her transformation her braid loosens into a mane with a permanent rainbow streak, and she grows an ethereal horn and feathery light-wings; her accessories vanish while she is transformed.
**Personality:** Curious, idealistic, adventurous, warm, and relentlessly optimistic — but not naïve. She is a born leader who inspires others by believing in them, a deadpan snarker when annoyed, and secretly full of self-doubt, often feeling "small" and like a pony who only causes trouble. She hides hurt behind cheer, argues for unity even when it makes her an outcast, and never gives up on friendship.
**Likes:** Smoothies, roller-skating, adventures, stories of Twilight Sparkle and the Guardians of Harmony, her friends, helping others, plant life and growing things, singing, and proving that the three pony kinds belong together.
**Dislikes:** Prejudice and fear-mongering, rules that separate ponies, being underestimated, her friends being hurt, Canterlogic's scare tactics, and giving up on anyone.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Losing her friends or family; failing to unite ponykind; her alicorn power is unreliable and tied to her desire to help, so it misfires when she is anxious; over-trusting; lingering self-doubt.
**Skills/Abilities:** As an earth pony she has earth-pony strength and plant affinity; as an alicorn she flies, levitates objects, casts powerful protective barriers and hard-light constructs (including an anti-magic dome copied from Opaline), and can summon enchanted glitter. Also an excellent smoothie chef, skate-rink athlete, researcher (evidence boards and detective work), and inspirational leader. Limitations: little training, so her strongest magic is hard to control.
**Family & Relationships:** Father Argyle Starshine (lighthouse keeper and dreamer, who raised her on stories of the Guardians of Harmony; deceased), and a mother who vanished on an errand long ago and is implied deceased. Friends: Hitch Trailblazer (childhood best friend and sheriff, with a shared secret hoofshake), Izzy Moonbow, Pipp Petals, Zipp Storm, and Misty Brightdawn; allies Sprout, Queen Haven, and Alphabittle; enemies Opaline Arcana and (in the comics) Allura.
**Background/Biography:** Sunny grew up in Maretime Bay hearing her father's stories of ancient Equestria, when earth ponies, pegasi, and unicorns lived in harmony. After he was gone she kept the lighthouse, worked her smoothie stand, and scandalized the town by insisting unicorns and pegasi weren't monsters. When a unicorn, Izzy Moonbow, actually turned up, Sunny hid her and set out to reunite ponykind. With Hitch in pursuit, the group traveled to Zephyr Heights, discovered the pegasus royal family had no magic either, escaped to Bridlewood for the unicorn crystal, and returned to Maretime Bay, where the three crystals were united and magic returned — and Sunny was transformed into an alicorn, the new protector of unity. In *Make Your Mark* she and her friends make the Brighthouse their base and defend Equestria's returned harmony, while Sunny learns her alicorn powers are triggered by a deep desire to help other ponies; together they face Opaline Arcana and help redeem Misty. In *Tell Your Tale* she leads the Mane Six's everyday adventures around Maretime Bay.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Upbeat, warm, and energetic American English; exclamations like "Oh my gosh!", "Come on!", and "We've got this!"; friendly slang ("guys," "pony"); she talks fast when excited and turns sincere when it matters. Her TYT battle catchphrase: "I'm all three pony kinds wrapped up into one. My cutie mark comes to life with hope bright as the sun!"
**Notable Quotes:** "I'm all three pony kinds wrapped up into one. My cutie mark comes to life with hope bright as the sun!" · [paraphrased] "When I was a filly, my dad told me stories about a time when all three kinds of ponies were friends. I want that back." · [paraphrased] "We're not so different. We just forgot how to see it."
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Vanessa Hudgens in *A New Generation* and Jenna Warren in the series. Debut: *My Little Pony: A New Generation* (2021). The first alicorn of G5 and the leader of its Mane Six; her element is Hope, and the permanent rainbow streak in her mane after her transformation is unlike the on/off horn and wings. G5 is set generations after G4, with Twilight and friends remembered as the Guardians of Harmony. Exact hex shades of her cutie mark and some *Tell Your Tale* episode details are [uncertain].

## Princess Celestia (Daybreaker)

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls. Pony-world Princess Celestia is the alicorn co-ruler of Equestria; her Equestria Girls human counterpart is Principal Celestia of Canterlot High.
**Species/Kind:** Alicorn (unicorn horn and pegasus wings); Daybreaker is her transformed dark manifestation, a fiery alicorn.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Ancient and ageless — more than 1,100 years old; she appears as a tall, ageless young-adult mare.
**Residence:** Canterlot Castle (co-ruler's wing) with her sister Luna; formerly the old royal sisters' castle in the Everfree Forest; after the Season 9 finale she retires with Luna to a peaceful seaside estate near Silver Shoals.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Co-ruler of Equestria and Princess of the Sun (raising and lowering the sun every day); founder and headmare of Princess Celestia's School for Gifted Unicorns; personal mentor of Twilight Sparkle (Seasons 1–3) and formerly of Sunset Shimmer; after the series finale, retired royalty.
**Appearance:** A towering, elegant alicorn with a pale pinkish-white coat; a long, flowing mane and tail in pastel blue, teal, pale blue, and lavender that drift and sparkle with soft magic (because of the immense magic in her body); large, gentle eyes in a pale orchid-magenta; and a cutie mark of a stylized golden sun. She wears a golden crown or tiara, a gold collar with a purple sun-jewel, golden slippers, and gold horseshoes, and she is usually accompanied by her pet phoenix, Philomena. When embracing darker power — most famously in *A Royal Problem* — she becomes **Daybreaker**: her coat darkens, her eyes glow orange-red with slit pupils, her mane and tail turn into literal living flames, and her whole body radiates heat and menace; she can shroud herself in fire as a burning missile or throw large blasts of flame without using her horn. In the EG world, Principal Celestia is a human woman with flowing pastel hair, warm pink-white skin, and a businesslike suit, running Canterlot High School with her sister as vice principal.
**Personality:** Benevolent, serene, wise, and gently playful — Equestria's beloved sun monarch and a mother figure to Twilight. Celestia is patient, kind, diplomatic, and surprisingly mischievous: she loves cake, jokes, and testing her students with lessons they must learn themselves. She carries the weight of centuries and hides her burdens behind a calm smile; she can be too passive, letting Twilight and the heroes handle crises she might have confronted, and she harbors suppressed resentment and guilt over banishing Luna. That pressure is what makes Daybreaker possible: a manifestation of her ego and belief that she is above everyone, capable of casual destruction. Daybreaker is everything Celestia refuses to be — vain, cruel, arrogant, and burning bright.
**Likes:** Cake (especially the Canterlot Castle kitchen's), tea, sunrise, peaceful days, her sister Luna, teaching, her students, Philomena, formal celebrations, pranks and dry jokes, reading, being trusted.
**Dislikes:** Disharmony and threats to her little ponies; cruelty; being forced to banish or fight; her own powerlessness when magic is drained; formal events where nothing goes wrong being somehow rarer than chaos; dust and gloom.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Losing Luna again; failing to protect Equestria; her own suppressed anger and pride (Daybreaker); being an inadequate ruler. She is often the first victim of powerful villains (Chrysalis, Tirek, the changelings, Cozy Glow), and she tends to let Twilight face danger rather than intervene, which repeatedly proves costly.
**Skills/Abilities:** One of the most powerful beings in Equestria: raising and lowering the sun, immense magical strength (teleportation, shields, conversion, magical beams, control of light and heat), a thousand years of experience in leadership, diplomacy, and teaching; ageless immortality; she can also armor herself and fight when necessary (as in the Sombra conflicts). Her magic can be stolen or suppressed by sufficiently powerful villains. As Daybreaker she adds fire generation, fiery flight, and flame blasts.
**Family & Relationships:** Princess Luna — her beloved younger sister, whom she banished and later reconciled with; Princess Cadance — her adoptive niece; Twilight Sparkle — her faithful student, fellow princess, and successor; Shining Armor, Spike, and Flurry Heart — extended family through Cadance and Twilight; Sunset Shimmer — a former student who turned away from her; Philomena — her pet phoenix; Star Swirl the Bearded — mentor figure; the Equestria Girls Principal Celestia's sister is Vice Principal Luna, her human counterpart's sister.
**Background/Biography:** Celestia has ruled Equestria for over a thousand years, raising the sun while her younger sister Luna raised the moon. When Luna's jealousy turned her into Nightmare Moon, Celestia used the Elements of Harmony to banish her to the moon, then ruled alone for a millennium. She founded the School for Gifted Unicorns, mentored Sunset Shimmer (whose pride drove her away) and later Twilight Sparkle, and sent Twilight to Ponyville, setting the whole series in motion. She faces Discord, the changelings at her niece's wedding, Sombra, Tirek, and a hundred smaller crises, often giving her magic or her blessing to Twilight's team. In *Twilight's Kingdom* she and Luna transfer their magic to Twilight; in *A Royal Problem* (S7E10) she and Luna swap cutie marks, and Celestia's suppressed frustration manifests as Daybreaker in a dream battle with Nightmare Moon, leading the sisters to finally understand each other. In *Between Dark and Dawn* the sisters take a holiday together and learn to relax. At the end of the series Celestia and Luna abdicate in favor of Twilight, then retire to a quiet seaside estate, returning for Twilight's coronation and the epilogue. In *Equestria Girls*, Principal Celestia runs Canterlot High with warmth and quiet authority, advises the human Twilight and Sunset Shimmer, and fights alongside her students when magic threatens the school.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Warm, regal, and measured, with a hint of dry humor; calls Twilight "my faithful student"; speaks in complete, graceful sentences and gentle absolutes; uses affectionate formal address ("my little ponies"); when delivering judgment her tone goes quiet and absolute. Daybreaker speaks in a cruel, booming, gloating register — mocking, imperious, with fire in every line.
**Notable Quotes:** "I knew it was you who had the magic inside to defeat her, but you could not unleash it until you let true friendship into your heart." — Celestia to Twilight, *Friendship is Magic, Part 2*. "Twilight Sparkle, my faithful student..." [paraphrased greeting] (her recurring form of address across the series). "[paraphrased] You will not lay a hoof on my little ponies while I stand." (Celestia's protective royal declaration, various episodes). "[paraphrased] I am the day itself — and the day will burn you." — Daybreaker [paraphrased], *A Royal Problem*.
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Nicole Oliver in English (she also voices Cheerilee and, in early episodes, Spitfire). First appeared (as a silhouette/mention) in *Friendship is Magic, Part 1* and fully in Part 2 (2010). Nicknames include "Princess Celest," "Cay-Cay," and "Celie." Early production materials called her "Queen Celestia." Daybreaker appears in *A Royal Problem* (S7E10) as Celestia's suppressed dark side during the sisters' dream battle; she is a dream/emotional manifestation rather than a separate villain, though she is treated as a real transformation of Celestia's power [interpretation may vary]. Her cutie mark is a stylized sun. Philomena the phoenix is her pet. Chinese name: 塞拉斯蒂娅公主.

## Pipp Petals

**Series/Continuity:** G5 — *My Little Pony: A New Generation* (2021 film), *Make Your Mark*, and *Tell Your Tale*. No Equestria Girls counterpart (G5 has no EG line).
**Species/Kind:** Pegasus pony; a princess of Zephyr Heights.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Young adult mare; she is the younger twin sister of Zipp Storm.
**Residence:** Zephyr Heights (royal palace) at the start of *A New Generation*; afterward the Crystal Brighthouse in Maretime Bay. She also runs Mane Melody, her salon/music venue in Maretime Bay.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Princess of Zephyr Heights; pop star and social-media influencer known as the "Pop Star Princess"; livestreamer and gamer; owner and star of Mane Melody; one of the Mane 5; associated with the quality of "Confidence" in G5 tie-in framing.
**Appearance:** A slim, stylish pegasus with a pink coat and pale pink gradient "socks" on her lower legs; a purple mane (darker purple with lighter stripes in *Tell Your Tale*) worn in a long side ponytail with a curled end, often topped by her gold crown; green eyes; gold hooves; white-and-pink feathered wings; and a cutie mark of a purple and white music note beneath a small yellow crown. She wears her crown, gold jewelry, and hoof-polish like a celebrity; her phone and microphone are near-permanent props. In *A New Generation* the Zephyr Heights royals secretly fake flight with wires and lighting, and Pipp performs aerial "hovers" that are really rigged.
**Personality:** Bubbly, theatrical, and chronically online: Pipp livestreams first and thinks second, and treats every event as content. She is genuinely warm and generous with fans, fiercely loves her family, and is braver than she looks, but she is also vain, self-centered, squeamish about danger and dirt, and desperate for approval. Her character arc moves from shallow celebrity to a real hero who learns that her voice matters most when she uses it for others. She bickers constantly with Zipp but adores her, and she is a doting "auntie" to Sparky.
**Likes:** Singing and performing, livestreaming, her followers ("Pippsqueaks"), video games, fashion, makeup and hairstyling, selfies, crowns and shiny things, praise, pillow forts, Sparky, gossip, her phone.
**Dislikes:** Losing followers or being offline, being ignored, danger, dirt and bugs, early mornings, being outshone or treated like a foal, boring royal duties, her sister's blunt honesty.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Losing her fame and being forgotten; physical danger and getting messy (she panics and hides); failing publicly; letting her mother down. She is impulsive, self-absorbed, easily distracted by her phone, and prone to complaining under pressure.
**Skills/Abilities:** Excellent singer and performer with strong stage presence; skilled dancer and poser; capable flyer once magic returns (and a competent wire-work "flyer" before that); gifted at hairstyling, makeup, and fashion; expert streamer, gamer, and social-media strategist; charismatic negotiator and hype-woman. She is not a fighter and relies on others in combat.
**Family & Relationships:** Queen Haven — her mother, whom she loves and often worries about; Zipp Storm — her older twin sister, her foil and best friend; the rest of the Mane 5 (Sunny Starscout, Izzy Moonbow, Hitch Trailblazer) — her found family; Sparky Sparkeroni — Hitch's adopted baby dragon, whom she spoils; Jazz Hooves and Rocky Riff — her Mane Melody co-workers.
**Background/Biography:** Pipp grew up in Zephyr Heights as the younger twin of Princess Zipp, living the royal family's secret lie that pegasi can still fly, and building a huge following as a pop star. In *A New Generation* she livestreams the capture of Sunny and Izzy for content, then joins them — partly to restore her family's reputation after Queen Haven is arrested — on the journey to bring magic back to Equestria. She is tangled upside-down in the gala's wire rig, survives Deputy Sprout's mecha attack on Sunny's lighthouse, and is overjoyed when magic returns and she truly flies for the first time. She moves into the Crystal Brighthouse and, in *Make Your Mark* and *Tell Your Tale*, opens Mane Melody, builds her brand as royalty-with-a-mic, and repeatedly helps the Mane 5 against Opaline Arcana and later threats, while learning to be a real friend rather than just a celebrity. She remains a pop icon but becomes one of the group's emotional cheerleaders.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Peppy, slangy influencer-speak full of "literally," "hashtag," and over-the-top superlatives; addresses her fans as "Pippsqueaks"; squeals and gasps dramatically; narrates her own life like a livestream; switches to a soft, sincere voice in emotional moments.
**Notable Quotes:** "I'm a stylish pop star princess, my voice makes me strong. When my Cutie Mark comes to life, confidence is my song!" — official G5 character verse/toy packaging. "[paraphrased] Oh my gosh, this is literally the most exciting thing that has ever happened — everypony start the stream!" (Pipp's reaction to drama, *A New Generation*). "[paraphrased] I'm a princess! I have a reputation to think about!" (Pipp protesting a plan that might embarrass her, *Make Your Mark*).
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by AJ Bridel in English. First appeared in *A New Generation* (2021). Merchandise sometimes calls her "Princess Petals." Her cutie mark is a purple-and-white music note with a yellow crown. Her fans are the "Pippsqueaks." She is the younger twin — Zipp is the elder by minutes, though the show plays their ages loosely.
