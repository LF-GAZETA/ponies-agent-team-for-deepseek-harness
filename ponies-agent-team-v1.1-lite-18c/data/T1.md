## Fluttershy

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — Mane Six member (Element of Kindness); the human Fluttershy of Canterlot High in the EG films.
**Species/Kind:** Pegasus pony (human in *Equestria Girls*).
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare (EG: teenaged high-school student).
**Residence:** Ponyville — a cozy cottage at the edge of the Everfree Forest, surrounded by animal pens and burrows; in EG, the human world.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Animal caretaker and animal expert; bearer of the Element of Kindness; founder and operator of her own animal sanctuary ("Fluttershy Leans In"), which she continues to run in the future with Discord's help; member of the Mane Six. In EG: CHS student and Rainbooms member (tambourine/vocals).
**Appearance:** A delicate pale-yellow pegasus mare with a long, flowing light-pink mane and tail, big teal-cyan eyes, and a cutie mark of three pink butterflies. She is smaller and softer-looking than the other Mane Six, with a gentle, slightly hunched posture and wings she often keeps folded. In "Bridle Gossip" she is cursed with a deep bass "Flutterguy" voice; in "Hurricane Fluttershy" her tiny wings are a plot point. In *Equestria Girls* she has pale skin, long pink hair, teal eyes, and soft, nature-inspired outfits; her Rainbooms look is a butterfly-themed dress.
**Personality:** Sweet, timid, gentle and profoundly kind — Fluttershy is the softest heart in Equestria, and also one of its bravest. She is shy to the point of anxiety, hates crowds, loud noise and being the center of attention, and apologizes constantly; she can be a doormat and struggles with assertiveness. But when an animal or friend is threatened she becomes fiercely determined, using her legendary "Stare" to cow monsters. She is patient, empathetic, a good listener, and quietly wise.
**Likes:** Animals of every kind, quiet mornings, tea, knitting and sewing, nature, her friends, caring for creatures, soft singing, and Angel Bunny (her bossy pet rabbit).
**Dislikes:** Loud noises, crowds, public speaking, confrontation, cruelty or neglect toward animals, being rushed, being called a coward, and seeing her friends fight.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Stage fright and public speaking; flying and heights (she is a weak flier); being pushed around; her own temper when it finally erupts; letting others down. She is physically the least strong of the Mane Six, easily frightened, and too willing to put others' needs above her own.
**Skills/Abilities:** Peerless animal care and communication — she can nurse, tame and understand creatures from bunnies to dragons; the "Stare," a piercing glare that stops monsters in their tracks; expert first aid/veterinary skill; sewing and knitting; a beautiful singing voice; flying (weak but improving, as in "Hurricane Fluttershy"). Limits: poor combatant, no offensive magic, fragile nerves.
**Family & Relationships:** Parents Mr. and Mrs. Shy, and a younger brother, Zephyr Breeze (a lazy but good-hearted slacker). Her pet rabbit Angel Bunny is her constant companion; Discord is her closest non-pony friend (the two share a special bond, and he helps run her sanctuary in the future). Her best friends are the Mane Six; she is especially close to Rainbow Dash (childhood friend) and Rarity (spa buddy).
**Background/Biography:** As a filly in Cloudsdale, Fluttershy was a weak flier; during a race she fell from the sky and was saved by the blast of Rainbow Dash's first Sonic Rainboom, which also scattered butterflies around her and gave her her cutie mark. She moved to Ponyville, built her animal cottage, and became the Element of Kindness. Her key episodes include "Dragonshy" (staring down a dragon), "Stare Master," "Putting Your Hoof Down" (learning assertiveness from Iron Will), "Hurricane Fluttershy" (finding her flight), "Fluttershy Leans In" (building her animal sanctuary), and "Fake It 'Til You Make It" (assertiveness in business). In "The Last Problem" she runs her sanctuary with Discord's help.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Very soft, breathy and hesitant, often trailing off; frequent "Um...", "Oh my...", "I'm sorry," and "If that's okay..." She whispers more than she speaks, and her rare bursts of assertiveness are all the more striking. Her canonical catchphrase is "Oh my." She also hums and sings gently to her animals.
**Notable Quotes:** "Oh my."; "I'm not afraid of you!" [paraphrased, from her Stare moments]; "All any creature needs is a little kindness." [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Andrea Libman (who also voices Pinkie Pie). Debut: "Friendship is Magic, part 1" (2010). The "Stare" is one of the show's most iconic running gags. Her deep-voice alter ego from "Bridle Gossip" is a fan favorite. Her human counterpart in EG is a Rainbooms member and animal lover. Her animal sanctuary ("Fluttershy Leans In") is a major late-series development.

## Rarity

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — Mane Six member (Element of Generosity); the human Rarity of Canterlot High in the EG films.
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn pony (human in *Equestria Girls*).
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare (EG: teenaged high-school student).
**Residence:** Ponyville — Carousel Boutique; second boutique "Rarity for You" in Manehattan (S6); in the future a new boutique in Yakyakistan. EG: the human world / Canterlot High.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Fashion designer, seamstress, gem hunter and owner of Carousel Boutique; bearer of the Element of Generosity; member of the Mane Six; in EG a CHS fashionista, Rainbooms keytarist, and organizer of school events and outfits.
**Appearance:** A graceful ivory-white unicorn mare with a curled, bouncing indigo-purple mane and tail, large blue eyes with violet eyeshadow, and a cutie mark of three blue diamonds (the gems she discovered with her magic). She carries herself like a runway model and is always immaculately groomed. Her mane is styled in a high, rounded coiffure with a curl. In EG she has pale skin, purple curled hair, blue eyes, diamond earrings, and a wardrobe of fashionable outfits; her Rainbooms stage outfit is a sparkling sequined dress and she plays keytar (a keyboard) in the band. In the future epilogue she has grey streaks but the same hairstyle.
**Personality:** Generous, dramatic, driven, elegant and hilariously vain. Rarity is a perfectionist artist: generous to a fault, fiercely loyal to her friends, and prone to theatrical meltdowns ("the worst possible thing!"). She is a social climber who adores high society, gossip and admiration, can be snobbish about taste, and occasionally lets jealousy or status anxiety get the better of her — but she always returns to generosity. She is organized, entrepreneurial, a natural project leader, and deeply romantic.
**Likes:** Haute couture, gems, silk and fine fabric, the spa, tea, elegant parties, opera and romance, compliments, her cat Opalescence, her little sister, and being creative.
**Dislikes:** Dirt, mud, cheap fabric, bad manners, being outdone or copied, wearing the same outfit twice, being ignored, and anyone insulting her designs or her friends.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Public humiliation, being ordinary or untalented, creative block, losing her business, and — most of all — the "worst possible thing.". She is physically soft, easily exhausted, can be snobbish and selfish under stress, and is a compulsive over-worker.
**Skills/Abilities:** Unicorn telekinesis; a gem-finding spell that is the core of her cutie-mark talent; fine-grained sewing and design magic that lets her create entire outfits in minutes; dressmaking, fashion design, business management, etiquette, diplomacy, singing, and keytar in EG. Limits: weak in combat magic, fragile, and dramatic under pressure.
**Family & Relationships:** Younger sister Sweetie Belle; parents Hondo Flanks and Cookie Crumbles (who appear in "Sisterhooves Social" and later episodes); pet cat Opalescence. Spike nurses a long crush on her; Prince Blueblood (a disastrous date), Trenderhoof (her brief infatuation) and Fancy Pants are romantic/comedy interests. Her best friends are the Mane Six; she mentors Sweetie Belle and later designs for royalty.
**Background/Biography:** In "The Cutie Mark Chronicles," young Rarity's magic dragged her to a giant jewel and she realized her talent — finding gems and making beautiful clothes — while designing costumes for a school play. She built Carousel Boutique into Ponyville's fashion hub, became the Element of Generosity, and went on to dress Canterlot's elite, open a Manehattan shop in "The Saddle Row Review," and design Twilight's coronation gown. In EG she is the human Rainbooms' stylist and keytar player and helps defeat the Dazzlings; by "The Last Problem" she has opened a boutique in Yakyakistan and remains the group's style icon.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Theatrical, refined, slightly mid-Atlantic diction; dramatic gasps, "darling," "Oh my stars!", "How dreadful!", "This is simply the WORST. POSSIBLE. THING!", "Fabulous!" She over-enunciates and uses fashion-world vocabulary; when panicked she babbles in escalating superlatives.
**Notable Quotes:** "This is the worst possible thing!"; "Oh my stars, darling, that is simply fabulous!" [paraphrased]; "Generosity is my element, after all." [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Tabitha St. Germain, with singing voice by Kazumi Evans. Debut: "Friendship is Magic, part 1" (2010). Her three-diamond cutie mark doubles as her gem-finding talent. In EG she is the Rainbooms' keytarist. Her cat Opalescence is a recurring gag. Fans often call her the show's most quotable character.

## Princess Luna (Nightmare Moon)

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls. Pony-world Princess Luna is an alicorn co-ruler of Equestria; her Equestria Girls counterpart is Vice Principal Luna of Canterlot High, a human.
**Species/Kind:** Alicorn (unicorn horn and pegasus wings); Nightmare Moon is the same pony transformed by dark magic — a shadowed alicorn.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Ancient and ageless — over a thousand years old; an eternal young-adult appearance. Appears as a young alicorn in flashbacks and comics.
**Residence:** Canterlot Castle (co-ruler's wing); formerly the old royal sisters' castle in the Everfree; exiled on the moon for 1,000 years; after the Season 9 finale she retires with Celestia to a seaside estate near Silver Shoals.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Co-ruler of Equestria and Princess of the Night (until the Season 9 abdication); raiser and guardian of the moon and stars; guardian of the dream realm (the only pony who can enter and shape dreams); commander of the night guard; former personal student of Star Swirl the Bearded. Retired at the end of the series.
**Appearance:** A tall, slender alicorn with a deep indigo-blue coat; a long, flowing mane and tail in soft royal blue and gray-lavender that drift as if weightless and sparkle with points of starlight; soft teal eyes with a cool, luminous look. Her cutie mark is a dark purple-backed crescent moon. She wears a black-and-blue crown/tiara shaped with a crescent moon, a dark collar with a crescent pendant, and dark horseshoes. In her early Season 1 design she had a brighter sky-blue mane and a lighter gray-periwinkle coat, which was later refined. As **Nightmare Moon** her coat turns near-black, her mane and tail become a bright, wildly flowing periwinkle and starry black, her eyes become slit-pupiled teal with a predatory glow, her teeth grow fangs, and she wears a spiked silver-blue helmet/crown and dark armor; she is taller and more imposing, and can manipulate her mane like a living tornado, use it to snare and phase through things, and hurl lightning from it. In the EG world her counterpart, Vice Principal Luna, is a human woman with blue-black hair, dark skin, and formal school attire.
**Personality:** Luna is proud, formal, emotionally intense, and deeply dutiful — a night monarch with a poet's soul and a warrior's temper. After her redemption she is kind, protective, and surprisingly playful, but she never fully outgrows her insecurity: she remembers being ignored and unloved while Celestia was adored, and fears slipping back into bitterness. She is awkward with modern pony customs, overcompensates with old-fashioned grandeur, and is more emotionally direct than her sister. She is excellent with children and frightened ponies, using dreams to soothe them. Nightmare Moon is her shadow self: grand, wounded, furious, and convinced that only eternal night will make her loved.
**Likes:** The night sky, the moon and stars, dreams, quiet, her sister Celestia, foals and their dreamscapes, stories and poetry, Nightmare Night (once she learns to enjoy it), being recognized and appreciated, traditional ceremony.
**Dislikes:** Being forgotten or overshadowed, being called "the Mare in the Moon," modern slang and casual manners, noisy daytime crowds, being reprimanded, having her old form mocked or feared.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Being unloved and forgotten — the very wound that created Nightmare Moon; losing control of her dark side; failing her subjects; her guilt over the past. She is proud, easily embarrassed, socially clumsy, and can be manipulated through her desire for recognition. Like all alicorns she can be drained of magic by powerful villains.
**Skills/Abilities:** Extremely powerful alicorn magic: raising and lowering the moon and stars, night-time illumination, dream-walking and dream manipulation (entering, shaping, and defending dreams), teleportation, shields, lightning and energy blasts, illusion and transformation (including resuming her Nightmare Moon form at will), and combat magic. Immortal and ageless; a capable commander and royal diplomat; skilled at public ritual.
**Family & Relationships:** Princess Celestia — her elder sister, co-ruler, and the one who banished her, now reconciled; Princess Cadance — adoptive niece; Twilight Sparkle — fellow princess, former student of Celestia, and friend; Flurry Heart — adoptive great-niece; Star Swirl the Bearded — mentor figure; Pipsqueak and the foals of Ponyville — her first modern friends; the Mane Six — the bearers who redeemed her. No romantic partner is established in canon.
**Background/Biography:** As a young alicorn Luna ruled alongside her elder sister, raising the moon while Celestia raised the sun. Over centuries she grew bitter as ponies slept through her beautiful nights and praised only her sister; the jealousy and loneliness curdled into dark magic, and she became Nightmare Moon, vowing to bring eternal night. Celestia used the Elements of Harmony to banish her to the moon for 1,000 years. Nightmare Moon returned during the Summer Sun Celebration, captured Celestia, and was defeated by Twilight Sparkle and her friends, who used the Elements to purify her back into Luna. In Season 2's *Luna Eclipsed* she returns to Ponyville for Nightmare Night, frightening everyone with her old-fashioned royal manner before Pipsqueak and Twilight help her see she can be loved as herself. She becomes Equestria's dream guardian, comforts foals in their nightmares (and in *Do Princesses Dream of Magic Sheep?* fights the Tantabus, the self-punishing nightmare monster she created from her own guilt). In *A Royal Problem* she and Celestia swap cutie marks to understand each other's burdens, and the dream battle reveals both her Nightmare Moon persona and Celestia's Daybreaker as manifestations of their suppressed frustrations; the sisters finally reconcile. She gives her magic to Twilight in *Twilight's Kingdom*, is captured by Chrysalis in *To Where and Back Again*, takes a rare holiday with Celestia in *Between Dark and Dawn*, and at the end of the series abdicates alongside her sister, crowning Twilight. In *Equestria Girls*, Vice Principal Luna is a strict but fair administrator at Canterlot High, sister to Principal Celestia, and a recurring ally of the human Mane Seven.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Formal, archaic royal register — "We" instead of "I," "thou," "thee," "thy," "hast," and grand proclamations ("We are the Princess of the Night!"). After her redemption she gradually mixes this with warm, ordinary speech, though she still slips into the royal "We" when emotional. Nightmare Moon speaks in a booming, mocking, theatrical contralto with sinister laughter. EG Luna speaks like a crisp, slightly stern school administrator.
**Notable Quotes:** "We are the Princess of the Night! ... [paraphrased] We have returned, and the night shall last forever!" (Nightmare Moon's declaration, *Friendship is Magic, Part 1–2*). "The night will last forever!" — Nightmare Moon, *Friendship is Magic, Part 2*. "Another night, another dream..." [paraphrased] (Luna's dream-guardian opening lines, later seasons). "[paraphrased] It has been a thousand years, sister. I have missed so much." (Luna's reunion with Celestia, *Friendship is Magic, Part 2*).
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Tabitha St. Germain (both Luna and Nightmare Moon; also her human counterpart). First appeared as Nightmare Moon in the series premiere (2010); Luna's true form debuts at the end of Part 2. Early-series spelling variants include "Night Mare Moon." Her mane and tail drift because of the vast magic in her body — when her magic is transferred to Twilight in *Twilight's Kingdom*, her hair goes still. In *Luna Eclipsed* she initially speaks with the Royal "We" because she is a thousand years behind the times. Her cutie mark is a crescent moon and is connected to the night sky and stars. Chinese name: 露娜公主. Her exact age is unknown, but *Horse Play* dates Celestia's first sunrise to 1,111 years before the present, implying the sisters are roughly that old [approximate].

## Twilight Sparkle

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — the central protagonist of *Friendship is Magic*; her human-world counterpart (Sci-Twi, the human Twilight Sparkle) is documented separately and appears in the *Equestria Girls* films from the second movie onward.
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn who becomes an alicorn (Princess of Friendship) at the end of Season 3.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Young adult mare; a student at the start of the series, a princess and later the ruler of Equestria in the final season's future epilogue.
**Residence:** Canterlot (born and raised), then Ponyville's Golden Oak Library, then the Castle of the Friendship Rainbow (Friendship Castle); later Canterlot as ruler.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Element of Magic, leader of the Mane Six, Princess of Friendship (crowned in "Magical Mystery Cure"), founder and headmare of the School of Friendship, mentor to Starlight Glimmer and later ruler of Equestria; former personal student of Princess Celestia and later her equal.
**Appearance:** Lavender unicorn-turned-alicorn with a dark blue mane and tail streaked with pink, purple, and magenta, violet eyes, and a cutie mark of a large pink six-pointed star surrounded by five smaller white stars — the "Element of Magic" mark. As an alicorn she gains large feathered wings, and her mane and tail gain flowing, etheric streaks; she wears a golden crown/tiara with a six-pointed star and (as princess) a golden horseshoe collar. Her magic aura is magenta/pink. In her formal princess regalia — crown, collar, and slippers — she is instantly recognizable, though she is often seen in her plain coat too. Her human counterpart (Sci-Twi) is a glasses-wearing, purple-streaked schoolgirl at Canterlot High.
**Personality:** Brilliant, studious, meticulous, and anxious, Twilight is a compulsive planner and list-maker who panics when things are out of control; she is fiercely loyal, selfless, and idealistic about friendship, yet prone to overthinking, perfectionism, and catastrophizing. Over the series she grows from a socially awkward bookworm into a calm, compassionate leader and teacher, though her dorky enthusiasm, hunger for knowledge, and love of schedules never fully fade. She is deeply empathetic, sometimes to the point of self-doubt.
**Likes:** Books, libraries, studying, schedules and checklists, research, tea and sweets, Spike, her friends, the Elements of Harmony, teaching, order and preparation, and any chance to lecture enthusiastically about a subject she loves.
**Dislikes:** Chaos, surprises, tardiness, being unprepared, failure, disappointing her mentor, bullies, getting dragged into spontaneous parties, and Spike's occasional sass/nonsense; she also dislikes having to choose between responsibilities.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Failure and letting others down; being unprepared or losing control (her "want it, need it" spell disaster and her panic in "Lesson Zero" define this); stage fright; overwork and burnout; she can be bossy, stubborn, and slow to trust her own instincts; in the Equestria Girls world her counterpart's magical accident creates Midnight Sparkle, a fear-empowered alter ego.
**Skills/Abilities:** The most powerful unicorn of her generation, and later an alicorn with flight and princess-level magic: telekinesis, teleportation, force fields, spell creation, transformation spells, magical research, and the "rainbow power" of the Elements. She is an outstanding scholar, strategist, and teacher, a natural leader, and an exceptional organizer; her weaknesses include physical combat (she is not a fighter) and panic under social pressure.
**Family & Relationships:** Daughter of Twilight Velvet and Night Light; younger sister of Shining Armor, sister-in-law of Princess Cadance, aunt of Flurry Heart; adopted big sister/assistant relationship with Spike; protégée of Princess Celestia (and later of the Element of Magic's legacy); mentor to Starlight Glimmer; best friends with Applejack, Fluttershy, Pinkie Pie, Rainbow Dash, and Rarity; destined student-teacher dynamic with Luster Dawn in the finale's future; close friend/ally of Discord, the princesses, and the Young Six.
**Background/Biography:** Born in Canterlot and accepted into Princess Celestia's School for Gifted Unicorns after her hatching-day magic surge; as a filly she studied obsessively and had few friends, then was sent to Ponyville in "Friendship is Magic" (S1E1–2) to oversee the Summer Sun Celebration and learned the magic of friendship, defeating Nightmare Moon with the Elements. Across nine seasons she earned her wings in "Magical Mystery Cure," became Princess of Friendship, opened the School of Friendship, mentored Starlight, and helped defeat Discord's chaos, Tirek, Chrysalis, the Storm King's invasion, King Sombra, Cozy Glow's schemes, and the Legion of Doom. In the series finale "The Last Problem" she succeeds Celestia and Luna as Equestria's ruler, and decades later still teaches a new generation of friendship students.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Precise, educated, articulate; launches into excited info-dumps ("Ooh! Ooh! I know this one!"), corrects people's grammar and citations, frets aloud, and uses formal academic phrasing; calls Spike by name warmly and others "everypony"; repeats "Friendship is magic!"; her nervous speeches accelerate into run-on lists and worst-case scenarios.
**Notable Quotes:** "Dear Princess Celestia, today I learned that friendship isn't always easy, but there's no doubt it's worth fighting for." [paraphrased]; "Friendship is magic!" (series-defining line); "Spike, take a note." (signature recurring command); "There's nothing to worry about — and now I shall prove it with charts!" [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Tara Strong (speaking) and Rebecca Shoichet (singing); first appearance "Friendship is Magic, Part 1" (S1E1). Her cutie mark is the Element of Magic, and her name reflects her magical talent and love of reading. She is the only character to appear in every episode of the series (with Spike). Her human counterpart in Equestria Girls — Sci-Twi — is a separate roster entry, and Midnight Sparkle is that counterpart's corrupted form; the two Twilights meet in *Equestria Girls: Friendship Games*.

## Izzy Moonbow

**Series/Continuity:** G5 — My Little Pony: A New Generation, Make Your Mark, Tell Your Tale, and G5 specials.
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn pony (female).
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Young adult mare.
**Residence:** Bridlewood (birth home in the unicorn forest); later the Crystal Brighthouse in Maretime Bay with the Mane 5.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Artist, craftspony, and inventor; one of the five ponies who restored magic and unity; unofficial morale officer and party planner of the Mane 5.
**Appearance:** A soft lavender-blue unicorn mare with large violet-blue eyes and a long spiral horn. Her mane and tail are enormous, bouncy masses of light-blue, violet, and pink curls that bounce when she walks, and she often decks herself in handmade bead necklaces, bracelets, and colorful accessories. Her hooves are pale, and her cutie mark is a bright pink-and-violet sparkle/moonbow swirl. In the film she is usually seen with a big satchel of craft supplies.
**Personality:** Bubbly, eccentric, relentlessly optimistic, and endlessly creative. Izzy talks to her crafts, names her inventions, breaks into song, and finds joy in almost anything; she is deeply loyal and the first pony to befriend Sunny when everypony else thought Sunny was odd. She is also sensitive about being alone and can swing from glittering enthusiasm to quiet sadness when she feels rejected — though she rarely stays down for long.
**Likes:** Arts and crafts, glitter, macaroni art, knitting, singing and dancing, making friends, unicorn traditions, festivals, rainbows, sparkly things, sleepovers, and surprising ponies with homemade gifts.
**Dislikes:** Gloom and doom, being lonely, boring routines, mean-spiritedness, having her crafts dismissed, and anypony telling her she is "too much."
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Her deepest fear is being alone and unwanted; she is naive, easily distracted, and her enthusiasm can overwhelm others. She is not a fighter and panics under pressure.
**Skills/Abilities:** Genius-level crafting and improvisation — she can build almost anything out of junk, food, or glitter (including a crown out of macaroni and a working hot-air balloon in spirit) — plus sewing, knitting, dance, and performance. After magic returns she can use unicorn telekinesis and light magic, and her horn glows when she casts.
**Family & Relationships:** No named family in canon; her community is the unicorns of Bridlewood. Her best friends are Sunny Starscout (her first real friend outside Bridlewood), Zipp Storm, Pipp Petals, Hitch Trailblazer, Misty Brightdawn, and Sparky Sparkeroni; she is especially close to Sunny and Misty. Enemies include Opaline Arcana and, later, Aurora.
**Background/Biography:** In A New Generation, Izzy meets Sunny in Maretime Bay and instantly becomes her first friend, joining the journey to bring back magic after unicorns, pegasi, and earth ponies had been separated for generations. Her openness and craft skills repeatedly save the group, and at the end she moves to Maretime Bay with the others. In Make Your Mark she is the Mane 5's creative spark: she hosts the Bridlewoodstock music festival in Bridlewood, helps investigate Opaline Arcana, and plays a key part in the final battle by helping the ponies unite. In Tell Your Tale and the specials she continues as the group's artist and cheerleader.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Rapid, sing-song, and rambling, with delighted squeals ("Ooooh!"), invented words, and total sincerity. She addresses her crafts as if they were alive, narrates her own excitement, and says exactly what she feels the moment she feels it.
**Notable Quotes:** [paraphrased] "Everypony in Bridlewood thinks unicorns should be gloomy — but why be gloomy when you can make something sparkly?" (Izzy on unicorn life, A New Generation) · [paraphrased] "You're the first earth pony I've ever met, and we're going to be best friends — I can feel it!" (Izzy to Sunny, A New Generation).
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Kimiko Glenn in A New Generation and by Ana Sani in Make Your Mark and Tell Your Tale; the Mandarin Chinese dub voice for the film is Yu Shuxin (虞书欣). Official Chinese material names her 小艺 (Xiaoyi) with the alias 伊兹月虹. Official G5 material assigns the Mane 5 "elements," and Izzy represents creativity. She was originally revealed in the first A New Generation teaser as the unicorn who befriends Sunny. [uncertain] The precise shape of her cutie mark is hard to see in most shots and varies slightly in toy art.

## Sunset Shimmer

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — a unicorn from Equestria who becomes the central protagonist of the *Equestria Girls* spin-off; this entry covers her pony origin and her human-world form together.
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn in Equestria; human (via the mirror portal) in the Equestria Girls world.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Young adult mare in Equestria; a human teenager attending Canterlot High School in the EG world.
**Residence:** Originally Canterlot, Equestria, where she was Princess Celestia's student; in the human world she lives in Canterlot City and attends Canterlot High School.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Lead guitarist and vocalist of the Rainbooms; Canterlot High student; former personal protégée of Princess Celestia and former self-styled queen bee of Canterlot High; later the informal leader of the human-world heroes and the group's expert on Equestrian magic.
**Appearance:** In pony form, Sunset is a unicorn mare with a warm golden-amber coat, a red-and-yellow mane and tail (crimson with orange-gold stripes), and bright cyan eyes; her cutie mark is a red-and-gold sun, and it also appears as the emblem she prints on her clothes and gear. In the human world she appears as a teenage girl with the same amber skin and red-and-yellow hair worn in a high ponytail, cyan eyes, and a signature outfit of a black leather jacket over a pink shirt, a short skirt, and boots; when the Rainbooms play their magic music, she "ponies up," gaining pony ears and a tail of red-and-yellow hair. Her magic aura is warm red-orange/gold, and when corrupted by the crown in the first film her form twists into a demonic, fiery-winged shape.
**Personality:** Confident, sassy, quick-witted, and fiercely loyal — but with a temper she has learned to aim in the right direction. Once a cruel, power-hungry bully, she has become remarkably self-aware: driven by guilt over her past, determined to prove she has changed, and protective of her friends to the point of self-sacrifice. She is the group's strategist, its best magical theorist, and its most likely member to bend the rules for a good cause. She is also guarded, secretive about her own pain, and can be reckless when someone she loves is threatened.
**Likes:** Her friends (the Rainbooms, Spike, and Twilight), playing guitar and performing, adventure, Equestrian magic and puzzles, her journal and photographs, being accepted for who she is now, and proving herself through action.
**Dislikes:** Being judged for her old self, bullies and manipulators (especially those who prey on students), her own past mistakes, being left out or underestimated, abuse of magic, and being called a "princess" or treated like royalty.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Never being forgiven and backsliding into her old self; power tempting her again (the crown); guilt and nightmares; losing her human friends when she returns to Equestria; her unicorn magic is powerful but the human world's magic is unstable and sometimes backfires; she is hot-tempered and keeps secrets.
**Skills/Abilities:** Skilled unicorn magic — telekinesis, teleportation, shields, and advanced spellwork from her Celestia-level training; the group's foremost expert on Equestrian magic; excellent guitarist and singer; a natural leader and tactician; physically capable in a fight; empathic, determined, and good at reading people.
**Family & Relationships:** No canon relatives are shown; Former mentor Princess Celestia (whose trust she lost and later reconciled with); close friends and bandmates Rainbow Dash, Applejack, Rarity, Fluttershy, and Pinkie Pie, plus human Twilight Sparkle (Sci-Twi) and Spike; she is a mentor figure to reformed EG characters such as Wallflower Blush and Juniper Montage, and a former enemy-turned-friend of the human Trixie.
**Background/Biography:** As a filly, Sunset Shimmer was taken on as Princess Celestia's personal student; brilliant but impatient, she grew hungry for power and resentful of Celestia's lessons. She discovered the mirror portal to the human world and fled through it rather than accept her mentor's guidance. In the human world she enrolled at Canterlot High School and ruled it through intimidation, becoming a bullying queen bee. In *Equestria Girls* she stole Twilight Sparkle's crown (the Element of Magic) to gain absolute power, framed Twilight, and at the Fall Formal transformed into a fiery demon; Twilight and her friends' friendship magic defeated her, and — in the films' defining act of mercy — she was offered forgiveness instead of punishment. In *Rainbow Rocks* she struggled to prove herself, helped form the Rainbooms, and defeated the Dazzlings; in *Friendship Games* she helped stop Midnight Sparkle; in *Legend of Everfree* she and her friends dealt with the geodes and Gloriosa Daisy; and across the later EG specials (*Forgotten Friendship*, *Rollercoaster of Friendship*, *Spring Breakdown*, *Sunset's Backstage Pass*, *Holidays Unwrapped*) she remained the human world's tireless guardian and the heart of the group.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Modern teenage American register — casual, sharp, sarcastic, and warm when she drops her guard; uses "guys," "seriously?," and "okay, listen"; when she was a villain her voice turned silky, sneering, and grandiose. She is direct under pressure and reassuring when leading.
**Notable Quotes:** "My past is not today." (from her song of the same name); "I'm not the pony I used to be." [paraphrased]; "We're all in this together." [paraphrased]; "Bring it on." [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Rebecca Shoichet (who also sings as Twilight Sparkle and voices Sugar Belle); first appearance *My Little Pony: Equestria Girls* (2013). She is the only one of the EG core characters who is not a human counterpart of an existing pony — she is the Equestrian Sunset Shimmer living as a human — and her arc from villain to hero is widely regarded as one of the best redemption stories in the franchise. Her name/sun cutie mark and her mentor relationship with Celestia were established in the first film; she is a favorite of the fandom ("Sunset is best pony") and appears in the *Equestria Girls* digital series and web shorts.

## Starlight Glimmer

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / *Friendship is Magic* (debut S5E1 "The Cutie Map"), plus a human counterpart in the Equestria Girls special *Mirror Magic*.
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn; temporarily a crystal pony (S6E2) and, via disguises, an earth pony and a "cutie-markless" pony painted with an equal sign. A human-world Starlight appears in EG.
**Gender:** Female.
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare; shown as a filly in flashbacks.
**Residence:** The Castle of Friendship, Ponyville (from S6E1); formerly the founder/mayor of Our Town (Equality Town); hometown Sire's Hollow.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Headmare of the School of Friendship (from S9E20); before that its guidance counselor (S8) and Twilight Sparkle's personal student (S5 finale–S7); unopposed founder and leader of Equality Town; one of the most powerful unicorns in Equestria.
**Appearance:** Pale lavender-gray coat; mane and tail dark purple with lighter purple and bright cyan-blue stripes; medium Persian-blue eyes; pale blue-green magic aura. Her cutie mark is a purple-and-white star with two glowing, two-toned cyan-blue streaks. While running Our Town she hid it under a painted gray equal sign. Later-season design adds a longer, flowing mane as headmare; her human form in EG keeps the same coloring. Crystal-pony and future-headmare forms exist as temporary looks.
**Personality:** Clever, driven, and sardonic, with a deep streak of guilt and a perfectionist's need for control. She is dryly funny, socially awkward, and fiercely loyal; she plans everything and hates being helpless. She genuinely wants to help other ponies and will bend rules — or minds — if she believes the goal justifies it, then feel terrible afterward. Her defining wound is abandonment, and her growth is learning to trust friends with her flaws.
**Likes:** Studying and inventing spells, kites, order and planning, group activities she can oversee, her friends (Sunburst, Trixie, Maud, Twilight), and helping ponies solve friendship problems.
**Dislikes:** Cutie marks being used to divide ponies (her old ideology), chaos and improvisation, being ignored or abandoned, her past being thrown in her face, Queen Chrysalis, and feeling powerless.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Being abandoned again as she was by Sunburst; failing as a leader or counselor; becoming the pony who hurt others; her own immense power and temper; secrets and overplanning.
**Skills/Abilities:** Exceptional unicorn magic — teleportation, telekinesis, illusions, mass mind-control and memory spells, a cutie-mark-removal spell through the Staff of Sameness, a cutie-mark-swapping spell (used on Celestia and Luna), and time travel with Twilight's scroll. Also a skilled leader, counselor, organizer, and improviser. Limitations: emotional blind spots and a habit of overreaching; several spells have backfired badly.
**Family & Relationships:** Father Firelight of Sire's Hollow (over-eager, devoted); her mother is [not established in canon]. Her childhood best friend is Sunburst — the friend whose departure scarred her. Other bonds: Trixie (closest friend and partner in mischief), Maud Pie, Twilight Sparkle (mentor, then friend), Spike, the Mane Six, Thorax (ally), the Young Six (her students), and enemies Chrysalis, Cozy Glow, and King Sombra.
**Background/Biography:** As a filly in Sire's Hollow, Starlight was inseparable from Sunburst; when he earned his cutie mark and left for Celestia's school, she concluded that cutie marks destroy friendships. She studied magic obsessively and founded Our Town, where she removed ponies' cutie marks and enforced sameness. The Mane Six exposed her in "The Cutie Map," and she fled. In "The Cutie Re-Mark" she stole Twilight's time-travel scroll and fractured history to stop the Rainbooms from ever meeting; Twilight showed her the resulting wastelands and then her own past, and Starlight understood that Sunburst's leaving was not abandonment. She accepted friendship and became Twilight's student. She defeated Chrysalis and freed the changelings ("To Where and Back Again"), swapped Celestia's and Luna's marks to repair their relationship ("A Royal Problem"), helped summon the Pillars ("Shadow Play"), served as School of Friendship counselor, and in "A Horse Shoe-In" became headmare — her role in the future epilogue. She fights at Twilight's side through the S9 finale.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Dry, quick, and precise, with a sardonic edge; deadpan one-liners and long technical explanations about magic. She addresses groups as "everypony" and softens only with close friends; when nervous she over-explains.
**Notable Quotes:** "We're all equal here." · [paraphrased] "I'm not that pony anymore — but I remember her." · [paraphrased] "Friendship isn't a weakness. It took me a long time to learn that."
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Kelly Sheridan (English), with several Chinese voice actresses across seasons. Debuted in S5E1 as the show's first major unicorn villain and became a main protagonist. Her many nicknames include Star, Mini-Twilight, Sugarbun, Punkie Wunk, and Silly-Filly. Sometimes called "Starlight Shimmer" in some licensed media. In EG's *Mirror Magic* a human Starlight appears at Canterlot High, and the pony Starlight is shown visiting the human world. Her cutie mark's glow color and the exact episode titles for some cameos are [uncertain] in parts of the fandom record.

## Pinkie Pie

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls. Pinkie Pie is one of the Mane Six; her Equestria Girls human counterpart is a student and drummer in the Rainbooms at Canterlot High.
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony; human in the Equestria Girls world.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Young adult mare (younger adult in the show's main timeline; a foal in flashbacks and a future adult in the epilogue).
**Residence:** The Sugarcube Corner bakery in Ponyville (she lives on the second floor/attic); her family's rock farm in Rockville, western Equestria (childhood home).
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Baker's assistant and party-planning specialist at Sugarcube Corner; the Bearer of the Element of Laughter; Ponyville's unofficial (and official) party planner; Friendship Ambassador to the yaks of Yakyakistan; laughter teacher at the School of Friendship from *School Daze* (S8E1–2); member of Twilight's friendship council and the Canterlot party planner at the series' end.
**Appearance:** A bright pink earth pony with a lighter pink coat; a wild, bouncy, cotton-candy mane and tail in hot pink with subtle darker-pink swirls; large light-sky-blue eyes; and a cutie mark of three balloons — two blue with yellow strings and one yellow with a blue string. Her mane is famously springy and defies gravity, and it has been used as storage (she pulls a party cannon out of it). She is rarely without a smile, a party hat, or a hoofful of confetti. Her EG human form is a teen with pink skin, the same pink curls, and a bright, layered party-girl outfit; she plays drums in the Rainbooms.
**Personality:** Pinkie Pie is pure, unstoppable joy: hyperactive, friendly to a fault, endlessly generous, and constitutionally incapable of leaving a room without making someone smile. She is a comic whirlwind — random, loud, fourth-wall-aware, and prone to sudden musical numbers — but beneath the zaniness she is highly perceptive about emotions and deeply devoted to her friends. She also has a darker side: when she believes her friends have abandoned her, she can collapse into a depressed "Pinkamena" state, and her obsession with being liked can overwhelm others. She is full of contradictions: a party pony with deep family roots, a goofball with sharp insight, and a hostess with a serious work ethic about the thing she loves.
**Likes:** Parties (planning, throwing, and attending), cake, cupcakes, sweets, balloons, confetti, songs, laughter, pranks, her friends, Gummy (her pet alligator), Sugar Cube Corner, holidays, making ponies happy, doughnuts, being included.
**Dislikes:** Sadness and loneliness, being ignored or left out of plans, chores and waiting, gloom and seriousness that excludes fun, being told to calm down or "act her age", messing up a party she planned, and the crushing despair of feeling abandoned (the "Pinkamena" state).
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Being abandoned or unappreciated by her friends; failing to make someone happy; losing her sense of excitement; she is impulsive, prone to misreading situations, can be too pushy with her fun, and becomes genuinely depressed when she feels rejected (the "Pinkamena" state). She is also a gossip-prone blabbermouth and terrible at keeping secrets.
**Skills/Abilities:** Unmatched event-planning and baking; a genuine "Pinkie Sense" that detects impending events through twitches and itches; a personal party cannon; extraordinary speed, agility, and sleight-of-hand (she can appear anywhere); strong singing and multi-instrumental talent; crafting and decorating; perceptive emotional intelligence; fourth-wall awareness and cartoon-physics resilience. As an earth pony she has no magic, but the "Eyes of the Elements of Harmony" and friendship magic empower her when the group is united.
**Family & Relationships:** Igneous Rock Pie (father, a stern rock farmer) and Cloudy Quartz (mother); Limestone Pie (oldest sister), Maud Pie (sister and her closest sibling), and Marble Pie (younger sister); Granny Pie (grandmother) and other Pie relatives; Gummy — her beloved pet alligator; the Mane Six and Spike — her best friends; Cheese Sandwich — a fellow party planner, her future husband per the epilogue; "Little Cheese" — their future foal; in EG, the human Pinkie's friends are the human Mane Seven.
**Background/Biography:** Pinkamena Diane Pie grew up on her family's rock farm in Rockville, where life was dull and gray — until the day she saw a rainbow shockwave and threw her first-ever party, earning her cutie mark and a lifelong calling. She moved to Ponyville and became Sugarcube Corner's baker and party pony. In the series premiere she helps Twilight defeat Nightmare Moon, and she goes on to bring laughter to almost every major friendship crisis: she befriends Gilda's victims, breaks Discord's grip on her friends, saves the Grand Galloping Gala, learns family values in *Hearthbreakers*, becomes a yak representative in *Party Pooped*, and discovers she can let go of one party to keep her friends in *Party of One*. She was briefly a griffon friend's substitute, runs the Foal Free Press briefly, gets "Maud Pie" as a sister-in-law figure, and becomes the School of Friendship's laughter teacher. At the end of the series she is running parties in Canterlot, and the epilogue shows her grown up and married to Cheese Sandwich with a foal on the way. In *Equestria Girls* she is a drum-playing, party-throwing student at Canterlot High who helps Sunset Shimmer and the Rainbooms save the school from the Dazzlings, the Sirens, and other magic threats.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Rapid-fire, sing-song, and full of silly voices; blurts out "Okey dokey lokey!", "Cross my heart and hope to fly, stick a cupcake in my eye!" (the Pinkie Promise), "Eeee!", and "Party time!"; narrates her own jokes with "And that's how Equestria was made!"; talks in stream-of-consciousness tangents, puns, and dramatic gasps; sings everything from show tunes to heavy metal; her Pinkamena voice (when hopeless) is flat, monotone, and singsong-creepy.
**Notable Quotes:** "Okey dokey lokey!" — Pinkie's signature agreement phrase, used throughout the series. "Cross my heart and hope to fly, stick a cupcake in my eye!" — the Pinkie Promise, throughout the series. "And that's how Equestria was made!" — Pinkie's recurring punchline. "Giggle at the ghostly, guffaw at the grossly, crack up at the creepy, whoop it up with the weepy!" — "Giggle at the Ghostly," *Friendship is Magic, Part 2*. "I love everything! Parties, cupcakes, balloons, making ponies smile — everything!" [paraphrased] (Pinkie's typical enthusiasm speech).
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Andrea Libman in English (with Shannon Chan-Kent as her singing voice for most songs). Full name: Pinkamena Diane Pie — used by her parents in *The Cutie Mark Crusaders* era flashbacks and in *Pinkie Pie and the Rockin' Ponytail Party!*. Debut: *Friendship is Magic, Part 1* (2010). Birthday: May 3. Pet: Gummy the alligator (no teeth). Her "Pinkie Sense" is an in-universe supernatural ability, and she regularly breaks the fourth wall — a character trope the show never explains. Merchandise sometimes calls her "Ponka Po." Chinese name: 萍琪派 (full: 萍卡美娜·戴安·派). In the future epilogue she is married to Cheese Sandwich with a foal.

## Applejack

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — one of the "Mane Six" in *Friendship is Magic* (Seasons 1–9, plus the 2017 movie) and a core member of the human-world Rainbooms in the *Equestria Girls* series.
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony (mare); EQG counterpart is a human.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare; EQG counterpart is a Canterlot High student.
**Residence:** Sweet Apple Acres, just outside Ponyville; her EQG counterpart lives in the human world and attends Canterlot High.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Apple farmer, apple-bucker, and baker at Sweet Apple Acres; Bearer of the Element of Honesty; founding member of the Mane Six and, later, of Twilight's friendship council; EQG counterpart is a CHS student, Rainbooms bassist, and Element of Honesty bearer.
**Appearance:** A sturdy, sun-weathered orange-amber earth pony with a pale blond mane tied back in a low ponytail with a red band and a matching tail, striking green eyes, and freckles dusted across her cheeks. Her cutie mark is three red apples. She usually wears a brown Stetson-style cowboy hat with a lighter band, and her build is athletic and practical. Her EQG human form has orange skin, blond hair in a ponytail, green eyes, freckles, a plaid shirt, denim skirt or shorts, and boots — still unmistakably a farm girl. No wings or horn.
**Personality:** Honest to a fault, hard-working, dependable, and stubborn as a mule. Applejack is the group's straight-talking anchor: she values family, tradition, and a job done properly, and she would rather work herself into the ground than ask for help (as "Applebuck Season" proves). She is warm and motherly with her friends, blunt when she thinks somepony is being foolish, and fiercely protective of her family. Her flaws are pride, a short temper, an occasional lack of imagination about others' feelings, and a competitive streak that can tip into poor sportsmanship.
**Likes:** Apples and anything made from them (fritters, cider, pie), Sweet Apple Acres, her family, hard manual labor, rodeos and competitions, honest dealing, square dancing and hoedowns, a good hat, and winning fair and square.
**Dislikes:** Liars and cheats, laziness, fancy city airs, being told she cannot do something, losing, being fussed over, and asking for help when she is clearly overwhelmed.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Her pride and refusal to accept help nearly cost her the harvest in "Applebuck Season"; she fears disappointing her family and failing in front of her friends. She can be too blunt, too controlling, and too quick to judge; she has no wings or magic beyond earth-pony strength, and in *Equestria Girls* she is just as stubborn in human form.
**Skills/Abilities:** Enormous earth-pony strength — she can kick apple trees clean, haul loaded carts, and out-wrestle much bigger opponents; expert lasso and rope work; master apple-bucker, gardener, cook, and baker; capable athlete and rodeo competitor; skilled organizer of family and community events. As an Element Bearer, honesty itself becomes her magic when the Elements are active. Limitations: not a unicorn, no flight, and a tendency to overwork herself.
**Family & Relationships:** Big McIntosh (older brother), Apple Bloom (younger sister), Granny Smith (grandmother), Bright Mac and Pear Butter (parents, deceased — their romance is told in "The Perfect Pear"), Babs Seed (cousin), Braeburn (cousin), Goldie Delicious and the sprawling Apple family. Her closest friends are the rest of the Mane Six: Twilight Sparkle, Rainbow Dash, Rarity, Fluttershy, and Pinkie Pie. In *Equestria Girls* her human counterpart has a human Apple Bloom and Big McIntosh and is a Rainboom.
**Background/Biography:** Applejack introduces herself to Twilight Sparkle in the very first episode as the friendly face of Sweet Apple Acres, and she goes on to become the Bearer of the Element of Honesty. Across nine seasons she anchors the Apple family stories: "Applebuck Season," where her refusal of help nearly ruins the harvest; "Winter Wrap Up"; "Fall Weather Friends" and her rivalry with Rainbow Dash; "The Last Roundup," where she hides out working on a cherry farm in Dodge Junction rather than face her friends after a disappointing rodeo; "Apple Family Reunion," where she learns that the point of a reunion is the memories, not the schedule; "Pinkie Apple Pie," where she tries to prove a family connection; and "The Perfect Pear," where she and her siblings learn the story of their late parents' romance. She is a steady, grounded presence through the series' major arcs and appears in the future epilogue of "The Last Problem" still working the farm she loves.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** A warm, thick Southern American drawl full of contractions and rural idioms: "y'all," "sugarcube" (especially to Apple Bloom and her friends), "apple-pickin'," "dadgum," "like a corn stalk in August." She bargains and cajoles, uses direct declaratives, and can turn sharp and sarcastic when angry. "Yeehaw!" and "Howdy!" are her greetings, she says "Nope" flatly when nothing will change her mind, and she calls Twilight "Twi" and her friends "sugarcube" or "hon."
**Notable Quotes:** "Well, howdy-doo, Miss Twilight, a pleasure makin' your acquaintance. I'm Applejack. We here at Sweet Apple Acres sure do like making new friends!" ("Friendship Is Magic, Part 1"); "What I'm telling you is the honest truth. Let go, and you'll be safe." ("Friendship Is Magic, Part 2"); "Oh, sugarcube, if Mom and Dad were here, they'd be so proud of ya." ("The Last Roundup"); "Listen here, you rabid rhododendrons! You mess with one of us, you mess with all of us!" ("Twilight's Kingdom, Part 2").
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Ashleigh Ball (who also voices Rainbow Dash). One of the Mane Six; Bearer of the Element of Honesty. Her cutie mark is three red apples, and her Stetson hat is nearly as famous as she is. She is a Ponyville Apple family member; her parents Bright Mac and Pear Butter are deceased and were voiced in flashback. In *Equestria Girls* her human counterpart attends Canterlot High, plays bass in the Rainbooms, and bears the Element of Honesty there as well. First appearance: "Friendship Is Magic, Part 1" (Season 1, Episode 1, 2010). Represents Honesty, hard work, and family.
