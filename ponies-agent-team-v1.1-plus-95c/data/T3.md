## Soarin'

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — primarily *Friendship is Magic*; a human-world version appears only in the IDW comic *Equestria Girls 2013 Annual* (no on-screen EQG counterpart).
**Species/Kind:** Pegasus stallion.
**Gender:** Male (he/him).
**Age/Stage:** Adult stallion; shown as a foal at flight camp in *Parental Glideance* flashbacks and as a teenager in IDW comic material.
**Residence:** Cloudsdale (Wonderbolts home city); travels wherever the Wonderbolts perform or train.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Wonderbolt — elite flier of the Wonderbolts aerial team. He wears a commander's shoulder insignia in *A Canterlot Wedding* and gives the orders when Spitfire is absent (*Rarity Investigates*), so he is effectively the team's second-in-command/captain; the *Mini Pony Collector's Guide* and collector cards also call him a Wonderbolts captain. He serves as a senior instructor at Wonderbolts Academy and mentors new recruits, including Rainbow Dash (*Newbie Dash*).
**Appearance:** Very pale cornflower-blue coat, dark grey-blue ("phthalo") mane and tail, medium emerald-green eyes, athletic pegasus build. He is usually in the tight blue-and-yellow Wonderbolts flight suit with goggles and the winged-bolt emblem; in *Rainbow Falls* he is bandaged and grounded after a crash. His cutie mark has been drawn two ways — a yellow lightning bolt with a pair of wings, and a yellow lightning bolt bursting from a cloud — both Wonderbolt "bolt in the sky" designs. Human form in the IDW 2013 Annual is a young man with pale blue coloring.
**Personality:** Cheerful, unpretentious and generous, the friendly senior jock of the Wonderbolts. He is loyal to his team, quick to praise other fliers, and almost childishly delighted by food — apple pie in particular. He is a little naive and gullible, easily distracted, and tends to follow orders rather than question authority; he can be a pushover. At his best he is warm, encouraging and selfless, and he takes the Wonderbolt code ("Wonderbolts look out for each other") seriously.
**Likes:** Apple pie (especially Sweet Apple Acres pie), flying fast, performing and touring with the Wonderbolts, cheering crowds, food in general, rollercoasters, teaching and encouraging young fliers, practical jokes and easy banter with teammates.
**Dislikes:** Being injured or grounded, missing a performance, being lied to or framed (the Wind Rider affair), pomposity and formal feasts where pie is out of reach, ponies who treat flight as a chore.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Crashes and injury (his wing injury in *Rainbow Falls* leaves him hospitalized); boredom and inactivity while recovering; his appetite and his friendliness make him easy to manipulate; he is not a rules-challenger, so he sometimes fails to act until someone braver pushes him.
**Skills/Abilities:** Elite pegasus flight — speed, precision formation flying, aerobatics and endurance; Wonderbolt military-style drills and maneuvers; strong wing power; able to fly fast enough to compete at Equestria Games level; coaching and leadership (as instructor/deputy); famous "worst crash" stories shared as teaching tales; limitations: grounded when injured, no special magic beyond pegasus flight.
**Family & Relationships:** Wonderbolts teammates Spitfire (captain and commander), Fleetfoot and others; friend, mentor and eventual colleague to Rainbow Dash; a mother named Tippy Tappy was suggested in a 2013 writer's tweet but is not confirmed on screen [uncertain]. No canon romantic interest.
**Background/Biography:** Soarin' is first glimpsed in Rainbow Dash's imagination in *The Ticket Master* (S1E3) and first speaks at the Grand Galloping Gala in *The Best Night Ever* (S1E26), where his love of apple pie becomes a running gag. He appears as a Wonderbolts commander at Shining Armor and Cadance's wedding (*A Canterlot Wedding*, S2E25–26), trains cadets at Wonderbolts Academy (S3E7), and in *Rainbow Falls* (S4E10) he is injured while preparing for the Equestria Games qualifiers and hospitalized — touched that Rainbow's friends visit him, and supporting Rainbow's choice to fly for Ponyville. In *Rarity Investigates* (S5) he warns Rainbow Dash about the Wonderbolt bylaws and insists Wonderbolts look out for each other while the Wind Rider frame-up is solved. *Newbie Dash* (S6) makes him Rainbow's encouraging mentor during her induction and reveals his own rookie nickname, "Clipper." He returns for *Grannies Gone Wild*, a brief appearance in *My Little Pony: The Movie* (2017), and the future epilogue in *The Last Problem*.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Casual, friendly, informal; calls ponies by name (and Rainbow Dash "Dash"), sprinkles in foodie exclamations, and switches into a crisp "Yes, ma'am" Academy tone with Spitfire. He teases gently, laughs easily, and his sentences often perk up when pie is mentioned. Recurring line: "Wonderbolts look out for each other!"
**Notable Quotes:** "I'll take that big apple pie!" · "You're the best, Rainbow Dash." · "Wonderbolts look out for each other!" · "Check your nine, Crash!" · "My nickname's 'Clipper'. You know why they call me that? Because I clipped my wing on a flagpole as I was landing on my first day."
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Matt Hill (English). His name is spelled both "Soarin" and "Soarin'" in official material; the apostrophe version is the most common. His cutie mark design changed between seasons/merchandise. "Clipper" is his canon Academy nickname; merchandise also gives him the nicknames "So" and "Clipper." The *Rainbow Falls* pie scene and his childlike love of apple pie are fan-favorite memes. He appears in the 2017 movie crowd scenes and in IDW's *Equestria Girls 2013 Annual* as a human.

## Bon Bon

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — *Friendship is Magic*, with a human counterpart in the *Equestria Girls* films and digital series.
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony mare (human in EQG).
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare.
**Residence:** Ponyville; she shares a house with Lyra Heartstrings, and the two are longtime housemates.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Owner/operator of the candy shop "Sweetie Drops Candies" in Ponyville; secretly "Special Agent Sweetie Drops" of S.M.I.L.E., Canterlot's super-secret anti-monster agency (now defunct as a cover, after the bugbear escaped from Tartarus); skilled monster hunter and spy.
**Appearance:** Beige/cream coat with a curly two-tone mane and tail — cobalt blue with pale magenta-pink streaks — and ice-blue eyes. Her cutie mark is three wrapped candies (some game art interprets the same shape as three blue-and-yellow bows). As Agent Sweetie Drops she wears a black suit, tie and sunglasses; in everyday life she is usually seen in an apron at her candy shop. Her EQG human counterpart is a Canterlot High student with cream skin and the same blue-and-pink hair, appearing in *Rainbow Rocks* and later shorts alongside human Lyra.
**Personality:** Outwardly bubbly, sweet and a little gossipy — the friendly neighborhood candy-shop owner. Underneath she is a cool, competent agent: quick-thinking, brave, resourceful and fiercely protective of Ponyville. She is dramatic, prone to panic when her cover is threatened, and can be surprisingly blunt ("Never heard of you" to Rarity). She mothers Lyra, tends to over-worry, and is happiest when everyone is safe and well-fed on sweets.
**Likes:** Candy and chocolate, running her shop, her best friend Lyra, parties and festivals, doing small kindnesses for friends, spy gadgets and monster hunting, being useful in a crisis.
**Dislikes:** Bugbears and monsters, dishonest salesponies and bad food ("Mine's got rocks in it!"), having her secret identity exposed, being talked over or ignored, chaos that threatens her friends.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Bugbears — the monster she once captured and her personal nemesis; losing her friends if they learn about her past; high-pressure secrecy (she nearly cracks under the strain); she is not physically imposing and relies on plans, traps and gadgets.
**Skills/Abilities:** Expert confectioner and candy-maker; S.M.I.L.E. training in trapping, takedowns and monster capture, stealth, disguise and espionage; quick reflexes and improvisation; social engineering and customer service charm; a skilled party/wedding organizer (she helped set up Matilda and Cranky Doodle's wedding). Limitations: ordinary earth pony strength, and her anxiety under pressure.
**Family & Relationships:** Lyra Heartstrings — best friend, housemate, and in official cards, Valentine's posts and later expanded material, her romantic partner (often treated as her spouse); friends with the Mane Six and the townsfolk of Ponyville; former S.M.I.L.E. colleagues; her EQG human counterpart is friends with human Lyra.
**Background/Biography:** Bon Bon starts as a background earth pony in early Ponyville crowd scenes and gains small speaking parts in *Call of the Cutie* ("I didn't put those in my bag!"), *Green Isn't Your Color* (chasing Fluttershy sightings with Lyra), *Lesson Zero*, *The Super Speedy Cider Squeezy 6000*, *Putting Your Hoof Down* and later episodes. Her defining appearance is *Slice of Life* (S5E9), where a bugbear escapes from Tartarus and her cover shatters: she confesses to being "Special Agent Sweetie Drops," explains that the agency was shut down and every trace destroyed at Celestia's demand, then defeats the bugbear and is relieved when her friends accept her anyway. She returns in *Amending Fences*, *It Isn't the Mane Thing About You* and many crowd scenes through Season 9, and appears in the human world in EQG. The chapter book *Lyra and Bon Bon and the Mares from S.M.I.L.E.* expands her agency backstory.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Bright, chatty, perky customer-service voice ("Welcome to Sweetie Drops Candies, where all your sweetest dreams come true!"); serious and clipped when she switches into agent mode; comic squeaks and screams under stress; sassy and deadpan with strangers; warm and teasing with Lyra.
**Notable Quotes:** "Welcome to Sweetie Drops Candies, where all your sweetest dreams come true!" · "My name isn't Bon Bon. It's 'Special Agent Sweetie Drops.' I work for a super-secret anti-monster agency in Canterlot..." · "There is nothing like a best friend, is there?" · "Mine's got rocks in it!" · "I try to be a voice of reason for Lyra, but her enthusiasm is just so infectious sometimes."
**Trivia/Notes:** Her real/legal name is Sweetie Drops; "Bon Bon" is her Ponyville name, chosen because *bonbon* is French for candy and matches her cutie mark. Voice duties changed over the years — Tabitha St. Germain, then Ashleigh Ball for *Putting Your Hoof Down*, then Andrea Libman from *Slice of Life* onward — spawning the fandom joke about "Bon Bon's voice," which official merchandise acknowledged with a T-shirt reading "You just read this in one of my voices." The Lyra/Bon Bon pairing is one of the fandom's oldest and most-supported ships. Her cutie mark is officially described as candies, though some game art calls them bows.

## Shoeshine

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — background pony in *Friendship is Magic*; ponies with her colors appear briefly in the EQG films and in *My Little Pony: The Movie*.
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony mare.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare.
**Residence:** Ponyville.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Ponyville townspony with no canon job; seen as a market shopper and stall-goer, parade participant (cymbal player in the Summer Harvest Parade), member of the Winter Wrap Up plant team (briefly wearing the team captain's armband), Running of the Leaves competitor, and a background guest at weddings, parties and ceremonies. She is named only in merchandise.
**Appearance:** Pale cornflower-blue coat, pale blue-grey mane and tail, medium rose-red eyes; cutie mark of two interlocking horseshoes. Ordinary mare build with no signature accessories, though event gear changes her look: a yellow-striped plant-team vest with a yellow star in *Winter Wrap Up*, and a pink-and-white cowboy hat in Appleloosa in *Over a Barrel*. Animation errors have occasionally given her a musical-note cutie mark or placed a recolored pegasus version of her design in flashbacks.
**Personality:** An easygoing, sociable townspony. Merchandise cards describe her as "easy to get along with" and as a pony who "understands and listens to her friends." On screen she is a cheerful background presence who joins civic events, chats with friends and enjoys a good bargain; she can be pushy when excited (she once cut in line at Sugarcube Corner and was firmly sent to the back by Fluttershy, then accepted it meekly). She is curious, friendly, a little nosy, and quick to defer in the face of authority.
**Likes:** Carrots (she once had "a cartload of extra carrots" to trade for a Gala ticket), baked goods and pie, shopping and market browsing, parades and festivals, races and sports spectacles, chatting with friends such as Cherry Berry and Golden Harvest.
**Dislikes:** Waiting in line or losing her place, being scolded, scary monsters and panicky crowds, missed treats or festival events.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Cowers from monsters and villains like the rest of Ponyville's crowd; meek when confronted; no combat ability or special talent is established; easily swept up in mob excitement.
**Skills/Abilities:** Ordinary earth pony abilities; gardening and plant work (Winter Wrap Up plant team), cymbal playing in the Summer Harvest Parade, running (Running of the Leaves participant), shopping/errand-running, and solid crowd-watching stamina.
**Family & Relationships:** No canon family [not established in canon]; a background friend of Cherry Berry, Golden Harvest and Berryshine; briefly interacted with Fluttershy (the line-cutting incident and the paparazzi shout) and never with a canon romance.
**Background/Biography:** Present from the very first episode, *Friendship is Magic, Part 1*, among the Ponyville crowd greeting Twilight. In *The Ticket Master* she offers a cartload of carrots for a Grand Galloping Gala ticket and chases Twilight across town. In *Winter Wrap Up* she joins the plant team; in *Fall Weather Friends* she runs the Running of the Leaves; in *Green Isn't Your Color* she spots a disguised Fluttershy at the magazine stand and shouts "It's Fluttershy!", triggering a paparazzi swarm; in *Over a Barrel* she wears a cowboy hat in Appleloosa and helps paint a carriage and build defenses; in *Putting Your Hoof Down* she cuts in line at Sugarcube Corner; in *It's About Time* she laughs at Twilight's warning about the future. Across Seasons 2–9 she turns up in dozens of crowd scenes — the Canterlot wedding, parades, the Equestria Games, the Friendship Festival and Twilight's final coronation — and in the alternate timeline of *The Cutie Re-Mark* she is a changeling-resistance fighter; in Season 9 she is briefly mind-controlled by King Sombra. In EQG she appears at the Crystal Empire train station in *Equestria Girls*, in the opening of *Rainbow Rocks*, and in the rift scene of *Friendship Games*; she also appears in *Spring Breakdown* and in the crowds of *My Little Pony: The Movie*.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Ordinary, cheerful Ponyville-register speech with occasional excited outbursts ("It's Fluttershy!"); impatient when queuing ("We get it! Move on!"); polite and meek when corrected ("Uh... I guess, maybe."). No signature catchphrase.
**Notable Quotes:** "I have a cartload of extra carrots." · "It's Fluttershy!" · "We get it! Move on!" · "Uh... I guess, maybe." · "Oh! It's not even my birthday!"
**Trivia/Notes:** She is never named on screen — "Shoeshine" comes from blind-bag merchandise and collector cards (Wave 4, 2012, then Waves 6 and 9). The US collector card says "Shoeshine understands and listens to her friends!" and the European card calls her "easy to get along with." Her rare lines have been credited to Tabitha St. Germain (S2E11), Michael Dobson (S6E4) and Ashleigh Ball (S7E23) [per MLP wiki]. Fans note her resemblance to other recolored background ponies and the animation errors in her cutie mark.

## Babs Seed

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — *Friendship is Magic*; a human version appears in IDW's *Equestria Girls 2013 Annual*.
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony filly (human in the EQG comic).
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** School-age filly, roughly the same age as Apple Bloom and the other Cutie Mark Crusaders.
**Residence:** Manehattan (her home city); she visits Ponyville and Sweet Apple Acres during school breaks.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Student; founding member of the Cutie Mark Crusaders and the founder/first leader of the CMC's Manehattan branch.
**Appearance:** Bright golden-yellow coat, two-tone pale magenta and bright magenta-pink mane and tail (styled in a casual big-city cut), soft spring-green eyes. Her flank is blank through Season 3; after earning her cutie mark she has a pair of scissors with apple-shaped handles. She briefly wears a wolf costume in *One Bad Apple*; otherwise she has no signature outfit. Her human form in the EG Annual is a girl with golden-yellow skin and pink hair.
**Personality:** Street-smart, energetic and outspoken, a true Manehattan kid with a big heart under a tough shell. Because she was bullied in Manehattan for having a blank flank, she arrives guarded and turns her hurt outward, becoming a bully herself before Apple Bloom's honesty breaks through. Once she feels accepted she is fiercely loyal, warm, brave and generous — a natural leader who will defend her friends in a heartbeat. Her flaws are a quick temper, a defensive streak and a tendency to lash out when embarrassed.
**Likes:** Her cousin Apple Bloom, the Apple family, being a Cutie Mark Crusader, manes and tail styling (her cutie mark talent), her big city, hangouts with friends, the annual Apple family reunion.
**Dislikes:** Being teased or bullied for a blank flank, mean ponies, being made to feel like an outsider, the idea that she doesn't belong in the CMC anymore.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Rejection and exclusion — the root of her bullying behavior; losing her CMC friends after getting her cutie mark; she is still a kid and sometimes acts before she thinks.
**Skills/Abilities:** Haircutting and mane/tail styling (her cutie mark is scissors with apple handles, and her talent was foreshadowed in *Friends Forever* #13); street smarts and city confidence; leadership (she founded and ran the Manehattan CMC branch); athletic enough for Apple family work and reunions; strong social instincts.
**Family & Relationships:** Apple Bloom (cousin and closest friend); Applejack and Big McIntosh (cousins); Aunt Orange and Uncle Orange among her Manehattan relatives; an older sister named Sunny in the *Equestria Girls 2013 Annual* [expanded canon]; the Apple family; fellow Crusaders Scootaloo and Sweetie Belle.
**Background/Biography:** Babs debuts in *One Bad Apple* (S3E4) as Apple Bloom's Manehattan cousin, staying in Ponyville because she is being bullied back home. The CMC try to welcome her, but her pain turns her into a bully who mocks them as "Cutie Mark Crybabies." After the Crusaders confront her, she breaks down, admits she was bullied for her blank flank, apologizes and joins the CMC; the group celebrates with the song "Babs Seed" before she returns home and starts her own branch. She returns in *Apple Family Reunion* (S3E8), and in *Bloom & Gloom* (S5E4) she writes Apple Bloom a letter announcing she earned her cutie mark — scissors — and appears in Apple Bloom's nightmare-altered reunion photo. She has a cameo in *Made in Manehattan* (S5E16) and is referenced in later seasons and in IDW comics, including the *Equestria Girls 2013 Annual*.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** A broad, heavy New York City accent — "Heya, cuz!", "How y'been?", "Forget about it!", "See ya later" — punchy city slang and teasing patter; mocking and sing-song when hurt ("Looks like somepony's pumpkin just got squashed!"); warm and fierce when defending friends ("Hey! That's not how you talk to my friends!").
**Notable Quotes:** "Looks like somepony's pumpkin just got squashed!" · "See ya later, Cutie Mark Crybabies!" · "Hey! That's not how you talk to my friends!" · "What am I, gonna miss out on spendin' time with my favorite cousin? Forget about it!" · "Heya, cuz! How y'been? Sunny an' me have been lookin' forward to meetin' you."
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Brynna Drummond; called "Sweetie Babs" in some merchandise. Her cutie mark (scissors with apple-shaped handles) is revealed in *Bloom & Gloom*; IDW *Friends Forever* #13 had already hinted at her mane-styling talent, which writer Jeremy Whitley confirmed. Her bullying-and-redemption arc is often praised as one of the show's best anti-bullying stories, and the Ramones-flavored song "Babs Seed" became a fan favorite. She is one of the few G4 foals with her own off-screen CMC chapter.

## Lyra Heartstrings

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — *Friendship is Magic* (background pony and occasional speaking role), with a human counterpart in the EQG films and digital series.
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn mare (human in EQG).
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare; shown as a foal in *Amending Fences* flashbacks.
**Residence:** Ponyville — she shares a home with Bon Bon (Sweetie Drops); originally from Canterlot, where she was one of Twilight Sparkle's old friends.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Ponyville resident and musician — her lyre cutie mark marks her as a string musician; in expanded canon she is a member of the Ponyville choir (book *Rarity and the Curious Case of Charity*) and an agent of S.M.I.L.E. (book *Lyra and Bon Bon and the Mares from S.M.I.L.E.*); in the show she helps friends with event organizing and is a constant, beloved background presence.
**Appearance:** Very pale mint/teal-green coat, pale cyan mane and tail with white streaks, bright golden-yellow eyes, and a cutie mark of a small golden lyre (harp). Slim unicorn build with a curved horn. No signature accessories; she is famously associated with sitting upright on benches like a human — a canon behavioral quirk noted in official trading-card flavor text. Her EQG human counterpart has pale green skin, mint-green hair with lighter streaks and gold eyes, and wears a CHS student outfit.
**Personality:** Cheerful, quirky, curious and imaginative, with a dreamy, slightly spacey charm. She is warm and loyal, especially to Bon Bon, and loves music, festivals and new experiences. She asks odd, deadpan questions about everyday things (bowling balls, "two left hooves"), gets gleeful when confessing secrets, and wears her heart on her sleeve. Her flaws are being scattered, gullible and impulsive; she can be talked into things and sometimes overshares.
**Likes:** Music — playing her lyre and singing; Bon Bon and their candy shop; sweets; her old Canterlot friends; festivals and concerts; comfortable human-style sitting; learning silly facts.
**Dislikes:** Being left out or forgotten, dishonest friends, bad manners, being told what to do, losing touch with old friends.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Losing Bon Bon or her circle of friends; being abandoned (she remembers the Moondancer party snub); gullible and easily led; no combat feats — an ordinary unicorn who relies on her magic and friends.
**Skills/Abilities:** Unicorn magic (telekinesis/levitation, varied golden-amber aura); accomplished lyre/harp player and singer (Ponyville choir in expanded canon); S.M.I.L.E. agent training in the book canon; strong social and emotional intuition; event help and party setup.
**Family & Relationships:** Bon Bon / Sweetie Drops — best friend, housemate and, per official cards, Valentine's posts and later material, romantic partner (treated as her spouse in some sources); Twilight Sparkle — old friend from Canterlot, reunited in *Amending Fences*; the old Canterlot circle of Minuette, Twinkleshine, Lemon Hearts and Moondancer; Ponyville background friends; her EQG human counterpart is close to human Bon Bon.
**Background/Biography:** Lyra begins as a background unicorn in Season 1 crowd scenes and gets her first lines in *A Canterlot Wedding* (S2E25–26), voiced then by Britt McKillip. She becomes a fan favorite through small, characterful appearances: *Slice of Life* (S5E9) as Bon Bon's confidante through the S.M.I.L.E. reveal; *Amending Fences* (S5E12), which reveals that she was one of Twilight's Canterlot friends who attended Moondancer's party and was hurt when Twilight skipped it; *The Mane Attraction* (S5E24) at Coloratura's concert; a "vanilla strawberry cream is overused" line in *Triple Threat*; and dozens of market, festival and concert scenes through Season 9, plus the future epilogue in *The Last Problem*. She appears in *My Little Pony: The Movie* crowds, and expanded canon — chapter books, *Ponyville Mysteries*, IDW comics and trading cards — gives her music, spy and slice-of-life stories.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Breezy, warm and chatty; effusive about friends ("Have I ever told you how lucky I am to have a friend like you?"); non-sequitur questions delivered deadpan; gleeful, giggly when spilling secrets; calls Bon Bon by name with delight ("Bon Bon! BON BON! Over here!"); playful and musical vocabulary.
**Notable Quotes:** "Bon Bon! BON BON! Over here!" · "Anything's possible when you know somepony as well as we know each other!" · "I don't know why I'm the only pony in Ponyville who sits like this. It's comfy!" · "With you by my side, I knew we'd get it done in time." · "I love them."
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Britt McKillip in *A Canterlot Wedding* and by Ashleigh Ball from *Slice of Life* onward (English). "Heartstrings" evokes the phrase "tug at the heartstrings." Her human-like sitting posture on benches is a long-running fandom meme, acknowledged in official trading-card flavor text; her EQG human counterpart actually has hands, which feeds the "Lyra wants hands" joke. The Lyra/Bon Bon pairing is one of the most enduring ships in the fandom and has been supported by official Valentine's posts. Her lyre cutie mark is sometimes drawn as a seven-stringed harp in fan art.

## Lightning Dust

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — *Friendship is Magic*; no human-world counterpart is established (she does not appear in EQG).
**Species/Kind:** Pegasus mare.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Young adult mare; shown as a filly in a *Parental Glideance* flashback.
**Residence:** Cloudsdale (pegasus city); later travels with her stunt team, the Washouts.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Former Wonderbolts Academy cadet (expelled, becoming a "washout"); founder and captain of the Washouts, a renegade stunt-flying troupe of ex-Wonderbolt recruits; professional stunt performer.
**Appearance:** Bright turquoise/aqua coat, bright amber mane with bright yellow-gold stripes (windswept and swept up), bright golden-yellow eyes, athletic pegasus build. Her cutie mark is a white lightning bolt with three stars — two orange and one yellow. When she flies she leaves a distinctive blue-and-yellow lightning-bolt contrail, contrasting with Rainbow Dash's rainbow and the Wonderbolts' storm-cloud trails. She wears the Wonderbolts Academy cadet flight suit in Season 3 and a Washouts stunt suit later.
**Personality:** Brash, cocky, hyper-competitive and thrill-seeking, with an abrasive edge and a chip on her shoulder. She genuinely believes winning matters more than friendship and that safety rules are for lesser fliers; she is a sore loser, a grudge-holder and quick with a put-down. Yet she is also brave, charismatic in a cocky way, honest about her own ruthlessness, and impressively dedicated to her craft. She is a deliberate shadow archetype of Rainbow Dash — all the talent and ambition, none of the humility — and is one of the show's few antagonists who never reforms on screen.
**Likes:** Speed, danger, pushing her limits ("I wanna push my limits"), winning, high-risk stunts, the spotlight, being acknowledged as the best, mocking cautious fliers.
**Dislikes:** Safety rules and red tape, waiting her turn, being second-best or shown up, Rainbow Dash (whom she blames for her expulsion), being told she is reckless, "slow" or cautious ponies.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Her own recklessness endangers herself and everyone around her; pride and revenge drive her decisions; she cannot accept being inferior or wrong; she has no real friends, only admirers and crew; she never learns her lesson, which leaves her isolated.
**Skills/Abilities:** Outstanding natural flyer — extremely fast, agile, powerful wing power; master of high-speed stunts and trick flying; able to generate a devastating tornado when paired with another strong flyer; lead-pony caliber in the Academy; captain and choreographer of the Washouts; limitations: poor judgment, weak teamwork and zero willingness to take responsibility.
**Family & Relationships:** No canon family [not established in canon]. Rival: Rainbow Dash — from Wonderbolts Academy through *The Washouts*, where she calls Rainbow her "rival for life"; former commanding officers Spitfire and Soarin' expelled her from the Academy; her Washouts crew includes Rolling Thunder and Short Fuse; Scootaloo briefly idolized her and was recruited by her in *The Washouts*. In IDW comics she crosses paths with Rainbow Dash (issue #34) and appears in the *Siege of the Crystal Empire* storyline.
**Background/Biography:** In *Wonderbolts Academy* (S3E7) Lightning Dust is the star cadet, named lead pony over Rainbow Dash; she ignores safety protocols and creates a tornado that nearly kills the other cadets, then refuses to accept blame, so Spitfire expels her. She appears as a foal in a *Parental Glideance* (S7E7) flashback. She returns in *The Washouts* (S8E20), claiming that being kicked out "was the best thing that ever happened" to her: she now leads the Washouts, a stunt-flying troupe of former Wonderbolt washouts who break every safety rule for applause. She recruits Scootaloo as a fan-turned-performer, but Rainbow Dash intervenes before Scootaloo's rocket-powered debut ends in disaster and saves her. Tangled in a rope and dragged skyward by the runaway rocket, Lightning Dust screams that Rainbow Dash is her rival for life; she is unrepentant to the end. IDW comics continue her story as a recurring rival and cautionary figure.

## Scootaloo

## Pear Butter

She is playful and teasing with Bright Mac, musical (she loves to sing), and a tireless worker who made a happy home. ## Shining Armor

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — *Friendship is Magic*; a human counterpart appears in *Equestria Girls: Friendship Games*.
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn stallion (human in EQG).
**Gender:** Male (he/him).
**Age/Stage:** Adult stallion; older brother of Twilight Sparkle; shown younger in *A Canterlot Wedding* flashbacks and as a new father in *The Crystalling*.
**Residence:** The Crystal Empire (co-ruler with Princess Cadance); formerly Canterlot, his family home and captain's posting.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Prince of the Crystal Empire and co-ruler with his wife Princess Cadance; Captain of the Canterlot Royal Guard, serving Princess Celestia and Princess Luna; head of the Crystal Empire's defenses, which he and Cadance maintain with a joint protection spell.
**Appearance:** White coat, blue mane and tail with lighter sky-blue and darker navy-blue stripes, medium sky-blue eyes, blue hooves, unicorn horn, strong guard build. His cutie mark is a violet/purple shield bearing a pink six-pointed star with three small bright-blue stars above it — a guardian design. He wears gold Royal Guard armor with a plumed helmet as captain, and formal royal regalia as a prince. Temporary forms include a crystalline Crystal Pony (*The Crystal Empire*), a cutie-mark-less state when Tirek drains him, and comic-only alternate-universe and petrified-statue versions. His EQG human counterpart in *Friendship Games* is a young man with pale skin and blue hair — a Crystal Prep alumnus who encourages his sister and shows interest in Dean Cadance.
**Personality:** Brave, dutiful, honorable and protective — a born soldier and a devoted family pony. He is Twilight's "B.B.B.F.F." (Big Brother Best Friend Forever), a doting husband to Cadance and an adorably overwhelmed father to Flurry Heart. He is level-headed in a crisis and self-sacrificing to a fault (he holds the wedding shield alone until his strength gives out), but he can be overprotective, a little dense (he fails to notice the changeling impersonating Cadance until Twilight shows him) and endearingly dorky when panicked, especially about fatherhood.
**Likes:** Cadance, Twilight ("Twily"), Flurry Heart, guard drills and military order, protecting others, the Crystal Empire, family traditions and ceremonies, his role as big brother; comics add his flugelhorn recitals.
**Dislikes:** Threats to the ponies he protects, changelings and traitors (he is initially prejudiced against all changelings after the invasion, then befriends Thorax), being powerless when his magic fails, and his family being in danger.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Losing his loved ones or failing as their protector (his shield collapses when Chrysalis reveals herself); his trusting nature left him vulnerable to Chrysalis's deception; fatherhood stress; as a unicorn he has no wings and his greatest spells are defensive and physically draining.
**Skills/Abilities:** Elite unicorn magic — powerful defensive barriers and protection spells (the Canterlot wedding shield and the Crystal Empire's shield sustained with Cadance), energy beams, levitation and teleportation; Royal Guard combat training, command and strategy; physical strength and discipline; joint spellwork with Cadance to defend the Crystal Empire.
**Family & Relationships:** Father Night Light; mother Twilight Velvet ("Mr. and Mrs. Sparkle"); younger sister Twilight Sparkle; wife Princess Cadance, married in *A Canterlot Wedding*; daughter Flurry Heart, born in *The Crystalling*; Spike — Twilight's dragon assistant, a familial "little brother"; Cadance is Princess Celestia's niece, making Celestia and Luna his aunts by marriage; friends include the Mane Six and Thorax; foes include Queen Chrysalis, King Sombra and Tirek.
**Background/Biography:** Shining Armor debuts in *A Canterlot Wedding* (S2E25–26) as Twilight's beloved big brother and Captain of the Royal Guard, engaged to Princess Cadance. The "Cadance" he guards is actually Queen Chrysalis in disguise, whose spell enslaves him; Twilight's accusation gets her banished, the real Cadance frees him, his shield fails, and he and Cadance defeat Chrysalis with the power of their love before marrying. In *The Crystal Empire* (S3E1–2) he and Cadance are appointed co-rulers of the Crystal Empire and hold its protective shield against King Sombra; he is temporarily turned into a crystal pony. He attends Twilight's coronation in *Magical Mystery Cure* (S3E13), loses his magic and cutie mark to Tirek in *Twilight's Kingdom* (S4E25–26), and becomes a father to alicorn baby Flurry Heart in *The Crystalling* (S6E1–2), where he fights his panic about parenthood. In *The Times They Are A Changeling* (S6E16) he overcomes his anti-changeling prejudice and befriends Thorax. He appears at the Equestria Games ("Let the games begin!"), weddings, *Once Upon a Zeppelin* and the final season and future epilogue of *The Last Problem*. In EQG's *Friendship Games* his human counterpart encourages Sci-Twi and flirts with Dean Cadance; IDW comics add alternate-universe and petrified-storyline versions.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Warm, big-brotherly and often enthusiastic; military formality as captain ("The burden of keeping Canterlot safe and secure rests squarely on my shoulders"); goofy rambling when anxious (fatherhood); proud, affectionate family speech ("Love ya, Twily"); rallying exclamations for troops and friends ("Let the games begin!", "Are we gonna gallop, or are we gonna trot!?").
**Notable Quotes:** "None of this would've been possible without you, little sis. Love ya, Twily." · "You're my little sister. Of course you're important to me." · "Let's just say the Empire... isn't the only thing that's returned." · "Never better! Being a father is amazing! **Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Andrew Francis (English); his title is Prince Shining Armor, and family nicknames include "Shiny," "Shiny-Winey" and "B.B.B.F.F." (from Twilight). His cutie mark is the purple shield with a pink six-pointed star and three blue stars. He and Cadance are the first pony couple whose wedding is shown on screen, and his love for her literally powers the shield that defeats Chrysalis. The IDW comics give him a flugelhorn recital and an alternate-universe counterpart; in *The Crystalling* he shares a voice actor with his on-screen father, Night Light (Andrew Francis).
