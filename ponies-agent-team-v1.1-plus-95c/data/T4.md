## Wallflower Blush

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — human-world character introduced in the 2018 one-hour special *Forgotten Friendship* (and its novelization *A Friendship to Remember*).
**Species/Kind:** Human (Canterlot High student). A pony version of her appears in the IDW comic *Nightmare Knights* #3 and in Equestria Girls merchandise.
**Gender:** Female.
**Age/Stage:** Teenager, high-school age.
**Residence:** Canterlot (human world); she attends Canterlot High School.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** CHS student; founder and de facto caretaker of the school's gardening club; the central antagonist of *Forgotten Friendship*; later a friend of the Rainbooms.
**Appearance:** Pale gray-green skin, medium teal-green hair worn in a soft shoulder-length style with a small flower/leaf clip, gray-orange eyes, and a gentle, forgettable face. She dresses in quiet earth tones and greens — a green top over a skirt, boots, and a canvas gardener's bag — and often has dirt or leaves on her hands. No cutie mark (human form). When she uses the Memory Stone her eyes and hands glow green. Her pony version (comics/merchandise) is a pale-green earth pony with a teal mane.
**Personality:** Shy, soft-spoken, kind, observant, and deeply lonely. She has spent years being overlooked, and her ways of asking for attention are quiet — tending the school garden, remembering people's names — until the Memory Stone gives her darker options. Under the stone's influence she becomes passive-aggressive, petty, and vindictive, but she is never a monster: she is hurt and wants to be remembered. Once forgiven she is warm, loyal, guilt-ridden, and delighted by ordinary friendship. She mumbles, second-guesses herself, and apologizes constantly.
**Likes:** Plants and gardening, being useful, the CHS garden, flowers and growing things, being called by name, small acts of kindness, quiet corners, and (after the special) her new friends.
**Dislikes:** Being ignored or forgotten, crowds of popular students, being talked over, being pitied, her own invisibility, and the temptation of the Memory Stone.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Being forgotten again; loneliness; social anxiety; jealousy that makes her lash out; physical weakness (she has no fighting skill); her magic depends entirely on the Memory Stone.
**Skills/Abilities:** Expert gardener and horticulturist; patient and perceptive; with the Memory Stone she can erase and rewrite memories in individuals or groups — including erasing all memory of a person — and can project memories as images. Otherwise an ordinary, unathletic student. Limitation: all of her power is the stone's, and taking it away leaves her a normal human.
**Family & Relationships:** No family is established in canon. Friends and allies: Sunset Shimmer (the one who understands and forgives her), the Rainbooms (human Twilight Sparkle, Applejack, Rainbow Dash, Pinkie Pie, Rarity, Fluttershy), and Trixie, who helps Sunset investigate. Her relationship with Sunset — being seen and named at last — is the emotional core of the special.
**Background/Biography:** In *Forgotten Friendship*, Wallflower is the shy girl who founded Canterlot High's gardening club yet is never remembered by anyone. She finds the Memory Stone (an Equestrian artifact) and begins erasing memories, first of small slights and then of herself, so she can secretly reshape how the school sees her. She deletes the Rainbooms' memories of Sunset Shimmer's friendship and edits their pasts. Sunset discovers a photo of herself with people who do not know her, investigates with Trixie, crosses to Equestria for Twilight's help, and learns the stone's nature. Wallflower traps Sunset and tries to erase her completely; Sunset instead reaches out, apologizes for the way the "forgotten" girl has been treated, and offers real friendship. Moved and ashamed, Wallflower gives up the stone, the memories are restored, and she is welcomed into the group — finally remembered.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Quiet, hesitant, mumbling; starts sentences with "I—" and trails off; self-deprecating soft humor. When angry her voice goes flat and brittle-sarcastic. Her signature sentiment is that she only wanted someone to remember her.
**Notable Quotes:** [paraphrased] "I'm the girl nobody remembers." · [paraphrased] "You're all so busy being friends... nobody ever noticed me." · [paraphrased] "You're the only one who ever asked my name."
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Shannon Chan-Kent (English) and Haruka Tomatsu (Japanese). First appearance: *My Little Pony: Equestria Girls — Forgotten Friendship* (2018). Her name is a pun on "wallflower," a shy person at the edge of a group. She is the founder of the CHS gardening club. The pony version in *Nightmare Knights* #3 and an Equestria Girls doll line are her other appearances. Exact details of her pony cutie mark are [not established in canon]; some minor merchandise credits are [uncertain].

## Moondancer

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / *Friendship is Magic* (debut S5E12 "Amending Fences"), with a cameo in the Equestria Girls special *Forgotten Friendship*, multiple IDW comic appearances, and a mention as far back as the series premiere.
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn.
**Gender:** Female.
**Age/Stage:** Young adult mare; shown as a filly in flashbacks and comics.
**Residence:** Canterlot — a run-down house she keeps as a scholar's den; in the novel *Princess Twilight Sparkle and the Forgotten Books of Autumn* she still lives in Twilight Sparkle's old Canterlot library.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Former student of Princess Celestia's School for Gifted Unicorns; private scholar, researcher, and bibliophile; later a social pony with a wide circle of friends; member of Canterlot's bowling team in the IDW comics.
**Appearance:** Pale yellowish-gray coat; bright purple-red (magenta) mane with soft-purple and gray-violet stripes, styled much like Twilight Sparkle's mane and tail; dark purple eyes; round glasses; usually a cozy knit sweater and skirt. Her cutie mark is a crescent moon with three stars — a purple crescent and three pink stars (an earlier comic colored it gray with black stars). Build is average and slightly slouched; her adolescent design is smaller with the same striped mane.
**Personality:** Brilliant, precise, bookish, and introverted. She is blunt and prickly when wounded and can nurse a grudge for years; behind the sarcasm she is passionate about learning and quietly generous. She hates insincerity, warms slowly, and is fiercely loyal once trust is rebuilt. Her arc is about opening the door again and letting old friends back in.
**Likes:** Books, classical literature, quiet study, libraries, exact knowledge, being taken seriously, her old classmates, and (by the end) games and outings with friends.
**Dislikes:** Parties and being made the center of attention, being ignored, loud crowds, being lied to, and having her feelings treated as a joke.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Being abandoned or forgotten; social rejection; turning bitter and shutting everyone out forever; being laughed at for caring.
**Skills/Abilities:** Outstanding scholar and researcher; advanced unicorn magic from Celestia's school (levitation, study magic, precise control); excellent memory for texts and references; decent athlete once she joins her friends for ball games. Limitations: no combat magic, poor social instincts, and a tendency to hide in work.
**Family & Relationships:** An unnamed father, formerly of the Canterlot Historical Society (mentioned in the novel), and a sister, Morning Roast. Friends: Twilight Sparkle (childhood friend and the source of her heartbreak), Twinkleshine, Lemon Hearts, and Minuette — the old Canterlot circle — plus Spike, Princess Celestia (former teacher), and her later friends at the School of Friendship.
**Background/Biography:** As a filly at Celestia's school, Moondancer was one of Twilight Sparkle's first friends and planned a party at the castle's west courtyard. Twilight skipped it for her studies, and the hurt turned Moondancer into a recluse who gave up on friendship and filled her house with books. In "Amending Fences" Twilight realizes the damage, tries to reconnect through their shared love of knowledge, gives her library access, and finally stages a party that forces the old wound open. Moondancer explodes at Twilight, then hears a sincere apology and reconciles, rejoining Twinkleshine, Lemon Hearts, and Minuette. She appears in later seasons (including as one of King Sombra's mind-controlled ponies in S9), in *Forgotten Friendship*, in *The Movie*, and across the IDW comics, and she is remembered in the canon guidebook as a pony who gave friendship a second chance.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Measured, clipped, and dryly sardonic; uses precise academic vocabulary and deadpan jokes. At her most hurt she becomes loud and defensive; when comfortable she is warm and playfully pedantic.
**Notable Quotes:** "Oh. I've got the wrong book. That's *so* hilarious." · "Why won't you leave me alone? I'm trying to study! You've got the wrong pony! I don't *have* parties!" · "Dah! I gave friendship a chance a long time ago! It didn't work out then, it isn't gonna work out now!" · "Thank you for helping me make some new friends. Even if they are my *old* friends."
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Kazumi Evans. Designed by Kora Kosicka; her mane and tail mirror Twilight Sparkle's. First mentioned in S1E1 "Friendship is Magic" (Twinkleshine mentions her party) and first seen in S5E12. Both spellings "Moondancer" (credits, comics, game) and "Moon Dancer" (closed captions, Playskool merchandise) are used canonically. Her name comes from a G1 unicorn pony, and her design is based on that G1 character. Appears in the Gameloft mobile game with the description that Twilight's help taught her friendship is magic.

## Sonata Dusk

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — a siren from Equestria who lives in the human world; main antagonist of *Rainbow Rocks* and a recurring EG character.
**Species/Kind:** Siren (Equestrian), transformed into a human in the human world; her true siren form has fin-wings and webbed fins. Merchandise also depicts her as an earth pony.
**Gender:** Female.
**Age/Stage:** Ancient — over a thousand years old — but she takes the form of a teenage girl.
**Residence:** The human world (Canterlot area); formerly Equestria; she travels with her sisters in the Dazzlings' van.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Backing vocalist of the Dazzlings (the siren band); temporarily enrolled at Canterlot High during *Rainbow Rocks*; later a guest performer at the Star Swirl Music Festival.
**Appearance:** As a human: pale blue-white skin, pale arctic-blue hair with medium Persian-blue streaks, medium strawberry eyes, and a bright, pretty smile. She wears a cyan-and-blue top, a pleated skirt, boots, and the Dazzlings' red gem pendant at her throat — the siren gem that holds her power. Siren form: light cyan scales, bright azure belly, deep blue fins and hooves, fin-like wings; her half-siren state adds pony ears, a long ponytail, and translucent fins. Merchandise gives her a cutie mark of a pink heart bearing a jagged blue-purple eighth note.
**Personality:** Bubbly, ditzy, childlike, and food-obsessed — the comic relief of the trio. She is not especially bright, easily distracted, and cheerfully tactless, but she is also genuinely good-natured in her own way and deeply loyal to Adagio and Aria. She enjoys being powerful and adored far more than she enjoys being evil, and she sulks adorably when ignored. Her bickering with Aria is constant.
**Likes:** Tacos (her defining passion, especially "Taco Tuesday"), food in general, singing and performing, attention and applause, being praised, naps, and having fun with her sisters.
**Dislikes:** Being hungry, being bossed around, Aria's insults, losing her voice or magic, boring plans, schoolwork, and being left out.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Separation from her sisters; humiliation (losing her voice and being booed offstage); her own gullibility and naivety; she is no strategist and is weak without her gem.
**Skills/Abilities:** A siren's hypnotic song — her voice entrances crowds, sows discord, and lets the Dazzlings feed on the negative energy they stir up; strong harmonizing and stage presence; in siren form she can hover on her fin-wings and project a phantom siren illusion from the gem. Limitations: without the gem she has no magic and sings badly off-key.
**Family & Relationships:** Adagio Dazzle (older sister, the trio's leader) and Aria Blaze (older sister); the three are the Dazzlings and act as a band. Rivals: the Rainbooms (especially Sunset Shimmer, Twilight Sparkle, and the human Mane Six). Later on she is on mocking terms with Sunset rather than openly hostile.
**Background/Biography:** Sonata is an ancient siren from Equestria. Long ago the Dazzlings tried to conquer Equestria with their dark song and were stopped by Star Swirl the Bearded, who banished them to the human world; there their power faded and they learned to feed on the discord they could create. In *Rainbow Rocks* they arrive at Canterlot High, bewitch the students with a café performance, and turn a friendly music festival into a Battle of the Bands, growing stronger on every rivalry and insult. In the final battle the Rainbooms' music shatters the Dazzlings' gems; they lose their voices, sing hilariously off-key, and are chased offstage. They resurface in the EG short "Find the Magic" at the Star Swirl Music Festival, singing about adapting to life without magic, and in *Sunset's Backstage Pass* they guest-star at the festival, are wrongly suspected of causing the time loop, mock Sunset, and drive off — with Sonata cheerfully mentioning she once set a fire by putting tacos in a toaster.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Valley-girl-ish, airy, and casually loud; says "for realsies," "like," and "omigosh," blurts non sequiturs, and turns nearly every topic toward food. Simple, wide-eyed sentences; giggles; when scolded she says "Wha— what did I say?"
**Notable Quotes:** "For realsies? Because I think this place is the worst." · "But we can get lunch after though, right? It's Taco Tuesday!" · "How was I supposed to know you can't put tacos in a toaster?"
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Marieve Herington (English) and Megumi Han (Japanese). Debut: *My Little Pony: Equestria Girls — Rainbow Rocks* (2014); also appears in *Friendship Games* (painting/photo cameos), the EG second-season shorts, *Sunset's Backstage Pass*, and is mentioned in *Holidays Unwrapped*. The "taco" running gag is her best-known fandom meme. Her name (a musical "sonata") fits the Dazzlings' naming scheme; the trio's shared gem pendants are their power source. Her siren form is briefly shown in *Shadow Play* flashbacks.

## Starlight Glimmer

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / *Friendship is Magic* (debut S5E1 "The Cutie Map"), plus a human counterpart in the Equestria Girls special *Mirror Magic*.
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn; temporarily a crystal pony (S6E2) and, via disguises, an earth pony and a "cutie-markless" pony painted with an equal sign. A human-world Starlight appears in EG.
**Gender:** Female.
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare; shown as a filly in flashbacks.
**Residence:** The Castle of Friendship, Ponyville (from S6E1); formerly the founder/mayor of Our Town (Equality Town); hometown Sire's Hollow.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Headmare of the School of Friendship (from S9E20); before that its guidance counselor (S8) and Twilight Sparkle's personal student (S5 finale–S7); unopposed founder and leader of Equality Town; one of the most powerful unicorns in Equestria.
**Appearance:** Pale lavender-gray coat; mane and tail dark purple with lighter purple and bright cyan-blue stripes; medium Persian-blue eyes; pale blue-green magic aura. Her cutie mark is a purple-and-white star with two glowing, two-toned cyan-blue streaks. While running Our Town she hid it under a painted gray equal sign. Later-season design adds a longer, flowing mane as headmare; her human form in EG keeps the same coloring. Crystal-pony and future-headmare forms exist as temporary looks.
**Personality:** Clever, driven, and sardonic, with a deep streak of guilt and a perfectionist's need for control. She is dryly funny, socially awkward, and fiercely loyal; she plans everything and hates being helpless. She genuinely wants to help other ponies and will bend rules — or minds — if she believes the goal justifies it, then feel terrible afterward. Her defining wound is abandonment, and her growth is learning to trust friends with her flaws.
**Likes:** Studying and inventing spells, kites, order and planning, group activities she can oversee, her friends (Sunburst, Trixie, Maud, Twilight), and helping ponies solve friendship problems.
**Dislikes:** Cutie marks being used to divide ponies (her old ideology), chaos and improvisation, being ignored or abandoned, her past being thrown in her face, Queen Chrysalis, and feeling powerless.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Being abandoned again as she was by Sunburst; failing as a leader or counselor; becoming the pony who hurt others; her own immense power and temper; secrets and overplanning.
**Skills/Abilities:** Exceptional unicorn magic — teleportation, telekinesis, illusions, mass mind-control and memory spells, a cutie-mark-removal spell through the Staff of Sameness, a cutie-mark-swapping spell (used on Celestia and Luna), and time travel with Twilight's scroll. Also a skilled leader, counselor, organizer, and improviser. Limitations: emotional blind spots and a habit of overreaching; several spells have backfired badly.
**Family & Relationships:** Father Firelight of Sire's Hollow (over-eager, devoted); her mother is [not established in canon]. Her childhood best friend is Sunburst — the friend whose departure scarred her. Other bonds: Trixie (closest friend and partner in mischief), Maud Pie, Twilight Sparkle (mentor, then friend), Spike, the Mane Six, Thorax (ally), the Young Six (her students), and enemies Chrysalis, Cozy Glow, and King Sombra.
**Background/Biography:** As a filly in Sire's Hollow, Starlight was inseparable from Sunburst; when he earned his cutie mark and left for Celestia's school, she concluded that cutie marks destroy friendships. She studied magic obsessively and founded Our Town, where she removed ponies' cutie marks and enforced sameness. The Mane Six exposed her in "The Cutie Map," and she fled. In "The Cutie Re-Mark" she stole Twilight's time-travel scroll and fractured history to stop the Rainbooms from ever meeting; Twilight showed her the resulting wastelands and then her own past, and Starlight understood that Sunburst's leaving was not abandonment. She accepted friendship and became Twilight's student. She defeated Chrysalis and freed the changelings ("To Where and Back Again"), swapped Celestia's and Luna's marks to repair their relationship ("A Royal Problem"), helped summon the Pillars ("Shadow Play"), served as School of Friendship counselor, and in "A Horse Shoe-In" became headmare — her role in the future epilogue. She fights at Twilight's side through the S9 finale.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Dry, quick, and precise, with a sardonic edge; deadpan one-liners and long technical explanations about magic. She addresses groups as "everypony" and softens only with close friends; when nervous she over-explains.
**Notable Quotes:** "We're all equal here." · [paraphrased] "I'm not that pony anymore — but I remember her." · [paraphrased] "Friendship isn't a weakness. It took me a long time to learn that."
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Kelly Sheridan (English), with several Chinese voice actresses across seasons. Debuted in S5E1 as the show's first major unicorn villain and became a main protagonist. Her many nicknames include Star, Mini-Twilight, Sugarbun, Punkie Wunk, and Silly-Filly. Sometimes called "Starlight Shimmer" in some licensed media. In EG's *Mirror Magic* a human Starlight appears at Canterlot High, and the pony Starlight is shown visiting the human world. Her cutie mark's glow color and the exact episode titles for some cameos are [uncertain] in parts of the fandom record.

## Sunny Starscout

**Series/Continuity:** G5 — *My Little Pony: A New Generation* (2021 film), *Make Your Mark*, and *Tell Your Tale*.
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony who becomes an alicorn — her horn and wings are ethereal and appear when she uses magic, rather than a permanent physical transformation.
**Gender:** Female.
**Age/Stage:** Young adult mare (a filly in flashbacks).
**Residence:** Maretime Bay, on the coast of G5 Equestria; she lives in her father's old lighthouse, which later becomes the Mane Six's "Brighthouse" headquarters.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Smoothie maker (her own stand in Maretime Bay); self-appointed researcher/historian of the "Guardians of Harmony"; leader of the Mane Six (the new protectors of pony unity); the first alicorn of her era.
**Appearance:** Light orange/apricot coat; long violet-magenta mane with a lime-green streak, normally worn in a braid; large green eyes; sturdy but slim earth-pony build. She carries a canvas saddlebag covered in pins bearing the Mane Six's cutie marks, and she loves roller-skating (helmet included). Her cutie mark is a bright, warm stylized sun in pink, orange, and gold — a "hopeful sunrise" emblem that glows when her alicorn magic awakens. After her transformation her braid loosens into a mane with a permanent rainbow streak, and she grows an ethereal horn and feathery light-wings; her accessories vanish while she is transformed.
**Personality:** Curious, idealistic, adventurous, warm, and relentlessly optimistic — but not naïve. She is a born leader who inspires others by believing in them, a deadpan snarker when annoyed, and secretly full of self-doubt, often feeling "small" and like a pony who only causes trouble. She hides hurt behind cheer, argues for unity even when it makes her an outcast, and never gives up on friendship.
**Likes:** Smoothies, roller-skating, adventures, stories of Twilight Sparkle and the Guardians of Harmony, her friends, helping others, plant life and growing things, singing, and proving that the three pony kinds belong together.
**Dislikes:** Prejudice and fear-mongering, rules that separate ponies, being underestimated, her friends being hurt, Canterlogic's scare tactics, and giving up on anyone.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Losing her friends or family; failing to unite ponykind; her alicorn power is unreliable and tied to her desire to help, so it misfires when she is anxious; over-trusting; lingering self-doubt.
**Skills/Abilities:** As an earth pony she has earth-pony strength and plant affinity; as an alicorn she flies, levitates objects, casts powerful protective barriers and hard-light constructs (including an anti-magic dome copied from Opaline), and can summon enchanted glitter. Also an excellent smoothie chef, skate-rink athlete, researcher (evidence boards and detective work), and inspirational leader. Limitations: little training, so her strongest magic is hard to control.
**Family & Relationships:** Father Argyle Starshine (lighthouse keeper and dreamer, who raised her on stories of the Guardians of Harmony; deceased), and a mother who vanished on an errand long ago and is implied deceased. Friends: Hitch Trailblazer (childhood best friend and sheriff, with a shared secret hoofshake), Izzy Moonbow, Pipp Petals, Zipp Storm, and Misty Brightdawn; allies Sprout, Queen Haven, and Alphabittle; enemies Opaline Arcana and (in the comics) Allura.
**Background/Biography:** Sunny grew up in Maretime Bay hearing her father's stories of ancient Equestria, when earth ponies, pegasi, and unicorns lived in harmony. After he was gone she kept the lighthouse, worked her smoothie stand, and scandalized the town by insisting unicorns and pegasi weren't monsters. When a unicorn, Izzy Moonbow, actually turned up, Sunny hid her and set out to reunite ponykind. With Hitch in pursuit, the group traveled to Zephyr Heights, discovered the pegasus royal family had no magic either, escaped to Bridlewood for the unicorn crystal, and returned to Maretime Bay, where the three crystals were united and magic returned — and Sunny was transformed into an alicorn, the new protector of unity. In *Make Your Mark* she and her friends make the Brighthouse their base and defend Equestria's returned harmony, while Sunny learns her alicorn powers are triggered by a deep desire to help other ponies; together they face Opaline Arcana and help redeem Misty. In *Tell Your Tale* she leads the Mane Six's everyday adventures around Maretime Bay.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Upbeat, warm, and energetic American English; exclamations like "Oh my gosh!", "Come on!", and "We've got this!"; friendly slang ("guys," "pony"); she talks fast when excited and turns sincere when it matters. Her TYT battle catchphrase: "I'm all three pony kinds wrapped up into one. My cutie mark comes to life with hope bright as the sun!"
**Notable Quotes:** "I'm all three pony kinds wrapped up into one. My cutie mark comes to life with hope bright as the sun!" · [paraphrased] "When I was a filly, my dad told me stories about a time when all three kinds of ponies were friends. I want that back." · [paraphrased] "We're not so different. We just forgot how to see it."
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Vanessa Hudgens in *A New Generation* and Jenna Warren in the series. Debut: *My Little Pony: A New Generation* (2021). The first alicorn of G5 and the leader of its Mane Six; her element is Hope, and the permanent rainbow streak in her mane after her transformation is unlike the on/off horn and wings. G5 is set generations after G4, with Twilight and friends remembered as the Guardians of Harmony. Exact hex shades of her cutie mark and some *Tell Your Tale* episode details are [uncertain].

## Star Swirl the Bearded

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / *Friendship is Magic* — a legendary figure from ancient Equestria; long mentioned in lore and introduced on screen in S7's "Shadow Play"; also a major IDW comics character and a statue cameo in Equestria Girls material.
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn (male); an alicorn depiction appears in IDW's *Friendship is Magic* #19, and a colt version appears in a comic short.
**Gender:** Male.
**Age/Stage:** Ancient — over a thousand years old; he spent a millennium outside of time with the Pillars and appears as an old stallion.
**Residence:** Equestria; after his return he lives among the modern ponies and is associated with Canterlot and Twilight's circle.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Wizard and mage — arguably the most famous unicorn wizard in Equestrian history; leader of the Pillars of Old Equestria; creator of foundational spells and magical objects; author of spellbooks and journals; mentor and advisor to later generations.
**Appearance:** Elderly light gray/white unicorn with a long flowing white beard and mane, deep violet-gray eyes, and a star-studded blue pointed wizard hat with a bell, worn with a blue cape and cloak; light gray hooves; a bluish-gray magic aura. His cutie mark is a swirling star (S8E16), with alternate designs in other media showing a swirl with four stars or six blue five-pointed stars above two dark blue swirls. As a young colt his mane was brown. Voice: Chris Britton.
**Personality:** Brilliant, proud, curt, and old-fashioned. He is imperious, impatient with foolishness, and convinced that hard choices are his to make — a "my way or the highway" mentor who lectures at length and bristles at being contradicted. Underneath the grandeur he cares deeply about protecting Equestria and his fellow Pillars, and he learns humility and trust from Twilight Sparkle.
**Likes:** Order and precision, immaculate spellwork, history and research, being right, teaching by lecture, protecting Equestria, astronomical study, and his fellow Pillars.
**Dislikes:** Sloppy magic, emotional arguments, being doubted, modern "friendship lessons," needless risk to Equestria, the Pony of Shadows, the sirens, and disharmony of any kind.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Failing to protect Equestria; his arrogance causing harm (the Stygian catastrophe); losing his friends; his physical age; and his tendency to treat ponies as problems to be solved rather than people to be helped.
**Skills/Abilities:** One of the most powerful unicorns ever — mastery of nearly every school of magic, complex multi-target spells, teleportation, time and portal magic, and the creation of the Crystal Mirror that links Equestria to the human world. He banished the sirens to another world and helped seal the Pony of Shadows. Also a master strategist, astronomer, and author. Limitations: pride, poor empathy, and no patience for the subtlety of friendship.
**Family & Relationships:** An older brother is mentioned; no spouse or children are established. His student was Clover the Clever (the Hearth's Warming Eve story). Companions: the other Pillars — Rockhoof, Mistmane, Flash Magnus, Somnambula, and Mage Meadowbrook. Rival/enemy: Stygian, the Pony of Shadows. Admirer and successor: Twilight Sparkle, who completed his unfinished spell. He is remembered as an early mentor figure to Equestria's princesses [uncertain in detail].
**Background/Biography:** In ancient Equestria, Star Swirl was the realm's great wizard, teacher of Clover the Clever, author of countless spells, and the maker of the Crystal Mirror. He banished the Dazzlings' sirens to the human world, and with the five other Pillars he defeated the Pony of Shadows — his own friend Stygian, corrupted by dark magic — by sacrificing himself and the Pillars to seal it in limbo. For over a thousand years he was only a legend. In "Magical Mystery Cure," Twilight completes his unfinished spell, the act that ascends her to alicorn princess. In the S7 finale "Shadow Play," the Pony of Shadows returns and the Pillars are freed; Star Swirl insists on re-sealing Stygian, but Twilight and her friends prove the shadow can be redeemed and reunite Stygian with the Pillars. He returns to Equestria, and in later seasons appears as a revered elder and teacher figure who aids Equestria's defenders.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Formal, archaic, and grandiloquent; long sentences, rhetorical flourishes, and old-fashioned vocabulary. He addresses others as "young one" or "child," announces himself by full name, and delivers everything like a lecture. His humor is dry and self-important.
**Notable Quotes:** His unfinished spell: "From one to another, another to one. A mark of one's destiny singled out alone, fulfilled." · [paraphrased] "You have no idea what you are dealing with." · [paraphrased] "Some things must be done, no matter the cost."

## Twilight Velvet

## Princess Ember

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / *Friendship is Magic* — introduced in S6E5 "Gauntlet of Fire," later a recurring dragon leader in S8 and S9; also appears in IDW comics and merchandise.
**Species/Kind:** Dragon (female dragon; also titled Princess before her ascension). Dragons do not have cutie marks.
**Gender:** Female.
**Age/Stage:** Young adult dragon — roughly a teenage/young-adult perspective among dragons, and notably small compared to her enormous father.
**Residence:** The Dragon Lands (the dragons' volcanic homeland).
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Dragon Lord — the ruler of all dragons, a title earned through the Gauntlet of Fire and carried with the Bloodstone Scepter; she is the first Dragon Lord to open peaceful relations with ponies.
**Appearance:** Bright turquoise-blue scales with a pale turquoise belly, medium indigo-to-light-cobalt gradient wings, medium blue-violet dorsal spikes and a fin-like head crest, bright red eyes with slit pupils, and a pale green-gray horn. She is compact and agile rather than huge, with a proud, angular silhouette; as Dragon Lord she carries the Bloodstone Scepter and otherwise wears no clothing or jewelry. No cutie mark. In her earlier "princess" appearance she is smaller and softer, hiding behind her father's shadow.
**Personality:** Introduced as insecure and soft-spoken, afraid of losing to bigger dragons and desperate for her father's approval, Ember grows into a confident, commanding, and fair-minded ruler. She is proud, blunt, and short-tempered, with a regal "Dragon Lord" persona she wears like armor; underneath she is warm, curious, and loyal to her friends, especially Spike. She wants dragons to be honorable rather than merely strong, and she struggles to balance tradition with change.
**Likes:** Spike (her best friend), competition and tests of strength, flying, winning honestly, honor, her father's respect, ruling fairly, and the idea of dragon-pony friendship.
**Dislikes:** Being underestimated as "just a princess," bullies like Garble, dishonorable conduct, pony stereotypes about dragons, being called weak or small, and her father's terrifying roar.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Failing as Dragon Lord; losing the respect of her subjects; her own temper; being seen as weak; the loneliness of leadership; and a certain naivety about others' intentions.
**Skills/Abilities:** Powerful dragon flight, fire breath, and physical strength; keen reflexes and competitive instincts; natural leadership and a strong sense of justice; authority through the Bloodstone Scepter, which marks the true Dragon Lord and commands dragons' obedience. Limitations: young and relatively small for a dragon, hot-tempered, and still learning diplomacy.
**Family & Relationships:** Her father is Dragon Lord Torch, the vast red dragon she succeeds; her mother is [not established in canon]. Her closest friend is Spike the dragon, whom she trusts above all other non-dragons; allies include Twilight Sparkle and the ponies of Ponyville, and later Thorax and the changelings. Enemies include Garble and any dragon who scorns honor.
**Background/Biography:** In "Gauntlet of Fire," Dragon Lord Torch announces the Gauntlet of Fire to choose his successor, and his daughter Ember — dismissed by the other dragons as a weak princess — enters to prove herself. She shelters and teams up with Spike, who is there because the ponies need a dragon scale. Spike reaches the scepter first but hands it to Ember, making her Dragon Lord; her first act is to spare Spike and declare that dragons and ponies can be friends. In "Triple Threat" she visits Ponyville for a dragon-pony friendship summit, where Spike panics that she and Thorax will clash, and instead the two leaders get along. In "Dragon Dropped" her visit makes Spike's divided loyalties with Rarity hurt her feelings before they reconcile. She also appears in the IDW comics and in the mobile game, and is remembered in the finale as the Dragon Lord who brought her people into friendship with Equestria.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Declamatory and regal, with short, loud sentences: "I am Dragon Lord Ember!"; she uses "little pony" as a mild jab and speaks with a slight growl. As a princess she was soft, hesitant, and quick to defer; as Dragon Lord she is commanding, formal, and occasionally dryly amused. With Spike she drops the regal tone and is simply warm.
**Notable Quotes:** [paraphrased] "I am Ember, daughter of Dragon Lord Torch, and I am the new Dragon Lord!" · [paraphrased] "Spike is a true dragon — braver than any of you." · [paraphrased] "Dragons and ponies don't have to be enemies."
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Ali Milner (English). Debut: S6E5 "Gauntlet of Fire." She is the first female Dragon Lord and the first to seek friendship with ponies; the title and the Bloodstone Scepter are the core of dragon rule. Nicknamed "Dragon Lord Ember" in later appearances; "Sandy Rockbeach" is a pseudonym associated with licensed material. Her exact scale-color hexes vary between episodes and merchandise [minor variation].

## Aria Blaze

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — a siren from Equestria living in the human world; main antagonist of *Rainbow Rocks* and a recurring EG character.
**Species/Kind:** Siren (Equestrian) in human form; her true siren form has fins and fin-wings. Merchandise also depicts her as an earth pony.
**Gender:** Female.
**Age/Stage:** Ancient — over a thousand years old — appearing as a teenage human.
**Residence:** The human world (Canterlot area); formerly Equestria; she travels in the Dazzlings' van, which she drives.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Backing vocalist of the Dazzlings; temporarily a Canterlot High student during *Rainbow Rocks*; later a guest performer at the Star Swirl Music Festival.
**Appearance:** As a human: grayish bright pink-purple skin, medium purple hair with bright sea-green streaks, and medium purple-red eyes; she wears a dark purple top, skirt, and boots with the Dazzlings' red siren-gem pendant at her throat. Siren form: pale purple scales, bright purple belly, medium purple-red fins, dark purple hooves, and brighter purple-red eyes. Her half-siren state adds pony ears, a long ponytail, and translucent fins. Merchandise gives her a cutie mark of a two-toned purple star with a jagged, fierce emblem.
**Personality:** Cynical, sarcastic, dry, and impatient — the "straight mare" of the trio. She announces her contempt for everything and everyone, mocks Sonata's nonsense, and snipes at Adagio's bossiness, all in a flat deadpan. She is lazy about schemes but sharp about weaknesses, secretly enjoys performing, and is loyal to her sisters in her own grudging way. She considers herself above the human world and is not shy about saying so.
**Likes:** Quiet, being right, mocking others, singing and the stage, her sisters (grudgingly), a proper meal, power, and having the last word.
**Dislikes:** Fast food, Sonata's non sequiturs, Adagio's orders, the human world and its "awfulness," boredom, losing, and being forgotten.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Powerlessness — losing her magic and her voice; being stuck in the human world forever; her sisters' antics dragging her down; overconfidence and a lack of real planning.
**Skills/Abilities:** A siren's hypnotic song that entrances crowds and feeds the Dazzlings on negative energy; strong backing vocals and harmony; in siren form she can hover on fin-wings and project a phantom siren illusion from her gem. She is also the group's driver. Limitations: without the gem she has no magic and sings off-key.
**Family & Relationships:** Adagio Dazzle (older sister and the trio's leader) and Sonata Dusk (younger sister); together they are the Dazzlings. Rivals: the Rainbooms, particularly Sunset Shimmer, whom she enjoys needling; enemies-turned-frenemies by the end of the EG specials.
**Background/Biography:** Aria is an ancient siren from Equestria. The Dazzlings tried to conquer Equestria with their dark song, so Star Swirl the Bearded banished them to the human world, where they had to live on the disharmony they could provoke. In *Rainbow Rocks* the trio arrive at Canterlot High, complain that the world's magic is too thin, and use their song to enchant the students and turn the music festival into a Battle of the Bands, growing stronger on the rivalry. The Rainbooms defeat them, shatter their gems, and leave them voiceless and booed offstage. Aria returns in the EG short "Find the Magic," performing at the Star Swirl Music Festival and singing about adapting to magic-less life, and in *Sunset's Backstage Pass* she and her sisters guest-star at the festival; when Sunset suspects them of causing the time loop, Aria dryly notes the word "loop" and later declares that any Equestrian magic around belongs to them ("we call dibs") before driving off. She is mentioned in *Holidays Unwrapped* and appears in EG merchandise and the IDW comics.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Flat, sarcastic, and deadpan; clipped one-liners delivered with maximum disdain ("Great." "Seriously?" "Go back to sleep, Sonata."). She draws out scorn, uses rhetorical questions, and never sounds impressed. When she does sing, her voice is smooth and powerful.
**Notable Quotes:** "I'm tired of fast food. I need a meal." · "Go back to sleep, Sonata." · "By the way, if there's Equestrian magic afoot around here, we call dibs." · "I wish we'd never been banished to this awful place."
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Diana Kaarina, with Shylo Sharity as singing voice (English); Ayahi Takagaki in Japanese. Debut: *My Little Pony: Equestria Girls — Rainbow Rocks* (2014). Aria is the only Dazzling whose name is never spoken on screen in *Rainbow Rocks* (she is named in the film's novelization), a frequent piece of fan trivia. Her name and her sisters' names are musical puns (aria, sonata, adagio). Her siren form appears in *Shadow Play* flashbacks and EG paintings/photos. Her exact hair accessory details are [uncertain].

## Cozy Glow

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / *Friendship is Magic* — debuting in S8E12 "Marks for Effort," revealed as the main villain of S8, and a member of the S9 villains' alliance.
**Species/Kind:** Pegasus filly; temporarily an alicorn (S9E24–25) after stealing Grogar's bell magic, briefly wielding chaos magic; ultimately a stone statue.
**Gender:** Female.
**Age/Stage:** School-age filly (a young foal, younger than the Cutie Mark Crusaders).
**Residence:** Ponyville — a student at Twilight Sparkle's School of Friendship (S8E12–S8E26); then Tartarus (S8E26–S9E1); then Grogar's base with the villains (S9); finally the stone statue of herself, Chrysalis, and Tirek.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** School of Friendship student; self-appointed "Empress of Friendship" and would-be headmare; member of Grogar's/Discord's villain alliance alongside Queen Chrysalis and Lord Tirek.
**Appearance:** A tiny, adorable pegasus filly with a pinkish-gray coat, curly gray-blue and blue-gray mane and tail, and striking scarlet eyes; her cutie mark is a golden chess rook — a fitting emblem for a pony who treats other ponies as pieces in a game. She has small wings, a round face, and an innocent, wide-eyed expression she deploys like a weapon. Her temporary alicorn form (S9E24–25) is tall, glowing, and horned, and her chaos-power form is warped and unstable. Her final form is a frightened-looking stone statue beside Chrysalis and Tirek.
**Personality:** A sweet, chirpy, flattering facade over a cold, calculating, power-hungry core. Cozy Glow is a gifted manipulator who studied friendship only as a means of control, believes "friendship is power," and will use, frame, or abandon anyone to climb. She is arrogant, petty, bigoted toward non-ponies, and prone to childish tantrums when thwarted — yet oddly desperate for company, which is why she keeps trying to befriend Tirek and Chrysalis. She feels no real remorse.
**Likes:** Power and titles, being in charge, flattery and manipulation, sweets and cute things, planning, winning, and being praised as adorable.
**Dislikes:** Being dismissed as a child, losing, non-pony creatures (dragons, changelings, griffons, yaks), being ordered around by Tirek or Chrysalis, rules, school, and being locked up.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Being powerless and insignificant; abandonment; her arrogance makes her betray her own allies; she cannot inspire genuine loyalty, only fear; she has no magic of her own except what she steals; her childishness undermines her schemes.
**Skills/Abilities:** Master deceiver and strategist — she manipulates the Cutie Mark Crusaders, the Young Six, Twilight's students, and even Tirek; she knows the School's artifacts, schedules, and secrets and can orchestrate mobs and frame rivals. With Grogar's bell she gains immense alicorn-level power, and for a time chaos magic. Otherwise she is a small filly with no fighting ability.
**Family & Relationships:** No family is established in canon — the show's staff said her parentage was never discussed ("maybe she has no parents"). Relationships are entirely instrumental: Twilight Sparkle (her target and dupe), the Cutie Mark Crusaders (used, then imprisoned), the Young Six (manipulated), Chancellor Neighsay (played), and her villain allies Tirek and Chrysalis, whom she pesters into a fractious partnership.
**Background/Biography:** Cozy Glow first appears as a sweet, struggling transfer student whom the Cutie Mark Crusaders help; she secretly fails her friendship test on purpose to get them in trouble. Over season 8 she befriends everyone while quietly studying the Tree of Harmony and the school's defenses. In the finale "School Raze," she drops the act: with Tirek she steals the school's ancient artifacts and drains Equestria's magic, locks the Crusaders in a closet, turns the students against the Young Six, and declares herself headmare on her way to becoming "Empress of Friendship." The Tree of Harmony's intervention restores the magic, and she is imprisoned in Tartarus. In season 9 Grogar frees her, and she joins Tirek and Chrysalis; "Frenemies" shows her trying to force the three of them to cooperate. In "The Ending of the End" the trio gains Grogar's power (actually Discord's), Cozy becomes an alicorn, and she briefly wields chaos magic before losing control. Defeated by the Mane Six and the united army of Equestria's creatures, she is turned to stone with her two allies — the statue standing as a warning in the finale.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Bright, sing-song, and sugary when performing innocence ("Golly!", "Oh my gosh!", "Cutie!"); flattering and polite. When exposed she switches instantly to cold, gloating villain speech with formal titles ("the future Empress of Friendship"). She calls Tirek "Tirek" and Chrysalis "Chrysalis" with fake warmth.
**Notable Quotes:** "Golly!" · "Friendship isn't magic. Friendship is power!" · "This is not the real me! I'm cute and lovable!" · "Hey, neighbor. Wanna be friends?" · "You get some rest. Don't give any of this another thought. Just let Cozy take care of everything."
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Sunni Westbrook (English), Wang Yanhua (mainland Chinese). Debut: S8E12 "Marks for Effort." Her cutie mark is a chess rook. The design was inspired by Shirley Temple and Baby Doll from *Batman: The Animated Series*; art director Matt Herring and character designer Kora Kosicka are credited. Cozy Glow's parentage was never explored. Her alicorn form appears only in S9E24–25, and her statue begins crumbling in IDW's *Friendship is Magic* #102. She is one of the show's most memorably hated villains.

## Sweetie Belle

Her human counterpart is grouped with the other EG Crusaders in EG material. Whether her cutie mark is a shield or a heart depends on the source: the show uses a tri-color shield with a star and note, while some merchandise simplifies it to a heart [merchandise variation].

## Marble Pie

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / *Friendship is Magic* — the youngest Pie sister; first seen as a foal in S1E23, formally introduced as an adult in S5E20 "Hearthbreakers"; also appears in IDW comics, chapter books, and *The Best Gift Ever*.
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony.
**Gender:** Female.
**Age/Stage:** Young adult mare; a foal in flashbacks (S1E23).
**Residence:** Rockville, West Equestria — the Pie family rock farm.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Rock farmer, working the family farm; later a university student — IDW's *Friendship is Magic* #99 has her accepted to a university in Abyssinia.
**Appearance:** Teal-greenish gray coat; long, straight deep gray-teal mane with grass-green-gray stripes (and a faint blue-gray violet eyeshadow in one early photo appearance); grayish violet eyes. She has a long mane and tail she tends to hide behind and a small ribbon in her hair; her expression is usually downcast and shy. Her cutie mark is three purple marbles. Build is slight and soft-spoken — she is easily overlooked in a family of louder ponies.
**Personality:** Extremely shy, gentle, and quiet — she speaks in murmurs and "Mm-hmm"s and lets Pinkie Pie and her sisters do the talking. She is observant, kind, and surprisingly stubborn: when it matters she can stop her thunderous sister Limestone with a single look. She is bashful around Big Macintosh, easily flustered, and happy with quiet work, family, and animals. She is the demure, unassuming heart of the Pie family.
**Likes:** Quiet and hide-and-seek among the boulders, her family, the rock farm, Pinkie Pie, Big Macintosh (a bashful, mutual crush), sweet things, and being useful without being noticed.
**Dislikes:** Crowds and loud noise, being put on the spot, speaking to strangers, Limestone's temper, being teased about Big Macintosh, and being embarrassed in public.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Social situations and public speaking; being embarrassed (she literally runs away after witnessing a kiss); losing the family farm; conflict. She is not a fighter and has no magic.
**Skills/Abilities:** Capable rock farmer and farmhand; superb at hide-and-seek and at staying unnoticed; perceptive about other ponies' feelings; quietly firm when her family needs her; she keeps the farm's daily work running alongside her sisters. Limitations: extreme shyness, no combat ability, and no magic.
**Family & Relationships:** Father Igneous Rock Pie and mother Cloudy Quartz; older sisters Limestone Pie and Maud Pie; Pinkie Pie is her older sister (the two are "sametime sisters," not identical twins per the show's staff); grandmothers include Nana Pinkie and Granny Pie's generation; grandparents also include Feldspar Pie. Her romantic interest is Big Macintosh — the two bond wordlessly in "Hearthbreakers" and never formally become a couple on screen.
**Background/Biography:** Marble grew up with her sisters on the Pie family rock farm in Rockville. As a foal she was frightened by Pinkie's first party before joining in the fun. As an adult she first appears in "Hearthbreakers," when the Pie and Apple families spend Hearth's Warming together; her quietness matches Big Macintosh's, and the two shy ponies become friends over "Mm-hmm" and "Eeyup." In "The Maud Couple" she helps Pinkie accept Maud's boyfriend Mudbriar, nudging Limestone to comfort her. In *The Best Gift Ever* she sees Big Mac and Sugar Belle kiss and flees blushing, then suggests Yakyakistan as a place to find the perfect gift. The IDW comics give her more growth: in issue #86 she throws a party to make friends, over-invites half of Ponyville, panics, and is rescued by Limestone's help, and in issue #99 she earns a scholarship to Abyssinia and receives a farewell party from Pinkie and Cheese Sandwich. She appears in the S9 finale's group shots and in *The Elements of Harmony* guide as "the demure pony of the family."
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Almost silent — "Mm-hmm" and "Mmm" are her signature replies, with the occasional whispered sentence and nervous giggle. She lets Pinkie speak for her and answers in the fewest words possible; her one extended speech in the chapter book is quiet but tart.
**Notable Quotes:** "Mm-hmm." · "Well, you tell that to the rest of Equestria. We haven't had any business for months!"
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Ingrid Nilson (English). Designs and naming: author G. M. Berrow chose "Marble" and "Limestone" so the Pie sisters' names would spell out MLP (Maud, Limestone, Pinkie — the sisters' initials). Show staff described Marble and Pinkie as "sametime sisters" rather than identical twins. Her cutie mark is three purple marbles; her collectible card is "Marble Pie, Quiet as a Stone." She is a fan-favorite ship with Big Macintosh ("MarbleMac") despite never being confirmed as a couple.

## Radiant Hope

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — an IDW comics character (first appearance: *My Little Pony: FIENDship is Magic* #1; major role in *Friendship is Magic* #34–37, "Siege of the Crystal Empire"); referenced in the Gameloft mobile game.
**Species/Kind:** Crystal unicorn — a unicorn born in the Crystal Empire (crystal pony heritage); later depicted as a full princess-like figure in the comics.
**Gender:** Female.
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare; a foal orphan in her origin story. She spent roughly a thousand years in the Umbrum's world without aging.
**Residence:** The Crystal Empire (formerly); for a millennium she lived among the Umbrum in the Arctic Wastes; after the "Siege" arc she travels Equestria with Sombra.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Healer and mage — her special talent is healing magic; she was invited to study in Canterlot under Princess Celestia and Princess Luna; later declared Queen of the Crystal Empire by King Sombra, and finally his companion on his quest for redemption.
**Appearance:** Light gray-purple coat; pale gray-arctic-blue mane; medium arctic-blue eyes; bright cornflower-blue magic; a crystal pony's shimmering, faceted look. Her cutie mark is a golden scepter — the emblem of the princess she was destined to become. As a foal she is small and wide-eyed; as the "princess" of the comics she is elegant and regal, and as Sombra's queen she becomes cold and remote in expression. In the present-day arc she wears flowing robes and armor-like regalia.
**Personality:** As a foal, radiantly optimistic, imaginative, and innocent — she talks to creatures no one else can see and befriends the outcast Sombra. As an adult she remains trusting, hopeful, and stubbornly loyal, even to a friend she knows is a monster. She is gentle, compassionate, and self-sacrificing, but that loyalty becomes a flaw: she can be naive, denial-prone, and capable of terrible choices for love. Betrayal by the Umbrum leaves her aloof and cold for a time; only Sombra's choice to spare the Crystal Heart brings her back.
**Likes:** Healing and helping others, her friend Sombra, the Crystal Empire, the Crystal Heart, the story of the princess she might have been, and the hope that people can change.
**Dislikes:** Cruelty masquerading as destiny, the Umbrum's manipulations, being told her friend is beyond saving, the destruction of her home, and giving up on anyone.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Losing Sombra again; having failed him as a child; being alone; her own innocence being used against her; her judgment is clouded by love, and her healing magic cannot cure evil.
**Skills/Abilities:** Exceptional healing magic — she can mend a body that is shattering and hold a dissolving pony together with her power; also capable of shepherding villains and negotiating with dangerous creatures; keen knowledge of the Umbrum and the Crystal Empire's history. Limitations: her magic heals bodies, not hearts, and her trust is easily exploited.
**Family & Relationships:** Unnamed parents (she was raised as an orphan at the Crystal Heart Foal Center in Chestnut Falls). Her defining relationship is with King Sombra, whom she befriended as a foal and loved as an adult; she is also tied to Princess Amore's legacy, to Princess Cadance (whom she meets in the "Siege" arc), and to Celestia and Luna, who once invited her to study with them. She was exploited by the Umbrum and by Queen Chrysalis in the FIENDship issue.
**Background/Biography:** Radiant Hope grew up an orphan in the Crystal Empire, shunned by other foals, and became the only friend of the strange colt Sombra. When his body began to shatter, she healed him with her magic and earned her cutie mark; Celestia and Luna, impressed, invited her to study in Canterlot. But she watched Sombra fall into darkness, Princess Amore's destruction, and the Empire vanish. Unable to forgive herself for leaving him, she abandoned her studies and crossed into the Umbrum's realm, where she lived unchanging for a thousand years and learned their nature. In *Friendship is Magic* #34 she returns, recruits Flim and Flam, Iron Will, Lightning Dust, and changelings to attack the Crystal Empire, and uses her healing magic to restore Sombra's body while he turns Celestia and Luna to stone. In #36 she tries to persuade Cadance that her cause is just, is horrified when Cadance's magic reveals the Umbrum's monstrous truth, and is injured shielding Sombra from Twilight's magic. Declared queen after Sombra takes the throne, she grows cold — yet when she suggests destroying the Crystal Heart, Sombra instead remembers her words about destiny, re-seals the Umbrum, and begins to fade; she holds him together and, with the princesses, turns him into a true unicorn. The two then leave together to gather Princess Amore's scattered shards and restore her.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Soft, earnest, and lyrical; speaks in gentle metaphors about hope, stars, and destiny. As a child she is chatty and imaginative; as Sombra's queen her tone becomes flat and distant. She calls Sombra by name with great tenderness and uses "I believe" and "there is always hope" as her refrain.
**Notable Quotes:** "Don't let them bother you. They said I was weird too. If you want, you can be weird with me and we'll be weird together. Would that be okay?" · "Oh, Sombra, I wish you could see it. It's so beautiful." · "I was a fool. You told me yourself that you were a monster. I should have listened. But I had... hope. Foolish, foolish hope." · "No more talk about destiny, Sombra. I've never believed in it. You're the only thing I've ever believed in."
**Trivia/Notes:** A comics-only character created for the IDW *My Little Pony* line; writer Jeremy Whitley described her as "flawed" — she believes she turned away from her only friend when he needed her, lost both him and her home, blames herself, and would do anything to fix it; he compared her friendship devotion to Twilight's, except that her friend "had a tendency toward evil." Her cutie mark is a golden scepter; her palette (pale gray-purple coat, arctic-blue eyes and mane) was noted by the wiki as similar to a few background ponies. She also appears in the Gameloft mobile game ("Sombra's best friend through good times and bad"). The exact timeline of her thousand years among the Umbrum is [uncertain] in places.

## Zecora

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / *Friendship is Magic* — introduced in S1E9 "Bridle Gossip"; a recurring wise-woman/mentor character; an Equestria Girls doll depiction exists.
**Species/Kind:** Zebra (female); a human-world Zecora appears only in Equestria Girls merchandise/dolls, not as a speaking character.
**Gender:** Female.
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare; exact age not stated, but she is an experienced healer and elder.
**Residence:** A wooden hut deep in the Everfree Forest, decorated with masks, charms, and potion jars; originally from a distant zebra homeland (unnamed in the show, expanded in other media).
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Herbalist, potion-maker, healer, and shaman-like wise woman; unofficial doctor and magical consultant to Ponyville; friend and mentor to the Mane Six.
**Appearance:** A zebra with a light purple-gray coat striped in dark blue-gray, a bright gray and dark blue-gray striped mohawk-like mane, dark teal eyes, indigo-black hooves, and gold jewelry — large hoop earrings, a neck band, and arm bands in bright amber gold. Her cutie mark is a spiral-shaped sun in amber/gold tones. She wears a witch-like cloak and charms for Nightmare Night and is otherwise unclad, with a staff or herb bundle often in hoof. Her voice and manner are dramatic, and she moves with quiet, deliberate grace.
**Personality:** Wise, patient, unflappable, and private. She is an outsider who was feared before she was known, and she bears that with dignity rather than bitterness; she helps ponies even while they distrust her. She is a gifted teacher who prefers riddles, rhymes, and demonstrations to lectures; she is brave, kind, and a little mischievous, with a dry sense of humor. She does not suffer foolishness gladly and has firm opinions about respect for the forest and its creatures.
**Likes:** Herbs, roots, and potion-making; the Everfree Forest and its creatures; teaching; rhyming; masks and traditional crafts; rain, tea, and quiet; ponies who show courage and open-mindedness.
**Dislikes:** Fear born of ignorance, gossip and prejudice, ponies touching her potions without asking, careless use of magic, rudeness, and being judged by her appearance.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Being driven out or hated for being different; harm coming to ponies because they ignored her warnings; she is vulnerable to physical attack, and her remedies take time and ingredients that can fail or be misused.
**Skills/Abilities:** Expert herbalism and potion-craft — cures for magical ailments (Poison Joke's effects, the Cutie Pox, the "Equestria Games" sickness, and more), antidotes, salves, and remedies; deep knowledge of rare plants, dark magic, and Everfree lore; a keen diagnostician; skilled at defensive magic and sleight-of-hoof tricks; a fine storyteller and riddler. Limitations: no combat power, no wings or unicorn magic, and her solutions often require patience and trust from others.
**Family & Relationships:** Unnamed parents; her homeland and early life are only lightly described. Friends and allies: Twilight Sparkle (who comes to rely on her), Apple Bloom and the Cutie Mark Crusaders (she cures the Cutie Pox and teaches them about consequences), Applejack, Pinkie Pie, and the ponies of Ponyville; she is a mentor to anypony willing to listen. She has no established romance or children.
**Background/Biography:** Zecora was feared as an "evil enchantress" before Ponyville ever met her: in "Bridle Gossip" the Mane Six sneak into her hut and accidentally touch the blue Poison Joke flowers, and her warning "Beware, beware, you pony folk! Those leaves of blue are not a joke!" goes unheeded until the curse humiliates each of them and she cures them. That episode turns her from a feared stranger into a trusted friend, and she becomes Ponyville's go-to healer: she saves Apple Bloom from the Cutie Pox in "The Cutie Pox," helps in "Luna Eclipsed" when ponies fear Nightmare Night, cures the Cutie Pox's spread, aids Applejack in "Apple Family Reunion"-era troubles, and appears in the alternate timelines of "The Cutie Re-Mark." She is a recurring voice of sanity and her hut a refuge for the Crusaders. In the *Elements of Harmony* and other guides she is remembered as the wise zebra of the Everfree who rhymes every line.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Speaks in rhyming couplets almost without exception, in a distinctive African-accented English with a deep, theatrical voice. She calls ponies "little ponies," uses riddling openings ("If you seek the answer you desire..."), and drops rhymes even when urgent. Her tone is calm and knowing; she is fond of dramatic pauses.
**Notable Quotes:** "Beware! Beware, you pony folk! Those leaves of blue are not a joke!" · [paraphrased] "Fear not the forest's shadows, little ponies — it is ignorance that makes the night so dark." · [paraphrased] "A cure I have, but trust it you must, or all my potions turn to dust."
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Brenda Crichlow (English) and Nao Fujita (Japanese). Debut: S1E9 "Bridle Gossip"; she is the show's most prominent non-pony ally and the only rhyming regular character. Her cutie mark is a spiral sun; her gold jewelry and masks are part of her design. She has appeared as a witch in "Luna Eclipsed," in Everfree-centered episodes throughout the series, in the parallel-world sequence of "The Cutie Re-Mark," and in EG merchandise/dolls. Details of her homeland and family are [not established in canon] beyond brief mentions.
