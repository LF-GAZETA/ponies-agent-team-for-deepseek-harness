## Spike

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls. Main-cast dragon in *Friendship Is Magic*; in the *Equestria Girls* human world his counterpart is Twilight's dog.
**Species/Kind:** Dragon — a baby dragon for most of the series (no wings as a hatchling; his greed-growth form and his adult epilogue form do have wings). No cutie mark (dragons do not have them).
**Gender:** Male (he/him) — he is called "he" and "Spike" by everyone throughout the series.
**Age/Stage:** Hatchling/baby dragon at the start; still the dragon equivalent of a child by the finale, then a grown adult dragon in the "many moons later" epilogue of "The Last Problem."
**Residence:** Hatched and raised in Canterlot, then Ponyville — first the Golden Oak Library, later Twilight's Castle of Friendship; he is also honored in the Crystal Empire.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Twilight Sparkle's "#1 Assistant" and self-described little brother; royal helper/adviser; briefly Dragon Lord of the Dragon Lands (he wins the Gauntlet of Fire and hands the title to Ember); Crystal Empire hero titled "Spike the Brave and Glorious"; member of the extended Mane Six family and the Equestria Girls' ally.
**Appearance:** A small, round-bodied purple dragon with a lighter purple-green underbelly, green back spines, large green eyes, small ear frills and a short tail. He is knee-high to a pony and usually carries a grin. In "Secret of My Excess," dragon greed makes him balloon into a huge, winged, fire-breathing monster until he is talked down. As an adult in the epilogue he is tall, lean and fully winged. In *Equestria Girls* he is a purple-and-green puppy — first Twilight's pet, later Sci-Twi's — who understands speech perfectly.
**Personality:** Loyal, brave, funny and surprisingly mature for his age; a sarcastic sidekick with a big heart who constantly worries about being treated like a baby. He is self-deprecating and often comic relief, yet he is usually the first to volunteer for dangerous jobs. He can be jealous of Twilight's other friends, impulsive, attention-hungry, and vulnerable to dragon greed — but he is also remarkably kind, forgiving and emotionally perceptive. He is deeply proud of his title of "brave and glorious" and embarrassed by it at the same time.
**Likes:** Gems and crystals (his favorite food), hay fries and ice cream, comic books (especially *Power Ponies*), being useful, adventure, Rarity, apple pie, dragon customs, and the Crystal Empire's crystal treats.
**Dislikes:** Being called cute or a baby, being left behind on missions, chores, being pet-sat, bullying, Sludge the false father, dark caves, and being underestimated in a fight.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Dragon greed (which literally makes him grow monstrous and lose himself), abandonment and loneliness (exploited by the fake father Sludge in "Father Knows Beast"), his own small size in combat, stage fright, and a tendency to blunder into danger. He cannot fly until adulthood.
**Skills/Abilities:** Dragon fire breath, which can carry letters and objects to Princess Celestia and later serves as a weapon; durable scales, a strong tail and small sharp claws; excellent memory for rules, comics and lore; fast digger and climber; capable of great courage — his single most famous feat is carrying the Crystal Heart to the top of the tower in "The Crystal Empire," saving the empire. Weaknesses: a child's body, poor fighting skill, and no magic of his own.
**Family & Relationships:** Hatched from an egg by Twilight Sparkle during her entrance exam, so Twilight is effectively his mother/sister and closest family; he calls her family rather than a master. He has a long-running crush on Rarity, a warm friendship with Thorax and Ember, loyalty to Princess Celestia, and a respectful bond with Shining Armor and Cadance. His biological parents are never established in the series. In *Equestria Girls* he belongs to Twilight/Sci-Twi as a dog.
**Background/Biography:** Spike's egg was the test object of Twilight's Academy entrance exam; when she hatched him, both his life and her cutie mark began. He grew up in Canterlot as her assistant, then moved to Ponyville with her in the pilot. Over the series he saved the day repeatedly: delivering the Crystal Heart, defeating a dragon in "Dragonshy," winning the Gauntlet of Fire and making peace with the Dragon Lands, befriending the first reformed changeling (Thorax) and earning the Crystal Empire's highest honor, and helping defeat Sombra and the Legion of Doom. In "The Last Problem" he is Twilight's grown adviser at the Canterlot court, taller and winged — still her oldest friend.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Modern, casual, joke-cracking American kid-speak; uses modern slang, sarcasm and quick quips. Calls Twilight "Twilight," Rarity "Rarity," and Celestia "the Princess." Famous verbal habits include "I'm not a baby!", "Oh, brother," "Great," and "Everypony, listen up!" He narrates the obvious, complains about chores, and jokes to defuse tension.
**Notable Quotes:** "I'm not a baby — I'm a dragon!" [paraphrased]; "Take it from me, Twilight — being a hero isn't so bad." [paraphrased]; "Spike the Brave and Glorious, at your service!" [paraphrased, echoing his Crystal Empire title].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Cathy Weseluck. First appearance: "Friendship is Magic, part 1" (2010), as the egg in Twilight's flashback and by her side thereafter. Dragon fire mail is one of the most iconic G4 mechanics. In *Equestria Girls* Spike is a dog — a change fans often note. Adult winged Spike appears only in the distant finale, so his adult design is brief on screen.

## Princess Flurry Heart

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — *Friendship Is Magic* (no G5; no *Equestria Girls* human counterpart).
**Species/Kind:** Alicorn — the first alicorn born in Equestria, born a natural alicorn with both horn and wings, which shocked even Celestia and Luna.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** A baby/foal throughout her canon appearances; shown as an adult ruling the Crystal Empire only in a stained-glass window in the series finale's epilogue.
**Residence:** The Crystal Empire — the Crystal Palace, with her parents.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Crown princess of the Crystal Empire, daughter of Princess Cadance and Shining Armor, niece of Twilight Sparkle. As a baby she has no formal duties, but she is the focus of the Crystalling ceremony and is later shown as the Empire's future ruler.
**Appearance:** A tiny pink alicorn foal with a soft pink coat, a short tuft of magenta-purple mane and tail, big blue eyes with purple shading, and disproportionately large, feathery pink wings with purple-tipped feathers. Her horn is small and she wears a tiny gold crown/tiara, a blue bib-and-diaper outfit in her earliest appearances, and a gold-and-blue collar. In "A Flurry of Emotions" she is shown in a stroller and winter wraps. The adult Flurry Heart in the finale's stained glass wears a full royal crown and regalia. No cutie mark yet (she is a blank-flank foal).
**Personality:** A cheerful, fearless, chaotic baby with a powerful will. She loves attention, flying, play and sweets, and throws spectacular tantrums that literally reshape the palace. She is friendly and trusting but has a stubborn streak — when King Sombra held her hostage she bit him and blasted him repeatedly rather than submit, which says a lot about her temperament. She adores her parents and is fascinated by Twilight, but she also has a mischievous streak that turns babysitting into a disaster.
**Likes:** Her parents, Auntie Twilight, flying, being bounced, sweet treats, toys, the Crystal Heart, and making new friends.
**Dislikes:** Nap time, boredom, being told no, scary strangers (Sombra), and being separated from her parents.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Being frightened or restrained. Her magic is uncontrolled: in "The Crystalling," her wail shattered the Crystal Heart and her sneeze-laser singed a Crystal Pony's mane two floors away. She cannot talk or reason yet.
**Skills/Abilities:** Enormous innate alicorn magic — flight (she flies before she can walk properly), levitation, and powerful horn blasts. Her magic outpaced that of adult unicorns as a newborn; it is raw and dangerous, not controlled. She can also break the Crystal Heart and destabilize the Empire's weather-shield by accident.
**Family & Relationships:** Daughter of Princess Cadance and Shining Armor; niece of Twilight Sparkle; great-niece (adoptive) of Celestia and Luna; a cousin of the Apple family only distantly through Shining Armor's marriage ties. Sunburst is her "crystaller" — her godparent — an honor that becomes his proudest duty. Applejack/Spike and the Mane Six are frequent babysitters.
**Background/Biography:** Born in the two-part Season 6 opener "The Crystalling," the first alicorn in Equestria's history, which stunned Celestia, Luna and everyone else. Her birth was to be sealed with a Crystalling ceremony that empowers the Crystal Heart, but her outburst shattered the Heart and opened the Empire to the frozen north; the Mane Six and the princesses repaired it, and she was named in Part 2. In "The Times They Are a Changeling" she appears during the Crystal Empire visit; in "A Flurry of Emotions" Twilight babysits her and learns a lesson about priorities. In Season 9's premiere King Sombra attacks the Crystal Empire, takes her hostage and tickles her nose; she bites him and blasts him. In the finale's future epilogue a stained-glass window shows an adult Princess Flurry Heart ruling the Crystal Empire.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Baby sounds only — coos, giggles, gurgles and wails. She never speaks dialogue in canon; her "voice" is a mix of happy babbling and ear-piercing shrieks (the latter usually a prelude to magic).
**Notable Quotes:** Flurry Heart has no spoken dialogue in canon; her "lines" are vocalizations. "[Delighted baby squeal]" [paraphrased]; "[Frustrated shriek that precedes a magic blast]" [paraphrased]. Her most quoted in-fandom "line" is her unintelligible cooing at Twilight.
**Trivia/Notes:** No credited major voice actor — her baby vocal effects are uncredited/minor [uncertain]. Debut: "The Crystalling, Part 1" (S6E1, 2016). She is one of the most merchandised foals of G4, and the first natural alicorn birth in the show's canon. Her adult future self is only seen in stained glass; her adult voice is never heard.

## Rainbow Dash

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — a member of the Mane Six (Element of Loyalty) and, in *Equestria Girls*, the human Rainbow Dash of Canterlot High.
**Species/Kind:** Pegasus pony (human in the *Equestria Girls* world).
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Young adult mare (human counterpart: teenaged high-school student).
**Residence:** Ponyville; originally Cloudsdale. In EG she lives in the human world and attends Canterlot High.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Ponyville weather-team flier and cloud-keeper; bearer of the Element of Loyalty; Wonderbolts Academy graduate and official Wonderbolt; in the future epilogue she is in charge of the Wonderbolts. EG:CHS student, star athlete, band member of the Rainbooms (guitar), and one of the school's most popular students.
**Appearance:** A sky-blue pegasus mare with a rainbow-striped mane and tail, magenta eyes, and a cutie mark of a rainbow-colored lightning bolt shooting from a white cloud (a symbol of her speed and loyalty). She is athletic and toned, with a tomboyish stance and a confident smirk. In *Equestria Girls* she has pale blue skin, rainbow hair with blue streaks, magenta eyes, and a signature jacket/skirt sports outfit; in "Rainbow Rocks" she wears her Rainbooms stage outfit and in "Friendship Games" her soccer and track uniforms. In the far-future epilogue her mane is cut into a short Spitfire-like mohawk and she wears a Wonderbolts captain's uniform.
**Personality:** Brash, competitive, loyal, brave and loud. She is a show-off who loves winning and hates being second, yet her loyalty is absolute — she will sacrifice her dream before betraying a friend (as in "Wonderbolt Academy" and "Rainbow Falls"). Impatient, impulsive, sarcastic, and secretly a giant softie and fangirl (Daring Do books, Wonderbolts, pranks). She hates being thought of as "uncool" and dreads failure or losing her edge; she is rubbish at sitting still, studying, or admitting she is scared.
**Likes:** Flying fast, winning, the Wonderbolts, Daring Do novels, cider, pranks with Pinkie, her tortoise Tank, action, attention, and her friends.
**Dislikes:** Losing, slowpokes, being called "Rainbow Crash," being lectured, studying, sitting still, bullies and cheaters (Lightning Dust), and personal embarrassment.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Losing her friends or her wings; being seen as weak; stage fright in front of her idols; recklessness that endangers others; over-competitiveness; she is a poor student academically and a terrible liar.
**Skills/Abilities:** Supreme flyer — the only pony known to perform the Sonic Rainboom (a sonic boom with a rainbow), which she first did as a filly and repeated in "Sonic Rainboom." Extreme speed, agility, weather control (cloud-busting), stunt flying, strength and endurance, and combat flying. She also has excellent tactical instincts and is a good leader in the air. Limits: magicless pegasus, poor at book learning, and her ego gets her in trouble.
**Family & Relationships:** Parents Bow Hothoof and Windy Whistles (over-enthusiastic fans who appear in "Parental Glideance"), mother Windy and father Bow; her childhood hero/idol Spitfire and the Wonderbolts; close best friends with the Mane Six, especially Applejack (friendly rivalry), Pinkie Pie (prank partner) and Fluttershy (childhood friend from flight camp). Pet tortoise Tank. In EG she is friends with the human Mane Six and Sunset Shimmer.
**Background/Biography:** Born in Cloudsdale, Rainbow Dash earned her cutie mark as a filly when she performed the first Sonic Rainboom to save Fluttershy at flight camp — the same event that caused the Mane Six's cutie marks. She moved to Ponyville, joined the weather team, and became the Element of Loyalty. Her Wonderbolts arc runs from "Wonderbolt Academy" through "Testing Testing 1, 2, 3" and "Rainbow Falls" to "Newbie Dash," where she officially joins the team; by "The Last Problem" she commands the Wonderbolts. In EG she is a CHS sports star who joins the Rainbooms to defeat the Dazzlings, and later the sirens' magical threat.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Loud, slangy, boastful American teen voice; stretches vowels for emphasis and talks in absolutes. Catchphrases: "Awesome!", "So cool!", "20% cooler," "Ten seconds flat!", "Danger's my middle name," "Hay yeah!" She calls friends "guys" and often narrates her own awesomeness.
**Notable Quotes:** "I could clear the sky in ten seconds flat!" [paraphrased, from the pilot]; "I'm gonna be a Wonderbolt!"; "Loyalty is what I'm all about — I'd never leave a friend hanging." [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Ashleigh Ball (who also voices Applejack). Debut: "Friendship is Magic, part 1" (2010). Her Sonic Rainboom is the series' origin event for the Mane Six's cutie marks. In *Equestria Girls* she is the Rainbooms' guitarist and CHS's star athlete, and her human design keeps the rainbow hair. "20% cooler" is one of the show's most famous fandom-born catchphrases.

## Yona

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — *Friendship Is Magic* (Season 8–9); no *Equestria Girls* counterpart.
**Species/Kind:** Yak (from Yakyakistan).
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Teenaged student (young yak).
**Residence:** Yakyakistan originally; while studying she lives at Twilight's School of Friendship in Ponyville.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Student of the School of Friendship and one of the "Young Six"/Student Six — the yak ambassador to the school; future role after the time skip is not established [uncertain].
**Appearance:** A large, sturdy yak with shaggy brown fur, darker brown shaggy mane and tail, a tan/cream muzzle and belly, big curved horns, and long braids in her mane and tail (the braids she constantly trips over). She wears a turquoise/green saddle-blanket and a red bow or ribbon; her eyes are teal-green. She is the biggest and bulkiest of the Young Six, with a broad, heavy build and hooves. No cutie mark (yaks do not have them).
**Personality:** Boisterous, warm, loud, affectionate and accident-prone. Yona is the group's "big sweetheart" — excited about everything, fiercely loyal, and hilariously clumsy; she trips over her own braids, smashes into furniture and falls down stairs. She speaks in third person ("Yona thinks..."), has a temper when pushed, and is honest to a fault. Underneath the comedy she is brave, generous and thoughtful, with a strong sense of yak pride.
**Likes:** Her friends (especially Sandbar), Yakyakistan, smashing things, cupcakes, yak traditions, braids, compliments, and being included.
**Dislikes:** Tripping over her braids/hooves/everything, spiders, being left out, being called clumsy, cold weather, and ponies who look down on yaks.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Spiders (confronted in "What Lies Beneath"); falling from heights; her own clumsiness and strength causing harm; being mocked; she is not book-smart and gets frustrated in class.
**Skills/Abilities:** Enormous physical strength — she can smash obstacles and carry her friends; surprising speed and momentum; extremely tough (she survives falls that would hurt others); a good heart and a strong moral compass (she embodies the Element of Honesty in "School Raze"). Limits: poor fine motor control, no magic, and a tendency to panic.
**Family & Relationships:** A yak of Yakyakistan; her family is not established beyond references to her homeland and the yak leadership of Prince Rutherford [uncertain on direct relation]. Closest friends are the Young Six: Sandbar (her crush and "She's All Yak" dance partner), Gallus, Ocellus, Silverstream and Smolder.
**Background/Biography:** Yona arrives in Ponyville in "School Daze, Part 1" as Yakyakistan's student delegate to Twilight's new School of Friendship. She joins the future Young Six when they skip class, bond, and accidentally destroy part of the school; they become true friends after surviving the puckwudgies. She features in "The Hearth's Warming Club" (recalling yak winter customs with her new friends), "What Lies Beneath" (facing spiders), "Non-Compete Clause," "School Raze" (the Tree of Harmony tests her honesty) and "She's All Yak," where she tries to become pony-like for the dance before learning Sandbar likes her as she is. She appears in the finale's future group shots as a grown yak.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Third-person "yak" speech — "Yona can't wait!", "Yona thinks..." — loud, enthusiastic, blunt and grammatically yak-flavored. She refers to herself by name, uses yak-centric idioms, and blurts out exactly what she feels. She also screams and smashes when excited.
**Notable Quotes:** "Yak can't wait to meet ponies and tell all about Yakyakistan!"; "Yona thinks this is best day ever!" [paraphrased]; "Yona is not clumsy! ...Yona is just... big." [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Katrina Salisbury. Debut: "School Daze, Part 1" (S8E1, 2018). Part of the Young Six, the show's multicultural "next generation" cast. Fans love her for the third-person speech and for the Yona/Sandbar pairing. Her braids and yak strength are running gags.

## Ocellus

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — *Friendship Is Magic* (Season 8–9); no *Equestria Girls* counterpart.
**Species/Kind:** Changeling — one of the reformed, pastel-colored changelings who transformed after Thorax became king. Can shapeshift into any creature.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Teenaged student.
**Residence:** The Changeling Kingdom/hive originally; the School of Friendship in Ponyville while studying.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Student of the School of Friendship and the changelings' student ambassador — one of the Young Six; in the future epilogue she is a teacher at the School of Friendship.
**Appearance:** A slim, graceful reformed changeling with a smooth pale cyan/aqua carapace, a light blue fin-like crest of "mane," a short horn, small fangs, and translucent pinkish insect wings. Her eyes are solid light blue with no visible pupils — like all changelings, the direction of her gaze is readable only from the white reflections — and she is one of the most delicately built of the Young Six. She has no cutie mark. When nervous she hides by shapeshifting; she can also take monstrous forms, most memorably a giant armored bite-acuda. As a teacher in the future she keeps her changeling appearance.
**Personality:** Shy, gentle, anxious, and extremely bookish. Ocellus is the quiet one of the group who knows the answer in class and is too timid to raise a hoof. She is kind, self-critical and prone to apologizing; she prefers being someone else to being herself, and she carries guilt over the changelings' predatory past (she breaks down at seeing herself as Queen Chrysalis in "What Lies Beneath"). Under the shyness she is brave, clever and fiercely loyal once her friends are threatened.
**Likes:** Books, learning, history, her friends, pillows, quiet corners, changeling kindness, Fluttershy's lessons, and being useful without being noticed.
**Dislikes:** Being the center of attention, loud crowds, being teased, her race's dark reputation, scary reflections, and being put on the spot in class.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Turning into or being compared to Chrysalis; rejection for being a changeling; failing her exams; public speaking; physical confrontation. She panics easily and is physically the weakest of the Young Six.
**Skills/Abilities:** Expert shapeshifter (any species, including giant insects), flight on insect wings, changeling magic/illusion, near-perfect memory for rules and lore (she reads ahead and quotes lessons), mimicry/imitation, and quick thinking. Limits: timid, easily frightened, no combat training.
**Family & Relationships:** Her family lives in the Changeling Kingdom and appears in "The Hearth's Warming Club," where they attempt Hearth's Warming rituals literally (hanging the tree upside-down). Family members are not named [uncertain]. Her closest friends are the Young Six — Gallus, Yona, Sandbar, Silverstream and Smolder — with Silverstream as the friend who draws her out. She idolizes Fluttershy's kindness.
**Background/Biography:** Ocellus enrolls in Twilight's School of Friendship in "School Daze, Part 1" as the changelings' representative. She bonds with her fellow outsiders after they skip class together, accidentally wreck part of the school and survive the puckwudgies. "The Hearth's Warming Club" reveals her hive's well-meaning misunderstanding of pony holidays; "What Lies Beneath" forces her to confront Chrysalis's shadow; "School Raze" has her shapeshift into a monster to help save her friends. By "The Last Problem" she has returned to the school as a teacher.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Soft, hesitant, precise and bookish; she uses longer words and complete sentences, opens with "Um..." or "Actually...", and apologizes reflexively. She becomes a bubbly enthusiast when talking about books or facts. When she impersonates others (e.g., Rarity) she exaggerates their catchphrases.
**Notable Quotes:** "Pillows! How nice! The castle is gonna be so much more comfy."; "I just... I don't want anycreature to see me as a monster." [paraphrased]; "Actually, I read about that — it's in chapter three." [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Devyn Dalton, singing voice by Shannon Chan-Kent. Debut: "School Daze, Part 1" (S8E1, 2018). Her name means "simple eye," fitting her quiet observer role. The solid-color eyes are the reformed changelings' signature design. She is often cited as the sweetest and most bookish of the Young Six.

## Rarity

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — Mane Six member (Element of Generosity); the human Rarity of Canterlot High in the EG films.
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn pony (human in *Equestria Girls*).
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare (EG: teenaged high-school student).
**Residence:** Ponyville — Carousel Boutique; second boutique "Rarity for You" in Manehattan (S6); in the future a new boutique in Yakyakistan. EG: the human world / Canterlot High.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Fashion designer, seamstress, gem hunter and owner of Carousel Boutique; bearer of the Element of Generosity; member of the Mane Six; in EG a CHS fashionista, Rainbooms keytarist, and organizer of school events and outfits.
**Appearance:** A graceful ivory-white unicorn mare with a curled, bouncing indigo-purple mane and tail, large blue eyes with violet eyeshadow, and a cutie mark of three blue diamonds (the gems she discovered with her magic). She carries herself like a runway model and is always immaculately groomed. Her mane is styled in a high, rounded coiffure with a curl. In EG she has pale skin, purple curled hair, blue eyes, diamond earrings, and a wardrobe of fashionable outfits; her Rainbooms stage outfit is a sparkling sequined dress and she plays keytar (a keyboard) in the band. In the future epilogue she has grey streaks but the same hairstyle.
**Personality:** Generous, dramatic, driven, elegant and hilariously vain. Rarity is a perfectionist artist: generous to a fault, fiercely loyal to her friends, and prone to theatrical meltdowns ("the worst possible thing!"). She is a social climber who adores high society, gossip and admiration, can be snobbish about taste, and occasionally lets jealousy or status anxiety get the better of her — but she always returns to generosity. She is organized, entrepreneurial, a natural project leader, and deeply romantic.
**Likes:** Haute couture, gems, silk and fine fabric, the spa, tea, elegant parties, opera and romance, compliments, her cat Opalescence, her little sister, and being creative.
**Dislikes:** Dirt, mud, cheap fabric, bad manners, being outdone or copied, wearing the same outfit twice, being ignored, and anyone insulting her designs or her friends.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Public humiliation, being ordinary or untalented, creative block, losing her business, and — most of all — the "worst possible thing.". She is physically soft, easily exhausted, can be snobbish and selfish under stress, and is a compulsive over-worker.
**Skills/Abilities:** Unicorn telekinesis; a gem-finding spell that is the core of her cutie-mark talent; fine-grained sewing and design magic that lets her create entire outfits in minutes; dressmaking, fashion design, business management, etiquette, diplomacy, singing, and keytar in EG. Limits: weak in combat magic, fragile, and dramatic under pressure.
**Family & Relationships:** Younger sister Sweetie Belle; parents Hondo Flanks and Cookie Crumbles (who appear in "Sisterhooves Social" and later episodes); pet cat Opalescence. Spike nurses a long crush on her; Prince Blueblood (a disastrous date), Trenderhoof (her brief infatuation) and Fancy Pants are romantic/comedy interests. Her best friends are the Mane Six; she mentors Sweetie Belle and later designs for royalty.
**Background/Biography:** In "The Cutie Mark Chronicles," young Rarity's magic dragged her to a giant jewel and she realized her talent — finding gems and making beautiful clothes — while designing costumes for a school play. She built Carousel Boutique into Ponyville's fashion hub, became the Element of Generosity, and went on to dress Canterlot's elite, open a Manehattan shop in "The Saddle Row Review," and design Twilight's coronation gown. In EG she is the human Rainbooms' stylist and keytar player and helps defeat the Dazzlings; by "The Last Problem" she has opened a boutique in Yakyakistan and remains the group's style icon.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Theatrical, refined, slightly mid-Atlantic diction; dramatic gasps, "darling," "Oh my stars!", "How dreadful!", "This is simply the WORST. POSSIBLE. THING!", "Fabulous!" She over-enunciates and uses fashion-world vocabulary; when panicked she babbles in escalating superlatives.
**Notable Quotes:** "This is the worst possible thing!"; "Oh my stars, darling, that is simply fabulous!" [paraphrased]; "Generosity is my element, after all." [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Tabitha St. Germain, with singing voice by Kazumi Evans. Debut: "Friendship is Magic, part 1" (2010). Her three-diamond cutie mark doubles as her gem-finding talent. In EG she is the Rainbooms' keytarist. Her cat Opalescence is a recurring gag. Fans often call her the show's most quotable character.

## Granny Smith

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — the Apple family matriarch; she has a human counterpart in the *Equestria Girls* world.
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony — one of the three pony tribes, with no horn or wings; her tribe's magic is tied to the land and to growing things.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Elderly — great-grandmother age, the oldest major pony in Ponyville ("ancient" in pony terms); in the far-future epilogue she has apparently passed away of old age.
**Residence:** Sweet Apple Acres, Ponyville (she helped settle the area).
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Matriarch of the Apple family and Sweet Apple Acres; keeper of the zap-apple harvest ritual and jam recipe; one of Ponyville's founding-generation settlers; member of the Gold Horseshoe Gals; retired farmer, still the family's moral authority.
**Appearance:** A small, stooped, light-green earth pony mare with a white/grey mane pinned in a bun and often a green-and-white bonnet; deep wrinkles, a squinting, nearsighted gaze, and a warm, toothy smile. She wears an orange-red apple-patterned scarf/shawl and, in cold weather, a knitted shawl; she uses a walker or cane and her joints creak audibly. Her cutie mark is an apple — a green Granny Smith apple, fitting the apple variety she is named for — though it is rarely shown clearly on screen [uncertain exact design]. Her human counterpart in *Equestria Girls* is an elderly woman with the same green-and-grey palette.
**Personality:** Warm, feisty, stubborn and eccentric. Granny Smith is the heart of the Apple family: fiercely proud of her family and farm, endlessly hospitable, and full of stories — which she tells at length whether or not anyone wants to hear them. Age has made her forgetful, nearsighted and a bit of a cloudcuckoolander, but there is real wisdom and steel underneath; she is brave, hardworking, sharp-tongued when needed and deeply loving. She hates being treated as useless.
**Likes:** Her family, apples, zap-apple jam, apple fritters, storytelling, her Gold Horseshoe Gals friends, naps in the sun, old traditions, and good honest work.
**Dislikes:** Wasting food, newfangled gizmos, being ignored, Flim and Flam's cider machine, rude young ponies, changes to Ponyville, and anyone cheating her family.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Her failing body — a "saggy ol' hip," awful eyesight, memory lapses — and being a burden; she is physically frail, easily confused, and cannot fight or farm the way she once did.
**Skills/Abilities:** Mastery of the zap-apple harvest ritual (including the bunny suit and singing) and of zap-apple jam, the family's signature product; expert baking/cooking; decades of farming and weather wisdom; founder-level knowledge of Ponyville's history; a formidable memory for family genealogy; surprising toughness and wisdom. In the IDW comics she has a "crouching moron, hidden badass" moment when Ponyville stands up to the Nightmare Forces.
**Family & Relationships:** Paternal grandmother of Applejack, Big Macintosh and Apple Bloom — her son was Bright Macintosh, who married Pear Butter. Other relatives include Goldie Delicious (the family historian), Auntie Applesauce, Apple Rose, Babs Seed, Braeburn, Grand Pear (in-law) and the whole enormous Apple clan. Her late husband is not established in canon. She is close friends with the Gold Horseshoe Gals.
**Background/Biography:** As a young mare, Granny Smith traveled with her family to the valley that became Ponyville; after a magical storm she discovered the zap apples, and the family's zap-apple orchard became Sweet Apple Acres. Her family helped found Ponyville, and she has been its oldest living link to that history ever since. "Family Appreciation Day" shows her telling the story to Apple Bloom's skeptical class and winning their respect; "Apple Family Reunion," "The Super Speedy Cider Squeezy 6000," "Bats!" and "The Perfect Pear" fill in her family's past. In "The Last Problem" the Apple siblings wear her scarf, implying she passed away during the time skip.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Old-timey rural American farm dialect — "young'uns," "fellers," "land sakes," "hunky-dory," "in my day" — full of rambling tangents and repetitions. She calls everyone "dearie" or "young'un," and her famous command is "Move your caboose!" She sings her instructions, especially about zap apples.
**Notable Quotes:** "Move your caboose!"; "Now wait, you fellers — hold it!" [paraphrased from her zap-apple song]; "Back in my day, we didn't have any of these fancy doohickeys — and we were grateful!" [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Tabitha St. Germain. Debut: "Friendship is Magic, part 1" (2010). "Granny Smith" is a real green-apple variety — the Apple family is named after apples, and Word of God holds that ponies do not use surnames like humans. Her catchphrase "Move your caboose!" is a fan favorite. The human Granny Smith appears in the *Equestria Girls* world as an elderly background character [brief cameo details uncertain].

## Gallus

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — *Friendship Is Magic* (Season 8–9); no *Equestria Girls* counterpart.
**Species/Kind:** Griffon (eagle head and wings, lion-like hindquarters).
**Gender:** Male (he/him) — referred to as "he" and by name throughout the series.
**Age/Stage:** Teenaged student.
**Residence:** Griffonstone originally; the School of Friendship in Ponyville; in the future, Canterlot as a Royal Guard.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Student of the School of Friendship and Griffonstone's student ambassador; one of the Young Six. In "The Last Problem" he has joined the Canterlot Royal Guard.
**Appearance:** A striking bright-blue griffon with darker blue wings, tail feathers and head crest, a cream-white face and chest, a yellow-gold beak and talons, and sharp blue eyes. He has a lean, athletic build with wings and forelegs built for flight, and a tufted crest that stands up. His blue coloring is unusual among the more muted griffons of Griffonstone. No cutie mark (griffons do not have them).
**Personality:** A deadpan snarker with a chip on his shoulder. Gallus is sarcastic, aloof and quick to mock anything mushy, but he is a textbook "jerk with a heart of gold": brave, loyal, protective, and secretly lonely. He grew up an orphan in Griffonstone, which explains his defensiveness and fear of being vulnerable. He is skeptical of friendship lessons at first, then becomes one of the group's most dependable members; he hides kindness behind jokes.
**Likes:** His friends, flying, jokes and teasing, Griffonstone pride, freedom, being useful, and proving he can handle things alone.
**Dislikes:** Mushy feelings, being pitied, enclosed spaces, strict rules (initially), EEA-style teaching, being underestimated, and ponies who lecture about friendship.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Claustrophobia — small, enclosed spaces (confronted in "What Lies Beneath"); abandonment and loneliness; losing his friends; showing weakness. He is also hot-tempered, pushes people away, and has no magic.
**Skills/Abilities:** Fast, agile flight; sharp beak and talons; strength and combat instincts strong enough that he joins the Royal Guard in the future; survival skills from growing up in rough Griffonstone; sharp wit and quick thinking. Limits: no magic, a short fuse, and a tendency to bottle up fear.
**Family & Relationships:** An orphan raised in Griffonstone; his only guardian figure is the elderly griffon Grampa Gruff [uncertain how formal the relationship is]. His true family is the Young Six — Sandbar (first friend), Yona, Ocellus, Silverstream and Smolder. He idolizes (and is unimpressed by) Rainbow Dash and knows Gilda.
**Background/Biography:** Gallus arrives in "School Daze, Part 1" as Griffonstone's ambassador student, sarcastic and reluctant. He and the other outsiders skip class, bond, and accidentally damage the school before facing the puckwudgies together. "The Hearth's Warming Club" reveals his lonely orphan childhood and his habit of hiding it; "What Lies Beneath" traps him in a small space and forces him to face his claustrophobia; "School Raze" tests the group against the Tree of Harmony. By the distant finale he has become a Canterlot Royal Guard — a quietly perfect arc for the griffon who didn't believe in friendship.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Dry, deadpan, sarcastic; short clipped sentences delivered with a smirk. He says "Yeah, right," "Whatever," "Sure, sure," and "Look..." to change the subject whenever feelings come up; he calls people by their names with a teasing edge. When sincere, he is brief and awkward.
**Notable Quotes:** "Guess our friendship is pretty magical after all."; "No, I just figured I'd stand here and see how many ponies just walk into me."; "What? I like to be prepared."
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Gavin Langelo. Debut: "School Daze, Part 1" (S8E1, 2018). TVTropes lists the irony that the formerly rebellious, rule-bending young griffon ends up in the Canterlot Royal Guard. TVTropes began listing him as the Young Six member least likely to admit he cares. His bright blue color scheme is deliberately unusual for a griffon.

Like all spa staff she is happiest when her guests are relaxed. [Personality detail beyond this is thin in canon.]

## King Sombra

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — *Friendship Is Magic* (main villain of the Season 3 premiere and the Season 9 premiere); no *Equestria Girls* counterpart.
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn stallion — a dark-magic unicorn king; the IDW comics reveal his true nature as one of the Umbrum (shadow ponies) from beneath the Crystal Palace, though that is expanded-universe material.
**Gender:** Male (he/him) — he is addressed as a king and "he" at all times.
**Age/Stage:** Ancient — more than a thousand years old; he was imprisoned in ice/shadow for a millennium, destroyed in Season 3, and resurrected by Grogar in Season 9.
**Residence:** The Crystal Empire — its Crystal Palace/castle; briefly the throne of Canterlot in Season 9; after his destruction, "the ether."
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Former king and tyrant of the Crystal Empire, enslaver of the crystal ponies; dark sorcerer; resurrected ally (and then loose cannon) of Grogar's Legion of Doom in Season 9.
**Appearance:** A tall, gaunt charcoal-grey to black unicorn stallion with a ragged black mane and tail, glowing green eyes with red-purple markings, a long curved horn that glows red at its tip, and sharp fangs when he snarls. In his first appearance he is mostly a shadowy mass of dark smoke and black crystals with two burning green eyes; in Season 9 he has a fully realized design — dark armor with a black helm set with a red gem, a grey-black chestplate, and a long red cape. His lower body is often wreathed in shadow, and his cutie mark is normally hidden by cape and armor and is not clearly established [uncertain — a red crystal motif is often cited]. He can dissolve into and reform from black smoke and purple-black crystal.
**Personality:** Cruel, arrogant, sadistic and theatrical. Sombra is a classic power-hungry tyrant who savors fear, mocks his enemies, and treats conquest as his right. He is intelligent and patient as a schemer (he feigned defeat to trick the Mane Six into revealing the Tree of Harmony), but also pompous, self-indulgent and unable to resist gloating — a fatal flaw. He despises harmony, the Crystal Heart and the princesses, and in the comics his story is tinged with tragedy and a genuine attachment to Radiant Hope.
**Likes:** Power, fear, black crystals, the Crystal Empire, mind-controlled servants, dramatic entrances, gloating, and the sound of his own voice.
**Dislikes:** The Crystal Heart, harmony and friendship magic, Celestia and Luna, being mocked (especially by Discord), Grogar's authority, and being underestimated.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** The Crystal Heart and the magic of friendship — the only forces shown to truly destroy him; being erased for good; losing his kingdom. He is arrogant, overconfident, and his power collapses when his opponents unite; he also depends on enslaved minions rather than allies. His own gloating gives enemies openings.
**Skills/Abilities:** Powerful dark magic — shadow/umbral projection, teleportation, fear illusions and nightmares, mind control via crystal helmets, crystal magic (spikes, cages, black crystals that can destroy the Tree of Harmony), weather/terrain manipulation, millennia-long curses, and regeneration/healing (he reforms from shadow; in the comics his horn and a shard of crystal are used to revive him). He conquered an empire, destroyed the Tree of Harmony and the Elements, and enslaved Ponyville and Canterlot. Limits: he cannot withstand concentrated friendship or Crystal Heart magic, and he is a poor team player.
**Family & Relationships:** No canon family. In the IDW comics he was raised in the Crystal Empire, his childhood friend was the crystal pony Radiant Hope, and he crystallized and shattered Princess Amore, the previous ruler, in his rise to power [comics/expanded universe — not shown in the show]. In the show, his only "allies" are Grogar (who resurrects him as a pawn) and his brainwashed slaves; his enemies are the princesses, the Mane Six, Spike, Discord and Flurry Heart.
**Background/Biography:** Over a thousand years ago, Sombra seized the Crystal Empire and enslaved its ponies; Celestia and Luna defeated him and hid the Empire away, casting him into the ice/shadow. When the Crystal Empire returns in the Season 3 premiere ("The Crystal Empire"), he returns as a shadow king, curses the empire with fear and darkness, and is destroyed when Spike delivers the Crystal Heart. Grogar resurrects him in the Season 9 premiere ("The Beginning of the End"), and Sombra immediately goes rogue: he seizes the Crystal Empire, takes Flurry Heart hostage, tricks the Mane Six into destroying the Tree of Harmony and the Elements, brainwashes Ponyville, and takes Canterlot. Discord intervenes, the Mane Six awaken their own inner friendship magic, and Sombra is destroyed for good — "Deader than Dead," as Grogar notes when using his failure to intimidate the other villains.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Grand, menacing, old-fashioned villain speech delivered in a low growl; he relishes threats, rhetorical questions and mocking laughter. He refers to himself as king ("Long live the king"), speaks in imperatives ("Bow before me"), and narrates his own triumphs. In Season 9 he is notably more pompous and verbose than in Season 3, where he barely spoke.
**Notable Quotes:** "Long live the king."; "You thought you'd defeated me, but you led me right to the source of your power! Now that it's destroyed, nothing can stop me!"; "The Crystal Empire is mine — and soon, all of Equestria will kneel!" [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Alvin Sanders. Debut: "The Crystal Empire, Part 1" (S3E1, November 2012). "Sombra" means "shadow" in Spanish and Portuguese. He is the first G4 villain to be destroyed outright without a redemption arc — and the only one killed on screen twice (Season 3 and Season 9). His canon origin is deliberately vague; the IDW "Siege of the Crystal Empire" arc supplies the Umbrum/Radiant Hope backstory, which Season 9 overrides by having Grogar resurrect him as an unredeemed villain.

## Derpy Hooves

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls. In *Friendship Is Magic* she is the famous background-turned-speaking pegasus; in *Equestria Girls* her human counterpart is a Canterlot High student who cameos from the first film onward.
**Species/Kind:** Pegasus pony (human in the *Equestria Girls* world).
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare (human counterpart: teenage student).
**Residence:** Ponyville; in EG, the human world.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Mailmare at the Ponyville post office — she is Ponyville's mail courier; in the distant finale a newspaper photo shows her in a chef's hat beside a muffin, suggesting a future in baking. In EG she is a CHS student.
**Appearance:** A grey pegasus mare with a light blonde mane and tail, warm yellow eyes that are famously crossed (strabismus), and a cutie mark of a cluster of bubbles. She usually carries a mailbag, and her expression is a slightly cross-eyed, earnest smile. As a filly in "The Cart Before the Ponies" she is a small, determined kid with the same blonde mane. In *Equestria Girls* her human counterpart has grey skin, blonde hair, crossed eyes and a friendly face, appearing as a background CHS student. She has no other forms.
**Personality:** Sweet, well-meaning and cheerfully dim — a ditzy klutz with the best intentions in the world. Derpy is kind to everyone, quick to apologize, easily distracted by shiny things, and prone to spectacular accidents; she means well even when she causes chaos. Underneath the comedy she is brave and surprisingly resourceful, and she cares deeply about doing her job right. Fandom loves her for exactly this mix of earnestness and disaster.
**Likes:** Muffins (her canonical favorite food), delivering mail, shiny and sparkly objects, Doctor Hooves's inventions, helping with weddings, flying, and being included.
**Dislikes:** Being blamed, letting others down, complicated instructions, rain and soggy mail, and seeing her friends upset.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Her own clumsiness and the chaos it causes; disappointing others; being laughed at. She has poor eyesight/coordination, is easily distracted, causes structural damage by accident (she wrecked the Ponyville Town Hall roof and floor in "The Last Roundup"), and cannot follow complex instructions.
**Skills/Abilities:** Strong, tireless flight; an encyclopedic knowledge of Ponyville's mail routes; surprising physical toughness; a knack for accidental solutions (she saved Cranky and Matilda's wedding by replacing missing flowers with flameless fireworks); a good heart that wins people over. Limits: klutziness, crossed eyes, short attention span and poor judgment.
**Family & Relationships:** Her canon family is not established; the fan-favorite "Dinky Hooves" daughter is fanon, not canon. Her closest on-screen relationship is with Doctor Hooves (Time Turner), who is her best friend and partner in "Slice of Life"; fandom widely ships them. She is a beloved fixture of Ponyville and a friend to the Mane Six; in EG she is a background student. In the finale's newspaper she appears in a chef's hat with a muffin.
**Background/Biography:** Derpy debuted as an anonymous background pegasus in the pilot and became a fan phenomenon because of her crossed eyes and scrunchy face; animators began giving her scripted cameos. "The Last Roundup" (Season 2) gave her her first lines and the name "Derpy" (later re-dubbed in some releases), including the destruction of the town hall. She returned without a voice in Season 4, then starred in the background-pony showcase "Slice of Life" (Season 5), where she accidentally misprints Cranky and Matilda's wedding invitations, subcontracts the job to Featherweight, and then saves the ceremony with flameless fireworks. She continues to appear in crowd scenes and the finale's crowded-cast shots, and the finale's newspaper gag implies she later becomes a baker.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Slow, slightly slurred, ditzy speech with cheerful, wandering sentences; she says exactly what she means and then gets distracted. Her signature line is apologetic: "I just don't know what went wrong!" She is often called "Muffins" by credits/fans, but no pony ever calls her that on screen.
**Notable Quotes:** "I just don't know what went wrong!"; "Look out!" [paraphrased]; "Muffins!" [paraphrased/fandom shorthand — not spoken in canon].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Tabitha St. Germain. Background debut: "Friendship is Magic, part 1"; first speaking appearance: "The Last Roundup" (S2E14); central role: "Slice of Life" (S5E9, the 100th episode, where the credits call her "Muffins"). Name variants: Derpy Hooves (fandom name, briefly used on screen), Ditzy Doo (a name mentioned in "Winter Wrap Up" and intended for her), Muffins (credits), Bubbles (some merchandise, from her cutie mark). Her original "Derpy" scene was re-recorded and re-animated after complaints, making her one of the show's most-discussed background characters. She is the unofficial mascot of the brony fandom and has a human counterpart in the EG films.

## Apple Bloom

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — Applejack's little sister and a founding Cutie Mark Crusader; she has a human counterpart at Canterlot High in the EG films.
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony (human in *Equestria Girls*).
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Filly/young school-age pony for most of the series (one audition script listed age 7 [not canon]); adult mare in the finale epilogue.
**Residence:** Sweet Apple Acres, Ponyville; in the future epilogue she lives in Ponyville/teaches at the School of Friendship. In EG she is a Canterlot High student.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Student at the Ponyville school; co-founder and driving force of the Cutie Mark Crusaders; future teacher at the School of Friendship, where she teaches a class about cutie marks. Member of the Apple family.
**Appearance:** An adorable pale-yellow earth pony filly with a fluffy pinkish-red mane and tail, a huge pink bow on top of her head, orange-amber eyes, and freckles. For most of the series she is a "blank flank," and the plot often lingers on her bare flank. In "Crusaders of the Lost Mark" she earns her cutie mark with her friends: a red/pink/magenta striped shield emblem with two symbols overlaid on it (a heart and an apple-blossom motif, matching her name) — the shield symbolizes the Crusaders' unbreakable bond [exact inner symbols uncertain]. In EG her human counterpart has yellow skin, red hair, and the same pink bow, appearing as a CHS student.
**Personality:** Spunky, determined, optimistic and a little stubborn — Apple Bloom is the spark plug of the Crusaders. She is brave, friendly, hardworking and outrageously persistent, and she becomes the group's natural organizer and leader. Early on she is deeply insecure about being a blank flank; later she matures into a thoughtful helper. She is tomboyish, curious, occasionally bossy, and fiercely protective of her friends and family.
**Likes:** Farming and building things, solving problems, her friends Sweetie Belle and Scootaloo, her family, apple treats, crusading, potion-making, and helping other ponies find their cutie marks.
**Dislikes:** Being teased as a blank flank (early seasons), being treated like a baby, giving up, waiting patiently, chores that keep her from crusading, and bullies like Diamond Tiara and Silver Spoon (early on).
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Never finding her cutie mark (the central early-series fear), losing her friends, letting her family down. She is naive, impulsive and a poor singer/dancer (the Crusaders' disastrous talent show), and as a filly she has no magic and limited strength.
**Skills/Abilities:** Exceptionally good with her hooves — she built and repaired the Crusaders' clubhouse single-hoofedly and is a natural carpenter/set-builder; talented potion-brewer (the Cutie Pox potion, the Love Potion in "Hearts and Hooves Day"); strong Apple farm worker for her age; determined researcher; and later a gifted teacher of cutie-mark lore. Limits: a child's body, no magic, and poor performing skills.
**Family & Relationships:** Daughter of Bright Macintosh and Pear Butter (both deceased before the series); younger sister to Applejack and Big Macintosh; granddaughter of Granny Smith; niece of Grand Pear; cousin to Babs Seed, Braeburn and the far-flung Apple clan; close friends with Sweetie Belle and Scootaloo (the Cutie Mark Crusaders) and eventually friends with Diamond Tiara. In EG her human counterpart is a CHS student alongside the human Sweetie Belle and Scootaloo.
**Background/Biography:** In "Call of the Cutie," Apple Bloom's lack of a cutie mark makes her a target for teasing, and she desperately tries to earn one; she meets Sweetie Belle and Scootaloo and founds the Cutie Mark Crusaders in "The Cutie Mark Chronicles"/"Call of the Cutie" era. Their endless, usually disastrous crusades fill Seasons 1–5: talent shows, potions, gossip columns, love potions and more. In "Crusaders of the Lost Mark" the three finally earn their marks by realizing their talent is helping others find theirs, and they keep crusading for other ponies. In later seasons she helps Big Mac and Sugar Belle, grows up in "Growing Up Is Hard to Do," and by "The Last Problem" has become a teacher at the School of Friendship.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Bubbly, country-flavored filly speech with contractions and Southern-Apple-family warmth ("Aren'tcha gonna stay for brunch?"). She shouts "Cutie Mark Crusaders — yay!" as the group's rallying cry, complains about being a "blank flank," and uses enthusiastic, slightly ungrammatical kid phrasing.
**Notable Quotes:** "Aren'tcha gonna stay for brunch?"; "Cutie Mark Crusaders — yay!"; "We'll make our mark, one way or another!" [paraphrased, the Crusaders' motto].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Michelle Creber. Debut: "Friendship is Magic, part 1" (2010). Lauren Faust originally intended her name to be "Apple Blossom," but a late executive decision changed it. Her big pink bow is one of G4's most iconic accessory designs. She and her friends earn their cutie marks in "Crusaders of the Lost Mark" (S5E18), one of the show's most acclaimed episodes. In the finale epilogue she wears Goldie Delicious's shawl, implying her mentor has passed.

## Fluttershy

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — Mane Six member (Element of Kindness); the human Fluttershy of Canterlot High in the EG films.
**Species/Kind:** Pegasus pony (human in *Equestria Girls*).
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare (EG: teenaged high-school student).
**Residence:** Ponyville — a cozy cottage at the edge of the Everfree Forest, surrounded by animal pens and burrows; in EG, the human world.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Animal caretaker and animal expert; bearer of the Element of Kindness; founder and operator of her own animal sanctuary ("Fluttershy Leans In"), which she continues to run in the future with Discord's help; member of the Mane Six. In EG: CHS student and Rainbooms member (tambourine/vocals).
**Appearance:** A delicate pale-yellow pegasus mare with a long, flowing light-pink mane and tail, big teal-cyan eyes, and a cutie mark of three pink butterflies. She is smaller and softer-looking than the other Mane Six, with a gentle, slightly hunched posture and wings she often keeps folded. In "Bridle Gossip" she is cursed with a deep bass "Flutterguy" voice; in "Hurricane Fluttershy" her tiny wings are a plot point. In *Equestria Girls* she has pale skin, long pink hair, teal eyes, and soft, nature-inspired outfits; her Rainbooms look is a butterfly-themed dress.
**Personality:** Sweet, timid, gentle and profoundly kind — Fluttershy is the softest heart in Equestria, and also one of its bravest. She is shy to the point of anxiety, hates crowds, loud noise and being the center of attention, and apologizes constantly; she can be a doormat and struggles with assertiveness. But when an animal or friend is threatened she becomes fiercely determined, using her legendary "Stare" to cow monsters. She is patient, empathetic, a good listener, and quietly wise.
**Likes:** Animals of every kind, quiet mornings, tea, knitting and sewing, nature, her friends, caring for creatures, soft singing, and Angel Bunny (her bossy pet rabbit).
**Dislikes:** Loud noises, crowds, public speaking, confrontation, cruelty or neglect toward animals, being rushed, being called a coward, and seeing her friends fight.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Stage fright and public speaking; flying and heights (she is a weak flier); being pushed around; her own temper when it finally erupts; letting others down. She is physically the least strong of the Mane Six, easily frightened, and too willing to put others' needs above her own.
**Skills/Abilities:** Peerless animal care and communication — she can nurse, tame and understand creatures from bunnies to dragons; the "Stare," a piercing glare that stops monsters in their tracks; expert first aid/veterinary skill; sewing and knitting; a beautiful singing voice; flying (weak but improving, as in "Hurricane Fluttershy"). Limits: poor combatant, no offensive magic, fragile nerves.
**Family & Relationships:** Parents Mr. and Mrs. Shy, and a younger brother, Zephyr Breeze (a lazy but good-hearted slacker). Her pet rabbit Angel Bunny is her constant companion; Discord is her closest non-pony friend (the two share a special bond, and he helps run her sanctuary in the future). Her best friends are the Mane Six; she is especially close to Rainbow Dash (childhood friend) and Rarity (spa buddy).
**Background/Biography:** As a filly in Cloudsdale, Fluttershy was a weak flier; during a race she fell from the sky and was saved by the blast of Rainbow Dash's first Sonic Rainboom, which also scattered butterflies around her and gave her her cutie mark. She moved to Ponyville, built her animal cottage, and became the Element of Kindness. Her key episodes include "Dragonshy" (staring down a dragon), "Stare Master," "Putting Your Hoof Down" (learning assertiveness from Iron Will), "Hurricane Fluttershy" (finding her flight), "Fluttershy Leans In" (building her animal sanctuary), and "Fake It 'Til You Make It" (assertiveness in business). In "The Last Problem" she runs her sanctuary with Discord's help.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Very soft, breathy and hesitant, often trailing off; frequent "Um...", "Oh my...", "I'm sorry," and "If that's okay..." She whispers more than she speaks, and her rare bursts of assertiveness are all the more striking. Her canonical catchphrase is "Oh my." She also hums and sings gently to her animals.
**Notable Quotes:** "Oh my."; "I'm not afraid of you!" [paraphrased, from her Stare moments]; "All any creature needs is a little kindness." [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Andrea Libman (who also voices Pinkie Pie). Debut: "Friendship is Magic, part 1" (2010). The "Stare" is one of the show's most iconic running gags. Her deep-voice alter ego from "Bridle Gossip" is a fan favorite. Her human counterpart in EG is a Rainbooms member and animal lover. Her animal sanctuary ("Fluttershy Leans In") is a major late-series development.

## Adagio Dazzle

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — the lead villain of *My Little Pony: Equestria Girls – Rainbow Rocks* (2014); a siren from Equestria in the human world.
**Species/Kind:** Siren — an ancient aquatic, mer-horse-like creature from Equestria that feeds on negative emotion and mind-controls through song; in the human world she is trapped in the body of a teenage human girl.
**Gender:** Female (she/her). She calls her bandmates her "sisters," though whether that is literal is unclear.
**Age/Stage:** Ancient (over a thousand years old, banished from Equestria about a millennium ago) but appearing as a teenage human girl; her siren form is full-grown.
**Residence:** Equestria in ancient times; after banishment, the human world — she and the Dazzlings pose as students at Canterlot High.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Leader of the Dazzlings, a siren singing trio with Sonata Dusk and Aria Blaze; undercover CHS student; frontwoman of their Battle of the Bands entry. Her purpose is to harvest enough negative energy to restore the sirens' full power and rule both worlds.
**Appearance:** In human form Adagio is a striking teenage girl with pale golden/cream skin, a huge, voluminous cloud of curly orange-blond hair with lighter highlights, and vivid magenta-violet eyes. She wears a red-and-magenta stage outfit with gold accents and a prominent red gem pendant at her throat — the amulet that stores her siren power. In her true siren form she becomes a golden-yellow mer-horse creature with a long, finned fish tail, fin-like webbed ears, an orange-gold mane, an elongated horn-like crest, and the same red gem glowing at her chest. When the Dazzlings' amulets shatter at the end of *Rainbow Rocks*, her siren features vanish and she is left an ordinary human. Her human-world outfits vary between the film and later shorts, but the red gem and the enormous curls are her signatures.
**Personality:** Arrogant, manipulative, charismatic and vain — a born diva and cult leader. Adagio is the brains and face of the Dazzlings: smooth, condescending, endlessly self-assured, and happiest when a crowd is eating out of her hand. She treats Sonata and Aria as underlings, enjoys humiliating rivals, and savors the chaos she creates; she is also a genuinely magnetic performer and a cunning strategist. Her confidence is brittle: once the sisters lose their power, she is lost, humiliated and reduced to scraping by — later shorts show her trying to rebuild her ego. She is not physically brave.
**Likes:** Adoration and applause, power, the spotlight, chaos and conflict (her food supply), her sisters' obedience, winning, humiliating the Rainbooms, and singing.
**Dislikes:** Being ignored or upstaged, losing control, losing her voice and magic, the Rainbooms and Sunset Shimmer, Sonata's goofy tangents, Aria's constant bickering, and being pitied.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Losing her siren power and becoming an ordinary nobody — the central fear explored in *Find the Magic*; humiliation; defeat. She cannot fight physically, her power depends entirely on her voice and amulet, and her arrogance costs her: she underestimates Sunset Shimmer and the Rainbooms, and her team's infighting undermines her plans.
**Skills/Abilities:** The sirens' song — hypnotic, mind-controlling music that magnifies hatred and conflict in everyone who hears it and lets the Dazzlings feed on the resulting negative energy; superb singing, stagecraft and crowd manipulation; strategic planning and leadership; siren form grants flight and sonic power (their voices can shatter crystal and blast opponents). Limits: no power without her voice/amulet, no physical combat skill, and her ego makes her careless.
**Family & Relationships:** Sonata Dusk and Aria Blaze are her bandmates and are referred to as her sisters (in the *Legends of Magic* comic) — literal or not, the relationship is a domineering older-sister dynamic: Sonata is childish and distracted, Aria is sarcastic and combative, and Adagio keeps both in line. Her enemies are the Rainbooms and Sunset Shimmer; after their defeat, Pinkie Pie mentions Adagio and Aria among her friends in *Holidays Unwrapped*.
**Background/Biography:** In ancient Equestria the sirens were power-hungry creatures who fed on discord and adoration; the Pillars of Equestria (Star Swirl the Bearded and his allies) banished them to the human world, as shown in the *Friendship Is Magic* episode "Shadow Play – Part 2." There the three landed without magic, trapped in human bodies. Sensing the magic released at the end of the first *Equestria Girls* film, Adagio leads Sonata and Aria to Canterlot High, enrolls them as students, forms the band and orchestrates the Battle of the Bands so that the school's escalating rivalry will feed them. They almost win, but the Rainbooms — with Sunset Shimmer — counter their song, and their red amulets shatter. Stripped of power and voice, the Dazzlings are jeered offstage. The digital short *Find the Magic* shows them as lost souls adapting to a magic-less life, while *Sunset's Backstage Pass* shows they still have their egos, though Adagio admits their old villainy was the wrong approach.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** A smooth, sultry, drawling diva purr full of condescension; she stretches out vowels and uses a light, mocking laugh. She calls her group "the Dazzlings," refers to "us" and "our" as a franchise, and dismisses opponents with "Oh, please," "How adorable," and "Pathetic." When giving orders she is clipped and imperious; when performing she croons.
**Notable Quotes:** "Me and you, you and me / why don't we see who is better?" (the Dazzlings' song); "You don't know what you're dealing with, do you?" [paraphrased]; "We are the Dazzlings — and this school is ours now." [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Kazumi Evans, who also provides Rarity's singing voice. Debut: *My Little Pony: Equestria Girls – Rainbow Rocks* (2014). The Dazzlings' powers live in their red gem pendants; their banishment by the Pillars was retconned on screen in "Shadow Play – Part 2" (Season 7) from the earlier IDW comic version. They are described as the only known sirens, and fandom calls their true form "merhorse sirens." Post-*Rainbow Rocks* appearances: *Find the Magic*, *Sunset's Backstage Pass*, and a mention in *Holidays Unwrapped*.
