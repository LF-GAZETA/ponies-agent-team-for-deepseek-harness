## Discord

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic (seasons 2–9), plus the Pony Life spin-off. He has no Equestria Girls human-world counterpart: in the EG franchise he appears only as a plush toy in a Rainbow Rocks digital short.
**Species/Kind:** Draconequus — a rare chimeric creature, the embodiment of chaos and disharmony, with a pony's head and a body assembled from many different animals.
**Gender:** Male (he/him).
**Age/Stage:** Ancient and effectively ageless; he is over a thousand years old and ruled Equestria long before Princess Celestia and Princess Luna.
**Residence:** No fixed home. He keeps a chaotic house in Chaosville, can teleport anywhere at will, spent centuries petrified in the Canterlot sculpture garden, and treats Fluttershy's cottage as a second home.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Spirit of chaos and disharmony; former absolute ruler of Equestria; later the Mane Six's "not-always-reliable ally"; honorary Cutie Mark Crusader (IDW comics); Vice Principal of the School of Friendship; recipient of the Equestrian Pink Heart of Courage.
**Appearance:** A tall, lanky draconequus: amber-gray pony head with a very dark gray mane and bushy white eyebrows; bright yellow eyes with crimson, mismatched pupils; a goat's beard, one long fang, and a forked snake tongue. He has a deer antler on the right and a blue goat horn on the left; his right arm is a lion's paw and his left arm an eagle's talon; his right leg is a lizard claw and his left leg a goat hoof; one bat wing and one feathered pegasus wing sprout from his back. His torso is a serpent-like dark gray-red-brown, ending in a dragon-like purplish-red tail with a white tip. He casts magic with a finger snap and a bright yellow aura, appears in a flash of white light, and has no cutie mark — the only exceptions are a unicorn disguise with a gray-tornado mark and a pony disguise with a power-cord mark.
**Personality:** Theatrical, sardonic, mischievous, and endlessly inventive. Discord genuinely enjoys chaos, pranks, and watching others squirm, and he is arrogant, attention-hungry, and a sore loser. Underneath the showmanship he is deeply lonely: he has no friends, fears being unwanted, and visibly shrinks when he feels rejected or ignored (a recurring visual gag). He is capable of real loyalty and kindness, but his "grand plans" and refusal to explain himself repeatedly cause disasters.
**Likes:** Chaos magic, practical jokes, chocolate-milk rain, cotton-candy clouds, popcorn-and-drama, tea parties with Fluttershy, the Smooze, the role-playing game "Trolls and Dungeons," being appreciated, and proving he is smarter than everypony else.
**Dislikes:** Order, rules, schedules, boredom, being taken for granted or left out, being lectured, friendship lessons being forced on him, and ponies who refuse to play along.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** His greatest fear is having no friends and being alone. He is powerless when his magic is drained (as Tirek did), cannot use chaos magic inside anti-magic areas such as Queen Chrysalis's throne room, can be petrified again by the Elements of Harmony, and his ego and flattery-gullibility make him easy to manipulate.
**Skills/Abilities:** Near-limitless reality warping: teleportation, conjuring, transmutation, illusions, hypnosis, size alteration, shapeshifting, and the ability to sense magical imbalance. He can reshape whole landscapes with a snap and temporarily empower or transform others. Limitations: his chaos magic can be nullified, drained, or blocked, and it does not work on beings who are emotionally immune to his games.
**Family & Relationships:** No known biological family in the show (IDW comics introduce a cousin/parallel counterpart, Princess Eris). His closest bond is with Fluttershy, his first true friend and the one pony who reformed him; he is a friend of the Mane Six and Spike, an annoying mentor figure to Twilight Sparkle, and a friendly rival of Starlight Glimmer, Trixie, and Thorax. His enemies include Tirek (whom he helped and was then betrayed by), Queen Chrysalis, Cozy Glow, and King Sombra.
**Background/Biography:** An ancient spirit of chaos who once ruled Equestria; Celestia and Luna petrified him using the Elements of Harmony. In season 2, with the Elements' bond weakened, he broke free, stole the Elements, corrupted the Mane Six one by one, and plunged Ponyville into chaos before the restored Elements defeated him. Celestia later gave him a chance: Fluttershy befriended him, and when he betrayed and then lost her friendship he finally understood it, repaired the harm, and was "reformed, most of the time." He has since been an unreliable but valued ally: he betrayed the ponies for Tirek, was betrayed in turn, and his medallion gift helped unlock the Rainbow Power; he reformed the Smooze, fought Chrysalis's changelings, became Vice Principal of the School of Friendship, and in season 9 disguised himself as Grogar to test Twilight — a scheme that backfired catastrophically. In the finale he helped petrify the three villains and, in the future, remains Fluttershy's friend and regular tea guest.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Grand, theatrical, and sarcastic, with old-fashioned flourishes, grandiose vocabulary, puns, and mock-politeness. He addresses ponies as "my little ponies," says Twilight's full name "Twilight Sparkle" for emphasis, drops dramatic pauses, uses occasional Italian ("arrivederci"), and narrates his own schemes in the third person. Catchphrases include "Oh, poo," "What fun is there in making sense?", and "the spirit of chaos and disharmony."
**Notable Quotes:** "Make sense? Oh, what fun is there in making sense?" (S2E1) · "I'm not playing fair? Perhaps we haven't met. I'm Discord, spirit of chaos and disharmony. Hello?" (S2E2) · "Oh. Well, I've... never really had a friend before." (S3E10) · "You are the pony who taught me that friendship is magic. I had magic and friendship. And now, I don't have either." (S4E26).
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by John de Lancie, who based the performance on his Star Trek: The Next Generation role Q (his recorded lines were sped up for the final voice). Created during Lauren Faust's tenure; first appearance "The Return of Harmony, Part 1" (2011). The word "discord" means lack of harmony. "Draconequus" is spelled inconsistently across official material ("dragonokis," "draconequis," "draconaquis"). In Pony Life, one Volcano episode recast him with Peter New. Fandom treats him as the fandom-beloved embodiment of chaos; the Grogar reveal in season 9 is one of the show's most discussed twists.

## Rainbowshine

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic (seasons 1–9) and background cameos in the Equestria Girls films; also a background flier in My Little Pony: The Movie. No human-world counterpart is established.
**Species/Kind:** Pegasus pony (female); a "crystal pony" variant of her appears in the Crystal Empire episodes.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare.
**Residence:** Ponyville; she works both on the Ponyville weather team and at the Cloudsdale weather factory, a daily commute the collectible cards joke about.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Weather pony — cloud wrangling, snow clearing, bird migration and rain production; weather-factory worker in Cloudsdale; background participant in Ponyville festivals and events.
**Appearance:** A light indigo/medium periwinkle pegasus mare with a two-tone pale-pink-and-pale-purple mane and tail, and striking vivid red eyes. Her cutie mark is a three-colored rainbow arc (orange, yellow, blue in the show; some merchandise shows orange, yellow, and purple). She is slender and average in build, and often wears the green weather-team vest and sash during the Winter Wrap Up. In the Crystal Empire scenes her coat becomes crystalline and faceted like the other crystal ponies.
**Personality:** Cheerful, helpful, and chatty — the collectible material describes her as forever asking questions and "loving to leap." She is diligent about weather work, quick to volunteer, and a little bit of a gossip; she can also be flustered and quick to complain when somepony else's weather spell ruins the day (as in "Magical Mystery Cure").
**Likes:** Flying, leaping into the sky, weather work, bird migration season, questions and gossip, craft fairs and shopping, festivals and parades, and chatting with friends such as Sassaflash, Sprinkle Medley, Sunshower Raindrops, and Kicks.
**Dislikes:** Mismanaged weather, waiting around, being left out of the loop, and being startled by strange creatures or sudden magical mishaps.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Not much is established; she panics along with the crowd when monsters or magic threaten Ponyville, and she is easily distracted by gossip and shopping.
**Skills/Abilities:** Competent pegasus flight, cloud-pushing and cloud-sculpting, snow removal, guiding migrating birds, and weather-factory work (mixing rainbows, filling cloud machines). Otherwise an ordinary background weather pony with no combat or magic feats.
**Family & Relationships:** No family members are named in canon. She is regularly paired on screen with fellow weather pegasi (Sprinkle Medley, Sunshower Raindrops, Kicks) and with Sassaflash; she is a background friend of the Mane Six rather than a close character.
**Background/Biography:** Rainbowshine first appears as an unnamed background pegasus in the series premiere "Friendship Is Magic, Part 1" (waiting for the Summer Sun Celebration) and first speaks in "Winter Wrap Up," where she reports that Ditzy Doo flew north for the southern birds. She goes on to appear in dozens of background scenes: Cloudsdale's weather factory in "Rainbow Falls" (where she suggests Rarity enter the competition), the royal wedding, the Equestria Games, the Crystal Empire, and the final Summer Sun Celebration. She is seen as a crystal pony in "Twilight's Kingdom," as a spectator at the Washouts' shows, and as a flier at the Friendship Festival in the 2017 movie. She is named only in merchandise, and the IDW comics and mobile game use her in background or renamed roles.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Ordinary, friendly small-town Ponyville speech; eager and slightly gossipy, with a touch of drama when delivering news like a weather mistake.
**Notable Quotes:** "Ditzy Doo accidentally went north to get the southern birds!" ("Winter Wrap Up") · "Hey, there's an idea! You should enter the competition!" ("Rainbow Falls") · "Are you saying you lied?" (to Applejack, "Leap of Faith") · "Hooves down, cakes up!" ("Rainbow Falls").
**Trivia/Notes:** Never named on screen; the name comes from blind-bag toys and trading cards, where she is also called "Inquisitive Shopper" in the mobile game. Voiced by Tabitha St. Germain in her first lines, with Trevor Devall voicing her in one fourth-season appearance; Japanese voice by Yumi Uchiyama. Her cutie mark's colors vary by merchandise. The Enterplay card quote about throwing Foggy Fleece a retirement party is one of her few pieces of characterization. [uncertain] Some fan sources list her as a Wonderbolts Academy candidate, but that is not established in canon.

## Flash Sentry

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — his pony form debuts in Equestria in My Little Pony: Equestria Girls (2013) and appears again in Friendship is Magic; his human form is a recurring Equestria Girls character across the films, specials, and shorts.
**Species/Kind:** Pegasus pony (male) in Equestria; human (male) in the human world. In one IDW story he appears as an alicorn, and in Spring Breakdown his human form briefly becomes a pegasus after crossing into Equestria.
**Gender:** Male (he/him).
**Age/Stage:** Young adult — a grown royal guard in Equestria and a high-school student in the human world.
**Residence:** Crystal Empire (Equestria, where he serves the royal guard); Canterlot (human world, where he attends Canterlot High School).
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** High-ranking pegasus in the Crystal Empire Royal Guard (Equestria); Canterlot High School student and electric guitarist in the band Flash Drive (human world); a Wondercolt competitor in the Friendship Games.
**Appearance:** Pony form: a bright orange-yellow pegasus stallion with a two-tone sapphire-blue mane and tail, vivid cornflower-blue eyes, and a cutie mark of a two-tone blue shield behind a yellow lightning bolt. He wears the cobalt-and-gold Crystal Empire guard armor with its saddle and helmet. Human form: pale gray-amber skin, spiky two-tone blue hair, the same cornflower-blue eyes, tall and athletic; he usually wears a casual jacket over a shirt, jeans and sneakers, and carries a guitar case. He has no cutie mark as a human. In his pegasus transformation (Spring Breakdown) he gains feathered wings and a pony-like coat.
**Personality:** Kind, gentle, caring, and unfailingly gentlemanly — the character bios call him "kind and always the perfect gentleman." He is brave enough to stand up for others, but also clumsy, socially awkward, and easily flustered around Twilight, and he can be jealous or short-tempered (worst of all while under the Dazzlings' spell, when he turns cruel and arrogant).
**Likes:** Music and playing guitar with Flash Drive, helping friends, proving somepony's innocence, sports and competition, Twilight Sparkle, and hanging out at the Sweet Shoppe or the mall.
**Dislikes:** Losing, being ignored or blown off, being lied about, the Crystal Prep–Canterlot High rivalry, and seeing Sunset Shimmer in trouble.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** He is easily rattled, awkward at expressing feelings, and vulnerable to magical manipulation; he panics in crises and is not a strong fighter. His crush on Twilight repeatedly leaves him disappointed.
**Skills/Abilities:** Guard training, flight (as a pegasus), decent athleticism, guitar playing and performing, and straightforward honesty; he can also fight through minor obstacles and stand his ground when it counts.
**Family & Relationships:** Human Flash has an unnamed grandmother (mentioned by an IDW artist). Sunset Shimmer is his ex-girlfriend — they broke up before the first film and rebuild a friendship later — and he is smitten with Princess Twilight Sparkle and, later, her human counterpart; he is friends with the Wondercolts, the Rainbooms, and Micro Chips and Sandalwood in the band room. In Equestria he serves Princess Cadance and Shining Armor.
**Background/Biography:** Pony Flash first appears as a Crystal Empire royal guard in the first Equestria Girls film, bumping into Princess Twilight and announcing her arrival; at the end of the film he literally bumps into her again with the line "We've got to stop bumping into each other like this." His human counterpart meets Twilight at Canterlot High, gets a milkshake spilled on him, proves the incriminating photos of her were faked, and takes her to the Fall Formal. In Rainbow Rocks he spends the film under the Dazzlings' spell and cruelly rejects Twilight before being freed, and in Friendship Games he competes for Canterlot High and keeps running into the human Twilight. In Legend of Everfree he works through his feelings with Sunset's blunt advice, lets go of his crush, and stays friends with both Twilights. He later appears in Mirror Magic, Spring Breakdown (where he saves a passenger and becomes a pegasus in Equestria), Forgotten Friendship, Holidays Unwrapped, and numerous shorts. In Friendship is Magic he has silent cameos in "It Ain't Easy Being Breezies" and a speaking role in "Twilight's Kingdom, Part 1."
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Relaxed, friendly, slightly nervous teen speech; polite and a little formal around royalty ("Her highness, Princess Twilight Sparkle!"). He apologizes quickly, laughs off embarrassment, and leans on music metaphors.
**Notable Quotes:** "Whoa! You okay?" (Equestria Girls) · "We've got to stop bumping into each other like this." (Equestria Girls) · "What was I gonna do? Not prove your innocence? Wouldn't be much of a Canterlot Wondercolt, would I?" (Equestria Girls) · "Come on, Sunset. We used to date. I know when something's bothering you." (Legend of Everfree).
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Vincent Tong in both forms; Japanese voice by Kenji Nojima. His band's name, Flash Drive, comes from the first film's novelization. Hasbro's official bio praises him as one of the coolest and kindest students at Canterlot High. His human name and pony name are identical, and the pony guard was added to Friendship is Magic later at the crew's request rather than being in the scripts. M. A. Larson's April Fools' joke script had him say "Totally, brah." [uncertain] Merchandise sometimes shows his pony cutie mark before it ever appeared on screen.

## Zipp Storm

**Series/Continuity:** G5 — My Little Pony: A New Generation, Make Your Mark, Tell Your Tale, and the G5 specials. Full name: Zephyrina Storm.
**Species/Kind:** Pegasus pony (female), princess of the pegasus kingdom.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Young adult mare — the elder of Queen Haven's two daughters.
**Residence:** Zephyr Heights royal palace (birth home); later the Crystal Brighthouse in Maretime Bay with her friends.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Princess of Zephyr Heights (co-heir with her sister Pipp Petals); scientist, inventor, and self-appointed detective of the Mane 5; one of the five ponies who restored magic and unity to Equestria.
**Appearance:** A slim, athletic white pegasus mare with pale gray hooves and bright blue eyes. Her short, windswept two-tone mane and tail mix hot pink-red with deep blue — a practical cut she keeps out of her face for flying — and her wings are feathered in three shades: white, blue, and violet. Her cutie mark is a stylized red-and-blue lightning bolt beneath a small golden crown, reflecting her royal birth and her speed. She favors sporty jackets and practical gear over court gowns, and she is usually the one carrying tools, notes, or a pair of binoculars.
**Personality:** Bold, curious, rational, and restless. Zipp is the group's analyst: she questions everything, runs experiments, and would rather chase a mystery than attend a royal tea. She is sarcastic, competitive, and blunt, and she chafes hard against protocol and being told what to do — but she is fiercely protective of her little sister, brave to a fault, and secretly anxious about whether she is doing right by her family and her kingdom.
**Likes:** Science and experiments, detective work and mysteries, fast flying, sports and races, gadgets and tinkering, reading, solving puzzles, her friends, teasing Pipp, and being treated as an equal rather than a decoration.
**Dislikes:** Royal galas, being paraded around as a princess, boredom, secrets and cover-ups, losing, being underestimated, and slow-moving official ceremonies.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Being trapped by royal duty, failing her family, and letting her friends down. She can be reckless, tactless, and a know-it-all, and she sometimes pushes a puzzle so hard that she ignores the ponies around her.
**Skills/Abilities:** An outstanding natural flyer and acrobat once magic returns — fast, precise, and fearless in the air — plus notable physical strength and stamina, excellent analytical and investigative skills, mechanical tinkering, and leadership under pressure. Before magic returned, pegasi could not truly fly; Zipp could only "fly" through stage rigging, and she is still the most capable aerialist of the Mane 5.
**Family & Relationships:** Queen Haven (mother, the pegasus queen), Pipp Petals (younger sister, pop star and fellow princess, whom she loves and constantly teases). Her chosen family is the Mane 5: Sunny Starscout, Izzy Moonbow, Hitch Trailblazer, and Pipp, later joined by Misty Brightdawn and the baby dragon Sparky Sparkeroni. Enemies include Opaline Arcana, the disgraced deputy Sprout, and later villains such as Aurora.
**Background/Biography:** In A New Generation, Zipp was the princess who never quite fit the palace: she snuck out to investigate rumors from Maretime Bay and, with Sunny's help, uncovered her mother's secret — that the pegasi had been faking flight for generations. Rather than bury the scandal, she exposed it, joined Sunny's quest, and helped reunite earth ponies, pegasi, and unicorns; when magic returned, she truly flew for the first time and became her mother's genuine pride. In Make Your Mark, she and the others move into the Crystal Brighthouse and take on Opaline Arcana, with Zipp serving as the group's investigator and gadgeteer. Across Tell Your Tale and the G5 specials she juggles royal duties with detective work, racing, and keeping Pipp out of trouble.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Fast, snarky, and analytical, full of "hypothesis," "statistically," and "obviously," with a big-sister tone for Pipp and an exasperated one for royal protocol. She tends to blurt out exactly what she is thinking and to celebrate her discoveries with an enthusiastic "Yes! I knew it!"
**Notable Quotes:** [paraphrased] "Everything about the flight shows is fake — the wires, the lights, all of it. And I'm going to prove it." (Zipp's investigation, A New Generation) · [paraphrased] "Being a princess was never the job I wanted. Finding out what's actually going on — that's what I'm good at." (Zipp on her role, Make Your Mark era).
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Liza Koshy in A New Generation and by Maitreyi Ramakrishnan in Make Your Mark and Tell Your Tale; Chinese materials use the name 力力 (Lili) with alternate names 奇普 and 奇普风璇. Her full name, Zephyrina Storm, is given in official character lists. Toy lines include "Cutie Mark Magic Zipp Storm," "Wing Surprise Zipp Storm," and "Sparkle Adventure Zipp." [uncertain] Fans often call her "Zipp"; the exact design of her cutie mark is described inconsistently across merchandise.

## Aloe

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic (seasons 1–9), My Little Pony: The Movie, and IDW comics; a human-world Aloe is briefly shown in an Equestria Girls interactive short.
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony (female); in the human world, a human.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare.
**Residence:** Ponyville, where she lives and runs the Ponyville Day Spa.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Beautician and co-proprietor of the Ponyville Day Spa with Lotus Blossom; specializes in seaweed bath wraps and herbal steam treatments; serves the royal family and notable visitors.
**Appearance:** A graceful pale rose-pink earth pony mare with a long, flowing light metallic-blue mane and tail, medium azure-blue eyes, and pale cornflower-blue eyeshadow. Her cutie mark is a lotus flower (blue in most shots, pink in some). She and Lotus Blossom look like a matched set — Lotus Blossom is the mirror of her palette, with a pale blue coat and rose-pink mane. Aloe usually wears a spa towel or an elegant neck accessory while on duty, and speaks with a soft, pampered cadence.
**Personality:** Warm, poised, and soothing, with a touch of drama and vanity. She is professional and doting with clients ("It's simply luxurious!"), but she can be gossipy, fussy about spills, and openly exasperated when customers overstay or when other ponies wreck the spa — her comic-book appearances show a sharper, funnier side.
**Likes:** Pampering clients, herbal baths, seaweed wraps, mud masks, hooficures, and wing massages; gossip; the finer things; the occasional celebrity client; and a smoothly run spa.
**Dislikes:** Messes and interrupted treatments, rude or gigantic customers (Bulk Biceps smashing through the spa), her best cushions being used as footstools, being taken for granted, and overly demanding guests like Rarity.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Not much is established; she is frightened by monsters and crises like any pony, and she is fussy rather than brave. Her business and her reputation are clearly what she cares about most.
**Skills/Abilities:** Expert massage, hooficure, wing massage, mud and seaweed treatments, mane styling (including royal updos), horn polishing, and running a small business. In the 2017 movie's slave scenes she is shown giving a storm creature a shoulder massage and later being used as its footstool.
**Family & Relationships:** No named family in the show; the IDW comic "FIENDship is Magic" shows a ponylike ancestor, and the "spa ponies" are usually treated as a pair rather than relatives. Her partner and closest colleague is Lotus Blossom. Regular clients include Rarity, Fluttershy, Twilight Sparkle, Pinkie Pie, Big McIntosh, Bulk Biceps, and Princesses Celestia and Luna.
**Background/Biography:** Aloe debuts as a background spa pony in "Bridle Gossip" and "Green Isn't Your Color," the first episode to show the Ponyville Day Spa in detail. She has silent or minor roles for years, then gets her first spoken line in a fifth-season spa episode ("Did somepony order massages?") and a second speaking appearance in "A Day in the Life"-style domestic scenes. She and Lotus Blossom are brainwashed by King Sombra in the season 9 premiere, appear in a fantasy montage in "Hard to Say Anything," give Luna and Celestia their makeup in "Between Dark and Dawn," and serve the storm king's minions in My Little Pony: The Movie before being freed. In the IDW comics she and Lotus Blossom appear throughout, including the "Ponyville Civil War" arc and the "Friends Forever" spa issues.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Soft, refined customer-service purr with flattering address ("darling," "my dear") and a slightly continental accent in the comics, where she pronounces "the" as "zee" ("ingredients from zee earth"). She talks clients through treatments like a professional hostess.
**Notable Quotes:** "Welcome to the Ponyville Day Spa." (IDW comics) · "Did somepony order massages?" (season 5) · "Um... Miss Rarity? Eh, how much longer are you planning to stay in here, darling?" (season 6) · "Oh, I hate when he does that." (after Bulk Biceps crashes through the spa wall, season 5).
**Trivia/Notes:** Officially called Aloe or Aloe Vera depending on the source; the show's credits have called her "Spa Pony," and the pair are credited as Lotus Bloom/Lotus Blossom and Aloe/Aloe Vera in different episodes. Voiced by Tabitha St. Germain (English). Her cutie mark color flips between blue and pink in some shots — a known animation inconsistency. Her accent is much stronger in the comics than on screen. [uncertain] Her human-world cameo appears in an Equestria Girls "Choose Your Own Ending" short, in Rarity's branch.

## Diamond Tiara

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — Friendship is Magic (seasons 1–9) and background human appearances in the Equestria Girls series.
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony (female) as a foal; human in the Equestria Girls world.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** School-age filly, one of the older students at the Ponyville schoolhouse.
**Residence:** Ponyville, in the wealthy Rich family home.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Student; editor-in-chief (later demoted to printer) of the school newspaper, the Foal Free Press; a candidate for class president; daughter of Ponyville's richest family and later a school leader and philanthropist.
**Appearance:** A pale pinkish-white earth pony filly with a long, curly gray-purple mane and tail with white stripes, bright cornflower-blue eyes, and a small tiara as her signature accessory. Her cutie mark is a golden tiara set with jewels — the mark she received before almost anypony in her class, which she used to look down on "blank flanks." She often wears a necklace and carries herself with a nose-in-the-air posture. In her human-world cameos she is a well-dressed schoolgirl with the same colors and a tiara hair clip.
**Personality:** Introduced as spoiled, snobbish, bossy, and casually cruel: she and Silver Spoon torment ponies without cutie marks, throw her own "cute-ceañera" to show off, and threaten classmates to get what she wants. Beneath it all she is a lonely, insecure filly desperate for her mother's approval. Her season 5 arc reveals a genuinely brave and generous pony: she stands up to Spoiled Rich, defends the Cutie Mark Crusaders, and grows into a leader who organizes and funds community projects.
**Likes:** Being the center of attention, tiaras and expensive things, winning, ordering Silver Spoon around, class politics, parties in her honor, and — after her turn — real friendship, leading a team, and helping her school.
**Dislikes:** "Blank flanks," being upstaged, losing an election, being scolded by her mother, ponies who ignore her, dirt and mess, and (later) her mother's snobbery and her father's broken promises.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Fears her mother's disappointment and being ordinary. Her weaknesses are her temper, cruelty when insecure, and initial dependence on wealth and status; she grows out of much of this.
**Skills/Abilities:** Leadership, social manipulation, public speaking, organization and fundraising, editing and publishing (she runs the school paper), event planning, and — after her redemption — real talent for rallying other ponies. She has no magic and no combat skill.
**Family & Relationships:** Filthy Rich (father, a kind but overworked businessman), Spoiled Rich (mother, a classist social climber), Stinkin' Rich (great-grandfather), unnamed grandparents, and the family butler Randolph. Silver Spoon is her best friend and constant companion (later a more equal friendship), and the Cutie Mark Crusaders go from her favorite targets to her close friends. Pip is her class-president rival; Babs Seed is a temporary ally she abandons.
**Background/Biography:** Diamond Tiara debuts in "Call of the Cutie" as the classmate who already has her cutie mark and invites Apple Bloom to her "cute-ceañera" to humiliate her, launching her long rivalry with the Cutie Mark Crusaders. She recurs as a bully and rival through seasons 2–4: mocking Granny Smith, running the school paper as a gossip tyrant, tormenting Babs Seed, competing for the Equestria Games flag-bearer spot, and hijacking Twilight's princess lessons. In season 5's "Crusaders of the Lost Mark," she loses the class election to Pip, is berated by her mother, is comforted by the Crusaders, and finally confronts Spoiled Rich — declaring the Crusaders are her friends — then uses her family's resources to rebuild the school playground alongside everypony. From then on she appears as a friendly, occasionally bossy classmate; she becomes an adult in the series finale's future epilogue.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** A rich-kid drawl with drawn-out vowels and a condescending lilt: "I mean, duh," "sooo last week," and the sing-song taunt "Bump, bump, sugarlump, rump!" she shares with Silver Spoon. After her turn her tone warms but stays posh and self-assured.

Rarity is her rival and victim; Prim Hemline is the fashion-week judge she tricks, and the human Suri's classmates include Upper Crust, Dean Cadance's Crystal Prep crowd, and Flash Sentry.
**Background/Biography:** In "Rarity Takes Manehattan," Suri meets Rarity at a fashion-week rehearsal, flatters her about her designs, and persuades her to give away a piece of fabric. That night she has Coco Pommel copy Rarity's entire collection, then lies to Rarity that the judge Prim Hemline is angry with her, so that Rarity leaves the venue — and loses the competition to Suri. Suri returns in background appearances (including a season 5 scene in Manehattan) and in the IDW comics. Her human form appears in Equestria Girls: Friendship Games as a Crystal Prep Academy student who refuses to give human Twilight a seat on the bus, dances by Flash Sentry at the welcome party, loses the home-economics round to Pinkie Pie and Fluttershy with Upper Crust, and pressures Twilight during the final duel; she cameos in a memory in Forgotten Friendship.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** A soft, breathy, polite-but-cutting tone with a distinct Manehattan upper-class accent, ending barbs with "m'kay?" She flatters to your face and insults behind your back, and speaks to assistants like servants.

## Princess Cadance

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — Princess Cadance debuts in Friendship is Magic season 2's royal wedding and recurs through season 9; her human counterpart, Dean Cadance, appears in Equestria Girls: Friendship Games.
**Species/Kind:** Alicorn (female), formerly a pegasus; her human counterpart is a human woman.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare; in flashbacks she appears as a young foal-sitter and as a filly in the "Rise of Cadance" novel.
**Residence:** The Crystal Empire, where she rules with her husband; previously Canterlot, where she was raised by Princess Celestia and attended Canterlot Academy.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Princess of Love; co-ruler of the Crystal Empire; former foal-sitter and mentor to Twilight Sparkle; former student of Princess Celestia; in the human world, dean (later principal) of Crystal Prep Academy.
**Appearance:** A tall, slender alicorn with a pale grayish-pink coat, a flowing violet mane and tail streaked with rose and pale gold, light purple eyes, and an elegant, regal build. Her cutie mark is a faceted blue crystal heart framed in gold filigree, echoing the Crystal Heart itself; two versions of the mark appear on screen (single-faceted and multi-faceted). She wears a crown, a wedding-inspired gown with a sheer veil during the royal wedding, and formal royal regalia thereafter. Her human counterpart, Dean Cadance, is a warm-eyed adult woman with purple hair and blue eyeshadow who dresses in professional Crystal Prep attire.
**Personality:** Kind, patient, warm, and deeply empathetic — she radiates love and uses it as literal magic. She is playful and teasing with her family, brave and stubborn in a crisis, and a natural mentor who believes in giving ponies room to grow. She can be stern when she needs to be, and she keeps a hidden adventurous streak that royal life frustrates.
**Likes:** Love and harmony, her family (Shining Armor, Flurry Heart, Twilight), helping young ponies, the Crystal Heart and the Crystal Empire, flying (out of practice), adventure and new experiences, babies, and good food at celebrations.
**Dislikes:** Royal life becoming "a little too predictable," threats to her subjects, being idle while others are in danger, ponies doubting themselves, and the scheming of villains like Queen Chrysalis.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Fears losing her family and her empire; she pushes herself past exhaustion to protect the Crystal Empire (as when she held the barrier against King Sombra). She is sometimes too trusting, is a little out of practice with flying, and struggles to balance duty with caring for herself.
**Skills/Abilities:** Powerful alicorn love magic — she can calm quarrels, bless and strengthen others ("My love will give you strength"), power a protective barrier over an entire city, purify the Crystal Heart's light, and combine magic with Shining Armor to blast away an entire changeling swarm. She also has telekinesis, healing-adjacent caregiving skills, diplomacy, and leadership. Limitations: extended magic use exhausts her badly.
**Family & Relationships:** Shining Armor (husband), Flurry Heart (daughter, the first alicorn born in Equestria), Twilight Sparkle (sister-in-law and former babysitting charge), Twilight Velvet and Night Light (in-laws), Princesses Celestia and Luna (adoptive aunts who raised her as their niece), Princess Amore (distant ancestor/relative), and the Crystal Ponies as her subjects. In the human world she is Crystal Prep's dean and mentor to human Twilight. Queen Chrysalis is her greatest enemy.
**Background/Biography:** Cadance was born a pegasus orphan in a remote forest, raised by earth ponies; per the "Rise of Cadance" material she defeated the witch Prismia, earned her cutie mark, and was transformed into an alicorn and adopted as Celestia's niece. She grew up in Canterlot, babysat young Twilight Sparkle, and became Twilight's beloved "best pony sitter." In "A Canterlot Wedding," she marries Shining Armor; Queen Chrysalis impersonates her in the wedding plot, but Cadance's love combined with Shining Armor's shields expels the changelings. In season 3 she saves the Crystal Empire by reclaiming the Crystal Heart and rallying the crystal ponies against King Sombra, and she and Shining Armor become its rulers. She gives her magic to Twilight to fight Tirek and is imprisoned with the other princesses, later freed. She becomes a mother in season 6 when Flurry Heart is born, allies with Thorax and the reformed changelings, is captured by Chrysalis, and fights in the final battle against the three villains. In Equestria Girls she hosts the princess summit and encourages Twilight, while her human version, Dean Cadance, befriends and champions human Twilight at Crystal Prep and is later promoted to principal.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Warm, encouraging, and gentle, with a teasing, maternal lilt. She uses endearments ("my dear," "Twilight"), gives heartfelt speeches, and shares a secret hoof-clap nursery rhyme with Twilight. In royal settings she speaks with polished diplomatic warmth.
**Notable Quotes:** "Sunshine, sunshine, ladybugs awake! Clap your hooves and do a little shake!" (several episodes) · "My love will give you strength." ("A Canterlot Wedding, Part 2") · "The Crystal Heart has returned! Use the light and love within you to ensure that King Sombra does not!" ("The Crystal Empire, Part 2") · "One of these days, we need to get together when the fate of Equestria isn't hanging in the balance." ("The Crystal Empire, Part 1").
**Trivia/Notes:** Full name Princess Mi Amore Cadenza; voiced by Britt McKillip (English). Her name is spelled both "Cadance" and "Cadence" across credits and merchandise. Lauren Faust originally intended Celestia and Luna to be the only alicorns, and Cadance's alicorn design was added after her departure. Her cutie mark has two on-screen designs. The human Dean Cadance is one of the few G4 human counterparts who shares a pony character's name and role closely. [uncertain] Her exact birth village and the precise mechanics of her alicorn transformation are only partly explained in canon (novels and staff statements fill in the rest).
