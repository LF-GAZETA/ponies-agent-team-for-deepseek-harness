## Sprout Cloverleaf

**Series/Continuity:** G5 — *My Little Pony: A New Generation* (2021 movie), *My Little Pony: Make Your Mark* (chapters 2–6), *Tell Your Tale*, and the *A Maretime Bay Adventure* video game.
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony (G5 earth-pony stallion); no horn, no wings.
**Gender:** Male (he/him).
**Age/Stage:** Adult stallion, a young adult in Maretime Bay terms — old enough to serve as the town's deputy sheriff.
**Residence:** Maretime Bay (the earth-pony seaside town on G5's united Equestria).
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Deputy sheriff of Maretime Bay under Sheriff Hitch Trailblazer; self-appointed "sheriff" during Hitch's absence; son of Canterlogic CEO Phyllis Cloverleaf; after the movie he works assorted community jobs and keeps angling for attention and status.
**Appearance:** Crimson-red coat with white "socks" on all four lower legs, a straw-blond mane swept up in a pompadour-like wave and a matching blond tail, bright green eyes, and maroon/dark hooves. His cutie mark is a purple sprouting turnip/sprout with green leaves. As deputy he wears a brown leather harness with a gold sheriff's-style badge; his default expression is a smug, lopsided smirk, and his *Make Your Mark* design keeps the same colors with a slightly leaner build. Earth pony — no wings, no horn, no magic.
**Personality:** Egotistical, thin-skinned, and hungry for recognition, Sprout is a classic small-town striver: he wants to be the one in charge and to be seen as a hero, but he is fundamentally a coward who mistakes loudness for courage. He is a mama's boy who craves Phyllis's approval, and he is easily swept up in his own self-importance. He is theatrical, petty, and quick to catastrophize, yet he is not truly evil — just selfish, childish, and desperate to matter. After his humiliation he becomes a comic-relief hanger-on who still schemes for the spotlight, though he gradually turns up on the side of the community.
**Likes:** Being in charge, badges and uniforms, speeches, the spotlight, praise and applause, his mother's approval, Canterlogic gadgets and machines, feeling important, Maretime Bay parades and ceremonies.
**Dislikes:** Sheriff Hitch's popularity, being ignored, demoted, or laughed at, taking orders, chores, pegasi and unicorns (an inherited fear he exploits early on), being wrong in public, "ponies who think they're better than him."
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Deeply insecure underneath the bluster; afraid of the unknown and of other pony kinds, afraid of failure, and terrified of his mother's disapproval. A coward when real danger appears, with poor impulse control, a fragile ego, and a habit of escalating fear instead of solving problems. No magic, no wings, and no real combat skill.
**Skills/Abilities:** Surprisingly capable organizer and rabble-rouser — he can whip a crowd into a panic and convert a factory to war production. He understands and operates Canterlogic technology, built and piloted a giant mecha pony, and handles basic deputy duties (badges, whistles, barking orders). Limitations: physically ordinary, easily frightened, and a poor judge of risk.
**Family & Relationships:** Phyllis Cloverleaf (mother, CEO of Canterlogic); his father is [not established in canon]; no siblings are named. Sheriff Hitch Trailblazer (former boss and rival), Sunny Starscout (target of his mockery, later a tolerated neighbor), the citizens of Maretime Bay (whom he incited), and his mother's company staff.
**Background/Biography:** Sprout was born and raised in Maretime Bay, where earth ponies had lived in isolation and feared pegasi and unicorns. As Hitch's deputy he stayed in the shadows of a sheriff everyone liked, and when Sunny Starscout began preaching friendship with the other pony kinds, Sprout mocked and feared her ideas. After Hitch left town with Sunny, Sprout declared himself sheriff, whipped the town into a panic, converted Canterlogic into a weapons factory, and built a giant mecha pony, singing "Danger, Danger" as he worked. He attacked the pegasus/unicorn group and rammed the mecha into Sunny's lighthouse; his mother threw herself in front of the machine to stop him, and the mecha was destroyed. With magic restored to Equestria, Sprout was humiliated and forced back down to size. In *Make Your Mark* he returns in comic-relief roles — "Growing Pains," "The Traditional Unicorn Sleep-Over," "Winter Wishday," "Top Remodel," "Cutie Blossom Bash," "Mane Smelody," "Nightmare on Mane Street," and the "Roots of All Evil" two-parter — still scheming for attention and eventually swept up with everyone else in the fight for Equestria.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Loud, boastful, and wheedling, with a nasal, put-upon whine when things go wrong. He talks in bureaucratic deputy-speak and villain-monologue flourishes, refers to himself in the third person or by title ("Sheriff Sprout"), and loves ominous catchphrases like "Danger, danger!" He calls Hitch by name with heavy sarcasm and calls other ponies "citizens" when playing sheriff.
**Notable Quotes:** "Danger, danger!" (the song he sings while building his mecha pony, *A New Generation*); "I'm the sheriff now, and I'm gonna keep this town safe!" [paraphrased]; "We can't trust them — pegasi and unicorns are dangerous!" [paraphrased]; "As deputy, I'm officially in charge here!" [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Ken Jeong in *A New Generation* (2021); his *Make Your Mark*/*Tell Your Tale* voice credit is not confirmed on the sources checked [uncertain]. Merchandise calls him "Deputy Sprout." His cutie mark is a purple sprout/turnip with green leaves. He is the main antagonist of *A New Generation* and of the *A Maretime Bay Adventure* game. Song: "Danger, Danger." His mother Phyllis is the CEO of Canterlogic, the company that profited from Maretime Bay's fear.

## Octavia Melody

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — a background-turned-recurring pony in *Friendship is Magic*, with a human-world counterpart in the *Equestria Girls* series.
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony (mare); Equestria Girls counterpart is a human.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare; her EQG human counterpart is a high-school student.
**Residence:** Ponyville — she shares a curiously decorated cottage with Vinyl Scratch (expanded/canon-adjacent material); the EQG Octavia lives in the human world and attends Canterlot High.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Professional cellist and classical musician; performs at formal ceremonies (the Grand Galloping Gala orchestra, weddings and civic events); EQG counterpart is a Canterlot High student and cellist in the school's music scene.
**Appearance:** A poised, elegant earth pony with a pale gold-gray coat, a dark charcoal-gray mane and tail banded with lighter gray highlights, and light violet eyes with a cool, level gaze. She wears a white collar with a pink bow tie — her signature accessory — and her cutie mark is a purple treble clef, marking her musical talent. Her mane is styled in a smooth, precisely kept bob; she carries herself with upright, ballerina-like posture. The EQG human Octavia has pale gray-gold skin, very dark gray hair, and mauve eyes, keeps the same pink bow tie, and is usually seen with her cello at Canterlot High. No wings or horn (earth pony).
**Personality:** Dignified, cultured, and precise — a perfectionist who treats music as a serious calling, not a hobby. She is dryly sarcastic, a little prim and snobbish about standards, and easily rattled by chaos that interferes with a performance. Under the fussiness she is loyal, patient with Vinyl's opposite personality, and quietly kind. She prefers order, proper etiquette, and being thoroughly prepared, and she dislikes being caught unprepared in public.
**Likes:** The cello and classical repertoire, rigorous practice, order and tidiness, proper etiquette, elegant ceremonies, a quiet audience that listens, being on time, refined tea and formal occasions, and Vinyl Scratch's friendship (despite herself).
**Dislikes:** Monster attacks and other interruptions on performance day, musical sloppiness, lateness, chaos and noise, being upstaged, audiences that talk through her playing, and being told her standards are too high.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Performance anxiety and perfectionism; she frets intensely before big events and can freeze when preparations go wrong. She is socially reserved, physically ordinary (no magic, no wings), and her pickiness sometimes pushes other ponies away.
**Skills/Abilities:** A virtuoso cellist with deep knowledge of music theory, sight-reading, and formal performance; excellent poise, organization, and event preparation. In "Slice of Life" she demonstrates the comedic limits of those skills when a monster attack derails her wedding performance. No combat ability.
**Family & Relationships:** An unnamed female earth pony ancestor is referenced in IDW's *FIENDship is Magic: Sirens*; no parents or siblings are named in the show. Vinyl Scratch / DJ Pon-3 is her roommate and closest friend, an opposites-attract partnership of classical and club music. Her EQG human counterpart attends Canterlot High, and she is on friendly terms with the Rainbooms after the movies.
**Background/Biography:** Octavia debuted as a background pony in the Season 1 finale "The Best Night Ever," playing cello at the Grand Galloping Gala. Fandom affection turned the design into a recurring character: she appears at formal events throughout the series, and "Slice of Life" (Season 5) gives her a spotlight — she is booked to perform at Cranky Doodle Donkey and Matilda's wedding and spends the episode fretting about her set list while monsters rampage through Ponyville, with Vinyl Scratch helping her improvise. IDW comics expand her into a working musician with gigs, a cart, and a sharp tongue, and the *Equestria Girls* line gives her a human counterpart at Canterlot High.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Refined, precise, and slightly formal, with a touch of upper-class British flavor — exclamations like "Blast!" and constructions like "How am I meant to practice with a monster invading Ponyville?" She is understated and deadpan, corrects sloppy language, and addresses Vinyl with affectionate exasperation ("Vinyl…" / "Tavi" is Vinyl's nickname for her).
**Notable Quotes:** "A monster attack?! Blast! I'm performing at the ceremony this afternoon, and I still haven't sorted out what to play. How am I meant to practice with a monster invading Ponyville?" ("Slice of Life"); "That's more like it!" ("Slice of Life"); "I admit that I AM picky. I simply know good music when I hear it, and will accept nothing less." (*Enterplay* collectible card game); "This isn't a contest, Vinyl…" (*Enterplay* CCG).
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Kazumi Evans (also Rarity's singing voice and the singing voice of Adagio Dazzle); first appeared in "The Best Night Ever" (Season 1 finale). Her cutie mark is a purple treble clef, and her pink bow tie is her trademark. She was an unnamed fandom favorite ("Octavia," nicknamed "Tavi") before being officially named Octavia Melody. In *Equestria Girls* she is likewise voiced by Kazumi Evans. Her shared household with Vinyl Scratch is a fan-favorite detail. Talent: cello; manner: refined; no magic.

## Cloudpuff

**Series/Continuity:** G5 — *My Little Pony: A New Generation* (2021), *Make Your Mark*, *Tell Your Tale*, and the G5 IDW comics.
**Species/Kind:** A "flying Pomeranian" — a tiny fluffy winged dog (not a pony, not a "puff" creature); a G5 pet animal.
**Gender:** Male (referred to as "he").
**Age/Stage:** Adult small dog; a pampered royal pet of indefinite age.
**Residence:** The royal palace in Zephyr Heights, usually in the company of Queen Haven, Pipp Petals, or Zipp Storm.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Queen Haven's pet and the informal royal family mascot; frequent companion and sidekick of Pipp Petals, and an occasional helper/troublemaker for the Mane Five.
**Appearance:** A small, round, impossibly fluffy white Pomeranian with tiny white feathered wings, pink inner ears, large dark eyes, a small black nose, and a pink tongue that is usually out in a happy pant. He is roughly the size of a pony's head and appears to be mostly fur, with stubby legs. In merchandise he wears accessories such as gold heart-shaped sunglasses and spa-day bows. He is not an alicorn; his flight comes from his small feathered wings, which are not much bigger than his ears.
**Personality:** Playful, affectionate, excitable, and mischievous — a lapdog with delusions of grandeur. He loves being pampered and carried, adores attention, and greets friends with thrilled yips and tail wags. He is loyal to the royal family and especially fond of Hitch Trailblazer, and he has a nose for trouble: he grabs things he should not, sneaks into bags, and turns quiet moments into slapstick chaos, all with an innocent expression.
**Likes:** Queen Haven, Pipp Petals, Zipp Storm, Hitch Trailblazer, treats and snacks, belly rubs, being carried around, riding along in bags or on heads, chasing toys, being groomed, and any event where he is the center of attention.
**Dislikes:** Being left behind, missing snack time, being scolded or confined, baths that interrupt play, and anything that takes his family's attention away from him.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Very small, delicate, and easily startled; no fighting ability, limited understanding of danger, and easily distracted by food or squeaky toys. Separation from the royal family upsets him, and he cannot speak to explain himself.
**Skills/Abilities:** Flight despite his tiny wings, an excellent nose and tracking instinct (he followed Hitch Trailblazer's badge across Equestria to Bridlewood Forest), speed, agility, squeezing into tight spaces, stealing items, and melting hearts on sight.
**Family & Relationships:** Queen Haven is his owner; Pipp Petals and Zipp Storm are his favorite royal ponies and de facto caregivers; Hitch Trailblazer is his beloved non-royal pony friend. No blood relatives are established [not established in canon].
**Background/Biography:** Cloudpuff debuts in *A New Generation* as Queen Haven's pampered pet in Zephyr Heights. When Zipp Storm, Sunny Starscout, and Izzy Moonbow try to steal the Unity Crystal, Cloudpuff barrels in trying to grab it himself, kicking off a chase. Later, smitten with Hitch Trailblazer, he tracks the sheriff's badge across Equestria all the way to Bridlewood Forest. In *Make Your Mark*, *Tell Your Tale*, and the G5 comics, he remains the royal family's chaos gremlin — appearing at royal events, spa days, and picnics, usually where he is least expected to be and always somehow involved in the mess.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Cloudpuff has no spoken dialogue — he communicates entirely in yips, barks, whines, happy panting, and expressive tail wags, which the pony characters interpreting him tend to translate into whatever suits the joke.
**Notable Quotes:** Cloudpuff does not speak in canon; he "says" things through action — the delighted *yip-yip!* of his excited greeting when his family comes home [paraphrased], and the indignant *whine/growl* he makes when a treat or toy is withheld [paraphrased]. Any written dialogue for him must be invented, since he is a nonverbal pet.
**Trivia/Notes:** First appearance: *My Little Pony: A New Generation* (2021). He is described by official/merch sources as a "G5 flying Pomeranian dog." Released as a toy in the Royal Racing Ziplines playset (where he wears gold heart-shaped sunglasses), the *Friends of Maretime Bay* set, and several *Mini World Magic* sets. Appears across *Make Your Mark*, *Tell Your Tale*, and the G5 IDW comics. Fan-favorite pet; no voice actor listed because he does not speak.

## Luster Dawn

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — *My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic*, appearing in the series finale "The Last Problem" (Season 9, Episode 26) in the show's future setting; also in the Gameloft *My Little Pony* mobile game event "The Crowning Achievement."
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn (mare/young adult student); no wings.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Young adult student — the "next generation" of Twilight Sparkle's personal protégés; a late-teen/young-mare age, styled as a student rather than a child.
**Residence:** Ponyville (where Twilight sends her to learn friendship at the end of the finale); formerly Canterlot, where she studied at the School for Gifted Unicorns.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Twilight Sparkle's personal student and protégé at the School for Gifted Unicorns — the role Twilight once held under Princess Celestia; aspiring independent scholar.
**Appearance:** A slender unicorn with a light orchid/magenta-pink coat, a mane and tail graded from light vermilion to light amber with a pale yellow-gray streak, and a high, bouncy ponytail with a swept fringe. Her eyes are bright orange-amber. Her cutie mark is a rising sun — an orange-and-yellow sun cresting above a stylized water line — hinting at promise, new beginnings, and illumination. She wears no accessories, and her posture is neat, eager, and slightly nervous. She is drawn a little smaller than Twilight, with fine features and a scholar's alert expression.
**Personality:** Diligent, brilliant, and intensely self-disciplined, Luster is the model student: she would rather spend weeks alone in a library than attend a party, and she measures herself against Twilight's achievements. She is formal, polite, and earnest, with a habit of jumping to conclusions and making confident assumptions before she has all the facts. She opens with a cool, dismissive attitude toward friendship — calling it a distraction and a waste of time — but she is not cold; she is inexperienced, lonely, and secretly yearning to matter. Once her assumptions are challenged, she is honest enough to reconsider, and she throws herself into new goals with the same intensity she brings to research.
**Likes:** Studying and research, libraries, independent course work, academic achievement, Twilight Sparkle, logical arguments, order, self-improvement, and being taken seriously as a scholar.
**Dislikes:** Distractions, parties and frivolity, "wasting time," being underestimated, mushy sentiment she cannot analyze, and having her carefully reasoned conclusions challenged (at least at first).
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Afraid she will never accomplish as much as Twilight, and afraid of appearing foolish or unprepared. She is socially inexperienced — she does not know how to start a friendship, and admits as much. Her greatest weakness is jumping to conclusions and assuming she already understands a situation; she also over-works and isolates herself.
**Skills/Abilities:** Strong unicorn magic and a first-class scholarly mind; excellent research, note-taking, and study discipline. She is an academic prodigy, but canon shows no combat or athletic feats, and her social skills are her clear limitation. She can levitate objects and perform standard unicorn spells.
**Family & Relationships:** No relatives are named in canon [not established in canon]; show staff publicly debated whether she might be related to Starlight Glimmer and Sunburst, with Josh Haber saying "maybe" and Jim Miller arguing against it, leaving it up to fans [uncertain/non-canon]. Her mentor is Princess Twilight Sparkle, and she forms a friendship with a new circle of students at the end of the finale — a kirin, a griffon, a yak, and another pony.
**Background/Biography:** Luster Dawn appears in the future segment of "The Last Problem." As Twilight's student at the School for Gifted Unicorns in Canterlot, she comes to Twilight's castle and declares that she wants to set up an independent course of study because she believes friendship is a waste of time — after all, friendships eventually fade. Twilight tells her the story of the Mane Six's final adventure before her coronation, and Luster meets the grown-up "Friendship Council": Twilight's friends, whose bond has lasted decades. Watching them, Luster begins to reconsider. When she admits she does not know where to start making friends, Twilight sends her to Ponyville, the place where Twilight's own journey began. In the final scene, Luster departs with her new friends, ready to build friendships of her own.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Formal, articulate, and slightly academic, with precise vocabulary and full sentences; she addresses her teacher respectfully as "Princess Twilight" and apologizes politely ("I'm sorry, I didn't mean to…"). She speaks in reasoned arguments and lists, and her voice grows small and hesitant when she talks about her loneliness.
**Notable Quotes:** "Friendship looks like more of a distraction than anything else and ultimately a waste of time." ("The Last Problem"); "If friendships ultimately fade, why even make them in the first place?" ("The Last Problem"); "I never thought about friendship being something to work at, and I don't mind work." ("The Last Problem"); "I've enjoyed every moment at the School of Magic. But I'm just not sure it's the right place for me." ("The Last Problem").
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Sabrina Pitre. First and only show appearance: "The Last Problem" (Season 9, Episode 26, 2019). Her cutie mark is a sun rising over water. She is the last new character introduced in *Friendship is Magic* and functions as a deliberate parallel to young Twilight Sparkle — a gifted unicorn student assigned to learn friendship. Show staff (Josh Haber and Jim Miller) publicly disagreed about whether she is Starlight Glimmer and Sunburst's daughter; the matter was left to fan interpretation. She is playable in the Gameloft mobile game's "The Crowning Achievement" event, where her character bio reads that she is "living proof that some things never change… and some come around again" [paraphrased].

## Braeburn

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — *My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic*; also IDW comics and merchandise.
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony (stallion); no horn, no wings.
**Gender:** Male (he/him).
**Age/Stage:** Adult stallion; young adult — energetic, idealistic, and still something of a small-town booster.
**Residence:** Appleloosa, the apple-farming frontier town in southern Equestria.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Apple farmer and orchard keeper in Appleloosa; the town's self-appointed ambassador and welcoming committee; member of the extended Apple family.
**Appearance:** A sunny, light-golden-tan coat with a bright straw-blond mane that sweeps up and back and a matching tail, plus vivid grass-green eyes. His cutie mark is a large shiny red apple (in a couple of later crowd scenes an animation error swaps it for a guitar). He commonly wears a brown cowboy-style vest, and he stands with a proud, chest-out posture; his smile is wide and his eyes are eager. No wings or horn.
**Personality:** Friendly, open, and relentlessly enthusiastic — Braeburn is a born booster who genuinely believes Appleloosa is the best place in Equestria and wants everyone to love it too. He is warm, welcoming, and proud of his family and his town, but he is also naive, a little hot-headed, and not the sharpest pony in the orchard; once riled, he is stubborn and ready to stand his ground. He is at his best when he is showing off his home and at his worst when he lets fear and small-town pride do his thinking for him.
**Likes:** Apples of every kind (especially apple pie and cider), Appleloosa, welcoming visitors, the Apple family, rodeos and competitions, showdowns and celebrations, a good harvest, and telling tall tales about the frontier.
**Dislikes:** Intruders who threaten his orchard, high-and-mighty city attitudes, losing, buffalo stampeding through his apples (initially), and being told Appleloosa is nothing but a dusty little town.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Naive and easily led, especially by group panic; hot-headed in a crisis; his pride and stubbornness can make a small dispute worse; he has no magic or wings. He is also a little gullible — he can talk himself into a bad plan if it sounds patriotic.
**Skills/Abilities:** Expert apple-bucking and orchard farming, lasso and rope work, rodeo-level athleticism, horse-drawn cart and wagon handling, and practical frontier construction and irrigation work; he helped build Appleloosa into a thriving town. He is an excellent talker and host — a natural salesman for his town.
**Family & Relationships:** Applejack, Big McIntosh, and Apple Bloom are his cousins, and Babs Seed is a fellow member of the extended Apple family; Granny Smith is part of the wider clan. Chief Thunderhooves and the buffalo (former adversaries turned friends), Sheriff Silverstar and the Appleloosa townsfolk, and Troubleshoes (whom he, Applejack, and Apple Bloom help in "Appleoosa's Most Wanted") round out his circle. No parents or romantic interest are established [not established in canon].
**Background/Biography:** Braeburn debuts in "Over a Barrel" (Season 1, Episode 21), where the Mane Six arrive in Appleloosa and find the hometown pony waiting enthusiastically at the station. He shows off the town's new apple orchard and its proud settlers — but the orchard was planted on land the local buffalo use for their sacred annual stampede, and Braeburn's stubbornness helps push the standoff toward a showdown. After Twilight and her friends broker a reasonable compromise, the ponies and buffalo become friends, and the episode ends with the famous pie fight. Braeburn returns in later frontier and Apple-family stories — most notably "Leap of Faith," where he appears amid the Flim Flam brothers' tonic business, "Appleoosa's Most Wanted," where he helps clear Troubleshoes's name, and "Equestria Games," where he appears among the crowd — and in IDW comics, which show him as a teenager in comic issue 11.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** A warm, rapid-fire Western drawl: "Howdy!", "Welcome to Appleloosa!", "y'all," "ain't," and "friend/stranger." He speaks in superlatives about his town and his apples, gets louder and faster when excited, and occasionally slips into frontier bravado when he feels challenged. "Eeyup" belongs to Big Macintosh, not Braeburn.
**Notable Quotes:** "Howdy! Welcome to Appleloosa!" [paraphrased]; "We grow apples here — the best apples in all Equestria!" [paraphrased]; "This here's our orchard, and we ain't about to let anypony trample it!" [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Michael Daingerfield. First appearance: "Over a Barrel" (Season 1, Episode 21). His name comes from the Braeburn apple variety, and his cutie mark is a red apple — an animation error replaces it with a guitar in some scenes of "Leap of Faith" and "Equestria Games." He is the first Apple family member from outside Ponyville to be introduced. Later reappears in "Leap of Faith," "Equestria Games," IDW comics (teen Braeburn in issue 11), and Apple-family merch; unofficial fan nickname "Brae."

## Thorax

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — *My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic* (Seasons 6–9); also IDW comics and merchandise.
**Species/Kind:** Changeling — first an unreformed, love-starved changeling, then a transformed "reformed changeling"; later King of the Changelings.
**Gender:** Male (he/him).
**Age/Stage:** Adult changeling; a full-grown king, though a flashback in "To Change a Changeling" shows him as a young changeling and a newborn hatchling (the "newborn" design predates his adult debut in the show's timeline).
**Residence:** The Changeling Kingdom (the hive, rebuilt as a colorful home for reformed changelings); earlier he lived disguised inside the Crystal Empire, and the changelings' original hive lay in the badlands.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** King of the Changelings from the Season 6 finale "To Where and Back Again, Part 2"; leader and representative of the reformed changelings; ally of Equestria and friend of its royalty.
**Appearance:** In his original changeling form he is jet-black and deep green with a hole-riddled carapace, a curved antler-like horn, fangs, faceted teal eyes, and insectoid wings — every inch the scary changeling of old. After his transformation his body becomes vivid teal and emerald green with a bright orange-red snout, large branching antler horn, translucent iridescent purple-blue wings, dark blue and green back armor, and a glowing green-yellow chest; his eyes turn warm rose-red, and his magic aura is a pale cyan-green. As king he is taller and more confident in his stance. Changelings do not bear cutie marks, so none of his forms has one — his identity is written in color and silhouette instead.
**Personality:** Gentle, earnest, and a little nervous — Thorax is a changeling who always wanted friendship more than power. He is kind, empathetic, and quick to apologize, with a stammering nervous energy and a tendency to second-guess himself; as a leader he is unsure of his own authority and leans on his friends for advice. He is also braver than he gives himself credit for: when his friends are threatened he throws himself into danger without hesitation. He is forgiving to a fault — he even offers his worst enemy a second chance — and he is fiercely loyal to the oddball collection of friends who first believed in him.
**Likes:** Friendship and shared love, Spike, Starlight Glimmer, Ember, Discord, Trixie, being accepted for who he is, celebrations with his hive, bright colors, and being a good leader.
**Dislikes:** Chrysalis's cruelty, being feared or called a spy, war and conflict, his brother's aggression, the old changelings' predatory way of life, and hunger — the gnawing emptiness of an unreformed changeling.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Insecure about his leadership and terrified of rejection; he is naive and can be too trusting; physically he is less aggressive than his warrior brother Pharynx; in his old form he was weak when love-starved. He also struggles to assert authority and can be talked over in a crisis.
**Skills/Abilities:** Shapeshifting — he can mimic any pony or creature (his favorite alias is the crystal-pony persona "Crystal Hoof"); flight on insectoid wings; horn magic and energy blasts; and the reformed changelings' ability to share and spread love, which recharges and empowers them. As king he has the loyalty of an entire hive and a talent for diplomacy and de-escalation.
**Family & Relationships:** Pharynx (older brother, a hardline warrior changeling who eventually accepts reform); Queen Chrysalis (former queen and warlord — not blood family, but the changeling who ruled his hive); his hive as a found family. Close friends: Spike (his first real friend and defender), Starlight Glimmer, Discord, Trixie, and Dragon Lord Ember.
**Background/Biography:** Thorax is introduced in "The Times They Are a-Changeling" (Season 6, Episode 16) as a changeling who left his hive in search of friendship and is mistaken for a spy by the ponies; Spike befriends him, defends him, and shares love with him, causing Thorax to transform into a bright, colorful new form — proof that changelings do not have to be monsters. In the Season 6 two-part finale "To Where and Back Again," he joins Starlight Glimmer, Trixie, Discord, and the other secondary allies in an assault on Queen Chrysalis's hive to rescue the captured princesses and the Mane Six. When he offers Chrysalis the chance to change, she rejects it and flees; the hive then transforms around him, and Thorax becomes their new king. ## Vinyl Scratch

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — a background-turned-recurring unicorn in *Friendship is Magic* with a human counterpart ("DJ Pon-3") in *Equestria Girls*.
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn (mare); Equestria Girls counterpart is a human.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare; her EQG human counterpart is a Canterlot High student.
**Residence:** Ponyville — she shares a curiously decorated cottage with Octavia Melody (canon-adjacent/expanded material); her EQG counterpart lives in the human world and attends Canterlot High.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Professional DJ, remixer, and party sound engineer — officially billed as "DJ Pon-3"; EQG counterpart is a CHS student, resident school DJ, and music-store employee.
**Appearance:** A striking white (pale off-white) unicorn with an electric cobalt-blue mane shot through with lighter cyan highlights, styled in a swept bob with a long swoop over one eye, and vivid crimson-magenta eyes. She wears oversized, dark-purple-gradient shutter shades and often a white collar with a blue necktie; her cutie mark is a mirrored pair of connected eighth notes, marking her as a music pony. Her stance is loose and cool, always leaning or lounging. The EQG human DJ Pon-3 has pale yellow-gray skin, cobalt-blue hair with bright turquoise streaks, crimson eyes, the same striped shutter shades, and DJ headphones. Unicorn — her horn is used mostly to float her gear.
**Personality:** Cool, laid-back, and wordless — Vinyl lets her music and her body language do the talking. She is a party animal with impeccable DJ instincts, a smug showman's confidence, and a playful competitive streak (she treats everything, including her roommate's classical concerts, as a contest she intends to win). She is loyal, generous with her skills, and quietly kind: when Octavia is panicking before a performance, Vinyl is the one who shows up with a solution.

**Fears/Weaknesses:** She has no spoken dialogue in the show, which limits how she can communicate; she is a lover of noise and spectacle rather than a fighter, with no combat feats; her magic is mainly telekinesis for gear. Her cool facade can read as aloofness, and her competitive streak can steamroll other ponies' plans.

"Slice of Life" (Season 5, Episode 9) gives her a starring cameo: she is the DJ for Cranky and Matilda's wedding and the roommate who helps a frazzled Octavia salvage her performance while monsters rampage through Ponyville, showing the two musicians' odd-couple friendship. IDW comics give her actual dialogue and a louder personality, and the *Equestria Girls* series carries her human counterpart, DJ Pon-3, into Canterlot High as the school's resident DJ.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Silent in the show — she communicates with gestures, nods, smirk, and the occasional shrug, and lets her music answer for her. In IDW comics and the CCG she speaks like a hype DJ: fast, slangy, confident, full of music jargon ("sick," "mix," "the drop") and playful needling of "Tavi."
**Notable Quotes:** "Pinkie Pie parties. DJ Pon-3 parties HARD." (*Enterplay* collectible card game); "Horseapples! This is TOTALLY a contest, Tavi!" (*Enterplay* CCG); "Alright everypony! Let's hop on Equestria's newest dance craze… Do 'the Navigator'!" (IDW *My Little Pony* comic #10); "If you thought that one was good, just wait until my next mix!" (*Enterplay* CCG).

Cutie mark: two mirrored connected eighth notes. Her eye color is famously inconsistent across appearances (most often crimson/magenta). ## Coco Pommel

Recurring appearances: "Made in Manehattan," "The Saddle Row Review," "Equestria Games." She appears as a filly in a photo in "Made in Manehattan."

## Applejack

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — one of the "Mane Six" in *Friendship is Magic* (Seasons 1–9, plus the 2017 movie) and a core member of the human-world Rainbooms in the *Equestria Girls* series.
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony (mare); EQG counterpart is a human.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare; EQG counterpart is a Canterlot High student.
**Residence:** Sweet Apple Acres, just outside Ponyville; her EQG counterpart lives in the human world and attends Canterlot High.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Apple farmer, apple-bucker, and baker at Sweet Apple Acres; Bearer of the Element of Honesty; founding member of the Mane Six and, later, of Twilight's friendship council; EQG counterpart is a CHS student, Rainbooms bassist, and Element of Honesty bearer.
**Appearance:** A sturdy, sun-weathered orange-amber earth pony with a pale blond mane tied back in a low ponytail with a red band and a matching tail, striking green eyes, and freckles dusted across her cheeks. Her cutie mark is three red apples. She usually wears a brown Stetson-style cowboy hat with a lighter band, and her build is athletic and practical. Her EQG human form has orange skin, blond hair in a ponytail, green eyes, freckles, a plaid shirt, denim skirt or shorts, and boots — still unmistakably a farm girl. No wings or horn.
**Personality:** Honest to a fault, hard-working, dependable, and stubborn as a mule. Applejack is the group's straight-talking anchor: she values family, tradition, and a job done properly, and she would rather work herself into the ground than ask for help (as "Applebuck Season" proves). She is warm and motherly with her friends, blunt when she thinks somepony is being foolish, and fiercely protective of her family. Her flaws are pride, a short temper, an occasional lack of imagination about others' feelings, and a competitive streak that can tip into poor sportsmanship.
**Likes:** Apples and anything made from them (fritters, cider, pie), Sweet Apple Acres, her family, hard manual labor, rodeos and competitions, honest dealing, square dancing and hoedowns, a good hat, and winning fair and square.
**Dislikes:** Liars and cheats, laziness, fancy city airs, being told she cannot do something, losing, being fussed over, and asking for help when she is clearly overwhelmed.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Her pride and refusal to accept help nearly cost her the harvest in "Applebuck Season"; she fears disappointing her family and failing in front of her friends. She can be too blunt, too controlling, and too quick to judge; she has no wings or magic beyond earth-pony strength, and in *Equestria Girls* she is just as stubborn in human form.
**Skills/Abilities:** Enormous earth-pony strength — she can kick apple trees clean, haul loaded carts, and out-wrestle much bigger opponents; expert lasso and rope work; master apple-bucker, gardener, cook, and baker; capable athlete and rodeo competitor; skilled organizer of family and community events. As an Element Bearer, honesty itself becomes her magic when the Elements are active. Limitations: not a unicorn, no flight, and a tendency to overwork herself.
**Family & Relationships:** Big McIntosh (older brother), Apple Bloom (younger sister), Granny Smith (grandmother), Bright Mac and Pear Butter (parents, deceased — their romance is told in "The Perfect Pear"), Babs Seed (cousin), Braeburn (cousin), Goldie Delicious and the sprawling Apple family. Her closest friends are the rest of the Mane Six: Twilight Sparkle, Rainbow Dash, Rarity, Fluttershy, and Pinkie Pie. In *Equestria Girls* her human counterpart has a human Apple Bloom and Big McIntosh and is a Rainboom.
**Background/Biography:** Applejack introduces herself to Twilight Sparkle in the very first episode as the friendly face of Sweet Apple Acres, and she goes on to become the Bearer of the Element of Honesty. Across nine seasons she anchors the Apple family stories: "Applebuck Season," where her refusal of help nearly ruins the harvest; "Winter Wrap Up"; "Fall Weather Friends" and her rivalry with Rainbow Dash; "The Last Roundup," where she hides out working on a cherry farm in Dodge Junction rather than face her friends after a disappointing rodeo; "Apple Family Reunion," where she learns that the point of a reunion is the memories, not the schedule; "Pinkie Apple Pie," where she tries to prove a family connection; and "The Perfect Pear," where she and her siblings learn the story of their late parents' romance. She is a steady, grounded presence through the series' major arcs and appears in the future epilogue of "The Last Problem" still working the farm she loves.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** A warm, thick Southern American drawl full of contractions and rural idioms: "y'all," "sugarcube" (especially to Apple Bloom and her friends), "apple-pickin'," "dadgum," "like a corn stalk in August." She bargains and cajoles, uses direct declaratives, and can turn sharp and sarcastic when angry. "Yeehaw!" and "Howdy!" are her greetings, she says "Nope" flatly when nothing will change her mind, and she calls Twilight "Twi" and her friends "sugarcube" or "hon."
**Notable Quotes:** "Well, howdy-doo, Miss Twilight, a pleasure makin' your acquaintance. I'm Applejack. We here at Sweet Apple Acres sure do like making new friends!" ("Friendship Is Magic, Part 1"); "What I'm telling you is the honest truth. Let go, and you'll be safe." ("Friendship Is Magic, Part 2"); "Oh, sugarcube, if Mom and Dad were here, they'd be so proud of ya." ("The Last Roundup"); "Listen here, you rabid rhododendrons! You mess with one of us, you mess with all of us!" ("Twilight's Kingdom, Part 2").
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Ashleigh Ball (who also voices Rainbow Dash). One of the Mane Six; Bearer of the Element of Honesty. Her cutie mark is three red apples, and her Stetson hat is nearly as famous as she is. She is a Ponyville Apple family member; her parents Bright Mac and Pear Butter are deceased and were voiced in flashback. In *Equestria Girls* her human counterpart attends Canterlot High, plays bass in the Rainbooms, and bears the Element of Honesty there as well. First appearance: "Friendship Is Magic, Part 1" (Season 1, Episode 1, 2010). Represents Honesty, hard work, and family.

## Glorisa Daisy

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — the human world only; she appears in *Equestria Girls: Legend of Everfree* (2016) as the camp director, and in IDW's *Equestria Girls* comics and *Nightmare Knights* (where she also has an earth-pony depiction).
**Species/Kind:** Human (adult woman); a magical transformed state ("Gaia Everfree") during the *Legend of Everfree* climax; an earth pony in IDW's *Nightmare Knights* comics.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult — a young woman in her twenties or thirties, old enough to run a summer camp and to have inherited it from her family's generation.
**Residence:** The human world; Camp Everfree, a summer camp in the mountains near Canterlot City.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Director and caretaker of Camp Everfree; descendant of the camp's founding family; temporary antagonist while corrupted by Equestrian magic as "Gaia Everfree."
**Appearance:** A warm, outdoorsy young woman with light pink skin, shoulder-length dark-cranberry/raspberry hair in two shades, and light green eyes. As camp director she wears practical camp clothing — a green Camp Everfree shirt or polo, khaki shorts or jeans, sturdy boots, and a counselor's whistle. For the camp's formal events she dresses up in a geometric-patterned dress and, for the climactic Crystal Gala, a long crystal-gala gown. Her corrupted "Gaia Everfree" form is dramatic: her hair turns teal and bright turquoise-green, her skin takes on a grayish cast, her eyes become glowing green, vines and leaves wrap her body like armor, and living plants writhe around her. After the magic is removed she returns to her normal appearance. In the *Nightmare Knights* comics her pony-world counterpart is a light-pink earth pony with a three-daisy cutie mark.
**Personality:** Warm, upbeat, nurturing, and endlessly busy — Gloriosa is the kind of camp director who knows every camper's name and believes camp can change a kid's life. Underneath the cheer she is deeply stressed: Camp Everfree is her family's legacy, it is in financial trouble, and she is trying to hold it together alone. The magic corruption twists that love into possessiveness and rage — she becomes controlling, imperious, and willing to trap people to "protect" the camp — but the real Gloriosa is generous, self-sacrificing, and quick to apologize once she is herself again.
**Likes:** Camp Everfree, the campers, nature, plants and gardening, camp traditions (campfire songs, geodes, canoeing, the climbing wall), her brother Timber, her family's history, and the idea that hard work and community can save something worth saving.
**Dislikes:** The camp's debts and the threat of it being sold, developers and buyers who see only land value, being helpless, failing her family, and being taken for granted.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Losing Camp Everfree and with it her family's legacy; being unable to protect the people she loves; taking all burdens on herself; and the corrupting influence of Equestrian magic, which amplifies her possessiveness. She is an ordinary human without magic of her own once the geodes are gone.
**Skills/Abilities:** Expert camp director and organizer, wilderness and camping skills, plant and nature knowledge, first aid, leadership, and the ability to inspire campers. In her corrupted form she commands plants and vines, creates barriers and root attacks, displays superhuman strength, and glows with green magic — a formidable forest-powered villain — before the Rainbooms and Sunset Shimmer free her.
**Family & Relationships:** Timber Spruce (younger brother, a fellow camp counselor and romantic interest of Sunset Shimmer's human circle); unnamed parents; Old Spice and Dove (great-grandparents, the camp's founders); no spouse or children named. She is friendly with the Rainbooms after being freed and is close to the camp staff and campers.
**Background/Biography:** In *Equestria Girls: Legend of Everfree*, Camp Everfree is struggling and in danger of being sold, and Gloriosa — its director and a descendant of its founders — has been secretly using magical geodes found in a crystal cave beneath the camp to "improve" camp life and protect the place. The Equestrian magic corrupts her, transforming her into "Gaia Everfree," a plant-wielding villain who traps campers and attacks the Rainbooms as she tries to keep everyone at camp forever. Sunset Shimmer, the human Twilight, and the Rainbooms confront her; Timber's help and Sunset's empathy reach the real Gloriosa, the magic is broken, and she is freed, horrified by what she did. The Rainbooms and campers rally with a fundraising event (the Crystal Gala) to save the camp, and Gloriosa is forgiven and remains as its director.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Warm, energetic camp-counselor patter: bright welcomes ("Welcome to Camp Everfree!"), nature metaphors, team-building enthusiasm, and a habit of turning everything into a camp activity. As "Gaia Everfree" she speaks in grand, ominous declarations about the forest, roots, and vines; once freed, she is apologetic and gentle again.
**Notable Quotes:** "Welcome to Camp Everfree!" [paraphrased]; "This camp has been in my family for generations — I won't let it be taken away!" [paraphrased]; "The forest will protect us… no one is ever leaving again!" [paraphrased, Gaia Everfree].
**Trivia/Notes:** **Canonical spelling is "Gloriosa Daisy."** The roster heading preserves the variant spelling "Glorisa Daisy" as supplied; the correct English name is Gloriosa Daisy, with the "o" after "Glori-." Voiced by Enid-Raye Adams, with singing voice by Kelly Metzger. First appearance: *My Little Pony: Equestria Girls — Legend of Everfree* (2016, the fourth EQG movie). Her corrupted alias is "Gaia Everfree." She is the older sister of Timber Spruce, and her great-grandparents Old Spice and Dove founded Camp Everfree. She has no pony counterpart in the show, but IDW's *Nightmare Knights* depicts a pony version. She is one of the few Equestria Girls antagonists who is an ordinary human rather than a siren or a magic thief.

## Daring Do

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — *My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic* (Seasons 2–9); also IDW comics and the in-universe "Daring Do" chapter-book tie-ins; a human-world Daring Do appears only in select merchandise.
**Species/Kind:** Pegasus (mare); a human-world figure exists only as a licensed toy/comic depiction.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare; as A. K. Yearling she reads as middle-aged, while her "Daring Do" adventurer persona is ageless and heroic.
**Residence:** A secluded cottage in the jungle near the Tenochtitlan Basin — far from any town, deliberately hard to find; earlier adventures range across Equestria.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Treasure hunter and professional adventurer; secretly the author A. K. Yearling, whose "Daring Do" novels are fictionalized accounts of her real exploits; works alone (until Rainbow Dash).
**Appearance:** A light-gold pegasus with a black mane and tail streaked in several shades of gray, rose-pink eyes, and a compass-rose cutie mark. As Daring Do she wears a safari pith helmet and a khaki field shirt with a brown sash and belt, and her wings are strong and well-toned — athletic, compact, and always ready to bolt. As A. K. Yearling she is an older-looking recluse: mane pulled back in a gray bun, round glasses, a tweed or olive shawl, and a permanently unimpressed expression. No horn.
**Personality:** Brave, clever, resourceful, and impossibly stubborn, Daring Do is the pulp hero come to life — and, off the page, she is prickly, sarcastic, proud, and deeply suspicious of strangers. She guards her secret identity fiercely, hates publicity, and would rather get hurt than accept help. Underneath the attitude she is principled: she does not want her discoveries to fall into the wrong hooves, and she comes to respect and even like Rainbow Dash's irrepressible hero worship. Her flaws are pride, a temper, and a refusal to be vulnerable.
**Likes:** Adventure and ancient ruins, tricky traps and puzzles, rare artifacts, flying, her hat, quiet anonymity, writing, and — very grudgingly — Rainbow Dash's enthusiasm.
**Dislikes:** Reporters and autograph hounds, her secret identity being exposed, Dr. Caballeron and Ahuizotl, thieves and treasure-hunters who want artifacts for money, being rescued or fussed over, and interruptions to her writing schedule.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Her pride is the biggest threat to her own safety — she will push through injury rather than admit weakness; she is mortal and gets hurt, captured, and outmatched; her identity as A. K. Yearling is a constant vulnerability; she has no unicorn magic, only pegasus flight and her wits.
**Skills/Abilities:** Expert pegasus flight and aerial acrobatics; hand-to-hoof combat and trap-dodging; archaeology, ancient languages, and puzzle-solving; expert survival skills; a talent for improvisation and misdirection; sharp deductive reasoning; and the writing talent behind her best-selling books. Limitations: no magic, a bad habit of working alone, and a battered body that does not always cooperate.
**Family & Relationships:** Gallant True is identified as her uncle in expanded material; no other relatives are named. Rainbow Dash is her fan-turned-sidekick-turned-partner; Dr. Caballeron is her scholarly rival; Ahuizotl is her archenemy; Quibble Pants is a devoted fan she meets at a convention; and her publisher and readership know her only as A. K. Yearling.
**Background/Biography:** Daring Do is first introduced as a fictional character — the heroine of the adventure novels that Rainbow Dash discovers while laid up in the hospital in "Read It and Weep" (Season 2, Episode 16). The books' author, A. K. Yearling, is a reclusive figure; in "Daring Don't" (Season 4, Episode 4) Rainbow Dash discovers that Yearling is Daring Do herself, and that the novels are based on her real adventures. The two team up to stop the villain Ahuizotl from completing a world-ending ritual in the Tenochtitlan Basin. Later episodes deepen the legend: in "Daring Done?" her confidence is shaken and Rainbow Dash has to help her find her heroic spark again, and in "Daring Doubt" her reputation is challenged by Dr. Caballeron's version of events. Through it all she keeps writing — and keeps flying into danger.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Clipped, confident adventure-serial dialogue with dramatic one-liners, and as A. K. Yearling a grumpy, dismissive, no-nonsense register: "No autographs." She speaks in mission-talk ("there's no time," "hold on," "the trail leads to…"), gets sarcastic when annoyed, and reluctantly softens into mentor mode with Rainbow Dash.
**Notable Quotes:** "I work alone." [paraphrased]; "You've got guts, kid — I'll give you that." [paraphrased]; "The Daring Do books are… mostly true." [paraphrased, A. K. Yearling].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Chiara Zanni. First mentioned/appears in fictional form in "Read It and Weep" (Season 2, Episode 16); first in-person appearance in "Daring Don't" (Season 4, Episode 4). Cutie mark: a compass rose; coat light gold; mane black with gray streaks. A. K. Yearling's initials and identity are one of FiM's best-kept twists. The character is an affectionate Indiana Jones pastiche, complete with hat, traps, and rival archaeologists. Canon book titles include "Daring Do and the Quest for the Sapphire Stone" and "Daring Do and the Griffon's Goblet"; a licensed chapter-book series by G. M. Berrow expands her world. A human Daring Do exists only in merchandise/comics, not in the EQG movies.

**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Geologist and "rocktorate" — holder of a doctoral degree in rock science — plus a rock farmer's daughter, and in later seasons a deadpan stand-up comedian specializing in rock material.
**Appearance:** A gray earth pony with a faint bluish cast to her coat, a grayish-purple mane and tail that fall flat and straight, and pale teal-green eyes that are usually half-lidded in an unreadable stare. She wears a simple gray-blue smock dress and skirt, rarely changes her expression, and moves with deliberate calm. Her cutie mark is a rock shaped like a cut gemstone — a rock chiseled into a diamond-like form. She carries a rock named Boulder, and later a small rock pouch for him. In "Equestria Girls: Rainbow Rocks" her human counterpart makes a cameo appearance.
**Personality:** Flat, monotone, and unflappable, Maud is the ultimate deadpan — she speaks in short sentences with no inflection, never seems to smile, and says exactly what she means. She is literally minded and socially oblivious, which makes her seem strange or rude, but she is deeply kind and fiercely devoted to her sister Pinkie. Rocks are her passion, poetry, and vocabulary; she can talk about geology for hours with a straight face. She is calm in danger, unnervingly strong, quietly funny, and emotionally steady — the only Pie sister who never panics.
**Likes:** Rocks of every kind, Boulder, geology and the Mohs hardness scale, rock poetry, Pinkie Pie, her boyfriend Mudbriar, stand-up comedy, peace and quiet, and the taste of apples (one of the few foods she comments on).
**Dislikes:** Candy ("I don't really like candy. But I do love Pinkie Pie."), Rarity's fancy dresses ("It doesn't talk. It's a dress."), small talk and social pressure, being rushed, other ponies misunderstanding her, and sentimentality she did not ask for.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Almost nothing scares her, which is itself a weakness — she can be oblivious to danger and to others' feelings. Her emotional distance makes her hard to know, her bluntness can wound, and she has no magic or flight. Her one raw spot is Pinkie Pie: harm to her sister is the fastest way to make Maud move.
**Skills/Abilities:** Terrifying earth-pony strength — she shatters a boulder with a single hoof strike and swings heavy rocks like toys; expert geological knowledge (identifying stones, strata, hardness, caves) and cave/climbing experience; rock poetry and deadpan comic timing; surprising grace on ice skates; and unshakeable loyalty under pressure.
**Family & Relationships:** Igneous Rock Pie (father), Cloudy Quartz (mother), Limestone Pie (oldest sister), Pinkie Pie (younger sister), Marble Pie (youngest sister), plus Granny Pie and other Pie relatives. Mudbriar is her boyfriend (introduced in "The Maud Couple"). Starlight Glimmer becomes a close friend in "Rock Solid Friendship," and she is friendly with the Mane Six, if baffling to them.
**Background/Biography:** Maud is first glimpsed as a filly in a photograph, then makes her adult debut in "Maud Pie" (Season 4, Episode 18), when she visits Ponyville and the Mane Six try — and fail — to find common ground with her. When a boulder threatens Pinkie, Maud explodes into motion and smashes it, revealing the depth of her love for her sister; the episode closes with her admitting she does not actually like candy and giving Pinkie her rock candy necklace anyway. She returns in "Make New Friends but Keep Discord" for a deadpan Gala set, and in "The Gift of the Maud Pie" she and Pinkie exchange gifts in Manehattan — she returns the beautiful rock pouch because Pinkie traded her beloved party cannon for it. In "Rock Solid Friendship" she earns her rocktorate and chooses to move near Ponyville, befriending Starlight Glimmer; in "The Maud Couple" she introduces the equally odd Mudbriar. She appears in alternate-timeline form in "The Cutie Re-Mark" as a soldier, and in the series finale she attends Twilight's coronation with Mudbriar.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** A flat, slow, monotone with long pauses and no rise or fall — statements delivered like facts. She uses short declaratives, speaks about rocks with total sincerity, and drops devastating one-liners without changing expression. She calls Pinkie "Pinkie Pie" or "Pinkie," refers to Boulder by name, and says things like "Okay" and "That's nice" with zero affect.
**Notable Quotes:** "It's a rock. His name is Boulder." ("Maud Pie"); "It doesn't talk. It's a dress." ("Maud Pie"); "I don't really like candy. But I do love Pinkie Pie." ("Maud Pie"); "If you thought quartz was high on the Mohs hardness scale, wait 'til I tell you about corundum." ("Hearthbreakers").
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Ingrid Nilson. Full name: Maudalina Daisy Pie; also called "Maud Rock Pie" in some merchandise. First seen as a filly in a photo; adult debut in "Maud Pie" (Season 4, Episode 18). Her pet rock is named Boulder. Her cutie mark is a gem-cut rock. She is Pinkie Pie's second-oldest sister, and her name is a pun on "mud pie" (a Mississippi mud pie dessert). Her rocktorate is from "Rock Solid Friendship" (Season 7). She has a human cameo in *Equestria Girls: Rainbow Rocks*. In "The Cutie Re-Mark" she appears as a soldier in the Sombra timeline. Rock poetry is canon.

## Cheerilee

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — the Ponyville schoolteacher in *Friendship is Magic*, with a human counterpart ("Miss Cheerilee") in *Equestria Girls*.
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony (mare); EQG counterpart is a human.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare; an experienced elementary-school teacher; EQG counterpart is an adult teacher and librarian.
**Residence:** Ponyville; EQG counterpart lives in the human world and works at Canterlot High.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Teacher at the Ponyville Schoolhouse — the school where Apple Bloom, Sweetie Belle, Scootaloo, and the other foals study; EQG counterpart is a teacher and librarian at Canterlot High.
**Appearance:** A cheerful-looking magenta-cherry earth pony with a light grayish-pink mane streaked in paler pink and styled in a soft updo with a flower-like accent, and gray-green eyes. Her cutie mark is three smiling daisy-like flowers — warm and welcoming, like her classroom. She wears a simple pink-and-white collar and a neat teacher's outfit, and her smile is the first thing most ponies notice. In *Equestria Girls* her human form has light cherry-pink skin, crimson hair with brighter stripes, and gray-green eyes, and she dresses like a friendly high-school teacher. No wings or horn.
**Personality:** Warm, patient, encouraging, and endlessly enthusiastic about teaching, Cheerilee is the kind of teacher every foal remembers: firm enough to keep a classroom in order, kind enough that students come to her with their problems. She believes in every student's potential, including the ones who are late bloomers, and she has a gentle, playful sense of humor. Off duty she is a little romantic and a little gossipy — "Hearts and Hooves Day" reveals her secret wish for love — and she can be disappointed when her students misbehave. Her temper is rare but real.
**Likes:** Teaching and her students, cutie-mark stories, flowers, school plays and field days, romance (secretly), holiday celebrations, good behavior, and the moment a student finally understands something.
**Dislikes:** Misbehaving students and pranks, being manipulated — the Cutie Mark Crusaders' love poison in "Hearts and Hooves Day" leaves her mortified — being interrupted mid-lesson, and disappointing her class.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Her romantic loneliness and her eagerness to be liked; she can be naive about schemes and gossip; she is an ordinary earth pony with no magic or combat ability; and her patience can be mistaken for being a pushover, though she has a firm side when pushed.
**Skills/Abilities:** An excellent, versatile teacher — literacy, math, history, science, and social lessons for foals; strong classroom management and patience; event organizing (school plays, field trips, the school newspaper); and a good singing voice. She also knits? [not established] — instead note her practical, no-magic skills.
**Family & Relationships:** No family members appear in the show; IDW comics give her a twin sister [uncertain]. Her students include Apple Bloom, Sweetie Belle, Scootaloo, Babs Seed (briefly), Diamond Tiara, Silver Spoon, and the rest of the Ponyville class; she is a respected colleague of the Mane Six and in "Hearts and Hooves Day" becomes temporarily infatuated with Big McIntosh after the Cutie Mark Crusaders' love poison, an experience that leaves both of them sheepish friends. Her EQG counterpart teaches at Canterlot High.
**Background/Biography:** Cheerilee debuts in "Call of the Cutie" (Season 1, Episode 12) as the teacher of the Ponyville Schoolhouse, reassuring Apple Bloom that a cutie mark will come in time. She becomes the show's recurring school figure: in "The Show Stoppers" she coaches the Cutie Mark Crusaders' disastrous talent-show act, in "Ponyville Confidential" she is the faculty adviser to the school gossip column, and in "Hearts and Hooves Day" the Crusaders' love-potion mishap makes her and Big McIntosh act like lovesick fools until the spell is broken — with an important lesson about not rushing love. She continues to teach through the later school episodes and is still part of Ponyville's community in the series' future-set finale. Her EQG counterpart appears as a Canterlot High teacher in the *Equestria Girls* series.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Warm, clear, and teacherly — encouraging praise ("Good work, everypony!"), gentle corrections, and a classroom voice that can go sweet or firm on a dime. She sings instructions, uses "class" and "everypony" as address terms, and speaks with a bright, maternal warmth; when flustered (or love-struck) she turns shy and giggly.
**Notable Quotes:** "Everypony settle down — class is starting!" [paraphrased]; "Your cutie mark will come when you're ready — there's no need to rush it." [paraphrased, "Call of the Cutie"]; "Good work, everypony — I'm so proud of you!" [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Nicole Oliver (who also voices Princess Celestia). First appearance: "Call of the Cutie" (Season 1, Episode 12). Her cutie mark is three smiling daisy-like flowers. The name "Cheerilee" was recycled from a Generation 3 pony. EQG counterpart: "Miss Cheerilee," a Canterlot High teacher and librarian. Recurring episodes include "The Show Stoppers," "Hearts and Hooves Day," and "Ponyville Confidential." In IDW comics she is given a twin sister [uncertain]. Fans often pair her with Big McIntosh after "Hearts and Hooves Day."

## Prince Blueblood

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — *My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic* (Seasons 1–9, intermittent), IDW comics, Enterplay cards, and merchandise; no Equestria Girls appearance.
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn (stallion); purebred royal unicorn.
**Gender:** Male (he/him).
**Age/Stage:** Adult stallion; a young-adult prince of the Equestrian royal family.
**Residence:** Canterlot — the royal palace and high society.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Prince of Equestria and a member of the royal family (a distant nephew of Princess Celestia and Princess Luna); a noble with no shown official duties beyond attending elite events.
**Appearance:** A tall, immaculate white/pale-gray unicorn with a light amber-blond mane combed and styled to perfection, bright arctic-blue eyes, and a golden compass-rose cutie mark. He carries himself with a chin-up, preening posture and an expression poised between disdain and boredom; his coat is always groomed, his hooves polished, and his mane never out of place. At formal occasions he wears royal formal wear with gold accents. No wings, no battle scars, no dirt. His unicorn horn glows with pale magic when he levitates something.
**Personality:** Arrogant, vain, snobbish, and utterly self-centered — Prince Blueblood is the G4 universe's textbook pampered aristocrat. He sees other ponies as servants, views anything "common" with theatrical disgust, and is interested only in his own comfort, appearance, and status. He is a coward, a name-dropper, and a bore who mistakes his bloodline for a personality. What makes him memorable is how cheerfully oblivious he is: he genuinely does not understand why anypony would be upset with him. He is a comedic villain rather than a dangerous one — a parody of entitlement.
**Likes:** Himself, mirrors and grooming, fine dining and imported delicacies, flattery, royal status and ceremony, being waited on, the Grand Galloping Gala, and having the best of everything.
**Dislikes:** "Common" food and common ponies, trains and crowded travel, manual labor, dirt, fried carnival food, being inconvenienced, and anypony who does not treat him with proper deference.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Terrified of getting dirty, injured, or embarrassed; a coward in any real danger; physically soft and untrained despite his unicorn magic; his selfishness makes him friendless, and he has no idea that he is the joke. He is easily manipulated by flattery and utterly useless in a crisis.
**Skills/Abilities:** Standard unicorn magic — levitation and simple spells — plus royal etiquette, formal dancing, grooming, and the social power of his title. No combat, athletic, or practical skills are shown; he is a liability in any physical situation. His true talent is self-preservation and looking good.
**Family & Relationships:** Princess Celestia (distant aunt), Princess Luna (distant aunt), Princess Cadance (a non-blood "cousin" of the royal family), and unnamed noble relatives. Rarity once idolized him as her dream Gala date; after "The Best Night Ever" she is thoroughly disgusted with him, and he never notices. He has no named friends; the Canterlot elite tolerate him, and Princess Celestia seems quietly amused by his comeuppance.
**Background/Biography:** Prince Blueblood first appears in "The Ticket Master" (Season 1, Episode 3) as the fantasy image of Rarity's ideal Gala date — handsome, royal, and perfect. That fantasy shatters in "The Best Night Ever" (Season 1, Episode 26): at the Grand Galloping Gala, the real Blueblood is a boorish, selfish snob who uses Rarity as a shield against a flying cake, complains about "common carnival fare" touching his "royal lips," recoils from any contact, and abandons her at the first inconvenience. Rarity dresses him down, and he remains an object of ridicule in Canterlot society. He resurfaces at elite gatherings — "Sweet and Elite" (Season 2, Episode 9) and crowd scenes like "Equestria Games" (Season 4) — and in IDW comics, where his snobbery and self-importance are played for comedy (he refuses to carry his own luggage and complains about train travel being "common").
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** A posh, nasal, drawling upper-class accent dripping with condescension; he stretches vowels, says "How… quaint," "Ewww," and "my royal lips," refers to himself in the third person or by title, and complains in exaggerated, theatrical terms. He treats sentences like inconveniences and never uses a plain word when a fancier one will do.
**Notable Quotes:** "My royal lips have touched common carnival fare!" ("The Best Night Ever"); "Ewww…! Uh, stay back! I just had myself groomed!" ("The Best Night Ever"); "Princes of Canterlot don't carry their own luggage. That's what servants are for." (IDW comics); "This really is a gorgeous castle you have here… train travel, you know? It just doesn't agree with me. It's so crowded and… common, you know?" (IDW comics).
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Vincent Tong (who also voices Flash Sentry and Garble). First appearance: "The Ticket Master" (Season 1, Episode 3) in Rarity's fantasy; his actual debut is "The Best Night Ever" (Season 1, Episode 26). His cutie mark is a compass rose; an animation error in "Equestria Games" briefly gives him Fancy Pants's crown mark. "Blue blood" is a term for aristocratic lineage — a deliberate pun on his personality. He is one of the most disliked one-off characters in the fandom and a frequent butt of jokes; his appalled reaction to Rarity's cake-shield moment is a classic meme. He appears in IDW comics and the Enterplay card game, and some expanded media also give him the name Vladimir [uncertain].
