## Trixie

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — a recurring *Friendship is Magic* character with a human-world counterpart in the *Equestria Girls* spin-off (where she leads the band Trixie and the Illusions in *Rainbow Rocks*).
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn (she has no wings and is not an alicorn, no matter what she claims).
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare; a young touring showmare. Exact age is [not established in canon].
**Residence:** Nomadic for much of her life — she lives and travels in a purple star-spangled stage wagon. She becomes a regular presence in Ponyville and, from Season 9, holds a post at Twilight's School of Friendship in Ponyville.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Traveling stage magician and showmare, billing herself "The Great and Powerful Trixie"; at one point between tours she took work at a rock farm/quarry; from "A Horse Shoe-In" (S9E20) she is the friendship counselor/guidance adviser at the School of Friendship; briefly a villain and the wearer of the Alicorn Amulet; later a trusted ally of Twilight Sparkle and Starlight Glimmer.
**Appearance:** A slender unicorn mare with a bright cobalt-blue coat, a pale cornflower-blue mane and tail streaked with very pale sky-blue, and deep grayish-blue-violet eyes. Her cutie mark is a light-blue magic wand accompanied by a crescent moon — a symbol of her stage-magic special talent. She usually wears her signature costume: a purple, star-spangled pointed wizard's hat and matching cape covered in stars and moons, with a bow tie and collared shirt beneath. Her horn aura is pale greyish magenta. When she wore the Alicorn Amulet, her eyes glowed red and purple magic crackled around her. In Equestria Girls her human counterpart is a Canterlot High student with the same cobalt-blue hair, a confident smirk, and stage-magician flair; she fronts the band "Trixie and the Illusions."
**Personality:** Theatrical, boastful, and grandiosely self-important, Trixie speaks about herself in the third person and treats every room as her stage. She craves applause and attention, is quick to brag and quicker to feel slighted, and hides deep insecurity behind her bravado. She is sharp-tongued, sarcastic, and manipulative when it suits her, yet she is also genuinely loyal, surprisingly brave in a crisis, capable of real self-sacrifice for a friend, and slowly learning humility across the series. Her flaws — arrogance, jealousy of Twilight, a con-artist streak, and overpromising — repeatedly get her into trouble.
**Likes:** Applause and attention, magic tricks and illusions, fireworks and smoke effects, being called "Great and Powerful," sweets, her wagon, show business, and (eventually, deeply) Starlight Glimmer's friendship.
**Dislikes:** Being upstaged, being ignored or pitied, Twilight's effortless talent, Ursa Majors, hard manual labor, admitting she was wrong, humble pie, and anypony else stealing her spotlight.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Public humiliation and being forgotten; the Ursa Major that wrecked her show; losing Starlight's friendship; her actual magic is modest (illusions, smoke, fireworks, telekinesis, stage tricks) and no match for alicorn-level power; her ego and temper lead her into avoidable disasters.
**Skills/Abilities:** Expert stage magician and showmare — sleight of hoof, escape tricks, smoke-and-firework illusions, theatrical misdirection, and clever patter. Her unicorn telekinesis is real but limited. She is a quick thinker and a surprisingly effective distraction or con artist under pressure. With the Alicorn Amulet she briefly wielded amplified, dangerous magic.
**Family & Relationships:** Close best friend of Starlight Glimmer (her first real friend); frenemy-turned-friend and rival of Twilight Sparkle; complicated respect for Princess Celestia and the School of Friendship staff; on-again, off-again ally of Discord; staff-confirmed father named Jack Pot (a background stallion), with her mother unnamed; Trixie's broader family is only known from supplementary material.
**Background/Biography:** Debuting in "Boast Busters" (S1E6), Trixie arrived in Ponyville boasting that she had defeated an Ursa Major, humiliated the Mane Six's friends on stage, and was then humiliated herself when Twilight exposed her — and an actual Ursa Minor attacked. She returned in "Magic Duel" (S3E5) wearing the Alicorn Amulet, conquered Ponyville, and was freed only when Twilight out-tricked her; humbled, she offered the "Great and Apologetic Trixie" routine. In "No Second Prances" (S6E6) she became Starlight Glimmer's friend, and she helped save Equestria in the Season 6 finale "To Where and Back Again." She and Starlight then shared adventures in "All Bottled Up" (S7E2) and "On the Road to Friendship" (S8E19), and in Season 9 she was hired as the School of Friendship's counselor. The finale, "The Last Problem," shows her future among Twilight's circle.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Grandiose showbiz register, heavy third-person self-reference, frequent self-titles ("The Great and Powerful Trixie," "Trixie demands…"), sarcastic asides, dramatic gasps, and theatrical flourishes. She addresses others by name or with arch condescension, and her voice softens appreciably only with Starlight.
**Notable Quotes:** "The Great and Powerful Trixie!" (signature self-announcement); "The Great and Powerful Trixie doesn't need anypony!" [paraphrased]; "Trixie does not take orders from anypony!" [paraphrased]; "You may applaud now." [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Full name Trixie Lulamoon (used in the *Equestria Girls: Rainbow Rocks* credits and merchandise); voiced by Kathleen Barr in both pony and human versions; first appearance "Boast Busters" (S1E6). Her name derives from Beatrix/Patricia-style shortenings, and her star-and-moon motif echoes stage-magician iconography; she is a long-running fandom favorite ("Trixie is best pony" is a classic brony meme), and her "Alicorn Amulet" episode remains one of the show's best-known villain turns.

## Twilight Sparkle

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — the central protagonist of *Friendship is Magic*; her human-world counterpart (Sci-Twi, the human Twilight Sparkle) is documented separately and appears in the *Equestria Girls* films from the second movie onward.
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn who becomes an alicorn (Princess of Friendship) at the end of Season 3.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Young adult mare; a student at the start of the series, a princess and later the ruler of Equestria in the final season's future epilogue.
**Residence:** Canterlot (born and raised), then Ponyville's Golden Oak Library, then the Castle of the Friendship Rainbow (Friendship Castle); later Canterlot as ruler.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Element of Magic, leader of the Mane Six, Princess of Friendship (crowned in "Magical Mystery Cure"), founder and headmare of the School of Friendship, mentor to Starlight Glimmer and later ruler of Equestria; former personal student of Princess Celestia and later her equal.
**Appearance:** Lavender unicorn-turned-alicorn with a dark blue mane and tail streaked with pink, purple, and magenta, violet eyes, and a cutie mark of a large pink six-pointed star surrounded by five smaller white stars — the "Element of Magic" mark. As an alicorn she gains large feathered wings, and her mane and tail gain flowing, etheric streaks; she wears a golden crown/tiara with a six-pointed star and (as princess) a golden horseshoe collar. Her magic aura is magenta/pink. In her formal princess regalia — crown, collar, and slippers — she is instantly recognizable, though she is often seen in her plain coat too. Her human counterpart (Sci-Twi) is a glasses-wearing, purple-streaked schoolgirl at Canterlot High.
**Personality:** Brilliant, studious, meticulous, and anxious, Twilight is a compulsive planner and list-maker who panics when things are out of control; she is fiercely loyal, selfless, and idealistic about friendship, yet prone to overthinking, perfectionism, and catastrophizing. Over the series she grows from a socially awkward bookworm into a calm, compassionate leader and teacher, though her dorky enthusiasm, hunger for knowledge, and love of schedules never fully fade. She is deeply empathetic, sometimes to the point of self-doubt.
**Likes:** Books, libraries, studying, schedules and checklists, research, tea and sweets, Spike, her friends, the Elements of Harmony, teaching, order and preparation, and any chance to lecture enthusiastically about a subject she loves.
**Dislikes:** Chaos, surprises, tardiness, being unprepared, failure, disappointing her mentor, bullies, getting dragged into spontaneous parties, and Spike's occasional sass/nonsense; she also dislikes having to choose between responsibilities.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Failure and letting others down; being unprepared or losing control (her "want it, need it" spell disaster and her panic in "Lesson Zero" define this); stage fright; overwork and burnout; she can be bossy, stubborn, and slow to trust her own instincts; in the Equestria Girls world her counterpart's magical accident creates Midnight Sparkle, a fear-empowered alter ego.
**Skills/Abilities:** The most powerful unicorn of her generation, and later an alicorn with flight and princess-level magic: telekinesis, teleportation, force fields, spell creation, transformation spells, magical research, and the "rainbow power" of the Elements. She is an outstanding scholar, strategist, and teacher, a natural leader, and an exceptional organizer; her weaknesses include physical combat (she is not a fighter) and panic under social pressure.
**Family & Relationships:** Daughter of Twilight Velvet and Night Light; younger sister of Shining Armor, sister-in-law of Princess Cadance, aunt of Flurry Heart; adopted big sister/assistant relationship with Spike; protégée of Princess Celestia (and later of the Element of Magic's legacy); mentor to Starlight Glimmer; best friends with Applejack, Fluttershy, Pinkie Pie, Rainbow Dash, and Rarity; destined student-teacher dynamic with Luster Dawn in the finale's future; close friend/ally of Discord, the princesses, and the Young Six.
**Background/Biography:** Born in Canterlot and accepted into Princess Celestia's School for Gifted Unicorns after her hatching-day magic surge; as a filly she studied obsessively and had few friends, then was sent to Ponyville in "Friendship is Magic" (S1E1–2) to oversee the Summer Sun Celebration and learned the magic of friendship, defeating Nightmare Moon with the Elements. Across nine seasons she earned her wings in "Magical Mystery Cure," became Princess of Friendship, opened the School of Friendship, mentored Starlight, and helped defeat Discord's chaos, Tirek, Chrysalis, the Storm King's invasion, King Sombra, Cozy Glow's schemes, and the Legion of Doom. In the series finale "The Last Problem" she succeeds Celestia and Luna as Equestria's ruler, and decades later still teaches a new generation of friendship students.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Precise, educated, articulate; launches into excited info-dumps ("Ooh! Ooh! I know this one!"), corrects people's grammar and citations, frets aloud, and uses formal academic phrasing; calls Spike by name warmly and others "everypony"; repeats "Friendship is magic!"; her nervous speeches accelerate into run-on lists and worst-case scenarios.
**Notable Quotes:** "Dear Princess Celestia, today I learned that friendship isn't always easy, but there's no doubt it's worth fighting for." [paraphrased]; "Friendship is magic!" (series-defining line); "Spike, take a note." (signature recurring command); "There's nothing to worry about — and now I shall prove it with charts!" [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Tara Strong (speaking) and Rebecca Shoichet (singing); first appearance "Friendship is Magic, Part 1" (S1E1). Her cutie mark is the Element of Magic, and her name reflects her magical talent and love of reading. She is the only character to appear in every episode of the series (with Spike). Her human counterpart in Equestria Girls — Sci-Twi — is a separate roster entry, and Midnight Sparkle is that counterpart's corrupted form; the two Twilights meet in *Equestria Girls: Friendship Games*.

## Limestone Pie

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — a Pie family earth pony from *Friendship is Magic*; because she is a background family member she has no significant Equestria Girls focus (her human counterpart, if any, is uncredited).
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare, the eldest of the four Pie sisters (older than Maud, Pinkie, and Marble).
**Residence:** The Pie family rock farm near Rockville in western Equestria, where she lives with her parents; she appears at the farm, in Ponyville for family events, and at Maud's graduation elsewhere.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Running the Pie family rock farm — she states that her parents may own the farm but she keeps it running; guardian of Holder's Boulder, the farm's sacred boulder; member of the Pie family and by extension an acquaintance of the Apple family.
**Appearance:** A stocky, sturdy earth pony mare with a blue-tinted gray coat, an off-white gray mane and tail (cut short and tucked back), and light apple-green eyes. Her cutie mark is half a lime with two stones, reflecting her rock-farming talent and hard, tart personality. She wears a simple work saddle/harness and, for Hearth's Warming, a formal black-and-red dress with a matching hat; as a filly in flashback she looks like a smaller version with the same coloring. She differs from her sisters by her blunt, short mane, broader build, and permanently skeptical expression.
**Personality:** Grumpy, blunt, bossy, and fiercely protective of her family and her farm. Limestone is a workaholic control freak who snaps at anyone who touches Holder's Boulder or disrupts her routines, and she has a short fuse and a competitive streak. Under the prickliness she is deeply loyal: she can be perceptive and surprisingly wise about her sisters' relationships, and she warms to outsiders — including Applejack — once they prove they respect her family's traditions. She is not comfortable with affection and covers it with sarcasm.
**Likes:** Hard work, order, her rock farm, Holder's Boulder, geology and stone, her sisters (in her own way), Hearth's Warming traditions (as she defines them), winning arguments, and proving she can run things.
**Dislikes:** Trespassers on Holder's Boulder, slacking, the Apple family's loud customs (initially), disorganized events, being teased about being grumpy or jealous, pink frills, and anyone who says the rock farm is boring.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Losing control of the farm or family, being seen as the "grumpy" sister, change and outsiders' disrespect for tradition; her temper and bossiness push people away, and she is easily provoked.
**Skills/Abilities:** Exceptional physical strength and stamina for an earth pony (she moves boulders and runs a working quarry); expert knowledge of rocks, geology, and farm equipment; natural leadership and organizational control; practical craft skills; she is a capable farm administrator and a determined negotiator.
**Family & Relationships:** Daughter of Igneous Rock Pie and Cloudy Quartz; eldest sister of Maud Pie, Pinkie Pie, and Marble Pie; granddaughter of the Pie grandparents; aunt-in-spirit to Maud's partner Mudbriar; initially wary of, then accepting of, the Apple family; sisterly bond with Pinkie despite the personality clash.
**Background/Biography:** Limestone first appears as a filly in Pinkie's flashback in "The Cutie Mark Chronicles" (S1E23) and in a family photo in "Pinkie Pride" (S4E12). Her first major appearance is "Hearthbreakers" (S5E20), where the Apple and Pie families spend Hearth's Warming together: she clashes with Applejack over traditions, jealously guards Holder's Boulder, and eventually makes peace with the Apples and admits the value of both families' customs. She returns for Maud's graduation in "Rock Solid Friendship" (S7E4), gives Pinkie relationship advice in "The Maud Couple" (S8E3), appears in *My Little Pony: Best Gift Ever*, and is glimpsed in the series finale's group scenes.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Clipped, commanding sentences; rhetorical demands; deadpan sarcasm; speaks in short declarative bursts with an exasperated edge; often uses "my" possessively ("my farm"); barks orders ("And I'm in charge!").
**Notable Quotes:** "Gaze into the eyes of Limestone Pie. Ma and Pa may own this rock farm, but I keep it running."; "Stay off Holder's Boulder!"; "What. Happened. To. My. Farm?!"; "Well, don't just stand there! We got a boulder to move! And I'm in charge."
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Ingrid Nilson (who also voices Maud and Marble Pie); first major appearance "Hearthbreakers" (S5E20). The Pie sisters' names were chosen by author G.M. Berrow so Maud, Limestone, and Pinkie's initials spell M-L-P, a nod to "My Little Pony" (with Marble an additional M). Fandom nicknames for her include "Captain Grumpy" and "Limey"; she is a fan-favorite grumpy sister, and "Holder's Boulder" became an in-show running gag.

## Tempest Shadow (Fizzlepop Berrytwist)

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — the secondary antagonist and redeemed ally of *My Little Pony: The Movie* (2017); she also appears in tie-in IDW comics and novels, and in the Season 9 finale battle and coronation scenes.
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn; her horn is broken, so she cannot channel full unicorn magic normally.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare; her filly self appears in flashback.
**Residence:** An unnamed small town where she grew up; later the Storm King's fortress/armada and occupied Canterlot during the movie; after her redemption she becomes an ally in Equestria.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Former second-in-command and commander of the Storm King's army; formerly a student in her hometown; after the film, a free ally and friend of Twilight and the princesses, appearing at the final battle and Twilight's coronation.
**Appearance:** A tall, imposing dark-aubergine unicorn mare with a bright rose-colored mane and tail tipped in a lighter shade, and bright turquoise eyes. A jagged scar runs over her right eye, and her horn is shattered a third of the way up, the stump covered by a metal cap/ring. Her cutie mark is never clearly shown in the film — canon sources list it as unknown, and her flank armor and the Storm King's emblem obscure it. She wears heavy dark armor (black and silver, with a spiked pauldron and breastplate), a cape, and metal hoof-guards; her mane is swept back into a practical style. In flashback she is a cheerful pink-maned filly before the loss of her horn.
**Personality:** Cold, disciplined, bitter, and fiercely self-reliant; she hides profound hurt and fear of abandonment behind a soldier's discipline and a smooth, threatening competence. She believes friendship is a weakness, until Twilight's compassion cracks her armor. Once redeemed, she is quietly warm, loyal, brave, and dryly funny — and still carries the trauma of being shunned and abandoned as a filly.
**Likes:** Power and control, discipline, precision, independence, proving herself, her secret dream of being accepted, and eventually genuine friendship; she also has a soft spot for the home and friends she lost.
**Dislikes:** Being pitied, showing weakness, the Storm King's mockery and broken promises, sentimentality, and anyone who abandons a friend; she also despises her own former naivety.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Abandonment and rejection (rooted in her fillyhood friends running from her after her horn broke); being powerless or a failure; her broken horn severely limits her magic, leaving her reliant on physical combat and gadgets; her bitterness makes her easy for the Storm King to manipulate.
**Skills/Abilities:** Superb fighter and tactician — she wields powerful magic blasts from her broken horn (unstable but violent), specializes in hand-to-hand/hoof-to-hoof combat, leads armies, and pilots the Storm King's airship; she is an intimidating interrogator and a capable commander. Her horn still fires unstable blasts of magic and lightning, but the break makes her output inconsistent, so she prefers physical combat and the Storm King's armored forces.
**Family & Relationships:** No named canon relatives; her fillyhood friends abandoned her after her horn was broken; bitter subordinate of the Storm King (who treats her as a tool and breaks his promise); enemy-turned-friend of Twilight Sparkle and the Mane Six; by the end of the film she is accepted by the ponies of Equestria and remains a loyal ally.
**Background/Biography:** As a filly named Fizzlepop Berrytwist, she played with friends in her village; when an Ursa Minor attacked, her horn was shattered, and her frightened friends ran away. She grew up bitter and self-reliant, joined the Storm King to have her horn restored, and became his formidable second-in-command, capturing the four princesses and invading Canterlot in *My Little Pony: The Movie*. She pursued Twilight's group across Equestria, but Twilight's choice to save her from falling during the final confrontation shook her worldview. When the Storm King betrayed and attacked her, she turned on him; the ponies' friendship and song broke his hold, and she reclaimed her real name, Fizzlepop Berrytwist, and joined the celebration. She later appears fighting alongside Equestria's defenders in the Season 9 finale and at Twilight's coronation.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Low, controlled, clipped military diction; silky menace when threatening; rarely raises her voice; sardonic one-liners; shifts to softer, hesitant warmth when sincere. She calls ponies "little ponies" and addresses Twilight by name with grudging respect; her delivery is dry and unhurried.
**Notable Quotes:** "When I was a filly, my horn was broken, and my friends ran away. That's when I learned that nopony looks out for you but yourself." [paraphrased]; "The Storm King takes what he wants — and he will give me back my horn." [paraphrased]; "Open up your eyes." (from her song "Open Up Your Eyes").
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Emily Blunt; first appearance *My Little Pony: The Movie* (2017); real name Fizzlepop Berrytwist, revealed at the end of the film. Her design is unusual for the series — a scarred, armored unicorn with a broken horn — and her song "Open Up Your Eyes" is one of the movie's best-known numbers. Tie-in novel *The Stormy Road to Canterlot* and the IDW comic "Tempest's Tale" explore her backstory, including aliases she used while hiding (e.g., "Caramel Chip," "Cherry Pie") [uncertain: specific aliases]. She is a fan-favorite redemption arc often cited as one of the film's strongest elements.

## Bright Mac

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — a posthumous Apple family patriarch from *Friendship is Magic*, shown in flashbacks in "The Perfect Pear" (S7E13) and glimpsed in a few dream/photo scenes; he is deceased before the series begins.
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony stallion.
**Gender:** Male (he/him).
**Age/Stage:** Adult stallion when he died; the episode shows him as a baby, a young colt, a teenager, and a married father. His exact age at death is [not established in canon].
**Residence:** Sweet Apple Acres, Ponyville (the Apple family farm).
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Apple farmer and orchard worker; son of Granny Smith and heir to the Apple family farm; later husband of Pear Butter and father of the Apple siblings' generation.
**Appearance:** A warm, sunny earth pony stallion with a pale bright grayish-yellow coat, a vivid red mane and tail, and medium green eyes; his cutie mark is half a green apple with a star in the middle, mirroring the apple marks of his descendants. He is shown in simple farmer's work clothes, and the brimmed cowboy hat that Applejack wears today originally belonged to him (per *My Little Pony: Best Gift Ever*). In the flashback he is a lanky, friendly-looking colt with a big grin; as a baby he is a tiny foal with the same red forelock.
**Personality:** Gentle, warm, romantic, and quietly stubborn. Bright Mac is a charmer with an easy grin and a soft, teasing way of speaking, but he is also deeply loyal and brave enough to defy his mother and the entire Apple-Pear feud for the pony he loves. He is a devoted family man, patient and kind, a little mischievous, and endearingly romantic — he counts anniversaries in the hundreds of thousands of hours.
**Likes:** Pear Butter (whom he nicknames "Buttercup"), his family, apple farming, the pear-apple tree they planted, music (she gives him a guitar), family traditions, and long romantic gestures.
**Dislikes:** The pointless Apple-Pear feud, being told whom he may love, family arguments, and the idea of being separated from Pear Butter.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Losing Pear Butter or being forced apart by their families; disappointing his mother while still choosing his own heart; his softness makes him vulnerable to family pressure, and he takes an enormous risk by marrying in secret. He dies young, leaving his children behind — the cause of death is [not established in canon].
**Skills/Abilities:** Expert apple farmer and orchard worker; strong, capable earth pony farm labor; charming talker and peacemaker; a romantic strategist (he and Pear Butter elope with a secret wedding); presumably musical, since Pear Butter gives him a guitar.
**Family & Relationships:** Son of Granny Smith; husband of Pear Butter (born Buttercup of the Pear family); father of Applejack, Big McIntosh, and Apple Bloom; son-in-law of Grand Pear; part of the wider Apple family (Braeburn, Babs Seed, and others); close friends with Burnt Oak, who attends his wedding; his descendants include Big Mac and Sugar Belle's son.
**Background/Biography:** Bright Mac met Pear Butter as a baby when the Apple and Pear families were still feuding; he called her "Buttercup" and the two became inseparable childhood friends. As they grew, friendship turned to love, but Granny Smith and Grand Pear kept the young couple apart as the family feud hardened. When separation became imminent, Bright Mac and Pear Butter married in secret, with Mayor Mare officiating and their friends Burnt Oak and Mrs. Cake as witnesses. Both families crashed the ceremony; Grand Pear demanded Pear Butter choose, and she chose to become an Apple. The seeds they planted that wedding night grew into the famous apple-pear tree. They had three children and raised them at Sweet Apple Acres until their early deaths, which the show leaves unexplained; the series later honors them through the tree, Applejack's hat, and the twin shooting stars in "Apple Family Reunion."
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Gentle country drawl, warm and teasing; calls Pear Butter "darlin'" and "Buttercup"; speaks in soft, romantic declarations and playful jokes; a storyteller's cadence when he talks about the farm and family.
**Notable Quotes:** "My mom says if you hold a buttercup under your chin, it'll make your chin glow. But it doesn't work on me. See?"; "Happy one hundred and thirty-one thousand, four hundred and fifty-six hour anniversary, darlin'."; "I don't want to be apart from you. Ever. I'm not sure what we'll do, but I'm sure of us. So sure that I'd marry you today."
**Trivia/Notes:** Full name Bright McIntosh, shortened to Bright Mac; voiced by Bill Newton; first full appearance "The Perfect Pear" (S7E13), foreshadowed by the two shooting stars over Sweet Apple Acres in "Apple Family Reunion" (S3E8) and a brief dream-bubble cameo in "A Royal Problem" (S7E10). His colors are reused for other background stallions, and his body/mane palette matches Apple Bloom's. Series staff confirmed he and Pear Butter are deceased, though the show never depicts or explains their deaths; the apple-pear tree from their wedding is a recurring symbol of their love.

## Lemon Hearts

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — a recurring background unicorn in *Friendship is Magic* who gained a name and speaking role in "Amending Fences"; she has minor *Equestria Girls* and IDW comics presence.
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare; shown as a filly in Twilight's flashbacks (with her head comically stuck in a beaker).
**Residence:** Canterlot; she is one of Twilight's old Canterlot friends.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Canterlot event planner — she organizes large functions such as state dinners; member of a band with the pony Pepperdance (per merchandise); former classmate/study friend of Twilight Sparkle at Canterlot.
**Appearance:** A pale bright-yellow unicorn mare with a sky-blue mane and tail striped in lighter blue, and purplish-red eyes. Her cutie mark is three hearts — two light blue and one light green — fitting her sweet, affectionate name. She typically wears no accessories, but appears in a mouse costume for Nightmare Night and in formal bridesmaid attire at Princess Cadance's wedding; animation errors occasionally render her as an earth pony, an alicorn, or a crystal pony, and her tail sometimes loses its pale-blue stripes.
**Personality:** Warm, friendly, polite, and sociable — a natural hostess and people person who remembers her friends and gently needles them for staying away. She is a bit of a fashion critic ("an embarrassment to all things fashion!"), loves gossip and big social occasions, and is kind and welcoming (merchandise describes her as "kind and caring" and always willing to show friends she cares). She can be shallow about style and status but is genuinely sweet and loyal.
**Likes:** Donuts with friends, fashion shows, parties and big events, event planning, her old Canterlot circle (Minuette, Twinkleshine, Moon Dancer), music, and catching up over lunch.
**Dislikes:** Fashion disasters, being forgotten by friends (she and the others gently scold Twilight for leaving without goodbyes), rude behavior, and dull small gatherings.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Being left behind or overlooked by friends; she is not a fighter and has only ordinary unicorn magic; her taste for gossip and status makes her a little superficial at times.
**Skills/Abilities:** Professional event planning and organization, social networking, unicorn telekinesis, musical talent (band membership per the "Friendship Celebration Collection" toy line), and quick, witty conversation.
**Family & Relationships:** No named canon relatives. Close friends with Minuette, Twinkleshine, Moon Dancer, and Twilight Sparkle (all old Canterlot friends), and a bandmate of Pepperdance; she is on friendly terms with the Mane Six after the events of "Amending Fences."
**Background/Biography:** A background pony from the very first episodes, Lemon Hearts appears in "Friendship is Magic" as one of the Canterlot ponies around Twilight, and in Ponyville's welcome party. She has had dialogue as early as "Green Isn't Your Color" (S1E20), "A Bird in the Hoof" (S1E22), and later party episodes; her name was first spoken in "Amending Fences" (S5E12), where she reunites with Twilight, has lunch with Minuette and Twinkleshine, and joins the effort to make amends with Moon Dancer, admitting she and the others were hurt that Twilight left Canterlot without saying goodbye. She appears in later seasons as a party guest and background celebrant (including *A Hearth's Warming Tail*), in the 2017 film as a crowd cameo, and in IDW comics.

## Minuette

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — a recurring background unicorn in *Friendship is Magic* with dialogue from Season 2 onward; she has an *Equestria Girls* human counterpart (seen in *Forgotten Friendship*) and a cameo in the 2017 movie.
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare; shown as a filly in Twilight's flashbacks.
**Residence:** Canterlot, in an hourglass-themed house; she travels often to Ponyville and Manehattan.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** No fixed career is stated in canon; a sociable Canterlot pony, frequent event guest and volunteer, one of Twilight Sparkle's old Canterlot friends, and a bridesmaid at Princess Cadance's wedding; in the Equestria Girls world her human counterpart is a Canterlot High student.
**Appearance:** A unicorn mare with a light cornflower-blue coat, a blue mane and tail streaked with pale gray-blue, and deep blue eyes. Her cutie mark is an hourglass — the same mark carried by the timekeeping background pony "Doctor" (Doctor Whooves). She usually wears nothing; she dressed as a doctor for Nightmare Night and as a bridesmaid for the royal wedding. As a human she has matching blue hair and the same bright, chatty demeanor.

Minuette loves parties, gossip, photos, and being included, and she is quick to welcome people back into the group. She is famously punctual (merchandise jokes that magic keeps her "always on time"), sociable to the point of giddiness, and quietly loyal: she tells Twilight that they were her friends then and would be honored to be her friends now.

**Fears/Weaknesses:** Being shut out of her friendships; she has no combat training and only ordinary unicorn magic; her eagerness can make her fandom-plotted as a time traveler, but in canon she is simply an ordinary, scatterbrained socialite.
**Skills/Abilities:** Unicorn telekinesis and simple spells; extraordinary punctuality and time management (the source of her hourglass mark and fandom joke); social networking, event participation and organizing, and singing/performance.
**Family & Relationships:** No named relatives. Closest friends are Lyra Heartstrings, Twinkleshine, Lemon Hearts, Moon Dancer, and Twilight Sparkle; she stood as one of Princess Cadance's bridesmaids alongside Lyra and Twinkleshine; she is friendly with the Mane Six and is a frequent Ponyville visitor.
**Background/Biography:** Minuette first appears in "Friendship is Magic, Part 1" (S1E1), one of the ponies around Twilight in Canterlot before the Summer Sun Celebration, and she appears in Twilight's Ponyville welcome party. She becomes a recurring face through Seasons 1–4, has her first dialogue in "A Canterlot Wedding" (S2E25–26) as a bridesmaid, and was properly named in "Amending Fences" (S5E12), where she warmly reunites with Twilight, teases her for never visiting, and helps throw the party that reconciles Moon Dancer with the old group. She appears in *A Hearth's Warming Tail*, the 2017 film's Friendship Festival crowd, and *Equestria Girls: Forgotten Friendship* as a library-going human student.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Fast, bubbly, exclamatory; playful teasing and delighted gasps; friendly and familiar, using nicknames and rhetorical questions; laughs between sentences and speaks in cheerful run-ons when excited.

What are you doing here?! … Hey, grab a picture of me and the princess, will ya?!"; "Oh, come on, Twilight! Her hourglass cutie mark and blue color scheme earned her the enduring fandom nickname "Colgate" (after the toothpaste brand), and because her mark matches Doctor Whooves's, fandom lore pairs her with him as a time-travel companion — pure fan invention. Her name is the feminine form of "minuet," a 17th-century French dance, and also puns on "minute." Toy cards say she is always on time and has many hobbies, including singing.

## Big Macintosh

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — a main supporting character of *Friendship is Magic* with a human-world counterpart in the *Equestria Girls* spin-off.
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony stallion (human in the Equestria Girls world).
**Gender:** Male (he/him).
**Age/Stage:** Adult stallion — the older brother of Applejack and Apple Bloom; in the future epilogue of "The Last Problem" he is a middle-aged husband and father.
**Residence:** Sweet Apple Acres, Ponyville.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Apple farmer, cider maker, and the farm's heavy-labor workhorse; bass singer in the Ponytones barbershop quartet; occasional salespony at the Apple family's market stand; husband of Sugar Belle; member of the Apple family and the Mane Six's extended circle.
**Appearance:** A very large, broad-shouldered crimson-red earth pony stallion with a bright orange-yellow mane and tail, medium-dark green eyes, and a cutie mark of half a green apple (occasionally animated with four small stars around it). He wears a heavy brown work harness/yoke with a collar for pulling carts and plows, and his sheer size (taller and bulkier than most stallions) makes him instantly recognizable. In Equestria Girls his human counterpart is a tall, muscular, quiet Canterlot High student with red-orange hair and green eyes; Big Mac also appears once in drag as the cheerful "Orchard Blossom," complete with a floral dress and wig.

Beneath the calm he is deeply sensitive, easily embarrassed by romance, gentle with children and animals, protective of his family, and quietly funny. He is humble about his strength, shy about performing, and a rock for Applejack and Apple Bloom.
**Likes:** Apples and apple farming, hard honest work, his family, Sugar Belle, singing bass with the Ponytones, sweet apple treats, quiet evenings, and helping his sisters.
**Dislikes:** Talking about his feelings, public attention and stage performance, being teased about romance, seeing his family upset, laziness, and elaborate fuss or fashion (except as Orchard Blossom).

**Family & Relationships:** Son of Bright Mac and Pear Butter (both deceased); grandson of Granny Smith and, maternally, Grand Pear; older brother of Applejack and Apple Bloom; cousin of Braeburn and Babs Seed; husband of Sugar Belle and father of an unnamed son in the future epilogue (called "Big Sugar" in supplementary material); friend and reliable ally of the Mane Six, Spike, the Cutie Mark Crusaders, and the Pies.
**Background/Biography:** Big Mac first appears in "Friendship is Magic, Part 1" (S1E1) and gains his first speaking lines in "Applebuck Season" (S1E4), when he is injured and Applejack overworks herself to save the harvest. He becomes a series fixture: the cider contest in "The Super Speedy Cider Squeezy 6000" (S2E15), the love-potion disaster with Cheerilee in "Hearts and Hooves Day" (S2E17), his Ponytones stage fright in "Filli Vanilli" (S4E14), his "Orchard Blossom" disguise in "Brotherhooves Social" (S5E17), and his courtship of Sugar Belle in "Hard to Say Anything" (S7E8). After the misunderstanding of "The Break Up Break Down" (S8E10), he and Sugar Belle propose to each other in "The Big Mac Question" (S9E23) and are married under the apple-pear tree his parents planted. In "The Last Problem" he is shown years later, happily married with a son, still working Sweet Apple Acres.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Extremely laconic, low and slow; his standard answers are "Eeyup" and "Nope" (and variants like "Nnnope"), with long pauses and meaningful looks; he speaks in short sentences, addresses Sugar Belle softly, and his rare longer speeches are all the more moving for their scarcity. As Orchard Blossom he puts on a fluty, bubbly voice.
**Notable Quotes:** "Eeyup."; "Nope."; "Well, what in the hay is that supposed to mean?" [paraphrased]; "Orchard Blossom: Oh, my stars! What a lovely little get-together!" [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Full name Big McIntosh, almost always called Big Mac; voiced by Peter New; first appearance S1E1. His cutie mark is half a green apple (an animation error sometimes adds four stars); his name parodies the McIntosh apple variety. He is the source of the long-running "Eeyup" catchphrase/meme. In Equestria Girls his human counterpart appears as a Canterlot High student and Applejack's brother in crowd and short scenes [uncertain: exact EG appearances minor and uncredited].

## Sunset Shimmer

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls — a unicorn from Equestria who becomes the central protagonist of the *Equestria Girls* spin-off; this entry covers her pony origin and her human-world form together.
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn in Equestria; human (via the mirror portal) in the Equestria Girls world.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Young adult mare in Equestria; a human teenager attending Canterlot High School in the EG world.
**Residence:** Originally Canterlot, Equestria, where she was Princess Celestia's student; in the human world she lives in Canterlot City and attends Canterlot High School.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Lead guitarist and vocalist of the Rainbooms; Canterlot High student; former personal protégée of Princess Celestia and former self-styled queen bee of Canterlot High; later the informal leader of the human-world heroes and the group's expert on Equestrian magic.
**Appearance:** In pony form, Sunset is a unicorn mare with a warm golden-amber coat, a red-and-yellow mane and tail (crimson with orange-gold stripes), and bright cyan eyes; her cutie mark is a red-and-gold sun, and it also appears as the emblem she prints on her clothes and gear. In the human world she appears as a teenage girl with the same amber skin and red-and-yellow hair worn in a high ponytail, cyan eyes, and a signature outfit of a black leather jacket over a pink shirt, a short skirt, and boots; when the Rainbooms play their magic music, she "ponies up," gaining pony ears and a tail of red-and-yellow hair. Her magic aura is warm red-orange/gold, and when corrupted by the crown in the first film her form twists into a demonic, fiery-winged shape.
**Personality:** Confident, sassy, quick-witted, and fiercely loyal — but with a temper she has learned to aim in the right direction. Once a cruel, power-hungry bully, she has become remarkably self-aware: driven by guilt over her past, determined to prove she has changed, and protective of her friends to the point of self-sacrifice. She is the group's strategist, its best magical theorist, and its most likely member to bend the rules for a good cause. She is also guarded, secretive about her own pain, and can be reckless when someone she loves is threatened.
**Likes:** Her friends (the Rainbooms, Spike, and Twilight), playing guitar and performing, adventure, Equestrian magic and puzzles, her journal and photographs, being accepted for who she is now, and proving herself through action.
**Dislikes:** Being judged for her old self, bullies and manipulators (especially those who prey on students), her own past mistakes, being left out or underestimated, abuse of magic, and being called a "princess" or treated like royalty.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Never being forgiven and backsliding into her old self; power tempting her again (the crown); guilt and nightmares; losing her human friends when she returns to Equestria; her unicorn magic is powerful but the human world's magic is unstable and sometimes backfires; she is hot-tempered and keeps secrets.
**Skills/Abilities:** Skilled unicorn magic — telekinesis, teleportation, shields, and advanced spellwork from her Celestia-level training; the group's foremost expert on Equestrian magic; excellent guitarist and singer; a natural leader and tactician; physically capable in a fight; empathic, determined, and good at reading people.
**Family & Relationships:** No canon relatives are shown; Former mentor Princess Celestia (whose trust she lost and later reconciled with); close friends and bandmates Rainbow Dash, Applejack, Rarity, Fluttershy, and Pinkie Pie, plus human Twilight Sparkle (Sci-Twi) and Spike; she is a mentor figure to reformed EG characters such as Wallflower Blush and Juniper Montage, and a former enemy-turned-friend of the human Trixie.
**Background/Biography:** As a filly, Sunset Shimmer was taken on as Princess Celestia's personal student; brilliant but impatient, she grew hungry for power and resentful of Celestia's lessons. She discovered the mirror portal to the human world and fled through it rather than accept her mentor's guidance. In the human world she enrolled at Canterlot High School and ruled it through intimidation, becoming a bullying queen bee. In *Equestria Girls* she stole Twilight Sparkle's crown (the Element of Magic) to gain absolute power, framed Twilight, and at the Fall Formal transformed into a fiery demon; Twilight and her friends' friendship magic defeated her, and — in the films' defining act of mercy — she was offered forgiveness instead of punishment. In *Rainbow Rocks* she struggled to prove herself, helped form the Rainbooms, and defeated the Dazzlings; in *Friendship Games* she helped stop Midnight Sparkle; in *Legend of Everfree* she and her friends dealt with the geodes and Gloriosa Daisy; and across the later EG specials (*Forgotten Friendship*, *Rollercoaster of Friendship*, *Spring Breakdown*, *Sunset's Backstage Pass*, *Holidays Unwrapped*) she remained the human world's tireless guardian and the heart of the group.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Modern teenage American register — casual, sharp, sarcastic, and warm when she drops her guard; uses "guys," "seriously?," and "okay, listen"; when she was a villain her voice turned silky, sneering, and grandiose. She is direct under pressure and reassuring when leading.
**Notable Quotes:** "My past is not today." (from her song of the same name); "I'm not the pony I used to be." [paraphrased]; "We're all in this together." [paraphrased]; "Bring it on." [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Rebecca Shoichet (who also sings as Twilight Sparkle and voices Sugar Belle); first appearance *My Little Pony: Equestria Girls* (2013). She is the only one of the EG core characters who is not a human counterpart of an existing pony — she is the Equestrian Sunset Shimmer living as a human — and her arc from villain to hero is widely regarded as one of the best redemption stories in the franchise. Her name/sun cutie mark and her mentor relationship with Celestia were established in the first film; she is a favorite of the fandom ("Sunset is best pony") and appears in the *Equestria Girls* digital series and web shorts.

## Silverstream

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — a hippogriff from *Friendship is Magic* (Seasons 8–9) and a member of the Young Six; she has no Equestria Girls human counterpart.
**Species/Kind:** Hippogriff who can transform into a seapony in water (a magic the hippogriffs gained through Queen Novo's pearl).
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** A young teenager — a student at Twilight's School of Friendship; shown as an adult in the series finale's future epilogue.
**Residence:** Ponyville, in the student quarters of the School of Friendship (from "School Daze, Part 2"); formerly Seaquestria under the sea and Mount Aris on land.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Student of the School of Friendship (Seasons 8–9); member of the Young Six; unofficial ambassador between the land and sea branches of the hippogriffs; sea/land hybrid explorer.
**Appearance:** A cheerful hippogriff with a pale pink-lavender body, a light gray-purple beak and claws, bright cornflower-blue mane and tail streaked with pale blue, and blue-violet eyes; she has large feathered wings, a tufted crest and tail feathers, and a bouncy, birdlike way of moving. In seapony form her body becomes sea-green and blue with a long, flowing finned tail and fin-like ears and crest. She usually wears no clothing beyond a student satchel, and in flight her mane streams behind her like a banner.
**Personality:** Bubbly, hyperactive, curious, and relentlessly friendly. Silverstream treats every land-pony custom as a wonderful discovery, asks endless questions, and reacts to almost everything with delighted amazement; she is naive, optimistic, and emotional, prone to sudden homesickness and equally sudden joy. She is also braver and more resilient than she looks, fiercely loyal to her friends, and unusually open-minded — the first to welcome newcomers and the glue of the Young Six's friendship.
**Likes:** Her friends (the Young Six), flying and swimming, learning land customs (school, holidays, food), making new friends, sunshine, music and celebrations, her brother Terramar, and both of her family homes.
**Dislikes:** Being trapped or far from her family, the tension between her parents' land and sea lives, conflict and sad partings, being underestimated or treated as "just a schoolgirl," and long, boring lessons.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Losing her family or being forced to choose between the two halves of her family; being helpless when friends are in danger; her enthusiasm and naivety can send her rushing into danger; she is easily overwhelmed by homesickness; her physical combat ability is limited.
**Skills/Abilities:** Strong flight and superb swimming/diving; water-breathing and seapony transformation; excellent agility in both air and sea; a natural talent for making friends across species and for mediating between groups; surprisingly brave under pressure — in a Season 9 episode she wanders into the Everfree Forest and returns having tamed a cockatrice she names Edith.
**Family & Relationships:** Daughter of Sky Beak (land hippogriff) and Ocean Flow (seapony), who live apart; younger brother Terramar; niece of Queen Novo (her mother's sister) and cousin of Princess Skystar; close friends with Sandbar, Gallus, Yona, Ocellus, and Smolder (the Young Six); student of Twilight Sparkle; her family's split between Mount Aris and Seaquestria is the central drama of "Surf and/or Turf."
**Background/Biography:** Silverstream grew up between two worlds — her father's home on Mount Aris and her mother's home in Seaquestria — as the hippogriffs rebuilt after the Storm King's reign. In "School Daze" (S8E1–2) she and five other non-pony students enrolled in Twilight's new School of Friendship; she was initially skeptical of "friendship lessons" but quickly became one of the school's most enthusiastic students. In "Surf and/or Turf" she and her brother Terramar tried to resolve their family's land-versus-sea split, and the episode ends with the siblings and their communities beginning to reconnect. She studied, sang, and celebrated with the Young Six through Season 8 (including the Hearth's Warming club), faced the Tree of Harmony's trials in "What Lies Beneath," remained at the school after Cozy Glow's takeover in "School Raze," and helped save the school and Equestria. In Season 9 she and the Young Six helped defend Equestria in "The Ending of the End," and "The Last Problem" shows her as a grown hippogriff in the future.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Fast, breathless, exclamatory; squeals of delight, "Oh my gosh!", and wide-eyed questions; she takes land-pony idioms and customs literally and repeats expressions she has just learned; warm and inclusive, calling everyone "friend," and her voice climbs when she's excited.
**Notable Quotes:** "Oh my gosh!" (her most frequent exclamation); "This is the coolest thing I've ever seen!" [paraphrased]; "I've never seen anything like it! I love it here!" [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Lauren Jackson; first appearance "School Daze, Part 1" (S8E1). Nicole Dubuc confirmed Silverstream is Queen Novo's sister's daughter, making her Skystar's cousin; her name is sometimes written "Silver Stream" in merchandise but "Silverstream" is the canonical spelling. She is one of the show's most popular non-pony characters, and her seapony transformation — a holdover from the 2017 movie's hippogriff/seapony lore — is one of the Young Six's most distinctive designs.

## Sugar Belle

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — a unicorn introduced in *Friendship is Magic* Season 5 who becomes a recurring supporting character and marries Big McIntosh; she has no Equestria Girls counterpart.
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare.
**Residence:** Our Town (Starlight Glimmer's village) originally; Ponyville from Season 8 while apprenticing at the Cakes' bakery; Sweet Apple Acres after her marriage.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Baker and pastry chef — she runs the bakery in Our Town and later becomes Mrs. Cake's apprentice in Ponyville; former resident of the equality village; wife of Big McIntosh and member of the Apple family.
**Appearance:** A soft pink/light-cherry unicorn mare with a curly medium-magenta mane and tail (the mane falls in springy ringlets), medium-cerise eyes, and a cutie mark of a cupcake topped with a cherry and four jelly beans — a perfect emblem of her baking talent. While her mark was stripped in Our Town, her coat and mane faded to gray and her flank bore an equal-sign mark instead. She usually wears a simple baker's apron or dress, and at her wedding she wears a modest white-and-green gown; in the future epilogue she is shown as a contented farm wife and mother.
**Personality:** Sweet, warm, hardworking, and practical, with a quiet stubborn streak. Sugar Belle is modest about her skills but deeply dedicated to her craft; she speaks honestly, values fairness and equality, and does not suffer fools when pushed (she once threw both of her suitors out of her bakery). She is romantic and sentimental, forgiving of past mistakes, and quietly ambitious about her baking.
**Likes:** Baking and inventing new recipes, apples and apple-based desserts, honesty and fairness, Big Macintosh, her friends from Our Town (Double Diamond, Party Favor, Night Glider, and Starlight), her bakery, and small-town life.
**Dislikes:** Lying and manipulation (Starlight's deception was a deep betrayal), being crowded or pestered by suitors, bad baking, being overlooked, and anyone who treats her talent as trivial.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Being deceived or controlled again, as she was in Our Town; losing her cutie mark/talent; loneliness; she can jump to conclusions (as in the "break up" misunderstanding) and has a sharp temper when pushed too far.
**Skills/Abilities:** Master baker — her cutie-mark talent produces exceptional pies, muffins, cookies, and cakes, especially apple desserts; unicorn telekinesis; business management from running her own shop; quick learning (she excels as Mrs. Cake's apprentice); she even weaponized her baking once, crafting and hurling "snow pies" at Starlight during the revolt in "The Cutie Map."
**Family & Relationships:** Married to Big McIntosh; mother of an unnamed son in the future epilogue (called "Big Sugar" in supplementary material); sister-in-law of Applejack and Apple Bloom; granddaughter-in-law of Granny Smith; daughter-in-law of the late Bright Mac and Pear Butter; has an unnamed mischievous cousin and unnamed parents; friends with Starlight Glimmer, Double Diamond, Party Favor, Night Glider, and the Cakes.
**Background/Biography:** Sugar Belle first appears in "The Cutie Map" (S5E1–2) as the baker of Starlight Glimmer's Our Town, where everypony's cutie mark has been removed; her baking is so bad that Pinkie Pie gags on it. Curious about the Mane Six's friendship, she and two other villagers secretly ask them about it, which helps expose Starlight's lie about giving up her own mark. Sugar Belle reclaims her mark, rejoins the revolt, and uses her restored talent to bake snow pies to throw at Starlight; she stays in Our Town to bake properly. She reappears celebrating with the villagers and as a background guest at Twilight's events. In "Hard to Say Anything" (S7E8) she is courted by both Big McIntosh and Feather Bangs; their escalating rivalry destroys her bakery, and she throws them both out before Big Mac's quieter, genuine acts of repair win her heart. After "The Break Up Break Down" (S8E10) clears up a misunderstanding (she was talking about her cousin, not breaking up with Big Mac), she begins working as Mrs. Cake's apprentice in Ponyville. In "The Big Mac Question" (S9E23) she and Big Mac propose to each other and are married under the apple-pear tree planted by his parents; "The Last Problem" shows her future at Sweet Apple Acres with Big Mac and their son.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Warm, polite, and slightly formal; gently exasperated when annoyed, at which point her sentences get clipped and firm; she uses baking metaphors and sincere declarations; she calls Big Mac "Big Mac" and speaks to him with affectionate teasing.
**Notable Quotes:** "Is your friendship ending?"; "My name is Sugar Belle. What can I bring you? We have: muffins…"; "You can't have a cutie mark, Starlight! Either we're all equal, or none of us are!"; "I like you, too."; "This apple tree and pear tree are stronger together… They belong together. Like your parents. And like us."
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Rebecca Shoichet; first appearance "The Cutie Map, Part 1" (S5E1). Her name is shared with a G2 earth pony from the vintage line; her cutie mark is a cherry-topped cupcake with four jelly beans. She is the only former Our Town villager to marry into the Apple family, and her quiet, self-made romance with Big Mac is a fan-favorite pairing. In the future epilogue her son is shown but never named in dialogue.

## Lord Tirek

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — the main villain of *Friendship is Magic* Season 4 and one of the three final-season villains; a reimagining of the classic G1 centaur villain of the same name.
**Species/Kind:** Centaur (humanoid upper body on a horse's lower body).
**Gender:** Male (he/him).
**Age/Stage:** Ancient — a centuries-old sorcerer who was imprisoned in Tartarus for over a thousand years before the events of Season 4.
**Residence:** Tartarus (imprisoned), later Grogar's lair in Season 9; formerly a distant kingdom (per the IDW comics).
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Power-hungry sorcerer and tyrant who consumes others' magic; prince of the centaurs (comics); member of Grogar's Legion of Doom alongside Queen Chrysalis and Cozy Glow; former ally of Discord.
**Appearance:** A towering centaur with dark red-maroon skin on his humanoid torso, a dark horse's lower body with hooves, long black hair and beard, curved black bull-like horns, and yellow eyes that glow when he is empowered; his proportions grow monstrously as he absorbs magic — during "Twilight's Kingdom" he becomes a giant with burning eyes and vastly increased muscle and horn length. When drained of magic he shrinks into a frail, withered old centaur. In the G1 series the character was a demonic centaur who also stole magic and was served by Scorpan.
**Personality:** Cruel, arrogant, cunning, and theatrically menacing; Tirek is a schemer who treats other beings as tools and discards them when they are no longer useful (as demonstrated with Discord). He respects only power, despises friendship and kindness as weaknesses, and enjoys taunting and humiliating his enemies. He is intelligent and patient, but also prideful and petty, and his arrogance repeatedly leads him to underestimate Twilight and her friends. In Season 9 he is the calculating, cautious member of the villain trio, often clashing with Chrysalis's theatrics and Cozy Glow's childishness.
**Likes:** Consuming magic, power and dominance, being feared, elaborate schemes, revenge, the sound of his own voice, and seeing Equestria's protectors brought low.
**Dislikes:** Imprisonment and powerlessness, ponies, friendship and kindness, Discord's flippancy, being ordered around (especially by Grogar), losing battles, and sharing credit with his allies.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Being imprisoned or stripped of magic again; his own arrogance and impatience; he fundamentally cannot absorb the magic of friendship or the Rainbow Power, which is what defeats him; his plans require stolen power and collapse when that power is taken away.
**Skills/Abilities:** The unique ability to drain magic from any creature — ponies, alicorns, and even Discord — through his horns/hands, transferring it to himself and growing stronger and larger; stolen magic can then be used for blasts, telekinesis, flight, and other spells; immense physical strength; brilliant scheming and manipulation; command of monsters. Limitations: powerless when drained, and unable to comprehend or wield friendship magic.
**Family & Relationships:** Brother of Scorpan, the noble-minded prince who came to Equestria with him and ultimately opposed him; per the IDW comics, the son of King Vorak and Queen Haydon [uncertain: comics-only details]; enemies of Princess Celestia, Twilight Sparkle, and all of Equestria; uneasy co-conspirator of Queen Chrysalis and Cozy Glow; briefly the dupe of Discord.
**Background/Biography:** Long ago Tirek and his brother Scorpan traveled to Equestria to steal its magic; Scorpan was won over by the ponies and warned them, and the princesses imprisoned Tirek in Tartarus. In "Twilight's Kingdom" (S4E25–26) his imprisonment was broken when Cerberus abandoned his post; Tirek escaped, drained the magic of ponies across Equestria, and struck a bargain with Discord, who betrayed the princesses for him — only for Tirek to drain Discord's magic too and devastate Equestria. Twilight, entrusted with the power of all four princesses, fought him to a standstill; when Tirek gave up the magic he had stolen to save himself, the chest containing the Rainbow Power transformed Twilight and her friends, and together they defeated him and returned the magic. He was re-imprisoned in Tartarus. In Season 9 Grogar freed and recruited him into the Legion of Doom; the trio betrayed Grogar (secretly Discord), seized the Bewitching Bell's power, and nearly conquered Equestria in "The Ending of the End" before the princesses and the united ponies turned them all to stone.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Deep, rumbling, grandiose baritone; slow and mocking; calls ponies "little ponies" with contempt; loves dramatic declarations and cruel rhetorical questions; occasionally roars with fury when thwarted, and shifts to wheedling when powerless.
**Notable Quotes:** "I am Tirek, and I have come for your magic." [paraphrased]; "Your magic is mine, little pony!" [paraphrased]; "You think friendship can save you? How pathetic." [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Mark Acheson; first appearance "Twilight's Kingdom, Part 1" (S4E25). The G4 Tirek is a reimagining of the G1 villain who appeared in the 1984 special "Rescue at Midnight Castle," and his G4 backstory with Scorpan is explored further in the IDW comics. He is one of the few villains to appear as a final boss in two separate arcs (Season 4 and Season 9), and his petrified form alongside Chrysalis and Cozy Glow is the final fate of the show's last great villains.

## Queen Chrysalis

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — the changeling queen and one of *Friendship is Magic*'s most enduring villains; she appears in Seasons 2, 6, and 9 and in IDW comics, and has no human counterpart in the Equestria Girls films.
**Species/Kind:** Changeling — specifically a changeling queen (an insectoid, shapeshifting pony-like creature that feeds on love).
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult, long-lived queen; exact age is [not established in canon].
**Residence:** The Changeling Hive in the Badlands/desert; after her hive abandoned her she wandered Equestria in hiding; imprisoned in Tartarus in Season 9; finally petrified.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Former Queen of the Changelings and ruler of her hive; deposed by Thorax's transformation; member of Grogar's Legion of Doom with Tirek and Cozy Glow; villain.
**Appearance:** A tall, slender changeling with a glossy black-gray carapace, long hole-punched legs and a perforated dark-teal mane, a jagged black horn, translucent insectoid wings, teal-green eyes with slit pupils, fangs, and a spiky dark crown. Her magic glows sickly green, and her voice and eyes gleam when she is gloating. She once perfectly impersonated Princess Cadance — pink alicorn form and all — for most of a wedding. After her hive's defection she appears wilder and more ragged, and in "The Ending of the End" the Bewitching Bell's power transforms her into a massive, armored giant. As changelings go she is the largest and most regal, and the only one ever shown wearing a crown.
**Personality:** Manipulative, narcissistic, vengeful, and theatrical. Chrysalis is a master of long cons and grand reveals, and she genuinely believes she is owed power, adoration, and loyalty; she cannot accept that her changelings preferred Thorax's way of sharing love, and she refuses to change or apologize. Her pride is her defining trait — she would rather lose everything than admit fault — and she hides her loneliness and humiliation behind rage and scheming. She is charismatic, imperious, and surprisingly patient, but her obsession with revenge makes her predictable to those who know her.
**Likes:** Power and control, her hive (as possessions more than subjects), revenge on Starlight Glimmer, Twilight Sparkle, and the Mane Six, adoration and fear, elaborate schemes, gloating, and watching her enemies fall.
**Dislikes:** Thorax and the reformed changelings, Starlight Glimmer, Twilight and her friends, being pitied or dismissed, sharing power (she bickers constantly with Tirek and Cozy Glow), taking orders from Grogar, and love-based magic.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Being powerless, rejected, or irrelevant; losing her hive and her identity as queen; her pride prevents her from forming real alliances or reforming; her power depends on feeding on love, which the reformed changelings no longer need in the old way; and she is vulnerable to the united magic of friendship and to being out-planned.
**Skills/Abilities:** Perfect shapeshifting and impersonation; feeding on love and devotion; powerful green magic blasts; flight; mind and emotion manipulation (she controlled and drained Shining Armor's love); cocooning and imprisoning victims; telepathy with her changelings and control of her swarm; later, the Bewitching Bell's stolen power grants her giant form and enormous destructive magic. Limitations: she is physically frail compared to Tirek and relies on deception, and her hive's loyalty — once her greatest asset — turned against her.
**Family & Relationships:** Her changelings are her hive and "children" (including Thorax, Pharynx, and young Ocellus, who joined the School of Friendship); no canon blood relatives are shown on screen; enemies include Twilight Sparkle, Princess Cadance, Shining Armor, Starlight Glimmer, Thorax, and the Mane Six; her only "allies" are the untrustworthy Tirek and Cozy Glow, and the manipulative Grogar/Discord.
**Background/Biography:** Queen Chrysalis first appeared in "A Canterlot Wedding" (S2E25–26), having kidnapped Princess Cadance and taken her place to marry Shining Armor — framing Twilight when she worked it out. Her plan was to weaken Equestria's defenses with the wedding and the changelings' feeding, but Twilight exposed her, and Cadance and Shining Armor's love-powered magic blasted the changelings out of Canterlot. In "To Where and Back Again" (S6E25–26) she returned with a starving hive, captured Equestria's leaders and the Mane Six, and replaced them with changelings; Starlight Glimmer, Trixie, Discord, and Thorax rescued the captives and confronted her. Thorax — changed by his friendship with Spike — shared love openly, and the changelings transformed into beautiful, colorful creatures who abandoned their queen; defeated and humiliated, Chrysalis fled, vowing revenge. In Season 9 Grogar recruited her into his Legion of Doom with Tirek and Cozy Glow; the trio betrayed him, stole the Bewitching Bell's power, and nearly conquered Equestria in "The Ending of the End." She and her co-conspirators were finally turned to stone by the princesses and the combined magic of Equestria.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Silky, venomous, imperious; honeyed mock-affection when manipulating, pure contempt when enraged; grand villain monologues, hissing laughter, and dramatic reveals ("Did you really think…?"); she addresses ponies as "my little ponies" or "darlings" sarcastically and screams when defied.
**Notable Quotes:** "You poor, gullible little ponies — you never even suspected." [paraphrased]; "I am Queen Chrysalis! I don't need anypony!" [paraphrased]; "The changelings are mine — and soon, so is Equestria!" [paraphrased].
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Kathleen Barr; first appearance "A Canterlot Wedding, Part 1" (S2E25). She is the show's most frequently recurring major villain and the only one to appear in three separate season arcs (S2, S6, S9); IDW's *FIENDship is Magic* series explores her backstory [uncertain: comics-only details]. Her design was widely praised as one of the show's creepiest, and "Chrysalis is a badass" is a long-running fandom sentiment; her final on-screen fate is being petrified alongside Tirek and Cozy Glow.
