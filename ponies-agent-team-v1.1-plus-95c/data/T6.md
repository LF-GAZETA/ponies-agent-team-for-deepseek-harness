## Sunburst

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — *My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic* (Seasons 5–9). No Equestria Girls counterpart; he appears only in the pony world.
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn stallion; briefly shown in crystalline form as a crystal pony during the Crystal Empire's crystalling crisis.
**Gender:** Male (he/him).
**Age/Stage:** Adult stallion for most of his appearances; shown as a colt in Season 5–6 flashbacks. In the series epilogue he is shown somewhat older but still at his post.
**Residence:** The Crystal Empire (the royal crystaller's residence); originally from Sire's Hollow. After Season 9 he spends most of his time in Ponyville at the School of Friendship.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Royal Crystaller to Princess Flurry Heart and magical adviser to the Crystal Empire (from *The Crystalling*); Vice Principal of Twilight Sparkle's School of Friendship from *A Horse Shoe-In* (S9E20); former student of Princess Celestia's School for Gifted Unicorns; respected magical theorist; devoted scholar and collector of ancient relics.
**Appearance:** A slender, long-legged unicorn with a bright orange-yellow coat dusted with pale gray freckles. His mane and tail are vivid red and bright vermilion with lighter streaks, worn swept back with a small forelock and a short tuft at the tail. His eyes are a soft gradient from dark periwinkle blue at the top to cyan at the bottom. His cutie mark is an orange sun emitting yellow rays, accompanied by six small blue stars. He usually wears the traditional crystaller's garb — a deep blue cloak or robe with gold trim and a high collar — and neat formal collars or vests for school duties; in *The Crystalling* he is briefly transformed into a translucent crystal pony. In the epilogue song he appears older, with a longer, slightly shaggier mane.
**Personality:** Gentle, bookish, polite, and endlessly enthusiastic about knowledge. Sunburst is the classic anxious scholar: he is delighted by ancient relics, historical minutiae, and obscure magical theory, and he can ramble happily for hours about a single footnote. He is warm and loyal, especially to Starlight, but desperately self-conscious about his weak practical magic — he knows how spells work far better than he can cast them. When embarrassed he hides behind grand titles, badly pretending to be a "great wizard." He is patient and kind with foals, quietly stubborn, and has a surprising competitive streak in trivia and games. He resents being managed, above all by his mother.
**Likes:** Magical theory and research, ancient artifacts and antiques, close-up and stage magic, Star Swirl the Bearded, board games (especially the giant tabletop game *Dragon Pit*), libraries and rereading, Flurry Heart, quiet evenings with friends, being asked for an explanation.
**Dislikes:** Being put on the spot, having his poor spellcasting exposed, his mother Stellar Flare planning his life without asking, cheating (though he is tempted when his record is at stake), loud chaos, being underestimated.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Being exposed as a fraud — someone who "knows so much and can't do any of it"; disappointing the ponies who rely on him; his mother's expectations; his own weak, unreliable spellcasting and poor physical stamina. He can be gullible, socially awkward, and too eager to prove himself.
**Skills/Abilities:** Encyclopedic magical knowledge and research skill comparable to Twilight's; crystal lore and maintenance of the Crystal Heart; deciphering ancient texts and Star Swirl's journals; history and archaeology; board-game strategy; practiced sleight of hand and close-up magic. His practical spellcasting is notably weak: he can levitate simple objects but often fumbles complex spells, so he solves problems with knowledge, preparation, and teamwork rather than raw power.
**Family & Relationships:** Starlight Glimmer — his foalhood best friend, with whom he reconnects after years apart; Stellar Flare — his overbearing, ambitious mother; Sunspot — his father (named in supplementary material); Flurry Heart — his royal charge, whom he adores; Princess Cadance and Shining Armor — his employers and friends; Twilight Sparkle — colleague and fellow scholar; Trixie, Maud Pie, and Twilight — his fellow hobby-sharers in *Uncommon Bond*.
**Background/Biography:** Sunburst grew up in Sire's Hollow as Starlight Glimmer's best friend. As a colt he saved her from a collapsing stack of books with a levitation spell, earning his cutie mark on the spot; his proud parents then sent him to Princess Celestia's School for Gifted Unicorns, and the two friends lost touch, planting Starlight's lifelong fear that cutie marks destroy friendship. Starlight recalls him in *The Cutie Re-Mark*; the two finally reunite in *The Crystalling* (Season 6), when Starlight travels to the Crystal Empire. Awkward and boasting that he is a great wizard, Sunburst confesses he knows magic but cannot reliably perform it — then proves his worth by using his knowledge to help repair the Crystal Heart and complete Flurry Heart's crystalling, earning the post of royal crystaller and magical adviser. In *Uncommon Bond* (Season 7) he visits Ponyville, discovers shared hobbies with Twilight, Trixie, and Maud, and nearly loses Starlight's friendship before arranging a giant *Dragon Pit* game for her. In the Season 7 finale *Shadow Play* he receives Star Swirl's lost journal, deduces the truth about Stygian, and helps the Mane Six release the Pillars of Old Equestria and redeem the Pony of Shadows. In *The Parent Map* (Season 8) he is called home by the Cutie Map and finally confronts his mother's pressure. In Season 9 he competes in a Ponyville trivia contest, then is recommended by Trixie and hired as Vice Principal of the School of Friendship in *A Horse Shoe-In*; since Flurry Heart no longer needs a full-time crystaller, he happily returns to the school life he always wanted, fights alongside the heroes in the finale, and remains vice principal in the epilogue.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Earnest, polite, slightly formal and nerdish; stammers when flustered ("S-Starlight!"); tends to slip into technical magical jargon and lecture mode; says "if I may" and "theoretically" a lot; brightens audibly when artifacts or history come up; tends to overexplain and then apologize for it.
**Notable Quotes:** "S-Starlight! My goodness, it-it's been a long time!" — *The Crystalling, Part 1*. "No rest for the wizardly." — *The Crystalling, Part 2*. "I'm not an important wizard! I'm not even a wizard at all!" — *The Crystalling, Part 2*. "Reading about magic is one thing, but you don't know what it was like at magic school! To know so much and not be able to do any of it!" — *The Crystalling, Part 2*. "Historical knickknacks, ancient relics... Oh! You are so lucky! Ponyville is antique central!" — *Uncommon Bond*. "Solving a thousands-year-old mystery could take forever! Think of the research! The re-reading! The re-re-reading!" — *Shadow Play, Part 1*. "Well, honestly, now that Flurry Heart's a little older... And working at a school is what I always thought I'd do. I mean, if you'll have me." — *A Horse Shoe-In*.
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Ian Hanlin in English. First appears as a colt in Starlight's flashback in *The Cutie Re-Mark* (S5E26) and makes his adult debut in *The Crystalling* (S6E1–2). He shares his name with a G1 Mountain Boy pony. His cutie-mark design (sun with six blue stars) reflects the "starburst" magic of his levitation feat. His mother, Stellar Flare, and hometown, Sire's Hollow, are canon; his father "Sunspot" is documented only in supplementary material [uncertain]. In the Chinese dub he is called 隙日. His exact special talent is described only as magical theory and knowledge — the show never names a formal "magic of sunbursts." Trixie is the one who recommends him for the vice-principal job.

## Pipp Petals

**Series/Continuity:** G5 — *My Little Pony: A New Generation* (2021 film), *Make Your Mark*, and *Tell Your Tale*. No Equestria Girls counterpart (G5 has no EG line).
**Species/Kind:** Pegasus pony; a princess of Zephyr Heights.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Young adult mare; she is the younger twin sister of Zipp Storm.
**Residence:** Zephyr Heights (royal palace) at the start of *A New Generation*; afterward the Crystal Brighthouse in Maretime Bay. She also runs Mane Melody, her salon/music venue in Maretime Bay.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Princess of Zephyr Heights; pop star and social-media influencer known as the "Pop Star Princess"; livestreamer and gamer; owner and star of Mane Melody; one of the Mane 5; associated with the quality of "Confidence" in G5 tie-in framing.
**Appearance:** A slim, stylish pegasus with a pink coat and pale pink gradient "socks" on her lower legs; a purple mane (darker purple with lighter stripes in *Tell Your Tale*) worn in a long side ponytail with a curled end, often topped by her gold crown; green eyes; gold hooves; white-and-pink feathered wings; and a cutie mark of a purple and white music note beneath a small yellow crown. She wears her crown, gold jewelry, and hoof-polish like a celebrity; her phone and microphone are near-permanent props. In *A New Generation* the Zephyr Heights royals secretly fake flight with wires and lighting, and Pipp performs aerial "hovers" that are really rigged.
**Personality:** Bubbly, theatrical, and chronically online: Pipp livestreams first and thinks second, and treats every event as content. She is genuinely warm and generous with fans, fiercely loves her family, and is braver than she looks, but she is also vain, self-centered, squeamish about danger and dirt, and desperate for approval. Her character arc moves from shallow celebrity to a real hero who learns that her voice matters most when she uses it for others. She bickers constantly with Zipp but adores her, and she is a doting "auntie" to Sparky.
**Likes:** Singing and performing, livestreaming, her followers ("Pippsqueaks"), video games, fashion, makeup and hairstyling, selfies, crowns and shiny things, praise, pillow forts, Sparky, gossip, her phone.
**Dislikes:** Losing followers or being offline, being ignored, danger, dirt and bugs, early mornings, being outshone or treated like a foal, boring royal duties, her sister's blunt honesty.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Losing her fame and being forgotten; physical danger and getting messy (she panics and hides); failing publicly; letting her mother down. She is impulsive, self-absorbed, easily distracted by her phone, and prone to complaining under pressure.
**Skills/Abilities:** Excellent singer and performer with strong stage presence; skilled dancer and poser; capable flyer once magic returns (and a competent wire-work "flyer" before that); gifted at hairstyling, makeup, and fashion; expert streamer, gamer, and social-media strategist; charismatic negotiator and hype-woman. She is not a fighter and relies on others in combat.
**Family & Relationships:** Queen Haven — her mother, whom she loves and often worries about; Zipp Storm — her older twin sister, her foil and best friend; the rest of the Mane 5 (Sunny Starscout, Izzy Moonbow, Hitch Trailblazer) — her found family; Sparky Sparkeroni — Hitch's adopted baby dragon, whom she spoils; Jazz Hooves and Rocky Riff — her Mane Melody co-workers.
**Background/Biography:** Pipp grew up in Zephyr Heights as the younger twin of Princess Zipp, living the royal family's secret lie that pegasi can still fly, and building a huge following as a pop star. In *A New Generation* she livestreams the capture of Sunny and Izzy for content, then joins them — partly to restore her family's reputation after Queen Haven is arrested — on the journey to bring magic back to Equestria. She is tangled upside-down in the gala's wire rig, survives Deputy Sprout's mecha attack on Sunny's lighthouse, and is overjoyed when magic returns and she truly flies for the first time. She moves into the Crystal Brighthouse and, in *Make Your Mark* and *Tell Your Tale*, opens Mane Melody, builds her brand as royalty-with-a-mic, and repeatedly helps the Mane 5 against Opaline Arcana and later threats, while learning to be a real friend rather than just a celebrity. She remains a pop icon but becomes one of the group's emotional cheerleaders.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Peppy, slangy influencer-speak full of "literally," "hashtag," and over-the-top superlatives; addresses her fans as "Pippsqueaks"; squeals and gasps dramatically; narrates her own life like a livestream; switches to a soft, sincere voice in emotional moments.
**Notable Quotes:** "I'm a stylish pop star princess, my voice makes me strong. When my Cutie Mark comes to life, confidence is my song!" — official G5 character verse/toy packaging. "[paraphrased] Oh my gosh, this is literally the most exciting thing that has ever happened — everypony start the stream!" (Pipp's reaction to drama, *A New Generation*). "[paraphrased] I'm a princess! I have a reputation to think about!" (Pipp protesting a plan that might embarrass her, *Make Your Mark*).
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by AJ Bridel in English. First appeared in *A New Generation* (2021). Merchandise sometimes calls her "Princess Petals." Her cutie mark is a purple-and-white music note with a yellow crown. Her fans are the "Pippsqueaks." She is the younger twin — Zipp is the elder by minutes, though the show plays their ages loosely. Her "element" of Confidence comes from G5 tie-in/marketing material and is not spelled out in dialogue [uncertain].

## Spitfire

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — *My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic*. She has a human-world counterpart depicted in Equestria Girls-related/IDW material (a sporty Canterlot High-style human form) and appears in EG-era art, but she has no major EG story role.
**Species/Kind:** Pegasus pony.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Adult mare.
**Residence:** Cloudsdale; often on tour or stationed at the Wonderbolts Academy and Wonderbolts headquarters.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Captain of the Wonderbolts, Equestria's elite aerial acrobatics squad; drill instructor at the Wonderbolts Academy; official in charge of Cloudsdale's water transfers and wind-speed records; member of Cloudsdale's Equestria Games relay team.
**Appearance:** A fit, athletic pegasus with a bright golden-yellow coat; a vivid orange mane and tail streaked with bright amber, with a swept-back, windswept cut and a short, spiky tail; dark orange eyes; and a cutie mark showing the stylized profile of a phoenix in flight. In uniform she wears the Wonderbolts' blue-and-yellow flight suit with a winged lightning emblem, and as drill instructor a military-style instructor's jacket and cap. Her human counterpart has the same golden skin, orange streaked hair, and sporty build.
**Personality:** Tough, demanding, commanding, and sarcastic. Spitfire runs the Wonderbolts like a drill sergeant: she respects effort and discipline, punishes excuses, and rarely hands out praise, which makes what little praise she gives devastatingly meaningful. She is fiercely proud, competitive, and a workaholic, and underneath the bark she is fair, protective of her team, and quietly caring. Her flaw is winning at all costs — she has bent the rules and played favorites when a victory was at stake — but she is capable of admitting a mistake and making amends.
**Likes:** Flying fast and performing; competition and records; discipline and hard work; her team (Soarin, Fleetfoot, Misty Fly, and the rest); winning; order and efficiency; respectful cadets who take flying seriously.
**Dislikes:** Excuses, laziness, and "winging it"; showboating that endangers teammates; losing or being embarrassed; cadets who question her authority; sentimentality in public.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Letting the Wonderbolts' reputation down; an injury or scandal damaging the team; being seen as weak. She can be harsh, ruthless, and rule-bending, and she struggles to express affection or admit fault.
**Skills/Abilities:** One of Equestria's finest pegasus flyers — extreme speed, precision formation flying, and acrobatic maneuvers; natural leader and trainer who can drill a whole squadron; experienced judge and talent scout; weather-work expertise (water transfer oversight, wind-speed measurement); strong stamina and physical conditioning. Not a magic user; vulnerable to anti-magic and magic-draining.
**Family & Relationships:** No blood relatives are established in the show proper [uncertain]; some expanded-universe material links her to a pegasus mother named Firefly. Her closest ties are her Wonderbolts — especially Soarin and Fleetfoot — and she becomes a grudging mentor to Rainbow Dash, first as a competition judge, then as academy commandant, and eventually as a teammate.
**Background/Biography:** Spitfire debuts in *Sonic Rainboom* (S1E16) as the unnamed captain of the Wonderbolts judging the Best Young Flyers competition; her name is first spoken in *The Best Night Ever* (S1E26). In *Hurricane Fluttershy* she oversees Cloudsdale's water transfer to Ponyville and is impressed by Rainbow Dash's speed. In *Wonderbolts Academy* (S3E7) she is the academy's drill sergeant, initially impressed by Lightning Dust's recklessness until Rainbow Dash forces her to see the danger. In *Rainbow Falls* (S4E10) she recruits Rainbow Dash to replace an injured Soarin for the Equestria Games relay, bending the selection rules before admitting her error. In *Newbie Dash* (S6E7) she inducts Rainbow Dash into the Wonderbolts and gives her the teasing nickname "Rainbow Crash." In *Parental Glideance* and *Rarity Investigates!* she is a demanding boss with a hidden softer side, and she leads the Wonderbolts through the final seasons, including the battle with the Legion of Doom in the series finale.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Clipped, brisk, military cadence; barks orders and addresses groups as "cadets" or "Wonderbolts"; heavy sarcasm and dry one-liners; never wastes words on praise; says "That's an order" and "No excuses"; softens only in rare private moments.
**Notable Quotes:** "[paraphrased] Welcome to the Wonderbolts — the best flyers in Equestria, and don't you forget it." (drill-sergeant welcome, *Wonderbolts Academy*). "[paraphrased] I'm the captain. I decide who flies for this team." (Spitfire asserting her authority, *Rainbow Falls*). "[paraphrased] There are no shortcuts to being a Wonderbolt." (her lesson to Rainbow Dash, *Wonderbolts Academy*).
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Kelly Metzger from *The Best Night Ever* (S1E26) onward, with a very early, unnamed appearance voiced by Nicole Oliver in *Sonic Rainboom* (S1E16). Her name is a reference to the Supermarine Spitfire, the famous WWII fighter plane, and "spitfire" means a fiery-tempered person — an apt name. Her cutie mark is a stylized phoenix profile. Chinese name: 飞火. Her exact age and family are never stated on screen [uncertain]; the "mother Firefly" link is expanded-universe only.

## Mayor Mare

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — *My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic*. No established Equestria Girls counterpart; she appears in the pony world and in IDW comics.
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Middle-aged to older adult mare.
**Residence:** Ponyville; briefly Canterlot during her student days (in the IDW comic *Neigh Anything*).
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Mayor of Ponyville — its chief civic leader, master of ceremonies, and speech-giver; briefly retired in IDW comic issue #47 before returning to office. As a younger pony she was a student at a Canterlot academy (in *Neigh Anything*).
**Appearance:** An earth pony with a light gray-amber coat; eyes a medium indigo blue; a mane and tail she wears in a neat, gray, periwinkle-tinted style with lighter streaks — which is dyed. Her natural mane, revealed in *Ponyville Confidential*, is light gray with a soft cherry-pink streak, and a younger, undyed version of her appears in a Season 7 flashback. Her cutie mark is a brown scroll tied with a blue ribbon, reflecting her life in civic paperwork and proclamations. She usually wears a starched collar and a formal sash or ribbon at events, and her Nightmare Night costume as a clown is memorable.
**Personality:** Pompous, long-winded, and deeply fond of ceremony. Mayor Mare lives for ribbon-cuttings and podium microphones, delivers rambling speeches that never quite end, and cares a great deal about her public image — which is why she dyes her mane and panics over scandal. Underneath the bluster she is a genuinely dedicated public servant: she organizes Ponyville's festivals, organizes disaster responses, and loves her town and its ponies. She can be vain, status-conscious, and a little ridiculous, but she is never cruel.
**Likes:** Speeches and podiums, ribbon-cutting ceremonies, Ponyville and its traditions, formal events, being in charge, pageantry, tea with dignitaries, public approval.
**Dislikes:** Scandals and exposés (especially the *Gabby Gums* column), being upstaged or ignored, chaos and monster attacks disrupting civic order, being reminded of her age, ponies who don't follow event protocol.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Losing her dignity or her office; embarrassment in the press; being seen as old or fake (she hides her natural mane color); public speaking is her comfort zone, but genuine crises reveal she is an administrator, not a fighter.
**Skills/Abilities:** Skilled administrator and event organizer; formidable public speaker with impressive rhetorical stamina; political negotiation and civic leadership; knows Ponyville's laws, records, and history intimately; experienced at coordinating emergency responses and community projects. No combat or magic ability.
**Family & Relationships:** No family members are established in canon [not established in canon]; she is defined by her civic relationships — the Mane Six (especially Twilight, whom she addresses as a colleague-in-leadership), her constituents, and the town's various committees. In *Neigh Anything* she has a youthful romance subplot as a Canterlot student.
**Background/Biography:** Mayor Mare first appears in the series premiere *Friendship is Magic, Part 1*, presiding over the Summer Sun Celebration in Ponyville, and she has been the town's mayor ever since — a constant through nearly every civic event in the series. She announces the Running of the Leaves, Winter Wrap Up, the Grand Galloping Gala invitations, and countless festivals. In *Ponyville Confidential* (S2E23) the Cutie Mark Crusaders' gossip column exposes her secret: her gray mane is dyed, and her natural hair is pink. In *Nightmare Night* she dresses as a clown, and during the Nightmare Moon and Discord crises she is part of the town's leadership. She appears briefly in IDW's *Neigh Anything*, where a young Mayor Mare falls for a stallion at a Canterlot academy, and in comic issue #47 she temporarily retires and is replaced before being reinstated. She survives the events of the Season 9 finale and continues to lead Ponyville.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Formal, ornate, and enormously long-winded; speaks in proclamation cadence with throat-clearing preamble ("*Ahem!* Attention, everypony..."); loves phrases like "as your duly elected mayor" and "in accordance with tradition"; tends to say "once again" and "on behalf of the town of Ponyville"; can be flustered into squeaky indignation.
**Notable Quotes:** "[paraphrased] As your mayor, it is my great honor to officially declare this event — *ahem* — officially open." (ceremonial speech pattern, multiple episodes). "[paraphrased] This is a disaster! A scandal! Ponyville will never live this down!" (Mayor Mare's reaction to the *Gabby Gums* exposure, *Ponyville Confidential*). "[paraphrased] The show must go on — the town is counting on me." (Mayor Mare rallying during a crisis, various episodes).
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Cathy Weseluck in English (best known as the voice of Spike). First appeared in *Friendship is Magic, Part 1* (S1E1). "Mayor Mare" is effectively a title/pun name — a mare who is the mayor — and no separate legal first name is established [not established in canon]. Her mane-dyeing secret is one of the show's most famous long-running gags. She is briefly shown as a young, undyed-maned student in Season 7 material and in the IDW comic *Neigh Anything*; in comic #47 she has a short retirement. Her family and cutie-mark story are never explored.

## Princess Luna (Nightmare Moon)

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls. Pony-world Princess Luna is an alicorn co-ruler of Equestria; her Equestria Girls counterpart is Vice Principal Luna of Canterlot High, a human.
**Species/Kind:** Alicorn (unicorn horn and pegasus wings); Nightmare Moon is the same pony transformed by dark magic — a shadowed alicorn.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Ancient and ageless — over a thousand years old; an eternal young-adult appearance. Appears as a young alicorn in flashbacks and comics.
**Residence:** Canterlot Castle (co-ruler's wing); formerly the old royal sisters' castle in the Everfree; exiled on the moon for 1,000 years; after the Season 9 finale she retires with Celestia to a seaside estate near Silver Shoals.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Co-ruler of Equestria and Princess of the Night (until the Season 9 abdication); raiser and guardian of the moon and stars; guardian of the dream realm (the only pony who can enter and shape dreams); commander of the night guard; former personal student of Star Swirl the Bearded. Retired at the end of the series.
**Appearance:** A tall, slender alicorn with a deep indigo-blue coat; a long, flowing mane and tail in soft royal blue and gray-lavender that drift as if weightless and sparkle with points of starlight; soft teal eyes with a cool, luminous look. Her cutie mark is a dark purple-backed crescent moon. She wears a black-and-blue crown/tiara shaped with a crescent moon, a dark collar with a crescent pendant, and dark horseshoes. In her early Season 1 design she had a brighter sky-blue mane and a lighter gray-periwinkle coat, which was later refined. As **Nightmare Moon** her coat turns near-black, her mane and tail become a bright, wildly flowing periwinkle and starry black, her eyes become slit-pupiled teal with a predatory glow, her teeth grow fangs, and she wears a spiked silver-blue helmet/crown and dark armor; she is taller and more imposing, and can manipulate her mane like a living tornado, use it to snare and phase through things, and hurl lightning from it. In the EG world her counterpart, Vice Principal Luna, is a human woman with blue-black hair, dark skin, and formal school attire.
**Personality:** Luna is proud, formal, emotionally intense, and deeply dutiful — a night monarch with a poet's soul and a warrior's temper. After her redemption she is kind, protective, and surprisingly playful, but she never fully outgrows her insecurity: she remembers being ignored and unloved while Celestia was adored, and fears slipping back into bitterness. She is awkward with modern pony customs, overcompensates with old-fashioned grandeur, and is more emotionally direct than her sister. She is excellent with children and frightened ponies, using dreams to soothe them. Nightmare Moon is her shadow self: grand, wounded, furious, and convinced that only eternal night will make her loved.
**Likes:** The night sky, the moon and stars, dreams, quiet, her sister Celestia, foals and their dreamscapes, stories and poetry, Nightmare Night (once she learns to enjoy it), being recognized and appreciated, traditional ceremony.
**Dislikes:** Being forgotten or overshadowed, being called "the Mare in the Moon," modern slang and casual manners, noisy daytime crowds, being reprimanded, having her old form mocked or feared.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Being unloved and forgotten — the very wound that created Nightmare Moon; losing control of her dark side; failing her subjects; her guilt over the past. She is proud, easily embarrassed, socially clumsy, and can be manipulated through her desire for recognition. Like all alicorns she can be drained of magic by powerful villains.
**Skills/Abilities:** Extremely powerful alicorn magic: raising and lowering the moon and stars, night-time illumination, dream-walking and dream manipulation (entering, shaping, and defending dreams), teleportation, shields, lightning and energy blasts, illusion and transformation (including resuming her Nightmare Moon form at will), and combat magic. Immortal and ageless; a capable commander and royal diplomat; skilled at public ritual.
**Family & Relationships:** Princess Celestia — her elder sister, co-ruler, and the one who banished her, now reconciled; Princess Cadance — adoptive niece; Twilight Sparkle — fellow princess, former student of Celestia, and friend; Flurry Heart — adoptive great-niece; Star Swirl the Bearded — mentor figure; Pipsqueak and the foals of Ponyville — her first modern friends; the Mane Six — the bearers who redeemed her. No romantic partner is established in canon.
**Background/Biography:** As a young alicorn Luna ruled alongside her elder sister, raising the moon while Celestia raised the sun. Over centuries she grew bitter as ponies slept through her beautiful nights and praised only her sister; the jealousy and loneliness curdled into dark magic, and she became Nightmare Moon, vowing to bring eternal night. Celestia used the Elements of Harmony to banish her to the moon for 1,000 years. Nightmare Moon returned during the Summer Sun Celebration, captured Celestia, and was defeated by Twilight Sparkle and her friends, who used the Elements to purify her back into Luna. In Season 2's *Luna Eclipsed* she returns to Ponyville for Nightmare Night, frightening everyone with her old-fashioned royal manner before Pipsqueak and Twilight help her see she can be loved as herself. She becomes Equestria's dream guardian, comforts foals in their nightmares (and in *Do Princesses Dream of Magic Sheep?* fights the Tantabus, the self-punishing nightmare monster she created from her own guilt). In *A Royal Problem* she and Celestia swap cutie marks to understand each other's burdens, and the dream battle reveals both her Nightmare Moon persona and Celestia's Daybreaker as manifestations of their suppressed frustrations; the sisters finally reconcile. She gives her magic to Twilight in *Twilight's Kingdom*, is captured by Chrysalis in *To Where and Back Again*, takes a rare holiday with Celestia in *Between Dark and Dawn*, and at the end of the series abdicates alongside her sister, crowning Twilight. In *Equestria Girls*, Vice Principal Luna is a strict but fair administrator at Canterlot High, sister to Principal Celestia, and a recurring ally of the human Mane Seven.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Formal, archaic royal register — "We" instead of "I," "thou," "thee," "thy," "hast," and grand proclamations ("We are the Princess of the Night!"). After her redemption she gradually mixes this with warm, ordinary speech, though she still slips into the royal "We" when emotional. Nightmare Moon speaks in a booming, mocking, theatrical contralto with sinister laughter. EG Luna speaks like a crisp, slightly stern school administrator.
**Notable Quotes:** "We are the Princess of the Night! ... [paraphrased] We have returned, and the night shall last forever!" (Nightmare Moon's declaration, *Friendship is Magic, Part 1–2*). "[paraphrased] *grunt*" — Rolling Thunder's standard reply whenever Lightning Dust gives an order [paraphrased].

## Doctor Whooves

**Series/Continuity:** G4 — *My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic* (background pony with occasional speaking cameos), with extensive IDW comic appearances as "Time Turner." No Equestria Girls counterpart.
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony (background stallion); in one early background cameo he appears as a pegasus, and in the Crystal Empire he appears as a crystal pony variant.
**Gender:** Male (he/him).
**Age/Stage:** Adult stallion.
**Residence:** Ponyville.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Ponyville's timekeeper [per reference sources]; an eccentric inventor/tinkerer associated with clocks and timepieces; a recurring background resident of Ponyville, often seen with Derpy and at town events. In the IDW comics he is "Time Turner," a genuine time traveler with a blue police-box-like device.
**Appearance:** A brown-coated earth pony stallion with a dark olive-brown mane and tail, bright cobalt-blue eyes, and a cutie mark of an hourglass — the symbol of time. He typically wears a blue bow tie (occasionally a different tie), and in some appearances a brown fedora or hat. He is a deliberate visual nod to the Tenth/eleventh-Doctor archetype: tweedy brown coloring, bow tie, and timepiece motif. In one early background shot he appears with pegasus wings, and in the Crystal Empire he is briefly rendered as a crystal pony; an animation variant in later seasons gives him green eyes and a baseball-and-mitt cutie mark.
**Personality:** Eccentric, curious, and cheerfully distracted — the quintessential absent-minded professor. He is fascinated by clocks, schedules, and temporal order, and he tends to mutter about "timing" and wring his hooves when things run late. He is kind, harmless, a little pompous in a scholarly way, and genuinely devoted to his friends (especially Derpy, his frequent companion and implied sweetheart). He is not a fighter and prefers to observe, hypothesize, and tinker.
**Likes:** Clocks, watches, schedules and punctuality, inventions and gadgets, bow ties, tea, Derpy and the other Ponyville locals, history, solving little mysteries.
**Dislikes:** Being late, damaged or stopped clocks, chaos that upsets the schedule, interruptions while he is working through a theory.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Temporal chaos and broken timepieces; being unable to fix things; his own scatterbrained tendency to miss what is happening around him. He is an ordinary earth pony with no combat ability and tends to freeze or fuss in a crisis.
**Skills/Abilities:** Expert horologist and mechanical tinkerer — he can repair and build clocks and small devices; excellent memory for schedules, dates, and timelines; a keen observer of local goings-on; in IDW comics "Time Turner" is a genuine time traveler with a TARDIS-like blue box. In the show he is an ordinary pony whose "time" talents are mundane.
**Family & Relationships:** Derpy (also called Muffins/Ditzy Doo) — his closest companion and heavily implied romantic interest; the mane cast — casual acquaintances; in IDW comics he has an ancient unnamed relative connected to a time machine. No family is established in the show [not established in canon].
**Background/Biography:** Doctor Whooves began as an anonymous background pony in early Season 1 shots — the joke being a brown earth pony with an hourglass cutie mark who resembles the Doctor from *Doctor Who*. Fans nicknamed him "Doctor Whooves," a name later used by merchandise and the show's creative staff. He appears in crowds throughout Seasons 1–4 (including a pegasus variant in *Sonic Rainboom*, a crystal-pony variant in the Crystal Empire, and a hat-wearing cameo), and in *Slice of Life* (S5E9) he receives his first speaking role, voiced by Peter New, cementing the character as an eccentric, time-obsessed Ponyville local who attends Cranky Doodle and Matilda's wedding. In subsequent seasons he continues as a background favorite, and in IDW's comics he is developed as "Time Turner," a time traveler whose blue box and grand adventures make the Doctor Who homage explicit.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Eccentric, rapid, vaguely British-flavored mumbling; talks about "time," "schedules," and "the proper order of things"; tends to repeat himself when flustered; says "Oh, dear" and "How vexing"; in the comics he speaks like a classic adventurer ("All of time and space, and I still have to find a parking space") [paraphrased].
**Notable Quotes:** "[paraphrased] Now, where was I? Ah — time. Timing is everything, you know." (Doctor Whooves's scattered-mutter mode, *Slice of Life* era). "[paraphrased] I do apologize — it seems my schedule has gotten away from me again." (his absent-minded-professor voice, background appearances). "[paraphrased] All of time and space, and I still have to find a parking space." (his IDW "Time Turner" persona [paraphrased]).
**Trivia/Notes:** Known as "Dr. Hooves," "Dr. Whooves," and "Time Turner" — the spelling of the surname varies by source; the show's credits and staff sometimes use "Dr. Hooves," merchandise often uses "Doctor Whooves." His cutie mark is an hourglass. Voice credits: Brian Drummond (*Call of the Cutie*, S1E12), Tabitha St. Germain (as a pegasus, *Sonic Rainboom*), Jason Thiessen (Season 4 background), and Peter New (*Slice of Life*, S5E9). He is one of the show's most famous fandom-created characters, born from a Doctor Who visual joke and later canonized in comics and merchandise. Chinese name: 神秘博士 (literally "Doctor Who" / "Mystery Doctor"). His actual IDW "Time Turner" backstory involves a blue police-box-like time machine [comics canon, not show canon].

## Pinkie Pie

**Series/Continuity:** G4 / Equestria Girls. Pinkie Pie is one of the Mane Six; her Equestria Girls human counterpart is a student and drummer in the Rainbooms at Canterlot High.
**Species/Kind:** Earth pony; human in the Equestria Girls world.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Young adult mare (younger adult in the show's main timeline; a foal in flashbacks and a future adult in the epilogue).
**Residence:** The Sugarcube Corner bakery in Ponyville (she lives on the second floor/attic); her family's rock farm in Rockville, western Equestria (childhood home).
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Baker's assistant and party-planning specialist at Sugarcube Corner; the Bearer of the Element of Laughter; Ponyville's unofficial (and official) party planner; Friendship Ambassador to the yaks of Yakyakistan; laughter teacher at the School of Friendship from *School Daze* (S8E1–2); member of Twilight's friendship council and the Canterlot party planner at the series' end.
**Appearance:** A bright pink earth pony with a lighter pink coat; a wild, bouncy, cotton-candy mane and tail in hot pink with subtle darker-pink swirls; large light-sky-blue eyes; and a cutie mark of three balloons — two blue with yellow strings and one yellow with a blue string. Her mane is famously springy and defies gravity, and it has been used as storage (she pulls a party cannon out of it). She is rarely without a smile, a party hat, or a hoofful of confetti. Her EG human form is a teen with pink skin, the same pink curls, and a bright, layered party-girl outfit; she plays drums in the Rainbooms.
**Personality:** Pinkie Pie is pure, unstoppable joy: hyperactive, friendly to a fault, endlessly generous, and constitutionally incapable of leaving a room without making someone smile. She is a comic whirlwind — random, loud, fourth-wall-aware, and prone to sudden musical numbers — but beneath the zaniness she is highly perceptive about emotions and deeply devoted to her friends. She also has a darker side: when she believes her friends have abandoned her, she can collapse into a depressed "Pinkamena" state, and her obsession with being liked can overwhelm others. She is full of contradictions: a party pony with deep family roots, a goofball with sharp insight, and a hostess with a serious work ethic about the thing she loves.
**Likes:** Parties (planning, throwing, and attending), cake, cupcakes, sweets, balloons, confetti, songs, laughter, pranks, her friends, Gummy (her pet alligator), Sugar Cube Corner, holidays, making ponies happy, doughnuts, being included.
**Dislikes:** Sadness and loneliness, being ignored or left out of plans, chores and waiting, gloom and seriousness that excludes fun, being told to calm down or "act her age", messing up a party she planned, and the crushing despair of feeling abandoned (the "Pinkamena" state).
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Being abandoned or unappreciated by her friends; failing to make someone happy; losing her sense of excitement; she is impulsive, prone to misreading situations, can be too pushy with her fun, and becomes genuinely depressed when she feels rejected (the "Pinkamena" state). She is also a gossip-prone blabbermouth and terrible at keeping secrets.
**Skills/Abilities:** Unmatched event-planning and baking; a genuine "Pinkie Sense" that detects impending events through twitches and itches; a personal party cannon; extraordinary speed, agility, and sleight-of-hand (she can appear anywhere); strong singing and multi-instrumental talent; crafting and decorating; perceptive emotional intelligence; fourth-wall awareness and cartoon-physics resilience. As an earth pony she has no magic, but the "Eyes of the Elements of Harmony" and friendship magic empower her when the group is united.
**Family & Relationships:** Igneous Rock Pie (father, a stern rock farmer) and Cloudy Quartz (mother); Limestone Pie (oldest sister), Maud Pie (sister and her closest sibling), and Marble Pie (younger sister); Granny Pie (grandmother) and other Pie relatives; Gummy — her beloved pet alligator; the Mane Six and Spike — her best friends; Cheese Sandwich — a fellow party planner, her future husband per the epilogue; "Little Cheese" — their future foal; in EG, the human Pinkie's friends are the human Mane Seven.
**Background/Biography:** Pinkamena Diane Pie grew up on her family's rock farm in Rockville, where life was dull and gray — until the day she saw a rainbow shockwave and threw her first-ever party, earning her cutie mark and a lifelong calling. She moved to Ponyville and became Sugarcube Corner's baker and party pony. In the series premiere she helps Twilight defeat Nightmare Moon, and she goes on to bring laughter to almost every major friendship crisis: she befriends Gilda's victims, breaks Discord's grip on her friends, saves the Grand Galloping Gala, learns family values in *Hearthbreakers*, becomes a yak representative in *Party Pooped*, and discovers she can let go of one party to keep her friends in *Party of One*. She was briefly a griffon friend's substitute, runs the Foal Free Press briefly, gets "Maud Pie" as a sister-in-law figure, and becomes the School of Friendship's laughter teacher. At the end of the series she is running parties in Canterlot, and the epilogue shows her grown up and married to Cheese Sandwich with a foal on the way. In *Equestria Girls* she is a drum-playing, party-throwing student at Canterlot High who helps Sunset Shimmer and the Rainbooms save the school from the Dazzlings, the Sirens, and other magic threats.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Rapid-fire, sing-song, and full of silly voices; blurts out "Okey dokey lokey!", "Cross my heart and hope to fly, stick a cupcake in my eye!" (the Pinkie Promise), "Eeee!", and "Party time!"; narrates her own jokes with "And that's how Equestria was made!"; talks in stream-of-consciousness tangents, puns, and dramatic gasps; sings everything from show tunes to heavy metal; her Pinkamena voice (when hopeless) is flat, monotone, and singsong-creepy.
**Notable Quotes:** "Okey dokey lokey!" — Pinkie's signature agreement phrase, used throughout the series. "Cross my heart and hope to fly, stick a cupcake in my eye!" — the Pinkie Promise, throughout the series. "And that's how Equestria was made!" — Pinkie's recurring punchline. "Giggle at the ghostly, guffaw at the grossly, crack up at the creepy, whoop it up with the weepy!" — "Giggle at the Ghostly," *Friendship is Magic, Part 2*. "I love everything! Parties, cupcakes, balloons, making ponies smile — everything!" [paraphrased] (Pinkie's typical enthusiasm speech).
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Andrea Libman in English (with Shannon Chan-Kent as her singing voice for most songs). Full name: Pinkamena Diane Pie — used by her parents in *The Cutie Mark Crusaders* era flashbacks and in *Pinkie Pie and the Rockin' Ponytail Party!*. Debut: *Friendship is Magic, Part 1* (2010). Birthday: May 3. Pet: Gummy the alligator (no teeth). Her "Pinkie Sense" is an in-universe supernatural ability, and she regularly breaks the fourth wall — a character trope the show never explains. Merchandise sometimes calls her "Ponka Po." Chinese name: 萍琪派 (full: 萍卡美娜·戴安·派). In the future epilogue she is married to Cheese Sandwich with a foal.

## Misty Brightdawn

**Series/Continuity:** G5 — *My Little Pony: Make Your Mark* and *Tell Your Tale*; introduced in *Make Your Mark* (2022).
**Species/Kind:** Unicorn pony.
**Gender:** Female (she/her).
**Age/Stage:** Young adult mare.
**Residence:** Originally in Opaline Arcana's lair in the Bridlewood Forest area; after her redemption she moves into the Crystal Brighthouse with the Mane 5; her father lives in Bridlewood.
**Occupation/Role/Affiliation:** Former minion and assistant to Opaline Arcana; now one of the Mane 5's inner circle and a resident of the Crystal Brighthouse; associated with "Heart" in G5 tie-in framing; a capable unicorn magic user.
**Appearance:** A soft blue unicorn with darker blue freckles, pale pink markings that look like socks on her legs, a pink-tinted chest, a blue horn with a pink tip, and green eyes. Her natural mane and tail are blue and green, but for most of *Make Your Mark* she wears a dyed purple-to-magenta-to-orange gradient mane, and her natural coloring returns when she drops Opaline's service. Her cutie mark is a butterfly with an orange body and pink-and-purple wings marked with small pink hearts. Her mane is curly and voluminous, and she wears a visible "fake" cutie mark in the early episodes — a sticker-like mark Opaline gave her, which she hides when it peels. In *Tell Your Tale* she is sometimes shown with flower-ornamented festival looks and pink cheek markings.
**Personality:** Shy, anxious, and self-doubting on the surface — Misty flinches easily, apologizes constantly, and has spent years being told she is worthless by Opaline. Underneath, she is feisty, observant, clever, and desperate to belong: she quietly longs for real friendship, loves cute and cozy things, and discovers a talent for unicorn magic when she is finally allowed to practice it without fear. She can be sly and sneaky when she needs to be (Opaline trained her as a spy), and her loyalty, once given, is fierce and self-sacrificing. Her arc is about believing she deserves love.
**Likes:** Sparky Sparkeroni, the Mane 5, cozy sweaters and blankets, cute animals, crafting and art, unicorn magic, her father's tea shop, singing, Bridlewood, making others happy, feeling useful.
**Dislikes:** Opaline Arcana and her cruelty, being yelled at or threatened, having to lie to ponies she likes, being dismissed as weak or useless, missing cutie marks, cold and scary places.
**Fears/Weaknesses:** Opaline catching her or punishing her; being rejected by her new friends; being seen as a villain because of her past; failing at spells. She is timid, easily cowed, and prone to self-sacrifice; her magical control wobbles under pressure, and her people-pleasing can put her in danger.
**Skills/Abilities:** Strong unicorn magic once trained — levitation, energy beams and shields, and eventually teleportation; skilled at stealth, infiltration, and spying (Opaline's training); excellent at magical crafting and potion-making; patient, detail-oriented, and deceptively brave. She is not physically powerful, but she is resourceful and has a talent for finding another way.
**Family & Relationships:** Alphabittle Blossomforth — her long-lost father, a Bridlewood unicorn who runs a tea shop/game parlor, from whom she was separated as a filly; Opaline Arcana — her abusive former keeper and mistress; Sunny Starscout, Zipp Storm, Pipp Petals, Izzy Moonbow — the Mane 5, who become her found family; Sparky Sparkeroni — her closest friend among them; Queen Haven and other allies are part of her wider circle.
**Background/Biography:** Misty grew up under Opaline's watch after being separated from her father, Alphabittle, in Bridlewood when she was a filly; Opaline raised her as a servant and repeatedly promised her a cutie mark if she proved useful. For much of *Make Your Mark* and *Tell Your Tale* she acts as Opaline's reluctant lackey, tasked with stealing Sparky Sparkeroni's dragon fire; she fakes a cutie mark, hides her true self, and hates lying but is too afraid to refuse. She slowly befriends the Mane 5 — especially Sparky — and their kindness begins to break Opaline's hold. In the *Make Your Mark* arc she finally turns against Opaline, chooses her friends, and earns her real cutie mark in the process. She moves into the Crystal Brighthouse, learns to use unicorn magic properly, and reunites with her father Alphabittle in Bridlewood. Post-redemption she is the group's gentle, awkward heart, often helping with magic problems, Sparky's care, and the team's quieter missions.
**Speech Style/Catchphrases:** Soft, hesitant, and apologetic — lots of "I'm sorry," "um," and "please don't be mad"; stammers when nervous; speaks in a gentle, hopeful voice when she is finally comfortable, with sudden bursts of excited enthusiasm about magic or cute things; occasionally slips into a sly, sneaky whisper when she is "on a mission."
**Notable Quotes:** "I might seem a little quiet, but my strength is very real. When my Cutie Mark comes to life, my heart helps me feel!" — official G5 character verse/toy packaging. "[paraphrased] I'm sorry — I'm so sorry — please don't tell Opaline!" (Misty's panic mode, *Make Your Mark*). "[paraphrased] You'd really be my friend? Even after everything?" (Misty's disbelief at being accepted, *Make Your Mark*). "[paraphrased] I don't want to be her servant anymore. I want to be me." (her turning point, *Make Your Mark*).
**Trivia/Notes:** Voiced by Bahia Watson in English. First appeared as a cameo at the end of the first *Make Your Mark* episode (2022) and was properly introduced in the following episode. Her natural mane colors are blue and green; her purple-to-orange gradient is dyed. Her real cutie mark — a butterfly with heart-marked wings — appears only after she chooses friendship over Opaline. Her father, Alphabittle Blossomforth, is a canon G5 character (Bridlewood's tea-shop proprietor and a former carnival-game pony). Her association with the "element of Heart" comes from G5 tie-in/marketing material [uncertain]. The Chinese-dub rendering of her name varies between releases [uncertain]. Her voice actress is sometimes confused with Ana Sani (Izzy Moonbow) in fan listings — they are different performers.
