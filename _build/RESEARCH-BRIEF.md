# RESEARCH BRIEF — ponies-agent-team-v1.1-plus-95c
## Data-volume members (T0–T6): parallel character-data collection

You are ONE of seven parallel "volume" members of an Agent Team. The Team Lead is `lead`.
You share one workspace (the current working directory) and its filesystem with the Lead and the
other six members.

### 1. Your assignment
- The Lead's spawn message gives you your team id (`T0` … `T6`).
- Open and read `_build/team-<YOUR TEAM ID>.json`. It contains:
  - `team`: your team id,
  - `count`: the exact number of characters you must document,
  - `roster`: the ordered list of EXACT character names you must cover.
- Treat that roster as fixed. Do not add, drop, merge, rename or reorder characters.

### 2. Rules (non-negotiable)
- Work autonomously to completion. NEVER ask the user anything and NEVER use an
  "Ask Questions" / `ask_user_question` / interactive-prompt tool. Approval prompts are disabled;
  do not request them.
- Do NOT spawn subagents or other agents, and do NOT message the user.
- Write exactly ONE file: `ponies-agent-team-v1.1-plus-95c/data/<YOUR TEAM ID>.md`.
  Do not create, modify or delete any other file (no README, no manifest, no shared files).
- All file content must be in English (American English). No Chinese text inside the data file
  except inside official Chinese names/quotes if strictly needed; write "Chinese name: …" in
  English when relevant.
- Remain available after writing: you are the persistent "volume" for your team. The Lead may later
  ask you about characters on your roster; answer from the data you collected (keep it in context).

### 3. Task: collect COMPLETE personal-characteristic information
For every character on your roster, gather complete, canon-accurate personal information from the
My Little Pony universe:

- G4 characters: *My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic*, plus their *Equestria Girls* (EG) human-world
  counterparts where they exist (all EQG characters are marked G4/EQG).
- G5 characters: *My Little Pony: Make Your Mark* / *Tell Your Tale* / *A New Generation*.

Use your own knowledge. Use `web_search` / `web_fetch` when you are not fully certain of a character
(background ponies, one-off characters, IDW-comic characters and G5 supporting characters are the
most likely candidates, e.g. Rolling Thunder, Short Fuse, Glorisa Daisy, Rainbowshine, Shoeshine,
Radiant Hope, Luster Dawn, Minuette, Lemon Hearts, Aloe, Lotus Blossom, Blaize Skysong, Cloudpuff,
Sparky Sparkeroni, Opalina Acrana). Keep web use focused: a few targeted lookups per uncertain
character are enough; do not stall trying to find one perfect source.

Do NOT trim, compress or summarize away information: the deliverable is the full version.
Write substantive content and never leave a field as "N/A" or "unknown" unless the canon truly
does not say, in which case say so in words and mark it `[not established in canon]`.

Special cases to handle inside the relevant fields:
- **Princess Celestia (Daybreaker)** — cover Celestia plus the Daybreaker alternate form.
- **Princess Luna (Nightmare Moon)** — cover Luna plus the Nightmare Moon alternate form.
- **Sci-Twi (Midnight Sparkle)** — the Equestria Girls human Twilight; cover Sci-Twi plus Midnight Sparkle.
- **Sunset Shimmer** — unicorn origin plus the EQG human-world version.
- Any G4 character with an EG human counterpart (`Twilight Sparkle` separately from `Sci-Twi`,
  Rainbow Dash, Rarity, Applejack, Fluttershy, Pinkie Pie, Spike, Apple Bloom, Scootaloo,
  Sweetie Belle, Princess Celestia, Princess Luna, Princess Cadance, Shining Armor, Trixie,
  Big Macintosh, Granny Smith, Cheerilee, Diamond Tiara, Silver Spoon, Flash Sentry, Vinyl Scratch,
  Octavia Melody, Bon Bon, Lyra Heartstrings, Adagio Dazzle, Sonata Dusk, Aria Blaze, Wallflower
  Blush, Juniper Montage, Glorisa Daisy, etc.) — mention the human-world form in Appearance.
- **Opalina Acrana** — request spelling; the canonical English spelling is **Opaline Arcana**. Use
  the roster spelling as the heading, but note the canonical spelling in `Trivia/Notes`.
- If a character name is spelled unusually (e.g. `Glorisa Daisy`), keep the roster heading exactly as
  given and explain the canon spelling/identity in `Trivia/Notes`.

Field requirements:
- **Series/Continuity** — `G4 / Equestria Girls` or `G5`; note EG involvement where applicable.
- **Species/Kind** — pony tribe (earth pony, pegasus, unicorn, alicorn), dragon, changeling,
  griffon, yak, hippogriff/sea pony, human (EG), etc.; note hybrid/transformed states.
- **Gender** — canon gender; pronouns if nonbinary/ambiguous.
- **Age/Stage** — foal / filly / mare / stallion / adult / ancient / ageless; approximate if canon
  does not state it.
- **Residence** — town/city/region (Ponyville, Canterlot, Cloudsdale, Crystal Empire, Everfree,
  Mount Aris, Zephyr Heights, Maretime Bay, Bridlewood, etc.).
- **Occupation/Role/Affiliation** — job(s), royal title, team/organization, special talent tie-in.
- **Appearance** — full physical description: coat/skin colour, mane/tail style and colours, eyes,
  cutie mark (describe it and its meaning), wings/horn, height/build, usual accessories/clothing,
  EQG human form and school outfits where applicable, alternate forms (Daybreaker, Nightmare Moon,
  Midnight Sparkle, Pony of Shadows, crystalline forms, etc.).
- **Personality** — main traits, virtues, flaws, quirks, temper, fears, emotional patterns; enough
  detail for roleplay.
- **Likes / Dislikes / Fears/Weaknesses** — concrete items, activities, foods, people, situations.
- **Skills/Abilities** — talents, magic (named spells where canon), combat/athletic ability,
  cutie-mark talent, leadership, intelligence, special powers; limitations too.
- **Family & Relationships** — named relatives, friends, rivals, enemies, romantic interests,
  mentors, students; how they relate to each other.
- **Background/Biography** — complete canon life story in chronological form: origin, key events,
  arcs, redemptions/falls, later status; include EQG events for EQG characters.
- **Speech Style/Catchphrases** — how they talk (register, accent/dialect, vocabulary, formality,
  verbal tics, catchphrases, ways of addressing others) — important for later language-patch roleplay.
- **Notable Quotes** — at least 2 lines, verbatim canon where possible; otherwise an accurate
  paraphrase marked `[paraphrased]`.
- **Trivia/Notes** — canon spellings/name variants, voice actor, first appearance, meme/fandom notes,
  relationship trivia, anything uncertain marked `[uncertain]`; keep it factual.

Quality bar: about 300–500 words per character, every field substantive. The file will be large
(roughly 5,000–8,000 words); that is expected. Do not pad with filler, do not truncate canon facts.
